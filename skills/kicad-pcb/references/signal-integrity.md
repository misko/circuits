# High-speed digital signal integrity

Use this adapter for digital interconnects whose edge rate makes transmission-
line behavior, return continuity, pair geometry, or timing material. USB is the
common case. It composes with the existing schematic and routing stages; it is
not an RF stage and creates no additional review wait.

RF/microwave work remains a separate source-triggered module. Only a project
whose authoritative RF contract says `rf.enabled: true` uses `rf/rf-context.md`
and the RF schematic, PCB, solver, or fabrication reviews.

## Selection versus applicability

The capability profile selects this reference for progressive disclosure when
`signal_integrity: high_speed_digital`. That selection is not an engineering
PASS. Project source and the owning checks determine which nets apply, their
denominators, and whether evidence is complete. Unknown applicability is
`INCOMPLETE`; ordinary boards must have an evidenced reason before using N/A.

## Source contract

Before placement spend, bind each high-speed path to independently checkable
source facts:

- logical endpoints and every series/protection/switch element in order;
- physical P/N pin identity and polarity through each footprint;
- permitted layers and the continuous reference plane for each section;
- differential impedance intent and the fabrication stack authority that will
  solve it;
- width/gap/via topology generated by the chosen routing engine;
- pair and inter-pair length or skew requirement, with its owning source;
- discontinuities, stubs, connector launch, protection capacitance, and return
  stitching expectations;
- measurement or first-article evidence required to close assumptions that CAD
  cannot prove.

Numeric limits belong in the project rules and cited part/interface authority,
not in this reference. Changing one must stale the relevant source and realized
receipts.

## Placement boundary

Reserve the whole physical corridor before general routing. Check connector
orientation and mating plane, protection/series adjacency, pad escape, pair
ordering, simultaneous layer reservations, reference-plane continuity, and
legal via transitions. Do not compensate for a wrong footprint or derivative
3D model by moving electrically correct pads.

If an escape, return transition, or timing budget cannot fit the locked stack
and placement, backtrack to package, stack, or placement authority. Router
retries cannot create missing physical capacity.

## Realized boundary

The accepted candidate must be reopened from the saved KiCad board and prove:

1. the declared endpoint chain is connected in the correct order and polarity;
2. only allowed layers/via transitions are used;
3. the intended reference plane remains continuous under every section;
4. width/gap and applicable length/skew predicates have nonzero denominators;
5. no orphan stubs or compensating detours hide a worse physical path;
6. fresh native DRC/parity binds the unchanged board and report;
7. fabrication evidence will bind the final stack and differential solve.

These predicates compose into `KICAD-ROUTING` and its final-route receipt.
Candidate, exploration, and promotion mechanics remain owned by
`route-candidate-contract.md` and `route-exploration.md`.

## Fabrication and first article

For manufacturer staging, capture the exact selected stack/material and the
order-time impedance result. Public capability tables establish feasibility,
not the actual order configuration. Where the design claim depends on channel
performance beyond geometric checks, retain a first-article eye, BER, TDR, or
functional stress test with stated fixtures and acceptance limits.
