## Summary of what I traced (primary evidence)

I pulled every clamp/protective net from `06_build/netlists/crow_mic_pod.net`, cross-checked pad-to-net on the physically sealed board `07_releases/v1.0-2026-07-21/source/crow_mic_pod.kicad_pcb`, and re-derived each margin from the `02_parts/*/part.yaml` `limits:` blocks. Unusually for the class of defect I was hunting, the protection topology is **correct AND self-consistent** across schematic, PCB, netlist, part.yaml, ADRs, and the (today-authored) `03_src/rules/electrical_invariants.yaml`:

- **D2 SS14 flyback — orientation verified correct from first principles.** Netlist net 11/10 and PCB both put pad1(K)=BZ_P, pad2(A)=BEEP_RET (`crow_mic_pod.net` L2551-2576; PCB D2 pad1→BZ_P, pad2→BEEP_RET). Low-side switch at central ⇒ BEEP_RET flies high on switch-open ⇒ cathode-on-supply / anode-on-flyback-node is the textbook freewheel. Reverse-biased during ON-time, so no crowbar. D3 (SMAJ6.0A, DNP) shares the identical orientation (PCB pad1→BZ_P). Correct.
- **D1 TPD2E2U06 ESD — correct.** PCB pads: 3→AUDIO_P (IO1), 5→AUDIO_N (IO2), 4→GND, 1/2 NC. R10/R11 (68R) sit between the op-amp outputs and the connector-side clamp (U1.1→R10→AUD_P_I→R13→AUDIO_P=D1/J1.1), so strike current dumps at entry as ADR-0001 claims.
- **RJ45 custom pinout — all 8 contacts match the doc/interop contract pin-for-pin.** `crow_mic_pod.net`: J1.1=AUDIO_P, .2=AUDIO_N, .3=BEEP_5V, .4=5V, .5=GND, .6=BEEP_RET, .7=5V, .8=GND — identical to `DETAIL_DESIGN.md` L15-23, `RJHSE-5384/part.yaml` pin notes, and central's D28 map.
- **Amplifier gains match BOM.** R6=10k/R7=20k ⇒ stage A = 1+10k/20k = 1.5; R8=R9=20k ⇒ stage B = −1; both output DC nodes bias to VMID (2.47 V). Netlist FB_A/FB_B/A_OUT/B_OUT wiring confirms.
- **Ratings all have margin:** SS14 40V/1A vs 5V/150mA; TPD2E2U06 VRWM 5.5V vs 2.47V bias; CMT-8504 5.0 Vo-p rated / 6.0 max vs ~4.7V at the pod after cable drop; OPA1678 CM-range max ~3V vs VMID 2.47V; RJ45 1.5A/contact vs 150mA. No limit is violated.

## Findings

**P2 — ADR-0001 §3 reverse-miswire electrical claim is wrong (stale, inherited from the screw-terminal v1.0).**
Evidence: `01_docs/decisions/0001-input-protection.md` §3 states a 5V/GND swap "reverse-biases only the electro C1 through 100R (survivable briefly)." But the netlist puts U1.8(V+) and U1.4(V-), C6, and C7 **directly** across nets `5V`/`GND` with no series resistance (`crow_mic_pod.net` net 1 nodes: C6.1, C7.1, U1.8; net 15: U1.4, C6.2, C7.2). On a rail swap, U1's substrate diode conducts and the op-amp is reverse-powered — the exposure is not "only C1." Reasoning: the claim is an inherited defect from the v1.0 screw-terminal analysis; the v1.1 RJ45 keying makes individual-pin swap essentially impossible, so real risk is low, but a MANDATORY protection ADR asserting a false electrical fact should be corrected next rev.

**P2 — No populated entry ESD/surge clamp on the 5V, GND, or beeper pairs; only the audio pair is protected.**
Evidence: D1 covers AUDIO_P/N only; D3 (the sole part touching BEEP_5V-side surge, and only across the pair) is DNP (`ARCHITECTURE.md` L71; PCB D3 present but the BOM marks "SMAJ6.0A TVS DNP"). For a 30-35 ft **outdoor** run, the 5V/BEEP entry pins ride on cable bypass (C7 10µF) and the D2 flyback alone. Reasoning: defensible per ADR-0001 §2/§5 (overcurrent + lightning owned at central), but it is a genuine robustness gap worth a one-line risk acceptance in ORDER_README, not silence.

**P2 (cosmetic) — ADR-0001 §1 mislabels the protected lines as "high-impedance."**
Evidence: AUDIO_P/N are op-amp *outputs* through 68R (`crow_mic_pod.net` A_OUT→R10→…→AUDIO_P), i.e. low-Z, not high-impedance. The ESD choice is still correct; only the rationale wording is off.

## Verdict

**ORDER** — every protection chain traces correctly from the netlist to the physical board, docs/BOM/netlist agree, and no rating is exceeded; the only issues are two documentation/robustness notes and one wording nit, none of which is an electrical blocker.
