All numbers below were pulled from the sealed `source/crow_mic_pod.kicad_pcb` with pcbnew, not from the ADRs. DRC/ERC/parity in `verification/` are 0/0/0 and I reproduced the geometry the reports rely on.

## Findings

**P1 — Balanced audio pair is routed asymmetrically; CMRR-limiting for a 30 ft outdoor pair.**
Evidence: `AUDIO_P` = 59.97 mm copper over 7 vias (F.Cu 19 seg / B.Cu 9); `AUDIO_N` = 62.43 mm over 5 vias (F.Cu 25 / B.Cu 6). The two legs of the *balanced* mic pair differ by 2.46 mm length and, more importantly, have different via counts and F/B layer splits — i.e. different parasitic C-to-plane and different loop geometry per leg. Balanced signalling buys its noise immunity from leg symmetry; this pair is the one high-impedance signal leaving the box on 30–35 ft of cable next to a switched beeper. Reasoning: 4 % length is minor, but asymmetric via/layer count converts common-mode ingress to differential. Route the two legs as a matched, same-layer, same-via pair next rev. Not a functional blocker (still differential, DRC-clean).

**P2 — Flyback clamp loop is looser than ADR-0002 claims.**
Evidence: D2(SS14) cathode→BZ1(+) = 10.51 mm, but D2 anode→BZ1(−) = **15.89 mm**, and net `BEEP_RET` totals 37.34 mm of copper. ADR-0002 asserts the pod clamp "keeps the loop 10 mm, not 10 m." The freewheel loop is really ~16 mm on the return side. Reasoning: the ADR's *comparative* claim (pod clamp vs central-only 10 m) still holds and the loop rings far less than a 35 ft pair, so this is an optimization, not a defect — D2 could sit directly against BZ1's pads to halve the loop.

**P2 — Beeper track runs 0.19 mm edge-to-edge alongside an audio track.**
Evidence: closest *same-layer* beeper↔audio approach is 0.64 mm centre-to-centre on B.Cu near (x≈82, y≈70) — with 0.6 mm/0.3 mm widths that is ~0.19 mm edge-to-edge over a ~3 mm parallel run; a separate cross-under sits 0.14 mm planar but on the opposite layer (near-perpendicular, low coupling). Reasoning: the beeper carries the hard 4 kHz/150 mA flyback edge ADR-0002 explicitly worries about coupling into audio. The adjacency is short and occurs in the J1 escape where the pinout (contacts 1/2 audio vs 3/6 beep) forces proximity, so it is tolerable — but it is the exact coupling path the design set out to avoid.

**P2 — Op-amp decoupling loop is not tight.**
Evidence: C6 (100 n) pad-to-U1 pin 8 (V+/5V) = 3.3 mm; C6's GND pad returns through the pour while U1's only ground pin (pin 4) is on the diagonally opposite corner. Reasoning: fine for an audio-band part (OPA1678), but this is a 7 mm-ish supply-decoupling loop, not the <2 mm you'd want; note for layout hygiene.

**P2 — Mechanical fit is a documented CONDITIONAL FIT with real worst-case interference; verify the ADR-0004 arithmetic is honored by the first-article gate.**
Evidence (independently recomputed): recess headroom = 24.00 − 2.50 − 2.20 − 4.00 − 1.60 = **13.70 mm** (ADR arithmetic confirmed). J1 body 13.46 mm ⇒ +0.24 mm nominal, **−0.14 mm at the +0.38 tol extreme**. Jack mating face independently located at board x = 70.0 (NPTH posts at x = 75.46 minus the 5.46 mm face-to-post offset); exposed plug tip ≈ 58.0 mm vs recess west edge 56.75 ⇒ **+1.25 mm** — matches ADR-0004. Reasoning: numbers check out, but the jack can interfere with the lid at tolerance corner; this is correctly parked on the FIRST-ARTICLE lid-close gate. That physical gate must actually be run before fleet build — it is the only thing standing between this and a non-closing lid. J1 has no STEP model, so no render ever showed the tallest part.

**P2 — ESD clamp D1 is ~10.7 mm from the audio contacts.**
Evidence: D1 IO3(AUDIO_P) pad to J1 pin 1 = 10.51 mm (centre-centre ~10.75 mm). Reasoning: far by "as close to the connector as possible" datasheet guidance, but the THT jack body physically blocks anything closer, topology is clamp-first (J1→D1→68 R→amp), and pinout (pads 1,2 = NC; 3=IO1; 4=GND w/ 2 GND vias within 2 mm; 5=IO2) is correct — a documented, defensible accepted deviation.

## Non-findings I checked (so they aren't re-litigated)
- **Thermal/ampacity:** beeper nets `BZ_P/BEEP_RET/BEEP_5V` are 0.60 mm wide — >10× margin over 150 mA peak; RJ45 contact 1.5 A ≫ 150 mA. No copper-width problem.
- **U1 SOIC-8 land (twin_report "PAD-GEOM 4.95 vs 5.42"):** footprint is stock `Package_SO:SOIC-8_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm`, toe-to-toe 6.90 / heel-to-heel 3.00 mm — IPC-correct for the OPA1678 D package. The JLC-library delta is a library nominal difference, not an assembly risk. Benign.
- **Plane continuity:** both F.Cu (3249 mm²) and B.Cu (3853 mm²) are solid GND pours; audio has a continuous reference on the opposite layer everywhere (the concern is the via-handoff asymmetry in P1, not a plane cut).
- **Polarity:** D2 cathode→BZ_P (supply) is correct for the far-end low-side-switch flyback; C1 pad1(+)=5VF correct. Silk F1–F10 are prior-gate results (fixed/dispositioned), not re-reported.
- **Transducer BZ1** (4.0 mm tall) intrudes 1.14 mm past the recess into the 7.90 mm perimeter band — clears easily; its top sound port faces the sealed IP68 lid (muffled, presumably intended for a self-test beeper).

## Verdict: ORDER
The board is DRC/ERC/parity-clean, mechanically gated, and every finding is a next-rev performance refinement (audio-pair symmetry, clamp-loop tightness, beeper crosstalk) rather than a manufacturing or functional defect — provided the ADR-0004 first-article lid-close gate is genuinely executed before the fleet is built, since the worst-case jack fit is −0.14 mm.
