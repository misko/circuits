## 3. Protection chain — traced from the netlist, not from a diagram

MEASURED (by me, s-expression parse of `source/cooksense.net`: 198 nets,
239 components):

```
J_PWR.1 ──5V_IN──> F1.1 (MF-MSMF200L-2 PPTC)      D_ESD_IN.1 on 5V_IN
F1.2    ──5V_FUSED──> Q_REV.3 (D)                 D_REVCLAMP.1 (K) on 5V_FUSED, .2 (A) → GND
Q_REV.2 (S) ──5V_RPP──> U_EFUSE.3/.4 (IN)         R_OVT.1, C_EFIN.1 on 5V_RPP
U_EFUSE.5 (OUT) ──5V_PROTECTED──> D_TVS.1 (K), CE1, C_IN1/2, C_LDOIN,
                                  U_LDO.3 (VIN), Q_COIL.2 (S), R_STOPRAIL.1,
                                  R_HSG.2, J_LOADCELL.1
```

**Every orientation checks out, and I derived each rather than reading a label:**

- **`Q_REV` reverse-polarity P-FET is the right way round.** Pad 1 (G) = `GND`,
  pad 2 (S) = `5V_RPP` (load side), pad 3 (D) = `5V_FUSED` (source side). A
  P-channel body diode runs D→S, so it conducts *forward* at power-on; with G at
  GND and S at +5 V, V_GS = −5 V (inside AO3401A's ±12 V gate abs-max) so the
  channel enhances and shorts the diode out. Under reverse polarity the body
  diode is reverse-biased and V_GS ≈ 0: it blocks. **The opposite arrangement —
  S on the input side — would have shipped a permanently-conducting body diode,
  and that is why this one is worth deriving rather than glancing at.**
- **`D_REVCLAMP` (SS34) is a crowbar, downstream of the fuse.** Pad 1 (K) on
  `5V_FUSED`, pad 2 (A) on `GND`. A reverse hookup forward-biases it and the
  fault current is forced *through* F1. Correct placement — upstream of F1 it
  would bypass the fuse. See P2-1 for what is *not* proven about it.
- **`D_TVS` (SMBJ5.0A, unidirectional) directionality is correct** — pad 1 (K)
  on `5V_PROTECTED`, pad 2 (A) on GND — and it is on the **eFuse OUTPUT**, so
  the OVLO cutoff is upstream of the part it must protect. That ordering is what
  makes the ADR-0021 threshold argument mean anything; the reverse ordering is
  the common mistake and it is not present here.
- **`D_ESD_IN` (PESD5V0S1BA) is bidirectional** (both pins are cathodes per the
  dossier's datasheet read), so it has no orientation to get wrong. See P2-2 for
  its rating.

### 3.1 eFuse OVLO setpoint — recomputed at the worst-case corner

MEASURED (by me) from the shipped BOM (`R_OVT` 100 kΩ C270658, `R_OVB` 26.1 kΩ
C407739, both ±0.5 %) and the `TPS259573DSGR` dossier's cited SLVSE57C
`V_OVLO(R)` = 1.13/1.20/1.27 V, `I_EN` = ±0.1 µA:

    k_nom = 26.1/126.1 = 0.206979  →  trip = 1.20/0.206979 = 5.798 V nominal

with ±0.5 % tolerance + ±100 ppm/°C × 45 °C = ±0.45 % drift (±0.95 % per leg)
and ±2.07 mV of I_EN error across the 20.698 kΩ source impedance:

| corner | my figure | bound it must clear | margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| earliest trip `(1.13 − 0.00207)/k_max` | **5.368 V** | 5.250 V supply ceiling — must not nuisance-trip | **+118 mV** |
| latest trip `(1.27 + 0.00207)/k_min` | **6.239 V** | SMBJ5.0A `V_BR` min 6.400 V @ 25 °C | **+161 mV** |
| same | 6.239 V | `V_BR` min at −20 °C = 6.400 × (1 − 0.00041 × 45) = **6.282 V** | **+43 mV** |
| same | 6.239 V | DIP05-1A72-13L coil 7.5 V max | **+1.26 V** |

**Reproduces the tree's numbers exactly.** Two things the arithmetic alone does
not say:

1. The divider sits on `5V_RPP`, i.e. **behind** F1 + `Q_REV`, so the trip
   referred to `J_PWR` is ~80 mV *higher* than these figures under load — the
   nuisance-trip margin is understated, which is the safe direction.
2. The **−20 °C `V_BR` margin is +43 mV on a 6.24 V threshold — 0.7 %.** It
   holds, but it is the tightest ratings pair on the board and it depends on
   both legs staying ±0.5 %. E-INV's `part_value` asserts on `R_OVT`/`R_OVB` are
   live (168/168 measured), so a *value* substitution is caught; a **tolerance-
   grade** substitution is caught by nothing but the pinned LCSC codes.

## 4. The safety chain — verified out of the netlist, gate by gate

MEASURED (by me). `SN74LVC1G11` is a 3-input AND in SOT-23-6 with inputs on pads
1/3/6, Y on 4, GND on 2, VCC on 5 — which is what the netlist uses:

```
KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED = U_AND3( AND1 , AND2 , FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR )
  AND1 = U_AND1( MODE_AUTO_HW , WD_OK , ESTOP_OK )
  AND2 = U_AND2( TEMP_OK , MCU_RELAY_ENABLE , HOST_AUTH )
```

Seven terms, exactly as `ARCHITECTURE.md` and ADR-0025 claim. The rail is gated a
**second** time in the physical world: `KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED → J_MODE.3 → external
DPDT pole A → J_MODE.4 → COIL_EN_IN → R_COILENS 680 Ω → COIL_EN →
Q_COILDRV (2N7002) → HS_GATE_COIL → Q_COIL (AO3401A) → 5V_KEY_RELAY`, with
`R_HSG` 100 kΩ holding the P-FET gate at `5V_PROTECTED` so the default is OFF.

**Fault latch** — `U_LATCHA` NAND(`FAULT_SET_N`, `FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR`) → `FAULT`;
`U_LATCHB` NAND(`REARM_PULSE_N`, `FAULT`) → `FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR`. A correct
cross-coupled NAND SR latch whose active-low set is `FAULT_SET_N` =
AND(`WD_OK`, `ESTOP_OK`, `TEMP_OK`). Set is level-sensitive, so while any fault
persists no re-arm can clear it. Correct.

**Re-arm one-shot — I got this wrong on my first pass, then measured it, and I
am writing that down because the wrong reading is the plausible one.** The '221
pin order is *not* symmetric between sections: section 1 is A(1)/B(2)/CLR̄(3),
but section 2 is A(9)/B(10)/**CLR̄(11)**. The netlist has pin 9 = `REARM_N`,
pin 10 = `3V3`, pin 11 = `WD_OK`, pin 12 = `REARM_PULSE_N`. Read with pin 11 as
`2A` — the mirror-image assumption — the board would have **no path to clear the
fault latch in the healthy state at all**, a P0-shaped conclusion. Read
correctly, with pin 9 = `2A` and pin 11 = `CLR̄` (which is both the standard
74x221 pinout and what `02_parts/CD74HC221M96/part.yaml`'s figure-verified pin
map says):

- `B` tied high and `CLR̄` = `WD_OK` high (healthy) ⇒ a **falling edge on
  `REARM_N`** (button pressed) fires one bounded pulse. Correct, and it is the
  intended edge-detect.
- `WD_OK` low ⇒ section 2 is **held in reset**, so `REARM_PULSE_N` cannot pulse
  and the latch cannot be cleared while the watchdog is unhealthy. A real and
  well-chosen interlock.

Pulse width, MEASURED from BOM values: `R_OS2` 10 kΩ × `C_OS2` 1 µF × K ≈ 0.7 =
**~7 ms**. Ample, and non-retriggerable so bounce inside the pulse is ignored.

**Caveat found while doing this:** the same `part.yaml`'s *notes* still describe
section 2 as **UNUSED** — `9: 2A_N # tie N3V3 (blocks section-2 trigger)`,
`11: 2R_N # tie GND (section 2 held in reset)`, and a gotcha reading "Unused
section 2: 2A_N=VCC, 2B=GND, 2R_N=GND". Section 2 *is* the re-arm edge detector.
The pin NAMES are right (which is what saved me); the usage prose is pre-ADR-0020
and, followed literally by a future agent "restoring" the dossier's intent, would
tie `2R_N` to GND and destroy the re-arm path. Folded into **P1-3**.

**`OS_CLR_N` = AND(`ESTOP_OK`, `STOP_REQ_N`)** — MEASURED at `U_OSCLR.1` =
`ESTOP_OK`. That is ADR-0025 D2 and it is in the netlist. `DETAIL_DESIGN.md`
still says `DOOR_OK` (P1-3).

**Restrictive defaults** — `J_ESTOP` is `SM03B-SRSS-TB`, pinout 1 = GND,
2 = 3V3, 3 = `ESTOP_RAW_IN` (MEASURED), with `R_ESTOPPD` 470 Ω to GND and
`R_ESTOPS` 680 Ω in series to the HC14. Unplugged, the node sits at ~1 mV against
a 0.5 V V_T− floor ⇒ `ESTOP_OK` LOW ⇒ inert. The "plug bridges 2–3, never 1–2"
warning is the first thing in the ORDER_README, which is where it belongs.

## 5. The analog over-temperature channel — recomputed from scratch

MEASURED (by me): `TH_CAM_A` carries `R_REF0` 10 kΩ from `3V3_ANALOG`,
**`R_CLMPA` 22 kΩ to GND**, `R_HYS1` 1 MΩ from `TEMP_OK`, `R_SER0` 1 kΩ to the
ADC, and the external NTC through `J_THERM_A.5`. Threshold `TCAM_THRESH` =
3.3 × 10/78 = **0.42308 V**.

Solving `(R_NTC ∥ 22 k)/(10 k + R_NTC ∥ 22 k) = 0.42308/3.3` gives
R_NTC = **1576 Ω**, and with B25/85 = 3987:

    T = 1/(1/298.15 + ln(1576/10000)/3987) − 273.15 = 72.80 °C

**MEASURED 72.80 °C, not the 74.9 °C that `ARCHITECTURE.md` line 64 and
`DETAIL_DESIGN.md` §1 both still state.** The whole difference is the 22 kΩ
clamp, which those documents predate. The trip is 2.1 °C *earlier* — the safe
direction, still inside the brief's 70 (stop) / 75 (hard) band — and
`ORDER_README.md` **already carries `72.8 (hw trip)`** in its host-inversion
table, so the fact is known and dispositioned. It is the design *documents* that
are stale (P1-3), not the board.

Two more numbers I recomputed while there:

- **Open-NTC bound vs the LMV393 common-mode ceiling.** Open node =
  VCC × 22/32; at `3V3_ANALOG` vout_max 3.399 V with ±1 % legs that is
  **2.351 V**, against a VICR ceiling of VCC − 0.7 = **2.699 V** — inside by
  348 mV. The tree states 2.2829 V against 2.50 V, which is a nominal-3.3 V
  reading against a 3.2 V ceiling; **both the reading and the ceiling scale with
  VCC, so my corner is the more conservative one and it still passes.** The 22 kΩ
  bleed is genuinely what makes the open-detect comparator work.
- **Hysteresis is ~8× smaller than `DETAIL_DESIGN.md` §1 claims.** The Thevenin
  impedance at the trip node is 10 k ∥ 22 k ∥ 1576 = **1282 Ω**, not the 10 kΩ the
  document uses, so `R_HYS1` 1 MΩ gives (3.3 − 0.423) × 1282/1 M = **3.69 mV ≈
  0.32 °C**, not "≈33 mV ≈ ~2 °C". Harmless — `TEMP_OK` LOW sets the fault latch
  on the first excursion, so chatter is not a failure mode — but it is another
  stale derivation in the same document (P1-3).

## 6. The 3V3 LDO rail — graded from source, not accepted

**Load.** `power_tree.yaml` itemises 116.859 mA of cited maxima, ×1.5 design
margin = 175.3 mA, declared **0.200 A**. I did not re-derive all forty terms, but
I checked the structural claims against the netlist and they hold: 76 pads on
`3V3` and 22 on `3V3_ANALOG` (MEASURED); `R_ESTOPPD` is **470 Ω** and
`R_COILENPD` is **680 Ω** in the shipped BOM (MEASURED — these are the two terms
an earlier draft carried as 100 kΩ, a 213× error, and they are now right); all
four `Q_SW*` sources are on `3V3` (MEASURED), so the switched sensor rails do
come out of this LDO. The direction of every correction in that file is *upward*
in the underlying sum and downward only in the rounding, which is the honest
shape.

**Dropout.** `vin_min` 4.728 V − `vout_max` 3.399 V = **1329 mV** against a cited
1300 mV ⇒ **PASS by 29 mV** (MEASURED — I ran E-TOPO and it printed exactly
this). I could not rebuild ADR-0027's nodal solve, but I reproduced its two most
checkable claims:

- **"No 5 V net has a zone anywhere on this board."** MEASURED (by me, pcbnew
  over `source/cooksense.kicad_pcb`): there are exactly **four filled zones** —
  `GND` on F.Cu (2701.43 mm²), `GND` on B.Cu (7385.83), `GND` on In1.Cu
  (8465.52), `3V3` on In2.Cu (8420.03). Every other zone object carries no net
  and zero filled area. **Confirmed: tracks are the entire 5 V conductor.**
- **The `5V_IN` segment.** MEASURED: 22.120 mm of 0.500 mm F.Cu with **0 vias** —
  the exact geometry ADR-0027 names. ρ·L/(w·t) at 1.72e-8 Ω·m and 35 µm, ×1.216
  for 75 °C, gives **26.4 mΩ** against the file's 26.29. Reproduces.

The 1300 mV is a 0.8 A datasheet figure applied to a 0.2 A rail; the low-load
dropout is honestly **OWED**. At +29 mV that OWED bench measurement is now the
load-bearing one, and the file says so itself.

**Dissipation and junction temperature — this is P1-1.** MEASURED (E-TOPO, run by
me): `PD 410 mW ((Vin_max 5.25 − Vout_min 3.201) × 0.2 A) vs rating 497 mW (82%)`.
Adding the LDO's own quiescent burn (Vin_max × Iq_max = 5.250 × 0.011 =
57.75 mW) at the cited θ_JA 90 °C/W and the declared Ta 75 °C:

    Tj = 75 + (0.4098 + 0.0578) × 90 = 117.08 °C   →   7.9 °C of margin to 125 °C

which agrees with ADR-0026 and with the `iout_max_A` block of `power_tree.yaml`,
and **disagrees with the same file's concluding block** (P1-1).

**Is 7.9 °C enough?** Break-even is θ_JA = (125 − 75)/0.4676 = **106.9 °C/W**,
19 % worse than the package figure — and ds1117's own footnote says θ_JA "can
vary from 46 °C/W to **>90 °C/W** depending on mounting technique", so the
datasheet does not exclude it, and θ_JA on this mounting is declared OWED.
Against that: Ta = 75 °C is the *enclosure HARD limit* (the system should be
stopping there); Vin_max 5.25 V and Iout 0.2 A cannot coexist (5.25 V is the
unloaded corner); Vout_min 3.201 V is the lowest trim; and the derived load is
0.117 A. At realistic values (Vin 5.12 V, Vout 3.30 V, Iout 0.117 A, Iq 5 mA typ)
I get **Tj = 96.5 °C**. The stack is conservative several times over. I do not
grade it a defect; it is a **P2-class watch item** that the already-scheduled G4
bench session retires.
