## 0. METHOD — TWO INSTRUMENTS THAT DO NOT SHARE A METHOD (canon M1)

| | instrument A | instrument B |
|---|---|---|
| source | the **SHIPPED gerbers** — `fab/cooksense-{F_Paste.gtp, B_Paste.gbp, F_Mask.gts, B_Mask.gbs, F_Cu.gtl, B_Cu.gbl}` and `fab/cooksense-PTH.drl` | `pcbnew` on `source/cooksense.kicad_pcb` |
| method | an independent RS-274X text parser (aperture macros `RoundRect`, `C`, `R`, `O`; D03 flashes; G36/G37 regions) + Excellon tool-table read | `PCB_VIA` enumeration + `PAD::HitTest()` |
| what it answers | is the **drilled hole** under a printed paste aperture; is the mask open on each face | which **land** owns the via, its size, net and layers |

**The parser is self-checked.** It asserts `len(flashes) == count of D03 commands
in the file` for every layer and refuses to return otherwise — an earlier
version silently lost **206 of 700** `F_Paste` flashes (splitting on `*` glued
the closing `%` of each `%TO.P,…*%` attribute block onto the next flash
command), which would have reported ~45 false "no paste over the hole". That
class of miss is exactly what a self-check catches and an eyeball does not.

**The classifier was proved able to fail.** Negative control: one real via
(`C_LATCHG.1`, fully under paste) walked out of its window in 0.025 mm steps
reaches **all four classes** and the transitions land exactly on the claimed
thresholds (A→B at signed distance −0.075 mm, B→C at +0.075, C→D at +0.125),
then re-enters the 0402's *other* terminal window at +0.625 mm. A classifier
that cannot change its verdict is worthless; this one can.

**Cross-check, A against B:** every one of pcbnew's 1097 vias is present in the
shipped `PTH.drl` at the 0.150 mm `ViaDrill` tool — **0 absent of 1097**. The
two instruments agree on the population before they are asked to disagree about
its meaning.

## 2. THE MASK SIDE, ESTABLISHED PER VIA — NOT ASSUMED

On rx2-v2 the decisive fact was *which face is open*. Established here for all
275 individually, from the shipped mask gerbers:

| | count |
|---|---|
| `F_Mask` (component side) **OPEN** over the hole | **275 / 275** |
| `B_Mask` (solder side) open over the hole | **0 / 275** |
| open on both faces | 0 |
| tented on both faces | 0 |
| **nearest `B_Mask` flash to any of the 275, minimum over all 275** | **2.519 mm** |

**`F_Mask` contains no circular aperture at 0.150 or 0.250 mm** — its circular
apertures are 0.350, 1.500, 1.700, 1.770, 2.200, 2.700, 2.800 mm, plus 35
`RoundRect`, 7 `R` and 1 `O`. **There is no per-via tenting relief anywhere on
the board.** Every one of the 275 openings is the **host land's own mask
window**, which is larger than the via and therefore removes the tenting the
board thinks it has. All 275 host lands are on the **component side**; every
component on this board is top-side (`B_Paste` carries **zero** flashes,
board-wide).

**So the board declares tenting and is fabricated open on the exact face the
solder arrives from.** `source/cooksense.kicad_pcb` `(setup …)`:

```
(tenting (front yes) (back yes))
(covering (front no) (back no))
(plugging (front no) (back no))
(capping no)
(filling no)
```

A global tenting flag cannot survive the mask aperture of the pad the via sits
in — the pad's own opening removes the mask over the via regardless — and
nothing in this pipeline compares the two. Whole board: **816 of 1097 vias are
genuinely tented on both faces; 281 are open on the component side**; 275 of
those 281 are inside a land.

## 5. THE SOLDER ARITHMETIC — MEASURED FROM THE SHIPPED STENCIL FILE

Board thickness **1.600 mm** (`source/cooksense.kicad_pro`, `board_thickness`).
One 0.150 mm × 1.600 mm barrel = **0.02827 mm³**. Solder delivered =
(aperture area from `F_Paste.gtp`) × (stencil thickness) × **50 % metal by
volume**, SAC305 T4.

| stencil | barrels ≥ 100 % of their joint | ≥ 50 % | worst |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.100 mm | **133** | 207 | **3.86 ×** (`U_EFUSE.5`) |
| 0.120 mm | **133** | 201 | **3.21 ×** |
| 0.127 mm | **133** | 199 | **3.04 ×** |
| 0.150 mm | 132 | 144 | 2.57 × |

**133 joints on this board have a barrel that can hold more solder than the
stencil prints for the entire joint, and the number barely moves with stencil
thickness** — because it is set by the land:aperture ratio, not the print.

Worst, at a 0.120 mm stencil:

| ref.pad | land | paste aperture | barrel : joint | net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `U_EFUSE.5` / `U_EFUSE.2` | 0.600 × 0.250 | 0.1466 mm² | **3.21 ×** | `5V_PROTECTED` / `EF_OVLO` |
| `D_ESD_IN.2`, `D_LCCLK.1/.2`, `D_COILEN.2`, `D_LCDAT.2`, `D_ESTOP.2` | 0.600 × 0.450 | 0.2591 mm² | 1.82 × | GND, `LC_CLK` |
| 12 × `R_*.2` (0402) | 0.540 × 0.640 | 0.3300 mm² | 1.43 × | `3V3`, GND |

**Board level:** total printed stencil area **606.4 mm²**; at a 0.100 mm
stencil that is 30.318 mm³ of solder, and the 275 open barrels can absorb
**7.775 mm³ = 25.6 %** of all the solder printed on the board (21.4 % at
0.120 mm). This is not a handful of joints — it is a quarter of the solder
budget with somewhere else to go.

### 7a. DECLARED `catalog_absence` — THERE IS NO PUBLISHED PRICE

**JLCPCB does not publish a price for Epoxy Filled & Capped (POFV) on 4-layer
boards: no setup fee, no rate, no unit, no POFV-specific MOQ.** Verified
against the three places it would have to live:

1. `https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities` — capability only, no price.
2. `https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/in-what-cases-will-there-be-charged-extra`
   (page states *Last updated on Jan 27, 2026*) — JLC's exhaustive 15-case FR-4
   extra-charge list. Grepped the full page for `via cover`, `POFV`, `epoxy`,
   `resin`, `plugged`, `plated over`, `copper paste`: **zero matches.** The page
   itself says *"Basically, all the prices are based on the online calculator
   when you select the corresponding options … but there are some exceptions."*
   Via covering is not one of the named exceptions.
3. The order form's own client code — the cart has named fee line items
   (*"Buried Via Fee"*, *"HDI Structure Fee"*, *"Lamination Fee"*, *"Special
   Process Fee:"*) and **no via-covering fee line item exists.**

It is a server-computed delta inside the instant calculator with no named line
item. **A number can only be obtained by running the live calculator in a
browser or asking JLC.** General PCB minimum quantity from the form is **5 pcs**
(*"custom quantities like 3pcs or 8pcs are not supported"*); nothing
POFV-specific.

**IPC-7093A's numeric bands are PAYWALLED and are NOT cited anywhere in this
review.** The percentages circulating on vendor blogs as "IPC-7093 numbers" are
unverified secondary sources and were refused. IPC-4761 via-protection *type
names* (Type I tented … Type VII filled and capped) are used as vocabulary
only, with no numeric acceptance criteria attached.

**Question 5 is not housekeeping for this board.** Cooksense's vias are 0.150 mm

## 8. VERDICT

**As fabricated, this board does not assemble correctly.** 271 of 275
via-in-pad instances have a drilled, unfilled, uncapped 0.150 mm barrel opening
into the printed paste window of a joint that is being soldered, on the face the
solder arrives from, with no mask dam and no plug. 133 of those barrels can hold
more solder than the stencil prints for the whole joint. The four exceptions are
test points with nothing soldered to them.

That is not a "recorded" P2. **A P2/"recorded" was answering the question *is
this worth writing down*; the question was *will the joints form*.**

**Severity: P1.** It is not P0 only because the remedy moves no copper.

## 11. THE FLEET GAP — WHY THIS RECURRED THREE TIMES

Three boards, three independent discoveries, three different dispositions, one
missing gate. There is **no check anywhere in the pipeline that compares the
board's via-covering declaration against the mask and paste apertures actually
emitted.**

**And the population is not accidental — it is asked for, in this board's own
source.** `03_src/cooksense/route.yaml` `stitch.pad_rescue`:

```yaml
  pad_rescue:
    nets:
      - {net: GND, layer: In1.Cu}
      - {net: 3V3, layer: In2.Cu}
    via_in_pad: true
```

with `route_and_stitch_generic.py`'s own comment: *"A via SERVES a plane pad
only if its barrel drops INSIDE the pad — a via-in-pad bonds pad→plane. A
merely-NEARBY via … does NOT connect this pad; counting proximity stranded 5
false-served plane pins (cooksense 2026-07-23)."* That mechanism is correct and
it is why **231 of the 275** are on GND (154) or a power rail (77).

**So the correction here is to rx2-v2's version of this finding, not a repeat of
it.** rx2-v2's lens said `fab_tiers.yaml`'s `via_in_pad:` *"has no consumer"*.
It does have consumers — two:

- `skills/kicad-pcb/scripts/escape_check.py` reads it as a **permission** that
  makes a ring escape feasible (`ring = min_via_diameter + min_space <= pitch
  or tier.get("via_in_pad", False)`);
- `route_and_stitch_generic.py` reads `via_in_pad` from the stitch config,
  **defaulting to `True`**, and deliberately drops barrels inside plane pads.

**What has no consumer is the OBLIGATION the permission creates.** The whole
repo contains the string `POFV` in exactly two places, both comments, both
unread by anything:

```
skills/kicad-pcb/references/fab_tiers.yaml:121:  via_in_pad: true   # POFV: resin-filled + capped, paid option
skills/kicad-pcb/references/proven-parts.yaml:205:  "0.5mm QFN-48: advanced tier REQUIRED (0.25/0.15 POFV) — …"
```

`tenting` has **zero** consumers in any Python file in the repo.

The remaining inputs all exist:

- The `.kicad_pcb` has `(tenting …)`, `(plugging …)`, `(filling …)`,
  `(capping …)` — **and no gate compares them to `F_Mask`/`B_Mask`.**
- The gerbers carry the truth and are already exported at seal.

A gate that reads the shipped `*_Paste` and `*_Mask` gerbers plus the drill file
and fails on any hole under paste that the order does not declare as
filled-and-capped would have caught all three at v1.2. **Proposed, not applied —
`skills/` changes are not this review's to make.** See the report accompanying
this file for the proposed `fab_tiers.yaml` provenance patch and the gate sketch.
