## What blocks the order

**R-01 — P0 — J_ESTOP and J_DOOR are an identical, unkeyed, adjacent, pin-compatible pair on the two safety inputs. A cross-plug is electrically silent.**
Seen in `twin_top.png` (east edge), `verification/render_top_bare.png` east edge, `fab/cpl.csv`, `fab/bom.csv`.

Both are C189896, JST GH `SM05B-GHS-TB`, 5-pin, 1.25 mm, horizontal, rotation 90, on the same east edge at (197.0, −70.88) and (197.0, −81.76) — **10.88 mm apart**. No keying, no colour difference, no mechanical differentiation of any kind. Their pinouts:

| pin | J_ESTOP | J_DOOR |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3V3 | 3V3 |
| 2 | ESTOP_RAW | DOOR_RAW |
| 3 | GND | GND |
| 4 | GND | DOOR_RAW |
| 5 | GND | GND |
| MP | GND | GND |

Swap the two harnesses and **power and every ground land correctly**. Nothing shorts, nothing smokes, no fault is raised — the board comes up and both signals read "valid", with the E-STOP loop driving the door input and vice versa. On an interlock whose job is to refuse to arm with the door open, that is a silent dangerous failure, and it is reachable by one wrong hand movement during service.

The only defence is the silk refdes, and that defence is itself broken (see R-04): `J_DOOR`'s own label sits 2.87 mm from J_DOOR and 2.80 mm from J_ESTOP — marginally **closer to the wrong connector**. `J_ESTOP`'s label is 4.14 mm from J_ESTOP and 1.93 mm from U_CAND2. `D_DOOR`'s label is 7.05 mm from D_DOOR and 2.30 mm from J_ESTOP.

Fix: give one of the two a different housing (the board already carries a ZH 4-pin at J_MODE and an XH 5-pin at J_LOADCELL, so a second family is already in the BOM), or a keyed/shrouded variant, or at minimum different pin counts. This is a layout and part-selection change, so it must be settled before the board is made.

## P1

**R-02 — Two refdes are printed into a milled notch and will not exist on the board.**
Seen in `render_top_bare.png` NE region and confirmed against `fab/cooksense-Edge_Cuts.gm1` and `fab/cooksense-F_Silkscreen.gto`.

The east edge carries a notch at x 191.5–200.0, y −49.8…−48.8 (1.0 mm tall, 8.5 mm deep — it is part of the board outline; there is no board there).

- `J_MODE` refdes occupies x[194.24, 197.66] y[48.70, 49.30] — **entirely inside the notch**. It will not be printed at all. The connector it names is 11 mm south at (195.95, 60.0).
- `D_COILEN` refdes occupies x[188.93, 193.49] y[49.30, 49.90] — the tail from x 191.5 is in the notch. Roughly "D_COIL" survives, "EN" is routed away. The part is 7 mm south at (191.21, 56.6).

66 silkscreen draw vertices fall inside the notch (x 191.52–197.52, y −49.56…−48.81).

This matters beyond cosmetics: `D_COILEN` is one of the four parts `twin_report.csv` marks **POLARITY-FIT-BLIND** — "no usable polarity marking on our footprint … ONLY the human order-preview gate stands between this part and a 180deg reversal". The one part with no polarity evidence is also the one with no readable refdes for a rework tech.

**R-03 — The DRC gate cannot see any of this: all four silkscreen checks are on the ignore list.**
`verification/drc.json` `ignored_checks` contains `silk_overlap`, `silk_over_copper`, `silk_edge_clearance`, and `text_thickness`. The "0 violations / 0 unconnected / 0 parity" result is therefore silent on silkscreen by construction — `silk_edge_clearance` is the exact rule that exists to catch R-02, and it was switched off. Every finding in this section was invisible to the machine.

**R-04 — Systemic refdes displacement: the labels do not identify their parts.**
Measured from `source/cooksense.kicad_pcb`, visible in every tile of `render_top_bare.png`.

- 228 visible refdes. **163 (71 %) are more than 3 mm from the part they name. Median displacement 5.20 mm. Worst 12.73 mm** (R_SER2, R_SER5, R_SER6, C_KR).
- Of the 75 refdes whose part has a courtyard, **16 sit closer to a different part than to their own**:

| refdes | dist to own part | dist to nearest other | that part |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q_SWRHE | 9.30 mm | **1.80 mm** | U_CAND1 |
| D_DOOR | 7.05 mm | **2.30 mm** | J_ESTOP |
| J_MODE | 5.75 mm | **1.05 mm** | K_STOP |
| Q_SWDRVB | 7.27 mm | **2.77 mm** | Q_SWB |
| D_COILEN | 5.40 mm | **1.65 mm** | K_STOP |
| U_ULNB | 3.97 mm | **1.26 mm** | K_PRESS |
| J_ESTOP | 4.14 mm | **1.93 mm** | U_CAND2 |
| **J_ISOLOOP** | 2.30 mm | **0.80 mm** | **J_RH_EXHAUST** |
| J_DOOR | 2.87 mm | **2.80 mm** | J_ESTOP |

The `J_ISOLOOP` row is the worst of these on consequence: the label on the **NOT-SELV isolated screw terminal** is three times closer to a SELV sensor connector than to the terminal it names, and both labels sit on the same baseline along the bottom edge. Someone landing mains-adjacent contactor wiring reads that label.

In the 0402 fields a 5 mm median offset means a label names nothing — there are a dozen candidate 0402s within that radius.

**R-05 — Mounting hole H_E at (193, 52) is not tool-accessible and its hardware bridges a comb slot.**
Clearance from the hole edge to the nearest courtyards: **J_MODE 1.40 mm, D_COILEN 1.77 mm, U_ULNB 1.87 mm, K_STOP 2.70 mm**. From the hole *centre* that is 2.75 mm to J_MODE's courtyard. An M2.5 washer (5–6.5 mm OD) overlaps the J_MODE connector body; a 5.5 mm A/F nut driver needs ≈3.5 mm and does not fit at all.

Separately, the hole centre is **2.2 mm from the east-edge notch**. A washer or screw head of radius > 2.2 mm spans it. That notch is the comb slot guarding K_STOP's contact column — bridging it restores a ≈4.4 mm surface path from the SELV pour to an isolated contact pad, under the 6 mm claim.

**R-06 — The order paperwork does not say which 16 parts are not assembled.**
`verification/assembly_coverage.txt` already reports **A-POP: FAIL**. The staged release directory contains no manifest file at all. BOM carries 218 designators, CPL carries 202. The 16 with no CPL row are `K_U1…K_U6, K_D1…K_D4, K_PRESS, K_STOP, J_ISOLOOP, J_TC, J_PI, J_LOADCELL`.

**R-06b — assembly.pdf is not usable as an assembly drawing.**
One A4 page. **Title, Date and Rev fields are all blank.** The board occupies only the top-left ~55 % of the sheet, and refdes + value + LCSC code are rendered on top of each other ("100kΩ100kΩ100kΩ") — unreadable across the whole centre-bottom. No distinction between assembled and hand-fit parts. All 9 pages of `pdf/pcb_layers.pdf` likewise have blank Title/Date/Rev.

**R-07 — Twelve comb slots are 0.60 mm wide, below JLC's standard minimum routed slot.**
`fab/cooksense-Edge_Cuts.gm1`. Row A (y −49.30…−49.90) and row B (y −26.00…−26.60) are each six closed rectangles 8.44–9.60 mm long × **0.60 mm wide**. JLCPCB's standard minimum slot width is 1.0 mm. The board's own east-edge notch is drawn at exactly 1.0 mm — so the 1.0 mm number was known and not applied to the internal slots.

This is not cosmetic: the slots *are* the creepage comb. If the fab silently widens them, quotes an upcharge, or (worst) omits them, the ≥6 mm surface-path claim silkscreened on the board changes. Confirm with the fab before ordering.

**R-08 — Two more identical, pin-identical connector pairs; both transpose silently.**
`J_THERM_A` (32.0, −96.75) / `J_THERM_B` (54.0, −96.75), both C265111 GH 8-pin, 22 mm apart — pinouts identical channel-for-channel. `J_RH_AMBIENT` (168.0, −96.75) / `J_RH_EXHAUST` (186.0, −96.75), both C189896 GH 5-pin, 18 mm apart — likewise identical. Either swap is silent and produces plausible-looking wrong data — ambient RH read as exhaust RH on a cooking appliance is a decision input.

Counting R-01 and R-08: **8 of the 10 wire-to-board connectors come from just two part numbers.**

**R-09 — The 3D twin does not cover a single one of the 14 through-hole parts, and `missing_models.txt` names only one of them.**
`twin_report.csv` contains **zero** `K_*` rows and no `J_TC` or `J_ISOLOOP` rows — the twin's population is the 202 machine-placed SMT parts. `missing_models.txt` lists J_ISOLOOP alone and reports "bodies mounted: 204/205". Read literally, that file is incomplete by 13. The tallest parts on the board and **both parts that carry the isolation** are unrepresented in the 3D evidence.

## P2

**R-10 — Three bare probe pads on KEYPAD_ISO nets sit inside the isolated domain with no warning.**
`TP_USEL` (100.0, 16.5) → `U_SEL_BUS`, `TP_DSEL` (103.0, 16.5) → `D_SEL_BUS`, `TP_RKEY` (100.5, 19.5) → `RKEY_MID` — all three are netclass **KEYPAD_ISO**. These are 1.5 mm exposed pads inviting a probe; a ground-referenced instrument on any of them bridges the barrier the whole board is built around. There is no silk warning within 80 mm of them.

**R-11 — The mounting-hardware warning understates the failure mode.**
(a) It is over-broad in one direction: H_NW and H_NE are *both* in the isolated domain and H_SW and H_E are *both* in SELV, so bonding either same-side pair breaks nothing. (b) More importantly it is **incomplete in the dangerous direction**: per R-05, a *single* metal washer at H_E bridges the K_STOP comb notch on its own — no plate and no second hole required.

**R-12 — `TP_ESTOP` and `U_AND3` refdes overlap** by 0.29 × 0.60 mm near (175.5, 68.0). Still readable, but it is the only refdes-on-refdes collision on the board and `silk_overlap` DRC is off.

**R-13 — The NOT-SELV screw terminal has no wire-entry orientation marking.** `TerminalBlock_KF350_4P.kicad_mod` draws a plain rectangle, near-symmetric front-to-back (local y −3.40 / +3.60). Nothing on the silk says which 14 mm face is the wire entry.

**R-14 — Revision marking.** The board's own marking is present and legible: **`cooksense  SMC0985KS  sidecar v1.7`** — consistent with the release directory. That is the only place the revision appears in the whole package. The schematic's title block carries `(rev "dev")`, and assembly.pdf / pcb_layers.pdf carry a blank Rev field. Three documents, three different answers.

**R-15 — `J_PI` MIRRORED flag left unadjudicated.** **My adjudication: this is not a board error.** The board's pads are byte-identical to the stock KiCad library part. It is a numbering-convention difference against JLC's model. But J_PI is not in the CPL, so the JLC order-preview human gate will never look at it — record the adjudication rather than leaving an open MIRRORED flag on the Pi host interface.

**R-16 — Opto land pattern and the ungraded barrier assertion.** `part_facts.txt` reports **P-FACT-DEFERRED** on the LTV-817 `keepout_region`. I checked it by eye and by geometry: the keepout **is** present as two all-layer rule areas covering x 181.4–192.8, y 85.8–94.2, and the SE crops of all four copper layers show the pour cut away coincidently on every layer. That assertion holds; it just is not machine-graded.

**R-17 — Trivial:** 4 silk vertices at x = 200.06, 0.06 mm past the east board edge (J_MODE's outline). The fab will clip it.

## What I checked and found sound

- **The isolation boundary is well built and it is the strongest part of this board.** The keypad comb is a genuine two-row staggered slot comb: row A sits directly *below* the six CONTACT-column pairs and row B directly *above* the six COIL-column pairs, so each domain's traces exit through a clear channel while any surface path between domains must detour around a slot end. Adjacent relays alternate rotation 180/0. Every copper pour on all four layers stops at y = 53. `cooksense.kicad_dru` enforces `keypad_isolation_6mm` and `opto_isolation_2mm`, DRC reports 0/0/0. The J_ISOLOOP island keepout is cut on all four layers; measured inner-plane-to-pad gap ≈2.2 mm.
- **Connector reachability:** every connector mouth faces off-board. No mating opening is blocked by a neighbouring body.
- **Mechanical:** 0 courtyard overlaps across all 239 footprints. 0 refdes over a pad. No body overhangs the board.
- **Bottom side is genuinely bare**, consistent with 202 top-side placements.
- **Caption text is otherwise coherent and useful.** All legible, none truncated except as noted in R-02. Only R-11 contradicts itself.
- **Unresolved-by-others, for the record:** `fab/rotation_human_gate.txt` still requires a JLC order-preview human check on 13 placements across 10 LCSC codes, and four parts are POLARITY-FIT-BLIND (D_COILEN, D_TVS, D_KSTOP, D_REVCLAMP). Those gates are open, not failed.

## Graded policy items

**S6 — readability — FAIL.**
`pdf/schematic.pdf` is **one page, 900 × 450 pt, carrying all 235 parts** in a force-directed blob. There is no sheet hierarchy (`(sheet` count = 0), no title block plotted, no zone/grid reference, and no functional grouping. Connectivity is carried by **601 global labels and 0 local labels** — following the safety chain or the power tree means text-searching net names. Worst sheet: **the only sheet, 1/1** — there is nothing to compare it against, which is itself the finding.

**S7 — decoupling — PASS.**
Every IC with a named supply pin has a dedicated local bypass whose far end is GND, verified from `source/cooksense.net` (17 logic ICs enumerated, plus U_LDO, U_EFUSE and both ULN2803 COM rails). **Zero unbypassed power pins.** This is the one thing the schematic does unambiguously well.

**S5 — design math — FAIL.**
The schematic contains **zero free-text items and zero text boxes**. There is not one annotation, note, calculation or derivation anywhere. Component values are printed but nothing states what any of them is *for* or what it computes to. Nobody can check the comparator thresholds, the thermistor dividers, the eFuse current limit set by R_ILM, the OVLO divider R_OVT/R_OVB, or the CD74HC221 one-shot period without reverse-engineering the netlist first.
