## A1. The pin-out-13 relay land — CONFIRMED, from the datasheet FIGURE, measured on its own grid

refused to accept it on the archive's word (its own method was "re-rendered at

`02_parts/DIP05-1A72-13L/datasheet-reed-relay-series-dip.pdf` at 220 dpi, cropped
sub-figure **13**, and MEASURED the drawing against its own stated 2.54 mm pitch

| what the figure shows | measured on the grid |
|---|---|
| leads **14** and **8** carry the SWITCH, on one row | 6 pitches apart = **15.240 mm** |
| leads **2** and **6** carry the COIL, on the other row | 4 pitches apart = **10.160 mm** |
| row-to-row separation | 3 pitches = **7.620 mm** |
| coil symbol | a bare inductor — **no internal diode** (option `L`) |

Mapped onto DIP-14 numbering (1–7 down one column, 8–14 up the other) with the
package centre at the mid-span, the four leads sit at:
lead 14 → (east, −7.62), lead 8 → (east, +7.62), lead 2 → (west, −5.08),

## A2. The eFuse EN/OVLO polarity — CONFIRMED active-LOW, from SLVSE57C's own text

This is the single claim that decides whether the board powers up at all: a
divider from IN to a pin at 5.798 V is an over-voltage cutoff if the pin disables
above threshold, and a board that never turns on if it enables above threshold.
Read from `02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/SLVSE57C.pdf`, §8.3.2.1, verbatim:

> *"Whenever the voltage at the EN/OVLO pin rises above a threshold VOVLO, the
> device turns OFF the FET."*

and the Device Comparison Table row: `TPS259573DSG | Programmable Overvoltage
Lockout | Auto-retry | Active low`. **Confirmed.** The EC table gives
`VOVLO(R)` **1.13 / 1.20 / 1.27 V**, `VOVLO(F)` 1.03 / 1.10 / 1.17 V,
`IEN` ±0.1 µA.

I then recomputed ADR-0021's two corners from scratch with `R_OVT` 100 k ±0.5%
and `R_OVB` 26.1 k ±0.5%, ±100 ppm/°C over −20…+70 °C, and IEN across the
divider's own source impedance:

| corner | my value | archive's | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| nominal trip | 1.20 × 126.1/26.1 = **5.7977 V** | 5.798 V | 0.3 mV |
| EARLIEST possible trip | 1.127919 × 4.75912 = **5.3679 V** | 5.3682 V | 0.3 mV |
| LATEST possible trip | 1.272058 × 4.90489 = **6.2393 V** | 6.2394 V | 0.1 mV |

**CONFIRMED.** The setpoint clears the 5.250 V supply ceiling by 118 mV and sits
under the SMBJ5.0A's 6.40 V V_BR(min) by 161 mV at 25 °C — and by **42.6 mV** at
−20 °C, where the TVS's own +0.041 %/°C coefficient has pulled V_BR(min) down to
6.2819 V. That 42.6 mV is the tightest number in the protection chain and it is
correctly declared as such.

## A3. The reverse-polarity FET orientation — CONFIRMED CORRECT, and it is the
subtle one

A high-side P-FET is wired one way as a load switch and the OTHER way as a
reverse-polarity blocker, and getting it backwards produces a board that works
on the bench and reverse-energises its load on a miswire. Traced from the
netlist:

| refdes | role | source | drain | gate | body diode in normal operation | correct? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Q_REV` | reverse-polarity blocker | `5V_RPP` (**load**) | `5V_FUSED` (**input**) | `GND` | forward — conducts | **YES** |
| `Q_COIL` | coil-rail load switch | `5V_PROTECTED` (**input**) | `5V_KEY_RELAY` (load) | `HS_GATE_COIL` | reverse — blocks when off | **YES** |
| `Q_SWA/B/RHA/RHE` | sensor load switches | `3V3` (input) | `3V3_SW_*` (load) | `SWG_*` | reverse — blocks when off | **YES** |

`Q_REV` is deliberately inverted relative to the four load switches — drain to
the input so the body diode conducts forward while the channel enhances, and
reverse-biased the moment the supply is reversed. That is right, and it is right
in the one place a copy-paste from the load-switch cell would have made it wrong.

## A4. The reed coils' only flyback path — CONFIRMED to exist

Twelve of the thirteen coils have **no external flyback diode**; only `K_STOP`
has one (`D_KSTOP`, SS34, cathode on `5V_STOP`, anode on `COIL_STOP_N` —
correct orientation). Everything else depends on the driver's internal clamp.
Read from `02_parts/TBD62083AFWG/TBD62083A_20160511.pdf`: p.1 *"array with 8
circuits. It has a clamp diode for switching"*; the p.2 equivalent circuit names

`U_ULNB.10` (COM) are on `5V_KEY_RELAY`. **The freewheel path exists.**

`A.NetClass=='KEYPAD_ISO' && B.NetClass!='KEYPAD_ISO' && !B.memberOfFootprint('J_KEY_MATRIX')`

# PART C — E-OFF (MANDATORY)

`power_tree.yaml` declares `source_type: external_5v_selv` and **no
`off_control`**. Verified against the artifact rather than accepted:

- **No self-contained source exists.** All 239 components enumerated from the
  netlist: no battery, cell, pack, coin cell or supercapacitor. The only energy
  stores are `CE1` 220 µF, ~90 µF of ceramics, `FB1`, and thirteen reed coils.
- **Stored energy** ½·220 µF·5.25² = **3.03 mJ**. Not a hazard.
- **Nothing is asserted-but-absent.** `grep -rn off_control 03_src/` returns
  exactly one hit, in `03_src/rules/contracts.md`, describing the schema. No
  off-control is claimed anywhere, so there is none to fail to find.
- **De-energisation is by unplugging `J_PWR` and nothing on the board can do
  it.** The eFuse's only enable pin is committed to the OVLO divider; `J_PI`
  pins 1/2/4/17 are `no_connect`, so the Pi cannot back-feed a rail either.
- **Fail-safe by construction:** removing input power drops all coil rails, and
  a reed relay with a dead coil opens. Every keypad contact opens.

**E-OFF is satisfied.** The declaration matches the artifact.

Recorded quiescent cost, because it is a design choice and not free: with the
`J_ESTOP` shorting plug fitted, `R_ESTOPPD` 470 Ω draws **7.02 mA** from 3V3
continuously (23.2 mW in an 0402 rated 62.5 mW — 37 %), 2.3 % of the rail's
declared 0.30 A budget, permanently, for one static logic level.

## P1

### P1-A — **NEW.** The PRESS one-shot's 500 ms HARD bound is claimed from a tolerance stack that omits the timing capacitor's dielectric. Worst case is 502–525 ms, not 436 ms. **CONFIRMED by recomputation.**

`BRIEF.md` §4 sets **"max key 500ms"** as a hard sequencing bound, and it is one
of the four pillars of the "the LLM can at most press valid keys" safety
argument. Four documents assert the same number:

- `DETAIL_DESIGN` §2: *"max: 0.77·515k·1.1u = 436ms < 500ms HARD (brief §4.6)"*
- `ARCHITECTURE.md`:23: *"caps PRESS <=436ms worst (<500ms HARD)"*
- `verification/electrical_invariants.yaml`:118: *"the hardware <=436ms
  worst-case (<500ms) PRESS cap"*
- `02_parts/CD74HC221M96/part.yaml`: *"worst-case high (K=0.77, R+1%, **C+10%
  X5R**) ~ 436ms"*

**The capacitor term is `C+10%` — the ±10 % PART TOLERANCE, measured at 25 °C.**
The fitted part is `C_OS` = **`CL10A105KB8NNNC`** (`fab/bom.csv`:17), a Samsung
0603 1 µF 25 V **X5R** ±10 %. X5R is an EIA Class-II code and its second and
third characters mean **±15 % capacitance change over −55 to +85 °C, relative to
the 25 °C value**. That term is multiplicative with the tolerance and it is
absent from every one of the four stacks:

```
C_max = 1.00 uF x 1.10 (tol, 25 C) x 1.15 (X5R temp char) = 1.265 uF
tw_max = K x Rx x Cx = 0.77 x 515.1k x 1.265u          = 501.7 ms   > 500 ms HARD
```

And `K` itself is understated. SCHS166F's own EC table gives, at VCC = 5 V,
`tW` **630 min / 770 typ / 595 min / 805 max** over the temperature range — a
+4.5 % spread above typ. Applied to Figure 6's ~0.75 at 3.3 V, `K_max` is
**0.784**, not 0.77:

```
tw_max = 0.784 x 515.1k x 1.265u = 510.9 ms
```

and at the EC table's own upper ratio, **524.5 ms**.

**MEASURED CONSEQUENCE:** the claimed headroom is 64 ms; the X5R temperature
coefficient alone is ±65 ms at this pulse width. **The stated margin is exactly
the size of the term that was left out.** DC-bias derating on a 25 V 0603 acts
in the helpful direction and the real part is probably inside 500 ms — but that
is a hope, not the stack, and the stack is what four documents publish.

Also worth recording from the same arithmetic: `tw_min` = 0.63 × 505.1k ×
(1.00 × 0.90 × 0.85) µF = **243 ms**, so the board *cannot* produce the brief's
"typical 100–200 ms" press. The one-shot is always at least ~240 ms.

**Remedy for the next rev, stated so it is actionable:** make the timing cap a
**C0G/NP0** (±30 ppm/°C — the tempco term vanishes) or drop `R_OS` to ~430 kΩ,
which restores >100 ms of true headroom against the hard bound.

### P1-B — **CONFIRMED and EXTENDED.** The LDO dropout margin that unblocked E-TOPO goes NEGATIVE once the measured board copper AND a cited connector resistance are counted.

`power_tree.yaml` derives `vin_min = 4.850 − 0.0952 = 4.7548 V` from three cited
datasheet maxima (F1 70.0 + Q_REV 73.5 + eFuse 47.0 = 190.5 mΩ at 0.50 A). All
three are right. **Two whole classes of series resistance are missing: the
board's own copper, and the connector.**

Recomputed with MY copper measurement (§A5), at 70 °C, with the Micro-Fit's
10 mΩ-per-contact figure:

```
shared J_PWR -> eFuse OUT, at 0.50 A
    F1 70.0 + Q_REV 73.5 + eFuse 47.0                    = 190.5 mOhm
  + measured copper 44.89 x 1.1965 (70 C)                =  53.71 mOhm
  + J_PWR Micro-Fit, 2 contacts x 10 mOhm                =  20.00 mOhm
                                                           264.21 mOhm  -> 132.11 mV
eFuse OUT -> U_LDO.3, at 0.30 A
    59.66 x 1.1965                                       =  71.38 mOhm  ->  21.41 mV
                                                     TOTAL              = 153.52 mV

V_IN(LDO)  = 4.850 - 0.15352                             = 4.6965 V
V_required = Vout_max 3.399 + dropout_max 1.300          = 4.699  V
MARGIN                                                   = -2.5 mV
```

For the ladder: **+55 mV** declared → **+15.4 mV** with copper at 20 °C
(the prior lens got +16.3 mV; two methods agree) → **+5.4 mV** with the
connector at 20 °C → **−2.5 mV** with the connector at 70 °C. Using the eFuse's
−40…125 °C RON row (53 mΩ instead of 47) takes it to **−5.5 mV**.

**This is a DECLARATION defect, not necessarily a board defect,** and the
distinction is the whole point: the 1300 mV dropout is a datasheet MAX at
**0.8 A**, applied here at **0.3 A**, where the same datasheet says only that
dropout "decreas[es] at lower load currents" and publishes no curve. The archive
is admirably honest that this number is OWED. The physical board is very
probably fine. What is not fine is that **E-TOPO's PASS is owed to the omitted
terms, not to margin** — a +55 mV pass on a stack missing ~99 mV of real,
measurable, board-resident resistance is a gate reporting a state the artifact
does not have.

**The bring-up measurement in ORDER_README §0 step 2 retires this completely**
and should be treated as mandatory rather than encouraged.

### P1-C — **NEW.** E-MARGIN cannot fail on this board, and the rail it would have caught declares a drop no AO3401A can deliver.

Two halves.

**(a) The gate is inert.** `policy_audit` prints `E-MARGIN | N-A | no rail
declares load_uv_threshold — no regulated rail feeds a known fixed-brownout
load`. The board has at least six such loads (TPS3823-33 at 3.00 V, MAX31856 at
3.00 V, MCP3208 at 2.70 V, two MLX90640 at 3.10 V, HX711 at 2.60 V). `E-MARGIN`
is OPTIONAL-ACTIVATED by a key nobody wrote, so it grades nothing and prints
N-A — a green-looking row that has never had the chance to be red. This is the
`jlc_twin`-exit-0 shape named in `tests/README.md`, in the power gate.

**(b) The rail it would have graded tightest carries an impossible declaration.**
`power_tree.yaml`:239–247 declares the four switched sensor rails
`vin_min: 3.201` / `vout_min: 3.2` at `iout_max_A: 0.1` — i.e. **1 mV of drop at
100 mA, a 10 mΩ switch.** The switch is an AO3401A whose own dossier cites
**60 mΩ MAX at V_GS = −4.5 V, 25 °C**, i.e. 6.0 mV at 100 mA — six times the
declared budget before any temperature or copper term.

**And these four are the one place on the board where the FET's R_DS(on) is not
specified at all.** `Q_SWA/B/RHA/RHE` have their source on `3V3` and their gate
pulled to GND, so **V_GS = −3.3 V**. The AO3401A datasheet publishes R_DS(on) at
V_GS = −4.5 V and −10 V and **nothing below −4.5 V**, while V_GS(th) max is
−1.3 V — so −3.3 V is on the knee, where a 1.5–2× multiplier is normal. Every
other AO3401A on this board (`Q_REV`, `Q_COIL`) runs at V_GS ≈ −5 V and the
cited figure is legitimate there; these four are outside the data.

Hand-computed margin to the actual load, with cable and connector (§B):

```
LDO Vout min                                              3.2010 V
- 3V3 In2.Cu plane at 0.1 A                              -0.0002
- Q_SWA R_DS(on): 118 mOhm (60 x 1.6 knee x 1.225 hot)   -0.0118
- board copper Q_SWA.3 -> J_THERM_A.1, 11.92 mOhm        -0.0012
                            at J_THERM_A.1  =             3.1878 V   (declared: 3.200)
- 1.0 m loop of 28 AWG @70 C + 4 x 20 mOhm GH contacts
  = 0.331 Ohm, at the MLX90640's ~23 mA                  -0.0076
                            at the pod      =             3.1802 V
MLX90640 VDD min                                          3.1000 V
MARGIN                                                   +80.2 mV
IR budget = (3.201-3.100)/0.023 = 4.39 Ohm  vs  0.461 Ohm actual  -> PASS 9.5x
```

**It passes — at the bare sensor.** The caveat that nothing in this archive
states: `BRIEF.md` §8 names the **Adafruit 4407** breakout, which carries its own
3.3 V regulator and level shifters. Any series element on the module — a
regulator with 40 mV of dropout at 25 mA, a reverse-protection diode — consumes
the whole +80 mV. **The pod-side series budget is outside this tree and
undeclared, on the rail that feeds the camera-temperature sensing the hardware
over-temperature interlock is built on.** Declare `load_uv_threshold: 3.1` and
`ir_budget_mohm` on these four rails and E-MARGIN starts doing work.

## P2

### P2-a — **NEW.** Six CMOS inputs float in the board's DEFAULT, SAFE state.

`SR_OE_N = NAND(MCU_RELAY_ENABLE, WD_OK)` with `R_OE` 10 kΩ pulling it HIGH, so
`U_SR1`'s outputs are **Hi-Z whenever either permission is absent — which is the
board's resting state.** In that state these nets have no driver and no
resistor anywhere in the netlist:

`DECU_A`, `DECU_B`, `DECU_C` (→ `U_DECU` A/B/C), `DECD_A`, `DECD_B`
(→ `U_DECD` A/B), and **`PRESS_REQ` (→ `U_ONESHOT.2`, the one-shot's rising-edge
trigger)**.

Each is a 2-node net: shift-register output plus logic input, nothing else.
`DETAIL_DESIGN` §4's "deterministic pulls" table covers `DECU_G1_RAW` and
`DECD_G1_RAW` (which do have `R_DECUPD`/`R_DECDPD` 100 kΩ) and stops there.

**Not a hazard, and I checked rather than assumed:** the decoders' `G1` is held
low by those same pull-downs so all sixteen `SEL_*` outputs are low regardless of
the address; and a noise-triggered PRESS one-shot cannot close `K_PRESS` because
`5V_KEY_RELAY` is dead in exactly the same condition (`MCU_RELAY_ENABLE` is both
an OE term and an AND-chain term). But five floating 74HC inputs draw crowbar
current continuously in the resting state, and a floating edge-trigger on a
safety one-shot is a thing you do not leave to a downstream interlock. Five
0402 resistors fix it.

### P2-b — **NEW.** `TC_FAULT_N` is the only net on the board with no pull anywhere, and §7a's firmware invariant names every bit except that one.

`TC_FAULT_N = [TP_TCFAULT.1, U_EXP.7 (GPB6), U_TC.13 (FAULT_N)]` — three nodes,
**no resistor**. `TC_DRDY_N` is the same shape into `J_PI.22`.

`02_parts/MAX31856MUD+T/part.yaml` describes both as *"DRDY_N/FAULT_N
open-drain-ish → test points only (no Pico pins left)"* and its `verified:` line
says *"pin map from EasyEDA CAD (JLC C2653162); **PENDING datasheet-figure
confirmation** in pin review"*. No MAX31856 datasheet is committed to this tree,
so **the archive cannot cite the output structure of the pin it routes into a
logic input.**

Meanwhile `ORDER_README` §7a instructs the host firmware, as a hardware-cannot-
enforce invariant, that MCP23017 **`GPPUB` must be written `0x00`**, and its
v1.7 box names *"bits 1, 2 and 7"*. Bit 6 is `TC_FAULT_N` and it is the one bit
whose net has no external pull. If `FAULT_N` is open-drain — which the dossier's
own words suggest — then `GPPUB = 0x00` makes GPB6 **deterministically floating**
and the thermocouple's open/short fault flag, a `BRIEF.md` §3 requirement, is
unreadable by construction. Either fit a pull-up or make §7a say
`GPPUB = 0x40`; but first commit the datasheet and settle the pin type.

### P2-c — **NEW.** The reverse-polarity crowbar and the polyfuse are not coordinated at the supply current the brief specifies, and the crowbar creates the fault current the series FET alone would not draw.

`D_REVCLAMP` (SS34, `D_SMA`) has cathode on `5V_FUSED` and anode on `GND` —
correct orientation for a shunt crowbar downstream of `F1`, as its dossier says.
But `Q_REV` **already blocks reverse polarity with zero current**: with the input
at −5 V its body diode is reverse-biased and V_GS = 0. Adding the crowbar
converts a clean, currentless blocking condition into a conducting one that
depends on the polyfuse to interrupt it.

Measured against the parts actually fitted:

- `MF-MSMF200L-2`, from its committed Bourns table: **I_hold 2.00 A at 23 °C
  (1.43 A at 70 °C), I_trip 3.50 A**. Between hold and trip the behaviour is
  indeterminate and time-to-trip is long.
- `BRIEF.md` §3.5 specifies a **"5V SELV ≥2A (pref 3A)"** supply.
- **A brief-compliant 3 A supply sits inside the fuse's indeterminate band.**
  With the reverse fault current-limited at 3 A, the SS34 conducts ~3 A at
  ~0.55 V ⇒ **~1.65 W in an SMA**, at the top of its 3 A I_F(AV) rating, for as
  long as the fuse takes to decide — which at 3 A may be tens of seconds or
  never.

Consequence: a reverse hookup that the series FET would have made a non-event
becomes a thermal event in `D_REVCLAMP`. Mitigating context, stated fairly: the
connector is a **keyed 2-pin Micro-Fit** and cannot be mated reversed, so this
requires a miswired or rebuilt cable. Remedy options for the next rev: a lower
I_hold PPTC (1.1 A holds the 0.50 A worst case with 2.2× margin and trips at
2.2 A), or delete the crowbar and let `Q_REV` do the job it already does.

### P2-d — **CONFIRMED and EXTENDED.** The 600 W TVS is behind everything it should protect, and `Q_REV`'s gate has no clamp.

The prior lens recorded that `D_TVS` (SMBJ5.0A, 600 W) sits on `5V_PROTECTED` —
the eFuse **output** — while the input side carries only `D_ESD_IN`, a SOD-323
`PESD5V0S1BA` whose dossier in this tree publishes **no ratings at all**
(`02_parts/PESD5V0S1BA/part.yaml` has no `limits:` block; my grep for
`vrwm|stand.off|ifsm` returns nothing). Confirmed.

**The extension is the mechanism it exposes.** `Q_REV`'s gate is tied
**directly to `GND`** — no series resistor, no zener, no clamp
(`GND` net: `Q_REV.1 [G_1]`). Therefore

```
V_GS(Q_REV) = -V(5V_RPP) = -(the input voltage, whatever it is)
```

and the AO3401A's V_GS absolute maximum is **±12 V**. An input transient above
12 V destroys the gate oxide of the reverse-polarity FET — the first active
protection element on the board — and the only thing between the connector and
that gate is an unrated SOD-323. `D_TVS` clamps at 9.2 V, comfortably inside
±12 V, but it is **downstream of the eFuse**, so it does not see the transient
until after `Q_REV` has already taken it.

An input-side TVS at the connector (which is where a TVS conventionally belongs,
and what `BRIEF.md` §3.5's "TVS" reasonably means) resolves both this and the
prior lens's P2-4 in one part.

### P2-e — **NEW.** Two of the eight commissioned thermistor channels have no connector.

`BRIEF.md` §3 commissions **CH0–CH7 = TH_CAM_A, TH_MOUNT_A, TH_PORT_A,
TH_CAM_B, TH_MOUNT_B, TH_PORT_B, TH_ENCLOSURE, TH_SPARE**, and gives
`TH_ENCLOSURE` its own limit band (*"enclosure ≤50 / 55 / 65 / 75"*).

Traced from the netlist:

| channel | ADC | ref | landing for the NTC |
|---|---|---|---|
| CH0–CH2 | `R_SER0..2` | `R_REF0..2` | `J_THERM_A.5/.6/.7` ✔ |
| CH3–CH5 | `R_SER3..5` | `R_REF3..5` | `J_THERM_B.5/.6/.7` ✔ |
| **CH6 `TH_ENCLOSURE`** | `R_SER6` | `R_REF6` | **`TP_ENCL` — a bare test pad. No connector.** |
| **CH7 `TH_SPARE`** | `R_SER7` | `R_REF7` | **`TP_SPARE` — a bare test pad. No connector.** |

Both nets are 3-node: series resistor, reference resistor, test point. There is
no ADR withdrawing the enclosure channel. The consequence is not electrical
(unfitted channels read full-scale and `BRIEF.md` C14 already declares that
invalid) — it is that the enclosure thermistor, one of the four commissioned
temperature domains, can only be attached by soldering to a test pad with no
strain relief.

### P2-f — **NEW.** The brief's per-bus I2C requirements are not implemented and no ADR withdraws them.

`BRIEF.md` §3.10, verbatim: *"Per bus: selectable 2.2k/4.7k pullups (default OFF
with Adafruit onboard 4.7k); **22-33R damping; low-C ESD; test points**;
bus-stuck recovery; optional differential-extender footprint; optional PCA9548A
fallback."* Traced against the netlist, per bus:

| commissioned | implemented |
|---|---|
| selectable 2.2k/4.7k pull-ups, **default OFF** | `R_SCLA/R_SDAA/R_SCLB/R_SDAB` 2.2 kΩ **hard-fitted**, no jumper, no second position |
| **22–33 Ω series damping** | **absent.** `SDA_A` = `[J_PI.7, J_RH_AMBIENT.3, J_THERM_A.3, R_SDAA.1]` — four nodes, no series element. Same on all four lines. |
| **low-C ESD** | **absent.** The five `PESD5V0S1BA` are on `5V_IN`, `ESTOP_RAW_IN`, `COIL_EN_IN`, `LC_CLK`, `LC_DAT` — none on I2C. |
| **test points** | **absent.** Seventeen TPs exist; none on `SDA_A/B` or `SCL_A/B`. |

The pull-up value is also the one thing the brief's own contingency **C7**
("module pullups too strong: Board A pullups DNP") plans to remove — and with no
jumper, "DNP" means desoldering a fitted 0402. Loading is fine electrically
(2.2 k ∥ two module 10 k ≈ 1.57 k ⇒ 2.1 mA, inside I2C's 3 mA), so this is a
commissioned-feature gap, not an electrical error.

### P2-g — **NEW.** `02_parts/CD74HC221M96/part.yaml` describes section 2 as UNUSED, in every pin position, and the board uses it.

The dossier, verbatim: *"Unused section 2: 2A_N=VCC, 2B=GND, 2R_N=GND (held
reset), 2Q/2Q_N open, 2CX open, 2CXRX pulled to VCC via 10k"*.

The shipped netlist:

| pin | dossier says | netlist has |
|---|---|---|
| 9 `2A_N` | VCC | **`REARM_N`** (the trigger) |
| 10 `2B` | GND | **`3V3`** |
| 11 `2R_N` | GND (held reset) | **`WD_OK`** |
| 12 `2Q_N` | open | **`REARM_PULSE_N`** → `U_LATCHB.1` |

| 7 `2CXRX` | 10k to VCC | **`OS2_RC`** (`C_OS2` + `R_OS2` 10 k) |

Section 2 **is** the 7 ms re-arm edge detector — the fix that
`electrical_invariants.yaml`:474 calls *"THE WHOLE FIX IN ONE ASSERT"*, without
which a driver that holds `REARM_N` low forces the fault latch into its

`SN74HC14DR` dossier still describing the deleted door channel): the MPN dossier
is the file a reviewer reads to check the timing argument, and it describes a
different circuit.

### P2-h — **NEW.** The LMV393 dossier's open-detect argument is written against the superseded 6.2 kΩ resistor.

`02_parts/LMV393IDR/part.yaml` gotcha, verbatim: *"TCAM_OPEN **3.107V** is ABOVE
the 2.50-2.60V common-mode ceiling **AT ALL TIMES**, so … TH_CAM in range ⇒
case 3 ⇒ HIGH-Z (no fault); TH_CAM also above CM ⇒ case 4 ⇒ LOW (fault)."*

3.107 V is the **6.2 kΩ** `R_OPENT` — the v1.3 P0-B value, corrected to **62 kΩ**
(`C37825`) and shipped as such in `fab/bom.csv`:30. With 62 kΩ:

```
TCAM_OPEN = 3.3 x 100k/(62k+100k) = 2.0370 V    -- INSIDE the 2.50-2.60 V ceiling
```

**The circuit still works, and by a better mechanism than the dossier claims:**
with `R_CLMPA` 22 kΩ holding the open-thermistor node at
3.3 × 22/32 = **2.269 V**, both comparator inputs are inside VICR and the part
does an ordinary comparison — `IN−` 2.269 > `IN+` 2.037 ⇒ output LOW ⇒
`TEMP_OK` low ⇒ fault. Margin 232 mV, ratiometric, so rail drift cancels. But
the argument published in the dossier depends on an out-of-common-mode case
table that the fitted board never enters, and that argument is what a reviewer
would check.

(`electrical_invariants.yaml`:577 gets this right and says so explicitly — the
two files disagree, and the one that is right is not the MPN dossier.)

### P2-i — **CONFIRMED, my own number.** The open-thermistor detect false-trips below −10.4 °C, inside the declared envelope.

Solving `3.3·(R_NTC ∥ 22k)/(10k + R_NTC ∥ 22k) = 2.0370 V` gives `R_NTC` =
**60.43 kΩ**, and inverting the committed Beta model
(`R25` 10 kΩ, `B25/85` = 3987 K) gives **T = 262.80 K = −10.35 °C**.

Below that, a **healthy, correctly-fitted** thermistor reads above the
open-detect threshold, `U_COMP2` declares a fault, `TEMP_OK` goes low, the fault
latch sets and the board is inert. The declared operating envelope is
**−20 … +70 °C** (`02_parts/DIP05-1A72-13L` `t_op`, and the ADR-0021 worst-case
corners are computed over exactly that range), so the bottom **9.6 °C of the
declared envelope is a guaranteed no-start.** The prior lens said "the bottom
10 °C"; my independent solve puts the crossover at −10.35 °C. Fail-safe
direction, so P2 — but it is a functional restriction nobody has stated to the
buyer, and a cold garage or an unheated workshop reaches it.

### P2-j — **CONFIRMED.** The eFuse's programmed current limit is below the rail budget declared on top of it, and below the supply the brief specifies.

`R_ILM` = 1.2 kΩ (`C138040`). SLVSE57C publishes three `ILIMIT`-vs-`RILM` points
(487 Ω → 4.17 A; 1780 Ω → 1.17 A; 4420 Ω → 0.49 A). Fitting a power law through
the first and third gives `I = 1693.5 · R^−0.9705` (which reproduces the 1780 Ω
point at 1.185 A vs 1.17 A published), so at 1.2 kΩ:

```
I_LIMIT ~ 1.74 A typ,  ~1.62 A at the -7.5% accuracy corner
```

against `power_tree.yaml`'s `5V_PROTECTED: iout_max_A: 2.0` and `BRIEF.md`
§3.5's *"5V SELV **>=2A** (pref 3A)"*. **The board cannot draw the 2 A its own
power tree budgets or its brief specifies — the eFuse limits first.** The real
worst-case simultaneous draw is 0.50 A, so nothing physical is at risk; the
defect is that the limiter's setting is stated **nowhere in the tree** and two
declarations sit above it. Already filed as the prior lens's P1-9 and
`owed_skill_patches.md` P8; I reproduce the number here because I derived it
independently from the datasheet's own table rather than from the dossier's
single 487 Ω anchor.

# Bottom line

**`design_verdict: SOUND`.** I hunted for a design P0 and did not find one. The
two things this board was re-spun for — the pin-out-13 relay land and the eFuse
OVLO setpoint — I verified by methods the archive did not use (rendering and
measuring the datasheet figure on its own grid; reading the OVLO polarity out of
SLVSE57C's §8.3.2.1 text and recomputing both worst-case corners), and **both
are correct**. The reverse-polarity FET is oriented the opposite way to the four
load switches, which is right and is the one place a copy-paste would have been
wrong. Every coil has a flyback path. The AND chain is seven terms deep with a
restrictive default on each. DRC is 0/0/0 on the archive standing alone.

My P1s are one genuine new arithmetic defect (P1-A: a published HARD safety bound
whose stack omits the term that is exactly the size of its claimed margin), one
confirmed-and-worsened declaration defect (P1-B), and one inert gate with a
mis-declared rail behind it (P1-C). None of them makes the artifact wrong; all
three belong in the ORDER_README and the next-rev work order, which is what P1
means.

**`order_verdict: BLOCKED-SOURCING`.** Measured, not asserted, at
2026-07-31 00:05 UTC with my own code against a live-proven endpoint:
**`C265111` reads `stockCount` 5 against `minPurchaseNum` 21.** That is not a
shortage, it is unbuyable at any quantity, and it is one line of a 59-line coded
BOM. Every other coded line clears. The release's own shipped stock evidence says
BLOCKED and my independent read says BLOCKED on the same line, so the
cross-check agrees in both directions. `ORDER` is not available to me; a
design-side `DO-NOT-ORDER` would be false, because the design is not what is
blocking. This board is right and you cannot buy it today — which is the exact
sentence `BLOCKED-SOURCING` exists to carry.
