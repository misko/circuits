## E. Attack (a) — does the substitute's retention risk reach the safety chain?

`J_THERM_A` pad→net, read off the board:

```
1 = 3V3_SW_A   2 = GND   3 = SDA_A   4 = SCL_A
5 = TH_CAM_A   6 = TH_MOUNT_A   7 = TH_PORT_A   8 = SHIELD_DRAIN
```

Only **pin 5** reaches a safety permission. `TH_MOUNT_*`/`TH_PORT_*` land on
`R_REF1/2` + `R_SER1/2` (expander/ADC readback) and touch no AND-chain term.
`TH_CAM_A` = {`J_THERM_A.5`, `R_CLMPA.1`, `R_HYS1.2`, `R_REF0.2`, `R_SER0.1`,
`U_COMP.3` (IN1+), `U_COMP2.2` (IN1−)}, and `TEMP_OK` feeds `U_AND2.1`,
`U_CAND1.6` and `U_FAULTAND.6`.

Values read from `fab/bom.csv`: `R_REF0` 10 kΩ, `R_CLMPA` 22 kΩ, `R_OPENT`
62 kΩ, `R_OPENB` 100 kΩ. A retention failure — plug backing out, or the whole
connector lifting on its narrow tabs — opens pin 5:

```
TH_CAM_A, pod UNPLUGGED = 3.300 * 22/(10+22)   = 2.26875 V
TCAM_OPEN threshold     = 3.300 * 100/(62+100) = 2.03704 V
margin                                          = 231.7 mV
ratiometric (RAIL-INDEPENDENT): 22/32 = 0.68750  vs  100/162 = 0.61728
                                        -> 0.07022 of V_rail, positive at every rail voltage
at the 3V3 low corner 3.201 V: node 2.2007 V, threshold 1.9759 V, margin 224.8 mV
```

`U_COMP2` IN− (2.269 V) > IN+ (2.037 V) ⇒ open-drain output LOW ⇒ **`TEMP_OK`
LOW** ⇒ `U_AND2` drops `KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED`, `U_CAND1` drops `CTR_SAFE`, and
`U_FAULTAND` pulls `FAULT_SET_N` LOW which **latches** the NAND SR. Opening
pin 2 (GND) instead gives the same node voltage and the same result.

**Answer: no. The retention risk is confined to the connector and lands
restrictive in every direction I can drive it, at every rail voltage, because
the open-thermistor detect exists exactly for this.** The waiver does not
understate it. If anything §5-0 overstates it — see RG-P2-3.
