# P0 — blocks the release

## P0-1 — the LDO is graded at 0.30 A while the same file declares 0.40 A of rails downstream of it. At the file's own numbers PD is 120 % of the package ceiling and the dropout margin is NEGATIVE.

`verification/power_tree.yaml` (byte-identical to `03_src/cooksense/rules/power_tree.yaml`) is the file
the E-TOPO gate grades. It declares, on the ONE graded rail:

```
rails:
  - name: 3V3
    converter: AMS1117-3.3
    iout_max_A: 0.3            # logic: 595/238/HC14/1G-family/MCP23017/watchdog/one-shot
    pdiss_max_mw: 1200
    #   PD = (5.250 - 3.201) x 0.3 = 615 mW  vs  1200 mW  ->  51%, PASS
```

and, **73 lines lower in the same file**, under `linear_rails:`:

```
  - name: 3V3_SW_A / 3V3_SW_B / 3V3_SW_RHA / 3V3_SW_RHE
    kind: switched_load_switch
    iout_max_A: 0.1
    element: AO3401A
    note: "3V3 -> Q_SW{A,B,RHA,RHE} (enabled by MCP23017) -> switched sensor rail; ..."
```

The `note:` says these four rails come **from 3V3**. I did not take the note's word for it — MEASURED off
`source/cooksense.kicad_pcb` with `pcbnew`, every one of the four AO3401A load switches has its **source
pad on net `3V3`**:

| part | pad 1 (gate) | pad 2 (source) | pad 3 (drain) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Q_SWA` @ (30.00, 88.00) | `SWG_A` | **`3V3`** | `3V3_SW_A` |
| `Q_SWB` @ (46.00, 90.00) | `SWG_B` | **`3V3`** | `3V3_SW_B` |
| `Q_SWRHA` @ (164.00, 90.00) | `SWG_RHA` | **`3V3`** | `3V3_SW_RHA` |
| `Q_SWRHE` @ (176.00, 90.00) | `SWG_RHE` | **`3V3`** | `3V3_SW_RHE` |

and each drain lands on a connector that leaves the board carrying a sensor head
(`J_THERM_A.1 = 3V3_SW_A`, `J_THERM_B.1 = 3V3_SW_B`, `J_RH_AMBIENT.1 = 3V3_SW_RHA`,
`J_RH_EXHAUST.1 = 3V3_SW_RHE` — measured). All four are on in normal operation: the architecture runs
**two MLX90640 cameras and two SHT45s simultaneously**, one per rail.

**So the AMS1117's declared output current is 0.30 + 4 × 0.10 = 0.70 A, and `iout_max_A: 0.3` — whose own
comment enumerates only "595/238/HC14/1G-family/MCP23017/watchdog/one-shot" — is 43 % of it.**

Nothing sums them. `linear_rails:` is described in the file itself as *"Documentation-only (ignored by
power_topology.py)"*, and `rails:` is the only key the gate reads. This is the same defect class the
TPS259573 `part.yaml` was corrected for in v1.8 (a budget written on a net that does not resolve is a
check that cannot fail); here it is a budget written in a section the checker does not read.

Both graded numbers flip. Arithmetic, using the file's own terms and its own cited series resistances
(F1 70.0 + Q_REV 73.5 + eFuse 47.0 = 190.5 mΩ; J_PWR floor 4.850 V per ADR-0021):

| LDO Iout | PD = (5.250 − 3.201) × I | vs `pdiss_max_mw` 1200 | series I (+0.15 coil +0.02 stop) | drop | vin_min | headroom vs 1300 mV dropout |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0.30 A** — as graded | 0.615 W | **51 %  PASS** | 0.47 A | 89.5 mV | 4.7605 V | **+61 mV  PASS** |
| **0.70 A** — the file's own declarations | **1.434 W** | **120 %  FAIL** | 0.87 A | 165.7 mV | 4.6843 V | **−15 mV  FAIL** |
| 0.36 A — realistic (2× MLX90640 23 mA, 2× SHT45 1 mA, 4 pull-up pairs 3 mA) | 0.738 W | 61 % | 0.53 A | 101.0 mV | 4.7490 V | +50 mV |

The third row is why I am calling this P0 rather than "just a paperwork error". Even on the *realistic*
load the dissipation is 0.738 W, **20 % higher than the 0.615 W the release reports**, and the file's
whole dropout argument is a **+55 mV** pass that it describes, correctly, as "the fully-stacked worst
case". A 20 % load error is not absorbable inside a 55 mV margin, and the release does not know it has one.

**Verification: CONFIRMED** — topology measured on the board (not read from the netlist or the yaml),
arithmetic re-derived from the file's own cited constants.

**What the fix is (and is not).** It is NOT a layout change. It is: state the LDO's real total load
(`3V3` logic + the four switched rails, each with a cited sensor current rather than a round 0.1 A),
re-run E-TOPO, and see whether it still passes. It may well pass at a defensible 0.36–0.40 A. It has not
been asked. Sealing freezes a green number that was computed on 43 % of the declared load.

## P0-2 — `pdiss_max_mw: 1200` is a 25 °C figure used as an ambient-independent ceiling, on a board the brief places in a 50–75 °C enclosure.

`power_tree.yaml` cites the ceiling correctly and then applies it with no ambient term:

```
    # DISSIPATION — CITED. ds1117 p.3, Note 2: "the maximum power dissipation of
    # **1.2 W for SOT-223**, ...
    #   PD = (5.250 - 3.201) x 0.3 = 615 mW  vs  1200 mW  -> 51%, PASS
    pdiss_max_mw: 1200
```

A SOT-223 "maximum power dissipation" of 1.2 W is not a property of the package alone; it is
`(Tj_max − Ta)/θ_JA` at the datasheet's reference ambient. Inverting it: **θ_JA implied = (125 − 25)/1.2
= 83.3 °C/W at Ta = 25 °C.** Re-derived ceilings at the ambients the BRIEF itself commissions
(`01_docs/BRIEF.md` line 117, verbatim: *"enclosure <=50 / 55 / 65 / 75"* — preferred / warn / stop / hard,
and `TH_ENCLOSURE` is one of the eight monitored ADC channels precisely because the board lives in it):

| Ta | PD allowed at the datasheet's own implied 83.3 °C/W | vs PD 0.615 W (as graded) | vs PD 0.738 W (realistic, P0-1) | vs PD 1.434 W (declared, P0-1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.200 W | 51 % | 61 % | 120 % |
| **50 °C** (design ambient) | 0.900 W | 68 % | 82 % | 159 % |
| 55 °C (warn) | 0.840 W | 73 % | 88 % | 171 % |
| **65 °C** (stop) | 0.720 W | 85 % | **103 % — OVER** | 199 % |
| **75 °C** (hard) | 0.600 W | **103 % — OVER** | 123 % | 239 % |

**At the brief's own hard enclosure limit the LDO is over its package ceiling on the release's own
615 mW figure**, and at the *stop* threshold it is over on the realistic 738 mW. There is no ambient
anywhere in `power_tree.yaml`, and no `theta_ja` field in `02_parts/AMS1117-3.3/part.yaml`.

**And the mechanism the file names for its θ_JA is not what the board does.** `power_tree.yaml` states:
*"Not derated further: the tab is VOUT and is flooded with 3V3 copper (that part.yaml's layout `notes`),
which is the mounting Table 1 (p.5) characterises at 55-65 C/W."* MEASURED on this board:

| measurement | value |
|---|---|
| **F.Cu zones on net `3V3`** | **none — the only `3V3` zone on the board is `In2.Cu`** |
| total F.Cu copper on net `3V3` within 12 mm of the tab | **13.4966 mm² = 0.0209 in²** (pads only: `U_LDO.4` 7.3911, `U_LDO.2` 2.8833, `C_LDOOUT.1` 1.3984, `FB1.1` 1.0105, `C_3V3.1` 0.8132) |
| F.Cu **track** copper on net `3V3`, board-wide | 1.178 mm², nearest segment **45 mm away** at (65.86, 78.95) |
| vias in the tab pad (`U_LDO.4`) | **2**, both Ø0.150 mm drill / 0.250 mm pad |
| plane coverage under the LDO body (0.25 mm grid, x 18–26.5, y 66–74) | In1 GND **98.6 %**, In2 3V3 **98.9 %**, B.Cu GND **98.6 %**, F.Cu GND 56.5 % |

The tab is a **7.39 mm² island in a GND pour**, not a flood. Its real heat path is downward through
0.2 mm of prepreg into a nearly solid three-plane sandwich, plus two 0.15 mm vias — a *different*
mechanism from the one cited, and one that Table 1 (characterised on **1/16 in FR-4, 1 oz, single-layer
copper area**) does not describe at all. The number 55–65 °C/W is therefore not evidence about this
board; it is a quotation about a different mounting. **The claim is REFUTED as written**; whether the
real θ_JA is better or worse than 83 °C/W is genuinely unknown and is the thing that has to be measured.

**Verification: CONFIRMED** (geometry measured; the citation's mechanism refuted).

**What retires it:** at G4 bench bring-up, load `3V3` to its real total and measure the `U_LDO` tab
temperature against a known ambient. That single measurement produces θ_JA and closes P0-1's dropout
question at the same time (the file already owes a 0.3 A dropout measurement — same bench session).

## P1-1 — the fastener-disc creepage model was derived at H4 and applied nowhere else. At H1 it goes NEGATIVE against an isolated keypad line.

`03_src/cooksense/floorplan.yaml`, H4 cutouts block, establishes the project's own fastener model
explicitly and uses a **3.000 mm disc RADIUS** (a 6.0 mm washer — I verified the radius from the file's
own arithmetic: `y = 52 - sqrt(3^2 - 1.5^2) = 49.4019` and `|centre − notch SW corner| = 2.6627 < 3.000`,
both of which only close at r = 3.000):

> *"the 3.000mm fastener disc OVERHANGS the notch ... so a straight run at the NW corner would cross the void"*

That model is applied to H4 and to no other hole. Applied to all four, MEASURED from each hole centre to
the nearest real copper on any layer (NPTH pads excluded — KiCad declares them on F&B.Cu and they are
not copper):

| hole | nearest copper | dist from centre | vs r 3.000 (project's own disc) | vs 5.0 mm washer (r 2.50) | vs ISO14580 head (r 2.25) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** (24.000, 14.000) | **`KP_D1` track, F.Cu** | **2.9500 mm** | **−0.0500 mm** | +0.4500 | +0.7000 |
| H1, second | `KP_U3` track, F.Cu | 3.2500 mm | +0.2500 | +0.7500 | +1.0000 |
| H2 (194.000, 14.000) | `KP_U6` track, F.Cu | 4.5876 mm | +1.5876 | +2.0876 | +2.3376 |
| H3 (17.000, 90.000) | GND via | 2.8750 mm | −0.1250 | +0.3750 | +0.6250 |
| **H4** (193.000, 52.000) | **`5V_STOP` track, F.Cu** | **2.8500 mm** | **−0.1500 mm** | +0.3500 | +0.6000 |
| H4, and | F.Cu **GND pour** | 1.5500 mm | −1.4500 | −0.9500 | −0.7000 |

Two consequences, and the first is the one that matters on this board:

**(a) H1.** `KP_D1` at 2.9500 mm and `KP_U3` at 3.2500 mm are 0.30 mm apart radially. A conductive
fastener disc of radius ≥ 3.250 mm — i.e. **any washer ≥ 6.5 mm OD; DIN 9021 M2.5 is 8.0 mm** — spans a
**U-select line and a D-select line of the OEM membrane matrix**. Shorting a U line to a D line is a
**permanently pressed key at the appliance panel**, and it is downstream of every hardware gate on this
board: the AND-chain, the one-shot, the fault latch and the STOP preemption all gate the **relay coils**,
not the membrane copper. The 12-relay matrix cannot un-press it. Solder mask is the only barrier, at
~25 µm of LPI under M2.5 fastener torque.

**(b) H4.** A 6.0 mm washer covers the F.Cu GND pour (overlap 1.450 mm) **and** the `5V_STOP` rail track
(overlap 0.150 mm) simultaneously — that is `5V_PROTECTED` through a 0 Ω link, shorted to GND by the
fastener. The floorplan's H4 block asserts *"MEASURED to cross NOTHING (v1.3 placement): COIL_STOP_N ->
K_STOP.2 and 5V_STOP -> K_STOP.1 both route WEST of x191.5"*. That claim is **TRUE and beside the point**
— I confirm the track sits at x 189.65–190.15, west of 191.5. It is inside the *disc*, not inside the
*notch*, and the file only ever checked the notch.

**Verification: CONFIRMED** (all six rows measured on the board).

**ORDER_README:** at H1 and H2 fit **no washer**, or a head/washer ≤ 5.0 mm OD; at H4 fit a **nylon**
screw or none. **Next revision:** make the fastener disc a first-class keepout at every mounting hole,
with its radius stated once, and re-check the keypad-domain holes against it — the model already exists,
it was simply never generalised.

## P1-2 — no stackup and no copper weight are declared anywhere, so every ampacity and isolation number in this release is uncheckable at the fab.

MEASURED: `grep -c stackup` returns **0** on both `source/cooksense.kicad_pcb` and
`source/cooksense.kicad_pro`. The archive ships no gerber job file. The board is 4-layer with the plane
assignment F.Cu(signal + GND pour) / In1(GND) / In2(**3V3**) / B.Cu(signal + GND pour), and:

- every ampacity floor in `nets.yaml` (`PWR_IN` 0.5 mm at "2A worst-case", `COIL_RAIL` 0.4 mm, …) is an
  IPC-2221 statement that is meaningless without a copper weight. At 1 oz outer, 0.5 mm × 35 µm gives

- `ISO_CONTACTOR`'s 2.000 mm and `KEYPAD_ISO`'s 6.000 mm are creepage numbers against a **material group**
  (IIIa) that the release never asks the fab for, and against an etch tolerance it never bounds.

**Verification: CONFIRMED.** Not a P0 because JLC's default 4-layer stackup is what will actually be
built and it is 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner — but that is my inference, not the release's statement, and
the whole point of a fab package is that it not require one.

**ORDER_README:** state the ordered stackup (JLC04161H-7628 or equivalent) and the outer/inner copper
weights, and add the line to `nets.yaml` beside `fab_tier`.

# P2 — recorded

## P2-1 — `ISO_CONTACTOR` clears its rule by exactly 0.0000 mm, and the pour half of that clearance is self-fulfilling.

MEASURED, 41 isolated-copper items against every non-`ISO_CONTACTOR` item on the same layer:

| item | layer | nearest non-isolated copper | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `J_ISOLOOP.1/.2/.4` | F.Cu, In1.Cu, B.Cu | `ZONE[GND]` | **2.0000 mm** (all nine) |
| `J_ISOLOOP.1/.2/.4` | In2.Cu | `ZONE[3V3]` | **2.0000 mm** (all three) |
| `U_OPTO.3/.4` | F.Cu | `ZONE[GND]` | 2.0400 mm |
| **items below the 2.000 mm rule** | — | — | **0 of 41** |

The rule is met everywhere. Two notes:

- The binding neighbour is a **zone fill**, and a zone fill retreats to exactly the clearance it is told
  — so `opto_isolation_2mm` reporting zero violations against the pour is the fill and the check sharing
  a value (canon M1). The rule has real teeth only against tracks, pads and vias, and against those the
  worst measured is 2.0400 mm.
- 2.000 mm is IEC 60664-1 basic insulation at 30 V working, PD3, material group IIIa — a **creepage**
  number, correctly chosen (the brief commissions the loop at *"isolated open-collector/dry contact,
  <=30V/50mA ... no mains on Board A"*). At exactly nominal there is no allowance for etch tolerance;
  ±0.05 mm of under-etch on the pour puts it at 1.95 mm, 2.5 % under the standard.

**Verification: CONFIRMED (rule met, zero margin).**

## P2-2 — the `keypad_isolation_6mm` exemption is written by FOOTPRINT and evidenced by PAD, and its recorded worst-case number is stale by 0.42 mm.

The DRU exempts `!B.memberOfFootprint('J_KEY_MATRIX')` and defends it in-line: *"Measured on sealed v1.6:
dropping the exemption yields 67 clearance violations, 67/67 with J_KEY_MATRIX.MP on one side ... worst
0.5723mm. The board's only 2 unnetted copper items are both J_KEY_MATRIX.MP, so the exempted set is
FINITE and enumerated."*

Re-measured on **this** board, exemption removed, NPTH excluded:

- **55 items** measure below 6.000 mm. **55/55 have `J_KEY_MATRIX.MP` on the other side** — zero domain
  crossings. The finiteness argument holds, and I confirm all ten signal pads carry `KEYPAD_ISO` nets
  (`J_KEY_MATRIX.1 = KP_U1` … `.10 = KP_D4`), so the exempted set really is the two shell tabs today.
- **Worst is 0.9916 mm** (`KP_D1` track), not 0.5723 mm. The comment's number is a v1.6 measurement
  carried into a v1.7 file.
- The *form* is broader than the *evidence*: the condition exempts the whole footprint, so a future
  revision that lands any SELV net on `J_KEY_MATRIX` inherits a silent 6 mm exemption for it. The
  evidence is per-pad; the rule should be too (`!B.memberOfFootprint(...) || B.NetClass == 'KEYPAD_ISO'`
  does not express it — a pad-name predicate does).

**Verification: CONFIRMED** (exemption sound today; number stale; form broader than evidence).

## P2-3 — the eFuse exposed pad has ONE via.

MEASURED: `U_EFUSE` pad 9 (EP, net GND) spans x[59.550, 60.450] y[56.200, 57.800] = **1.440 mm²** and
contains exactly **one** Ø0.150 mm via at (60.000, 56.600). `02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/part.yaml` says,
twice and in the plural: *"the ONLY heat path — MUST be soldered to the GND plane with thermal vias"* /
*"EP MUST tie the GND plane with thermal vias"*.

Benign at this board's load — I derived the current limit independently rather than trusting the netclass:
the part.yaml's own calibration point is `487 Ω = 4.42 A`, giving K = 2152.5 A·Ω, and `R_ILM = 1.2 kΩ`
(BOM: `RC0402FR-071K2L`, C138040) ⇒ **I_LIM ≈ 1.794 A**, comfortably under the 2 A the `PWR_IN` floor is
sized for. At the 0.50 A worst case, R_ON 47 mΩ ⇒ **11.8 mW**. The obligation is unmet; the physics does
not bite here. It would bite on any board that used this part near its 4 A rating.

**Verification: CONFIRMED (one via) / dissipation benign.**

## P2-4 — 1097 vias, all Ø0.250/0.150, annular ring exactly 0.050 mm — the floor, board-wide, with no scoped relief.

MEASURED: a single via geometry is used everywhere (0.250 mm pad, 0.150 mm drill ⇒ **0.0500 mm annular

**Verification: CONFIRMED.**

## P2-5 — the LDO's compensation cap is a 10 V X5R that loses roughly half its value at the bias it works at.

`C_LDOOUT` = `CL21A226MAQNNNE` (C45783), 0805 22 µF X5R **10 V**. `02_parts/AMS1117-3.3/part.yaml` states
the requirement verbatim: *"22uF solid tantalum on VOUT ensures stability for all operating conditions"*
and *"needs >=22u-class output cap for stability"*. A 10 V X5R 0805 at 3.3 V DC bias typically retains
50–60 % ⇒ **≈ 11–13 µF effective**, with near-zero ESR where the datasheet assumes tantalum ESR.
Placement is good (MEASURED `C_LDOOUT.1` pad to `U_LDO` tab = **2.900 mm**, well inside the 5 mm
`keep_short` budget), and the In2 3V3 plane adds distributed bulk — this is a derating note, not a
predicted oscillation.

**Verification: CONFIRMED (part value and bias); stability impact UNVERIFIABLE-HERE.**

# Verdict rationale

**`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE`.** Not for a geometry defect — the geometry is, by my own independent
measurement, unusually well defended. The isolation comb holds (V1–V4), the DRU floors hold with an
honest scoped waiver (V5), the coil clamps exist (V6), the planes are intact (V10), DRC is genuinely
0/0/0 (V11), and every claim I set out to break in the mechanical domain survived.

It is DEFECTIVE because the one part on this board with a hard package ceiling and a 55 mV margin is
graded on **43 % of its own file's declared load** (P0-1), against a **25 °C ceiling on a board
commissioned for a 50–75 °C enclosure** (P0-2), with the θ_JA justification citing a copper flood that
**does not exist on this board** (measured: zero F.Cu `3V3` zone, 13.50 mm² of pad copper, two 0.15 mm
vias). Each of those three alone would be a P1. Together they mean the release cannot demonstrate that
its 3V3 supply stays inside the package it chose, at the load it declares, in the environment it
commissions — and the gate that should have said so passed because the four rails it must count are
written in the section the checker is told to ignore.

The fix is arithmetic and a bench measurement, not a respin. That is the best possible kind of P0: it may
well come back green at a defensible 0.36–0.40 A. But it has to be asked before the number is frozen,
because a seal makes it permanent.

**`order_verdict: BLOCKED-SOURCING`.** Measured, not inherited: C265111 stock 5 against MOQ 21 for a
need of 10, with live controls in the same session. Every other coded, placed line clears 5 × its refs.
The design question and the buying question are separate here and both are red for separate reasons.
