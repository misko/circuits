## 2. Findings

### P0-1 — the file that is said to CLOSE the thermal P0 still publishes the WRONG junction temperature, at a current it does not declare, and re-asserts a figure the accompanying ADR deletes

`03_src/cooksense/rules/power_tree.yaml` (and its byte-identical copy at `verification/power_tree.yaml`), lines 673–678, immediately above `pdiss_max_mw: 497`:

```
#   PD = (5.250 - 3.201) x 0.150 = 307.4 mW  vs  497.8 mW  -> 62 %, PASS
#
# RESULTING JUNCTION TEMPERATURE, which is the number that actually matters:
#   Tj = 75 + (0.3074 + 0.0578) x 90 = 75 + 32.9 = 107.9 C   (17.1 C margin)
#   + the <=1.1 s dual-heater excursion from the iout block, +3.8 C
#                                                = 111.7 C   (13.3 C margin)
```

`0.150` is **not** this rail's declared current (`iout_max_A: 0.20`, eight lines above) and **not** its derived one (0.1169 A). It is `linear_rails[5V_KEY_RELAY].iout_max_A`. MEASURED (by me, arithmetic at the file's own declared inputs):

| quantity | the file says | correct at `iout_max_A: 0.20` | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PD` pass element | 307.4 mW | **409.8 mW** | −25 % |
| % of the 497 mW ceiling | 62 % | **82.5 %** | |
| `Tj` continuous | 107.9 °C | **117.08 °C** | −9.2 °C |
| margin to `Tj_max` 125 °C | **17.1 °C** | **7.92 °C** | the published margin is **2.16×** the real one |

Three independent corroborations that 117.08 is the right number and 107.9 is not:

1. **The gate.** `power_topology.py` recomputes from the declared key and prints
   `PD 410 mW ((Vin_max 5.25 - Vout_min 3.201) x 0.2 A) vs rating 497 mW (82%)`. RAW_EXIT 0.
2. **ADR-0026**, shipped in this same `verification/` directory: *"`Tj = 75 + (0.4098 + 0.0578) × 90 = 117.1 °C`"*, and ADR-0027 §6: *"PD 410 mW / 82 %, `Tj` 117.08 °C, 7.92 °C of margin"*.
3. **The repo's own bound-regeneration gate**, run by me from the repo root: `adr_bound_provenance.py --adr smc0985-cooksense/01_docs/decisions/0026 .` → `nearest standard value 0.2 ... evaluates to 117.0795`. RAW_EXIT 0.

So four artifacts say 117.1 °C and the one canonical file that both the gate and every human reads says 107.9 °C.

**The second half is worse than staleness.** The trailing line adds *"+ the ≤1.1 s dual-heater excursion from the iout block, **+3.8 C**"*. ADR-0027 Decision 4, in this archive, says of exactly that figure:

> *"ds1117 publishes no thermal time constant, so the excursion is **NOT BOUNDED BY ANY CITED NUMBER** and must not be claimed at +3.0 °C."*

and gives the steady-state alternative as **+17.8 °C**, which would put `Tj` at 134.9 °C. `power_tree.yaml` still asserts +3.8 °C and a "13.3 C margin". The archive therefore ships a self-contradiction on the junction temperature of the one part this board has failed on three times, and the surviving assertion is the optimistic one.

**Why this is graded P0 rather than P1.** The claim under re-gate is that all three prior P0s are closed *"by `01_docs/decisions/0026-*.md` and `0027-*.md` **and by the current `03_src/cooksense/rules/power_tree.yaml`**"*. Round 2's P0 was *the declared thermal ceiling*. The **ceiling** was fixed (`pdiss_max_mw` 1200 → 497, correctly derived, and I re-derive 497.8 exactly). The file's **use** of that ceiling was not: it still publishes a false `PD`, a false `Tj` and a margin more than twice the truth, plus a deleted excursion. That is a load-bearing number in the canonical file being *wrong*, not merely un-derived — a strictly worse condition than the OWED items this board declares honestly elsewhere.

**Remedy** (regeneration, not hand-edit — canon M3 applies to the generator, but this block is hand-authored prose inside the rules file, so it is edited where it is authored): replace the four lines with `PD = (5.250 − 3.201) × 0.200 = 409.8 mW vs 497.8 mW → 82 %`, `Tj = 75 + (0.4098 + 0.0578) × 90 = 117.08 °C (7.92 °C margin)`, and delete the `+3.8 °C` line in favour of ADR-0027 §4's staggered `+0.2 °C adiabatic / +1.2 °C steady state`.

### P1-1 — the series sum finally counts the board's copper and still does not count the CONNECTOR, and the connector is 28 %–83 % of the whole remaining margin

ADR-0027's chain is `J_PWR.1 → F1 → Q_REV → U_EFUSE → U_LDO.3`, with the 4.850 V source applied at node **`J_PWR.1` — a PCB PAD**. ADR-0021 Decision 1 specifies the supply *"measured **at the connector** under full load"*. Those are not the same node. What separates them is the Micro-Fit mating interface.

**MEASURED (by me, Molex-origin literature downloaded 2026-07-30):** Micro-Fit 3.0 **Contact Resistance (max.) = 10 milliohms**, stated identically in Molex's Micro-Fit 3.0 Connector System family literature `987650-5984.pdf` (p. "Electrical", beside *Current (max.): 8.5 A*, *Mates With … Header (Series **43650**)*) and in the Molex 43030/43031 crimp-terminal sales drawings (`Contact Resistance: 10 milliohms max.`, *Product Specification: PS-43045/PS-43650*). **INHERITED (from a web search of `PS-43650-001`, which molex.com would not serve me — three fetch attempts timed out, NOT re-verified):** the product spec additionally allows **≤20 mΩ change from initial** after durability / thermal-aging / vibration / shock.

MEASURED (by me, my own nodal solve, declared corner, `I_board` 0.4024 A):

| case | `V(U_LDO.3)` | headroom | vs 1300 mV |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADR-0027 as published (no connector, no GND return) | 4.7282 V | 1329.2 mV | **+29.2** |
| + 1 Micro-Fit contact @ 10 mΩ | 4.7241 V | 1325.1 mV | +25.1 |
| **+ 2 Micro-Fit contacts @ 10 mΩ (supply and return legs)** | **4.7201 V** | **1321.1 mV** | **+21.1** |
| + 2 contacts + 2 mΩ GND-plane return (my estimate, below) | 4.7193 V | 1320.3 mV | +20.3 |
| + 2 contacts at the aged allowance (2 × 30 mΩ) | 4.7040 V | 1305.0 mV | **+5.0** |
| + 2 contacts + eFuse `RON` −40…125 °C row (53 mΩ) | 4.7177 V | 1318.7 mV | +18.7 |
| PEAK, heaters STAGGERED, + 2 contacts | 4.7178 V | 1318.8 mV | +18.8 |
| PEAK, heaters COINCIDENT, + 2 contacts | 4.6865 V | 1287.5 mV | **−12.5** (ADR-0027 has −2.6) |

and the **published bound moves**:

| connector term | `LDO_DROPOUT_IBOARD_MAX` break-even | ADR-0027 publishes |
|---|---|---|
| 0 mΩ | 0.4913 A | 0.4910 A ✓ |
| 20 mΩ | **0.4631 A** | −5.7 % |
| 60 mΩ (aged) | **0.4154 A** | −15.4 % |

At 20 mΩ the declared-case headroom to the bound falls from 88.6 mA to **60.7 mA**; at the aged allowance, to **13.0 mA**.

**Three things make this a finding rather than a quibble:**

1. `02_parts/43650-0224/part.yaml` has a `limits:` block that carries current, voltage, temperature and reflow — and **no contact resistance**. So the number is not merely uncounted, it is absent from the dossier that a future sum would read.
2. **ADR-0027's own "what is still UN-DERIVED on this rail after this pass, named so silence is not read as coverage" list does not contain it.** That list is the artifact's own control against exactly this failure, and it has the gap it was written to prevent.
3. This term was already on the table. `ORDER_README.md` §13 P1-B — the prior round's own ladder — reads `+15.4 mV with the board copper at 20 C` / `+5.4 mV **with the connector** at 20 C` / `−2.5 mV with the connector at 70 C`. **ADR-0027 adopted the copper half of that finding, re-measured it upward to 137.79 mΩ, and dropped the connector half without recording that it had.**

Honest counter-argument, stated because it may be the right reading: if *"at the connector"* means *at the header's solder pads*, the contact resistance belongs to the supply integrator and the board's sum is complete. **Nothing anywhere in the tree resolves which node is meant** — that ambiguity, worth 8–24 mV against a 29 mV margin, is the defect.

**Also uncounted, and I measured its size so it can be dismissed on evidence rather than by omission:** the analysis is single-ended — the 4.850 V is applied at `J_PWR.1` and `V(U_LDO.3)` is read against a single ground node, so the **GND-plane return between `J_PWR.2` and `U_LDO.1`** is in no sum either. MEASURED (by me, my own board reader): the two pads are **6.52 mm apart**, each has **exactly one GND via inside the pad**, and the F.Cu GND pour runs directly between them; `U_LDO.1` sits *west* of `J_PWR.2` while essentially the whole board is east of it, so almost none of the through-return crosses between them. ESTIMATE (by me, sheet resistance 0.598 mΩ/□ at 35 µm / 75 °C over ≲2 squares, plus one 3.52 mΩ barrel at each end shared with the pour): **1–3 mΩ, i.e. 0.2–0.6 mV.** Real, named, not verdict-moving.

### P1-2 — the "fourth class of load" sweep enumerated RESISTORS, and the class it declares is not made of resistors

ADR-0027 §5 closes the 11.8 mA OWED delta by *"Every resistor on the board (87) was enumerated from the netlist with BOTH endpoint nets and each pull-down's far node traced to its DRIVER"*, and names the class as **"current that leaves a 3V3-POWERED OUTPUT rather than the 3V3 NET, which a 'which pads are on net 3V3' sweep cannot see"**.

I reproduced that sweep independently (my own netlist parser): **87 resistors**, and every block of the budget to the third decimal — see §3. The method is sound for its own instances. But a resistor enumeration cannot see a member of the declared class whose **sink is an IC input**, and this board has one:

**MEASURED (by me, from the archived Toshiba `TBD62083A_20160511.pdf`, Electrical Characteristics p.5):** `IIN(ON)` **max 0.1 mA at VIN = 2.5 V** (TBD62083A series; the '84A row is 0.5 mA at 7.0 V). MEASURED (by me, netlist): `U_ULNA`/`U_ULNB` inputs are driven by `U_DECU`/`U_DECD` (SN74HC238, `VCC` on `3V3`) and by `U_ONESHOT.13` (CD74HC221, `VCC` on `3V3`). A '238 is one-hot, so the worst case is **one `SEL_U*` + one `SEL_D*` + `PRESS_TIMED` = 3 inputs high**, ≈0.3 mA at 2.5 V and ≈0.41 mA extrapolated to 3.399 V through the same input impedance.

**Magnitude: 0.3–0.4 mA on a 116.859 mA sum (0.35 %), entirely inside the ×1.5 design margin (58.4 mA of slack). It moves nothing.** The finding is the METHOD: the sweep that was written to close a class was scoped to the wrong object, so the class is still open by construction rather than by measurement — and the file now says the delta is "CLOSED, and closed UPWARD". Two other members of the same class are un-enumerated and un-bounded: the Raspberry Pi's internal GPIO pull-downs on the ~5 board-driven signals into `J_PI` (≈0.2–0.3 mA at ~50 kΩ), and MLCC/connector leakage (explicitly assigned to the ×1.5 margin, which is fair).

### P1-3 — `D_ESD_IN` is the lowest-breakdown clamp on the board, it sits upstream of every protection element, and its dossier declares no ratings at all

MEASURED (by me, netlist): `D_ESD_IN.1 = 5V_IN`, `D_ESD_IN.2 = GND`, and `5V_IN = {J_PWR.1, F1.1, D_ESD_IN.1}` — so the part is **directly across the input, ahead of the polyfuse and ahead of the eFuse.** Nothing is in series with it.

MEASURED (by me, archived Nexperia `PESD5V0S1BA_2024-04-26.pdf`, Table 6 Characteristics):

* `VRWM` reverse standoff voltage — **5 V max**
* `VBR` breakdown voltage, `IR = 1 mA`, 25 °C — **min 5.5 V**, max 9.5 V
* `IRM` at `VRWM = 5 V`, 25 °C — 5 nA typ / **100 nA max**
* `VCL` 10 V at `IPP = 1 A`

MEASURED (by me, `grep -c '^limits:' 02_parts/PESD5V0S1BA/part.yaml` = **0**): the dossier records the pinning at length and **no `limits:` block** — no `vrwm`, no `vbr`, no `ipp`, no power rating. It is the only protection part on this board whose ratings are nowhere in `02_parts`.

Consequences, in ascending severity:

1. The ADR-0021 supply ceiling is **5.25 V**, which is **above** this part's 5 V stand-off and only **250 mV below its MINIMUM breakdown**. At 5.25 V the leakage is unspecified; a two-point exponential fit between the datasheet's own 100 nA @ 5.0 V and 1 mA @ 5.5 V gives ≈**10 µA** — harmless, but a number nobody has written down.
2. Above ~5.5 V (a mis-selected adapter, the case ADR-0021 exists to bound) **the first device to conduct on this board is a SOD-323 ESD diode with no fuse, no eFuse and no series resistance in its path**, at a breakdown 0.9 V *below* `D_TVS`'s 6.40 V and *below* the eFuse's own earliest possible OVLO trip of 5.3682 V… which is on the wrong side: the OVLO disconnects the *load*, and `D_ESD_IN` is on the *source* side of it. This is precisely the failure mode `02_parts/SMBJ5.0A` names for `D_TVS` — *"a sustained over-voltage the eFuse fails to cut off makes this 600 W TRANSIENT part a DC regulator"* — applied to a part two orders of magnitude smaller, with the fuse removed.
3. The board's own canon makes the opposite call for the other shunt clamp and says why. `electrical_invariants.yaml` asserts `D_REVCLAMP.1` on `5V_FUSED` because *"the reverse fault current path supply→J_PWR→F1→clamp→GND must pass THROUGH F1 so the polyfuse trips; **on 5V_IN (upstream) the clamp current bypassed the fuse and was bounded only by the supply**"*. `D_ESD_IN` is on `5V_IN`.

**Honest counter-argument:** an ESD clamp belongs at the connector, ahead of everything, so the strike does not traverse the board — that is legitimate practice and probably the intent. But it is **nowhere recorded as a decision**, no invariant pins the node, the 5.5 V-vs-5.25 V derating is nowhere derived, and the dossier that would carry either fact has no ratings in it. A 6.0 V or 6.8 V stand-off SOD-323 would restore the derating at no layout cost; that trade is not evaluated anywhere.

I derived the margin by hand so the silence is not mistaken for danger: `vout_min` 3.201 V − the highest load brown-out 3.0 V = **201 mV**, distributed over a `3V3` **plane** (In2.Cu filled zone, one polygon, MEASURED by me) plus, for the switched rails, one AO3401A and a pod harness — a few mV. **The rail is comfortable; the gate is inert.** This is a P1 against the artifact because the artifact ships the inert grade as evidence.

### P2-1 — the TVS is operated above its stand-off, its leakage is unbounded at temperature, and it is graded against a different manufacturer's datasheet than the one being bought

`02_parts/SMBJ5.0A/part.yaml` declares this openly and I confirm the arithmetic. MEASURED (by me, from the committed `LITTELFUSE-SMBJ-SERIES-v4-2025-07-04.pdf`, SMBJ5.0A row): `V_R` **5.0 V**, `V_BR` **6.40 / 7.00 V** at `I_T` 10 mA, `V_C` 9.2 V at `I_PP` 65.3 A, `I_R` **800 µA at V_R**, `α(V_BR)` **0.041 %/°C**. The ADR-0021 OVLO coordination (`V_BR` min at −20 °C = 6.40 × (1 − 0.041 % × 45) = 6.2819 V vs the latest guaranteed trip 6.2394 V, **+43 mV**) therefore rests on a correctly-quoted tempco — I checked, because at the more common 0.057 %/°C the same arithmetic gives 6.2358 V and the coordination would fail by 3.6 mV.

Two residuals:

* `V_R` 5.0 V against a **5.25 V** spec ceiling. The dossier's two-point fit gives ≈1.26 mA at 5.25 V (power_tree carries 1.3 mA) and states plainly that *"it is UNBOUNDED at temperature: 800 µA is a 25 °C number and the vendor publishes no leakage-vs-temperature curve"*. **That term is a member of the 0.4024 A trunk sum, and it is not in ADR-0027's UN-DERIVED list** — the list that exists so silence is not read as coverage.
* `manufacturer: MDD`, `datasheet: Littelfuse`. The binding number for the OVLO setpoint (`V_BR` min 6.40 V) is another vendor's. I verified Littelfuse's row from the committed PDF; I did **not** verify MDD's, and LCSC `C113974`'s brand row was not re-read for `V_BR`.

### P2-2 — the switched-rail drop declaration is not achievable by the part that is fitted

`linear_rails[3V3_SW_A / 3V3_SW_B]` declares `vin_min 3.201` / `vout_min 3.2` at `iout_max_A 0.0314` → **1 mV at 31.4 mA = 31.8 mΩ**. MEASURED (by me, `02_parts/AO3401A/part.yaml` quoting AOS Rev3.1): the lowest published row is `RDS(on)` **60 mΩ MAX at V_GS = −4.5 V**; there is **no row at all** below −4.5 V, and these four switches run at `V_GS = −3.3 V`. So the declared drop is under-stated by ≥2× and the true value is not bounded by any published number. Real magnitude ≈2–3 mV; nothing downstream cares (the Adafruit breakout's own AP2112K-3.3 has ~30 mV of dropout at 31 mA and the MLX90640 floor is 3.0 V). Immaterial, wrong as declared.

### P2-3 — two uncounted terms in the 5 V trunk, named because the sum claims to be itemised

* **eFuse own quiescent current.** MEASURED (by me, archived SLVSE57C §7.5, `TPS2595x3`, `VEN ≤ VOVLO`): `IQ` **175 typ / 250 µA max** at IN. In no sum.
* **The OVLO divider.** `R_OVT` 100 k + `R_OVB` 26.1 k across 5.25 V = **41.6 µA**; `power_tree.yaml` lists `R_OVT.1 … 0.0000 A`.

## 7. What would change the verdict

* **`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE` rests on P0-1 alone.** Correct the four lines of `power_tree.yaml`'s dissipation worked example to the file's own declared `iout_max_A` (409.8 mW / 82 % / `Tj` 117.08 °C / 7.92 °C margin) and delete the `+3.8 °C` excursion in favour of ADR-0027 §4's staggered figures, and the P0 is closed by inspection — no gate re-run, no copper, no re-seal of anything upstream.
