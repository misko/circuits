## VERDICT: ORDER-OK-WITH-NOTES

**No P0.** The release's headline geometric claim — that the ≥6.0 mm keypad↔SELV
barrier survives the new pin-out-13 relay land — is **CONFIRMED by independent
measurement, with margin, and the new land made it BETTER, not worse.** Four P1s
and five P2s follow. Two of the P1s (silk stroke, slot width) are unmanaged
manufacturability classes that no gate on this board can see.

## METHOD

Every number below was measured on the built board with `/usr/bin/python3` +
`pcbnew` 10.0.4, deliberately **not** re-using `audit_board.py`'s primitives
(canon M1 — checker and checked must not share a method):

* Zones were **filled in memory** (`pcbnew.ZONE_FILLER`) before measuring, so
  pours are included. `audit_board.iso_min_creepage()` explicitly excludes pours
  ("Pours are NOT measured here", `audit_board.py:154`); this pass does not.
* Distances come from KiCad's own geometry kernel: `SHAPE::Collide(other, clr)`
  driven by a 34-step integer binary search on `clr`, i.e. true polygon-to-polygon
  edge distance to ~1 nm, on **true pad/track/via/zone shapes** — not the
  centre-line-minus-half-width circle approximation `audit_board.py` uses.
* Per-layer: F.Cu / In1.Cu / In2.Cu / B.Cu compared only against themselves; a
  through-hole pad or via counts on every copper layer.
* 10 322 copper shapes, 1 291 drilled holes, 239 footprints, 56 Edge.Cuts items.

Where a figure is load-bearing it is re-derived a second time in closed form and
both constructions are printed.

# REFUTATIONS — hypotheses I tried to kill the board with and could not

These are recorded because a refutation measured is worth as much here as a find.

### R1 — the ≥6.0 mm keypad↔SELV barrier HOLDS on the new land. **6.2200 mm.**

Minimum same-layer copper-edge distance between `KEYPAD_ISO`-netclass copper and
**every** other named net, over tracks + vias + pads + **filled pours**, all four
layers, whole board:

| | value |
|---|---|
| **measured minimum** | **6.2200 mm** |
| where | F.Cu, `K_D1.4` (`D_SEL_BUS`, THT ⌀1.50 @ 113.63, 45.62) ↔ `COIL_D1_N` track x120.80, y45.60→53.10, w 0.400 |
| requirement | 6.000 mm (`cooksense.kicad_dru` `keypad_isolation_6mm`; brief §4/§7) |
| margin | **+0.220 mm** |

Independent closed-form re-derivation of that same pair:
`120.80 − 113.63 − 0.75 (pad r) − 0.20 (track half-width) = 6.2200 mm`.
**The two constructions agree to 0.0000 mm.**

The second- and third-tightest pairs are the same geometry on other relays
(`K_D4.3`↔`COIL_D4_N` 6.2200; `K_D2.1`↔`D_SEL_BUS` 6.2300; `RSTOP_MID`↔`5V_STOP`
6.2700). Nothing on the board is under 6.2200 mm.

Note this figure is a **clearance** (straight line on one surface). Creepage is
the surface path and is ≥ the straight line, always — and the 12 milled slots
only lengthen it. So 6.2200 mm is a hard lower bound on the creepage, which is
the quantity the rule's comment names (ADR-0015 Decision 2).

I also checked the one path a same-layer metric misses: F.Cu-to-B.Cu creepage
**down a slot wall**. Every slot sits inside an all-layer `iso_gapN` (y 23.2–29.4)
or `iso_pktS` (y 46.9–52.9) keepout, so the nearest copper to any slot edge is
>2.4 mm and the wrap-around path (2.8 + 1.6 + 2.8 = 7.2 mm at the north slots,
2.93 + 1.6 + 3.0 = 7.53 mm at the pocket slots) is longer than the flat path.
No shorter route exists.

# P1 FINDINGS

## P1-1 — Six safety-connector refdes ship at a silk stroke BELOW the fab floor, in the one DRC class this project turned off

**Measured** (`04_kicad/cooksense.kicad_pcb`, F.Silkscreen):

| refdes | text height | **stroke** | bbox |
|---|---|---|---|
| `J_ESTOP` | 0.450 | **0.130** | x[190.483,193.517] y[74.669,75.591] |
| `J_DOOR` | 0.450 | **0.130** | x[192.789,193.711] y[76.372,79.148] |
| `J_MODE` | 0.450 | **0.130** | x[195.812,198.588] y[51.539,52.461] |
| `D_DOOR` | 0.450 | **0.130** | x[189.789,190.711] y[77.858,80.742] |
| `R_DOORPD` | 0.450 | **0.130** | x[188.908,192.692] y[76.689,77.611] |
| `D_COILEN` | 0.450 | **0.130** | x[187.001,190.419] y[49.139,50.061] |

Stroke histogram over all 249 silk texts: **243 @ 0.150 mm, 6 @ 0.130 mm.**
These six are the only ones.

**Cause**, in source: `03_src/cooksense/fix_silk_placement.py`, lines **289** and
**338**, identical statement in both:

```python
t.SetTextThickness(int(max(0.13, sz * 0.2) * 1e6))
```

At `sz = 0.45` this evaluates `max(0.13, 0.09) = 0.13` — a hard-coded 0.13 mm
clamp, 13 % under the floor.

**What it contradicts**, three ways:

1. `skills/kicad-pcb/references/fab_tiers.yaml`, `jlc_4layer_advanced`:
   `min_silk_stroke: 0.15  # JLC capability page`.
2. `04_kicad/cooksense.kicad_pro` `rules.min_text_thickness: 0.15` — the board's
   own declared floor.
3. The project's own waivers. `rules/policy_waivers.yaml` `SILK-TEXT-THICKNESS`:
   *"0.15mm stroke = JLCPCB's silk floor (legible at fab); **thinner would not
   print**"*. And `P-SILK-FN` line 227: *"(13 at 0.60 mm, 3 at 0.45 mm) with a
   0.13-0.15 mm stroke — **at or above** the `jlc_4layer_advanced` legibility
   floor the exporter enforces."* **0.13 is not at or above 0.15.** The waiver
   states the measurement and then draws the opposite conclusion from it.

**Why nothing caught it:** `apply_drc_policy.py` sets `text_thickness` to
`ignore` (with `silk_over_copper`, `silk_edge_clearance`, `silk_overlap`).
Confirmed in the built `.kicad_pro` `rule_severities`. This is the exact class the
brief flagged as ungated, and it is the class that broke.

**Why it matters and is not cosmetic.** These six are not a random sample. They
are precisely the refs the v1.7 silk pass existed to fix — `policy_waivers.yaml`
P-SILK-FN: *"pass B (ownership: `J_DOOR`/`J_ESTOP`/`J_MODE` must each be nearer
their own housing than any other) and pass C (cross-naming: `D_DOOR` and
`R_DOORPD` were printed at the E-STOP housing and were moved)"*. The pass moved
them and, in the same operation, dropped them below the stroke at which the
project itself says silk "would not print". If they do not print, the pass
achieved nothing, on an identical-housing E-STOP/DOOR pair on a mains-adjacent
cooking interlock — the exact hazard the render lens raised.

**Not P0** because the board still functions and the ORDER_README harness-labeling
ritual is an independent mitigation. **Fix is silk-only and free:** raise the
clamp to 0.15, or let those six sit at `sz = 0.6`. Then delete the "at or above"
sentence from P-SILK-FN, which is currently false.

## P1-2 — Twelve internal milled slots at 0.600 mm, against no declared fab floor anywhere in the fleet

**Measured** from `Edge.Cuts` in `04_kicad/cooksense.kicad_pcb`:

| group | count | y span | **width** | length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| coil-gap north slots | 6 | 26.000 → 26.600 | **0.600 mm** | 8.440 mm |
| pocket south slots | 5 | 49.300 → 49.900 | **0.600 mm** | 8.440 mm |
| west pocket slot | 1 | 49.300 → 49.900 | **0.600 mm** | 9.600 mm |
| H4 isolation notch | 1 | 48.800 → 49.800 | 1.000 mm | edge-reaching to x200.0 |

All twelve slots are **fully internal closed loops** — no open end. The H4 notch
is the only edge-reaching cut, which is exactly why ADR-0012/ADR-0015 argue it
carries no router-bit minimum. **That argument does not extend to the other
twelve, and nobody has made it for them.**

JLCPCB's published minimum routed/milled slot width is **1.0 mm** (0.8 mm as a
special-process request). 0.600 mm is below both. I cannot verify the current
capability page from this lens, and that is the finding: I grepped
`fab_tiers.yaml`, `design-policies.md`, `nets.yaml`, `route.yaml`,
`floorplan.yaml` and `policy_waivers.yaml` — **no slot-width floor is declared
anywhere in this repo, no gate measures one, and no waiver mentions it.** Canon
M6 requires every fab floor be verified against the capability page at order time;
for this dimension there is no record that it ever has been, on a board whose
whole isolation argument rests on those slots existing.

**Mitigation is free, and I measured that too.** Widening every slot to 1.000 mm
keeps each one inside its all-layer keepout with room to spare — coil-gap slots
would run y[25.8, 26.8] inside `iso_gapN_*` y[23.2, 29.4]; pocket slots
y[49.15, 50.15] inside `iso_pktS_*` y[46.9, 52.9] — and the nearest copper to
any slot on any layer is >2.4 mm, so no clearance moves. The creepage-lengthening
function is preserved (a wider slot lengthens the wrap path, it does not shorten
it).

**Grade:** P1 today. **It becomes a P0 the moment the JLC capability page confirms
a 1.0 mm minimum**, because the board as drawn would be unbuildable and the fix
touches the outline.

## P1-3 — The AMS1117's dissipation PASS is cited against a mounting this board does not have

`03_src/cooksense/rules/power_tree.yaml`, 3V3 rail, DISSIPATION block, states:

> "the tab is VOUT and **is flooded with 3V3 copper** (that part.yaml's layout
> `notes`), which is the mounting **Table 1 (p.5) characterises at 55-65 C/W**.
> PD = (5.250 − 3.201) × 0.3 = 615 mW vs 1200 mW → **51 %, PASS**"

**Measured on the built board:**

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| `U_LDO` pad 4 (tab, net 3V3) | 2.00 × 3.80 = **7.60 mm²** |
| 3V3 copper on **F.Cu** (the tab's own layer) within 10 mm of the tab | **13.96 mm²** |
| 3V3 copper on In1.Cu within 10 mm | **0.00 mm²** |
| 3V3 copper on B.Cu within 10 mm | **0.00 mm²** |
| 3V3 copper on In2.Cu within 10 mm | **0.00 mm²** |
| total 3V3 plane (In2.Cu, board-wide) | 8385.9 mm² |
| vias inside the tab pad | **2** (0.250 pad / 0.150 drill) |
| 3V3 vias within 5 mm of the tab | 3 |

The tab is **not** flooded with 3V3 copper. `floorplan.yaml` `zones:` puts GND on
F.Cu / B.Cu / In1 and 3V3 on **In2 only**; there is no 3V3 pour on the tab's
layer at all. The tab reaches the plane through two 0.15 mm barrels.

The AMS1117 datasheet's Table 1 characterises θJA against copper area **on the
tab's own layer**; 55–65 °C/W corresponds to roughly 1 in² ≈ 645 mm². Measured
13.96 mm² is **2.2 % of that** and sits in the datasheet's minimum-pad column.
The 1.2 W "maximum dissipation" is itself a figure conditional on a stated θJA,
so "615 mW vs 1200 mW = 51 %, PASS" is not a standalone result — it inherits the
mounting assumption that the layout does not satisfy.

An order-of-magnitude estimate of what the geometry does support (stated as an
estimate, not a measurement): the dominant path is tab → 0.21 mm prepreg → the
solid In1 GND plane, ≈ 79 °C/W over 7.60 mm² at k_FR4 ≈ 0.35 W/m·K, in parallel
with 2 vias × ≈ 291 °C/W = 145 °C/W, plus board-to-ambient spreading and θJC →
**θJA on the order of 90–100 °C/W**, i.e. ΔT ≈ 55–60 °C at 615 mW, not the
34–40 °C the cited figure implies. `power_tree.yaml`'s own operating range runs to
**+70 °C ambient**, which puts the junction at or over the AMS1117's 125 °C
maximum operating junction temperature.

**Why this is not cosmetic:** 3V3 is the rail that powers the entire safety
AND-chain, the TPS3823 watchdog and the STOP logic. An LDO folding back or
thermally shutting down takes all of them with it.

**Owed:** either a bench junction/case temperature at 0.3 A (the same bring-up
measurement `power_tree.yaml` already declares OWED for dropout — take both at
once), or tab-layer copper. There is no 3V3 pour available on F.Cu here, so the
practical fix is more thermal vias in and around the tab into In2. Today the
"PASS" is asserted, not measured.

## P1-4 — H4's SELV leg lost 2.7 mm to a v1.7 part, and ADR-0015's fastener-OD derivation was done against the old number

ADR-0015 makes H4's geometry load-bearing and closes with *"Any future attempt to
reclaim space around K_STOP or its east corridor must re-derive Decisions 3 and 4
first."* v1.7 put a new part in that corridor.

Modelling the fastener as ADR-0015 does — a conductive disc of radius 3.000 mm
(DIN 125 A2.7 washer, OD 6.000 mm) centred on the hole — and measuring the two
legs to the nearest copper of each domain:

| hole | → nearest KEYPAD copper | → nearest SELV copper | a + s |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 (24,14) | −0.0500 (`KP_D4` track) | 16.0420 (`K_U1.2`) | 15.9920 |
| H2 (194,14) | 1.1634 (`RSTOP_MID`) | 15.6241 (`K_STOP.1`) | 16.7874 |
| H3 (17,90) | 40.9324 (`K_U1.4`) | −0.1250 (GND) | 40.8074 |
| **H4 (193,52)** | **4.0286** (`K_STOP.3`/`RSTOP_MID`) | **0.6758** (`D_COILEN.2`/GND) | **4.7044** |

The H4 keypad leg reproduces **ADR-0015's own 4.0286 mm** figure to four decimals,
which is how I know the model matches theirs. The **SELV leg is the new number**:
**0.6758 mm** by the pad-circle model, **0.607 mm** against the true pad rectangle
(`D_COILEN.2` GND, x[190.985,191.435] y[55.250,55.850]).

`D_COILEN` is a **v1.7 part** (ADR-0018, PESD5V0S1BA at the new JST-ZH `J_MODE`).
On the v1.6 geometry the nearest SELV copper to H4 was `K_STOP.2` — and the -13
land actually moved that pad *away* (y45.62 → 43.08, disc gap ~3.37 → ~5.72 mm).
So the relay change improved this leg and the new diode then more than gave it
back.

**The floor still passes:** using ADR-0015's notch-lengthened keypad leg,
6.5984 + 0.607 = **7.21 mm ≥ 6.000 mm**. But the margin fell from ~3.97 mm to
~1.21 mm, and **ADR-0015's Consequences clause — "Maximum conductive OD 6.0 mm,
hard limit 6.3 mm" — was derived when s was ~3.37 mm.** Nobody re-derived it. At
OD 6.300 mm (r 3.150) the SELV leg becomes 0.457 mm and the total ~6.90 mm, so
the stated hard limit does survive; that is a conclusion, not an assumption, and
it needs to be written down before the next part lands in that corridor.

**Owed:** update ADR-0015 with the SELV leg as an explicit, gated quantity. The
`I-HW` gate measures both legs; nothing in the ADR records the s-side number, so a
2.7 mm erosion of it produced no signal anywhere.
