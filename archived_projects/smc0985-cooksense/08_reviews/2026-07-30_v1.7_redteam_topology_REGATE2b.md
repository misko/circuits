## 0. Method, and what "MEASURED" means below

Nothing in this review is taken from a number in `power_tree.yaml`, from
`verification/*.txt`, or from an ADR's arithmetic. Every load-bearing figure is
tagged:

- **MEASURED (by me, how)** — recomputed or re-extracted here, method named.
- **INHERITED (from where, NOT re-verified)** — accepted from a source I did
  not independently reproduce.

Tools used, all outside the archive (the archive was opened read-only; a scratch
copy of the board was used for every `pcbnew` run, under

## 2. P0 FINDINGS

### P0-1 — The dropout margin the whole v1.7 supply spec exists to produce omits the board's own copper; the file's stated 0.60 A robustness claim is FALSE

`03_src/cooksense/rules/power_tree.yaml` derives `rails[3V3].vin_min` from the
J_PWR spec minus three **named** series resistances (INHERITED — cited to the
respective datasheets, not re-verified individually):

```
F1      MF-MSMF200/16X   70.0 mOhm  (R1Max)
Q_REV   AO3401A          73.5 mOhm  (interpolated to TJ 70 C)
U_EFUSE TPS259573        47.0 mOhm  (RON MAX, -40..85 C)
                        ----------
                        190.5 mOhm
```

and asserts, verbatim: *"Every resistance is a datasheet MAXIMUM."*
**The board itself is not in that list.**

**MEASURED (by me: `pcbnew` + a Dijkstra minimum-resistance walk over the routed
tracks and vias of the scratch copy of `cooksense.kicad_pcb`; sheet resistance
1.72e-8 Ω·m / 35 µm = 0.4914 mΩ/sq at 20 °C, ×1.1965 at 70 °C = 0.5878 mΩ/sq;
0.5 mΩ allowed per via):**

| copper segment | routed min-R path @ 70 °C |
|---|---|
| `J_PWR.1 → F1.1` (`5V_IN`, 0.500 mm F.Cu) | **25.87 mΩ** |
| `F1.2 → Q_REV.3` (`5V_FUSED`) | **7.06 mΩ** |
| `Q_REV.2 → U_EFUSE.3` / `.4` (`5V_RPP`) | 18.64 / 17.47 mΩ → **9.00 mΩ** (parallel IN pads) |
| `U_EFUSE.5 → U_LDO.3` (`5V_PROTECTED`) | **67.09 mΩ** |
| **trunk (carries whole-board current)** | **41.93 mΩ** |
| **LDO branch (carries LDO input current only)** | **67.09 mΩ** |
| **total** | **109.02 mΩ** |

**MEASURED — there is no copper zone on any 5 V net.** A full zone census
returns exactly four filled zones: `GND`/F.Cu 2701.43 mm², `GND`/B.Cu 7385.83,
`GND`/In1.Cu 8465.52, `3V3`/In2.Cu 8420.03. `5V_IN`, `5V_FUSED`, `5V_RPP` and
`5V_PROTECTED` are **track-only** (netclass `PWR_IN`, `track_width` 0.5 mm, read
from `cooksense.kicad_pro`), so the tracks are the entire conductor. The GND
return is plane and is ≤ a few mΩ (not charged here).

Re-deriving the LDO input with correct current apportionment
(`V = 4.850 − I_total × 0.23243 − I_LDO × 0.06709`):

| case | I_total | I_LDO | V(U_LDO.3) | headroom | vs 1300 mV dropout |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **the file's own worst case, the file's own I_LDO** | 0.500 A | 0.161 A | 4.7230 V | 1324.0 mV | **+24.0 mV** (file declares **+55 mV**) |
| same, with my corrected 3V3 load (P1-2) | 0.500 A | 0.184 A | 4.7214 V | 1322.4 mV | +22.4 mV |
| the file's own robustness claim: *"still passes at 0.60 A (+37 mV)"* | 0.600 A | 0.184 A | 4.6982 V | 1299.2 mV | **−0.8 mV → FAIL** |
| + the eFuse's −40..125 °C RON row (53 mΩ), which the file says "costs 3 mV more" | 0.500 A | 0.184 A | 4.7184 V | 1319.4 mV | +19.4 mV |
| copper at 25 °C instead of 70 °C (sensitivity) | 0.500 A | 0.161 A | 4.7282 V | 1329.2 mV | +29.2 mV |

**Break-even whole-board current: 0.597 A** (was implicitly ~0.79 A on the
file's terms).

Why this is P0 and not P1:

1. **The gate is grading a wrong input, in the unsafe direction.** `E-TOPO` was
   run here (`RAW_EXIT=0`, output in §6) and prints
   `headroom 1355 mV ... vs dropout 1300 mV -> PASS`. That 1355 mV is
   4.754 − 3.399, and 4.754 is the number this finding falsifies.
2. **It falsifies a specific quoted claim.** The file's stated insensitivity
   argument — *"It still passes at 0.60 A (+37 mV) ... so the verdict does not
   hinge on the one interpolated term (Q_REV)"* — is exactly inverted. With the
   copper counted the verdict now **does** hinge on Q_REV's interpolated
   73.5 mΩ, because +22 mV is smaller than that single term's own uncertainty.
3. **It is the number a user-level commission ruling was made to produce.**
   D11 / ADR-0021 tightened the supply envelope to 4.850–5.250 V *specifically*
   to turn a 199 mV dropout FAIL into a 55 mV PASS. The real margin at the
   declared corner is 24 mV.

**Honest statement of physical risk: moderate, not severe.** The 1300 mV dropout
is itself a 0.8 A figure applied at a 0.3 A rail and is admitted **OWED**; the
real part will very likely drop far less. The defect is that the release seals
on a computed margin 2.4× smaller than declared, whose stated robustness
envelope does not exist. **The remedy is a number change plus the already-OWED
bench measurement, not copper** — but it must be made before the number is
sealed, because everything downstream of it (the supply spec, the ORDER_README
§0 buyer instruction, the E-TOPO PASS) is derived from it.

### P1-3 — The hardware watchdog window is 5–8× the brief's requirement, with no ADR and no brief amendment

BRIEF.md §3, verbatim: *"Watchdog 300-500ms; heartbeat loss drops relay power"*.

**MEASURED (read from `02_parts/TPS3823-33DBVR/part.yaml` limits, itself cited
to SLVS165O §6.8):** `t_watchdog: 0.9 / 1.6 / 2.5 s`. The board has exactly one
watchdog: `WD_PET` (`J_PI.11`) → `U_WD.4 (WDI)`; `U_WD.1 (RESET_N)` → `WD_OK`.
**MEASURED — there is no second, faster supervisor:** `grep -c 1G123
fab/bom.csv` = **0**, and the 239-component netlist contains no other timer.

The part dossier itself names the missing device: *"the fast 300-500ms
retriggerable window is the SN74LVC1G123 one-shot (see 02_parts). **Reconcile
the watchdog architecture in pin/power review.**"* That reconciliation never
happened. `SN74LVC1G123` was swapped out in v1.2 (ADR-0011 §6) for the *PRESS*
one-shot, a different function — `CD74HC221` section 1 is triggered by
`PRESS_REQ`, not by the heartbeat.

BRIEF.md's decision register (D1–D11) and its D-SPEC tension table (T1–T6)
contain no watchdog item. ADR-0025 shows this project knows how to write out a
brief amendment when it changes a §3 safety requirement; the watchdog window got
none. The 1.6 s figure appears only as a parenthetical *"flag 1"* note inside
`electrical_invariants.yaml` and as a bring-up step in ORDER_README §7.

**Residual hazard, bounded honestly:** a hung Pi cannot hold a key past the
`CD74HC221`'s ≤436 ms non-retriggerable PRESS cap, and a stale selector pair
presses nothing (MEASURED: `U_SEL_BUS` and `D_SEL_BUS` are joined only through
`K_PRESS` + `R_KEY`). What the 2.5 s worst case delays is de-energising the
selector coils, tri-stating the '595 and setting the fault latch. Real but
bounded — hence P1, not P0. It remains a straight violation of a numbered brief
requirement on the safety chain.

### P1-4 — The isolated contactor output cannot deliver its labelled current, and has no inductive clamp

**MEASURED (netlist):** `U_CAND2.Y (CONTACTOR_DRV) → R_OPTOLED 330 Ω →
OPTO_LED_A → U_OPTO.1 (anode)`, `U_OPTO.2 (cathode) → GND`; output
`U_OPTO.4 (collector) → J_ISOLOOP.1`, `U_OPTO.3 (emitter) → J_ISOLOOP.4`.
Nothing else is on either isolated net.

**MEASURED (by me):** with `3V3` at its cited `vout_min` 3.201 V, an
SN74LVC1G11 output impedance of ≈50 Ω (from TI's own V_OH/I_OH pair, 3.0 V →
2.4 V at −12 mA) and LTV-817 V_F max 1.4 V:

```
I_F = (3.201 − 1.4) / (330 + 50) = 4.74 mA   (worst case)
I_F = (3.300 − 1.15) / 380       = 5.66 mA   (typical)
```

`02_parts/LTV-817S-TA1/part.yaml` gives `ctr: "50-600% (TA1 bin)"` (INHERITED).
At CTR **min 50 %** the guaranteed *linear* collector current is
**I_C ≈ 2.37 mA at V_CE = 5 V**. For *saturated* switching the guarantee is far
lower: the LTV-817 V_CE(sat) is specified at I_F = 20 mA / I_C = 1 mA — a
saturation ratio of 5 % — so at 4.74 mA of drive the datasheet-guaranteed
saturated sink is **≈0.24 mA**. None of that carries the normal −20…−50 %
LED-aging CTR degradation, which at 5 mA drive is the aggressive end of the
curve.

Against that:

- BRIEF §3: *"Contactor output: isolated open-collector/dry contact,
  **<=30V/50mA**"*;
- **MEASURED silk** (`grep -o '(gr_text "[^"]*"' source/cooksense.kicad_pcb`):
  `"J_ISOLOOP (SE CORNER) = ISOLATED 30V CONTACTOR LOOP -- NOT SELV -- POLES
  1=C 2=LOOP 3=LOOP 4=E"` and `"ISO 30V"`.

There is **no drive budget, no load specification and no CTR-degradation
allowance anywhere in the tree**, and a builder reading "30 V contactor loop"
will size a coil or SSR input this board cannot sink.

**Second half, and the worse one: there is no freewheel path on the isolated
side.** The output exists to drive a contactor — an inductive load — and
`U_OPTO`'s V_CEO is 35 V (INHERITED, part.yaml). A 30 V coil opening into an
unclamped phototransistor exceeds V_CEO on the first release. Nothing on the
board, and nothing in the ORDER_README's J_ISOLOOP wiring table (§11, poles
1→E-stop pole B→2, 3→contactor→4), requires the user to fit an external flyback
diode or an RC snubber.

### P1-5 — `D_ESD_IN` is the only clamp on the **unfused** side of the input, it is above its stand-off at the specified supply ceiling, and its dossier records no ratings at all

**MEASURED (netlist + `pcbnew`):** net `5V_IN` = `{J_PWR.1, F1.1, D_ESD_IN.1}`.
`D_ESD_IN` (PESD5V0S1BA, `C5158048`, SOD-323) sits between `J_PWR.1` and `GND`
**upstream of the polyfuse**. The router in fact daisy-chained it: the 25.87 mΩ
minimum path from `J_PWR.1` to `F1.1` runs *through* the D_ESD_IN pads.

Consequences, all measured or read from the netlist:

1. **The eFuse's OVLO does not protect it.** Everything the ADR-0021 OV story
   describes — the 5.798 V cutoff, the SMBJ5.0A backstop — is *downstream* of
   `U_EFUSE`. On a wrong-supply event the eFuse turns **off**, disconnecting the
   load and leaving `D_ESD_IN` facing the input alone.
2. **Nothing limits its fault current.** F1 is behind it. A PESD5V0S1BA breaks
   down at V_BR min 6.0 V (INHERITED, Nexperia PESD5V0S1BA 2024-04-26); above
   that it absorbs the supply's full current capability with **zero series
   impedance to the connector**, until it fails. It is a 350 W-transient ESD
   device, not a power TVS.
3. **It is out of specification at the board's own declared maximum supply.**
   V_RWM = 5.0 V; the D11 / ADR-0021 ceiling is **5.25 V**.
4. **`02_parts/PESD5V0S1BA/part.yaml` has no `limits:` key at all.** It records
   pinning, directionality, a package-outline trap and a cosmetic silk defect —
   and not one electrical rating. For the one clamp sitting directly across the
   power connector, there is no recorded rating pair to check. (Contrast
   `02_parts/SMBJ5.0A/part.yaml`, which declares its own identical stand-off gap
   loudly and names the fix.)

The reverse-polarity half of the chain is correct by contrast, and worth saying
so: `D_REVCLAMP` (SS34) is on `5V_FUSED`, **downstream** of F1, so a reverse
hookup drives the crowbar current through the fuse. That is the right side of
the fuse, and the part.yaml says so explicitly.

### P1-6 — The AMS1117's 10 mA minimum load current is nowhere in a 545-line power tree, and the board's declared safe state is below it

**MEASURED (by me, `pdftotext` on the archived `ds1117_2009-08_RoHS.pdf`),
verbatim:**

```
Minimum Load    AMS1117    (VIN - VOUT) = 1.5V (Note 5)    5    10    mA
Note 5: Minimum load current is defined as the minimum output current required
to maintain regulation. When 1.5V <= (VIN - VOUT) <= 12V the device is
guaranteed to regulate if the output current is greater than 10mA.
```

`(VIN − VOUT)` here is 5.25 − 3.3 = 1.95 V, squarely inside the stated band. The
word "minimum" does not appear anywhere in `power_tree.yaml`.

**MEASURED — the board's declared safe state is below it.** DETAIL_DESIGN §4
declares `RAIL_EN_A/B/RHA/RHE` pulled **down**, i.e. all four switched sensor
rails OFF at power-up and after every reset (confirmed in the netlist:
`R_RAENAPD` / `R_RAENBPD` / `R_RAENRHAPD` / `R_RAENRHEPD`, 100 kΩ to GND). With
those 85.1 mA gone, and `J_MODE` / `J_ESTOP` unmated, the 3V3 load summed **at
datasheet maxima** is ≈7.2 mA; at typicals it is 2–3 mA. Both are under 10 mA.

An LDO below its minimum load rises above regulation. **MEASURED — nothing on
this board detects that:** `U_WD` (TPS3823-33) supervises 3V3 only from below
(`VIT-` 2.83/2.93/3.00 V). The most exposed load is `U_TC` (MAX31856,
`U_TC.5`/`.8` on 3V3), whose recommended operating range tops out at 3.6 V.

Honest hedge: the EC row's Device column reads bare `AMS1117` (the adjustable
part), and a fixed-output AMS1117's internal divider provides *some* load. The
datasheet does not say how much, and nothing in this tree measures it. This is a
real M-OWED gap on the one rail the file spends 300 lines on, not a demonstrated
failure.

declared; recorded, not re-litigated.** Note only that the same defect exists

### P2-2 — DETAIL_DESIGN §1's 74.9 °C hard-stop trip omits `R_CLMPA`/`R_CLMPB`; the real trip is 72.8 °C, and the open-NTC comparator false-trips below −10.4 °C
**MEASURED (netlist):** `R_CLMPA` / `R_CLMPB` = **22 kΩ from `TH_CAM_A`/`_B` to
GND**, in parallel with the external NTC. DETAIL_DESIGN §1's worked default
(`R_NTC = 10k·0.4231/(3.3−0.4231) = 1.4707k → 74.9 °C`) treats the bottom leg as
the NTC alone.

**MEASURED (by me, using the same B25/85 = 3987 and R25 = 10 k the doc uses):**
for the *parallel* combination to equal 1.4707 kΩ the NTC must be 1.5761 kΩ,
which is **72.8 °C** — a systematic −2.1 °C offset the doc's ±2.5 °C tolerance
stack does not carry. The direction is fail-safe (trips earlier) and 72.8 °C
still sits inside the brief's 70 (stop) / 75 (hard) band, hence P2.

Same measurement, the open-NTC detector (`U_COMP2`; `TCAM_OPEN` = `R_OPENT`
62 k / `R_OPENB` 100 k → 2.098 V): an open thermistor reads
3.399 × 22/(10+22) = **2.337 V**, clearing the threshold by 239 mV — the
detector works, and it closes the gap DETAIL_DESIGN §1 still describes as *"NOT
safe at the comparator … accepted for v1.2"*. **The artifact is better than its
own document here.** But solving for the cold end: the node reaches 2.098 V at
an NTC of 60.4 kΩ, i.e. **−10.4 °C**, so anywhere below that the open-detect
falsely holds `TEMP_OK` low — inside the −20 °C corner ADR-0021's own OVLO worst
case is derived at. Fail-safe direction, cold-start nuisance inhibit.

### P2-3 — The PRESS one-shot's worst-case width exceeds the brief's 500 ms HARD cap once the capacitor's own dielectric limit is carried
DETAIL_DESIGN §2 computes `max: 0.77·515k·1.1u = 436ms` — the ±10 % is `C_OS`'s
initial tolerance letter only. `C_OS` = `CL10A105KB8NNNC`, a **class-II X5R**,
whose EIA characteristic is ±15 % over −55…+85 °C *referenced to the 25 °C
value*.

**MEASURED (by me):** `0.77 × 515 kΩ × 1.0 µF × 1.10 × 1.15 = 501.6 ms`, against
BRIEF §4.6's 500 ms HARD limit. P2 rather than P1 because the direction is
probabilistic: class-II ceramics lose capacitance under DC bias and at
temperature extremes far more often than they gain it, so the physical part
almost certainly runs shorter. The finding is that the declared bound is not a
bound.

### P2-4 — E-MARGIN returns N-A because no rail declares `load_uv_threshold`, yet the board has a hard fixed-brownout load
**MEASURED (`power_topology.py … --margin`, `RAW_EXIT=0`):**
`E-MARGIN N-A: no rail declares load_uv_threshold - no regulated rail feeds a
known fixed-brownout load`. That is false of this board.

**E-MARGIN, computed by hand instead (MEASURED by me):** the binding brownout on
`3V3` is `U_WD` — the TPS3823-33 whose `VIT-` is 2.83 / 2.93 / **3.00 V max**
and whose `RESET_N` *is* `WD_OK`, the term that gates the coil rail, the fault
latch, the '595 output enable, the contactor chain and the expander's RESET.
Second is `U_TC` (MAX31856, 3.0 V min).

```
setpoint (cited, full-temperature)   3.201 V
binding brownout                     3.000 V
margin                                 201 mV
```

Two corrections in the honest direction: (a) ds1117's EC table header reads
*"Electrical Characteristics at IOUT = 0 mA"*, so **3.201 V is a zero-load
number** and the ≤25 mV full-temperature load-regulation term is *not* inside it
— loaded worst case 3.176 V, margin **176 mV**; (b) the IR budget itself is
negligible: **MEASURED**, `3V3` distributes on the In2.Cu plane (8420.03 mm²,
76 vias, only 3.93 mm of track), so plane IR at ≤0.18 A is <1 mV. The rail
**passes with margin** — but it passes because I computed it, not because the

to catch exactly this was skipped.

### P2-5 — The declared inrush control is unperformed: `C_DVDT` was never sized for the bulk it exists to control
`02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/part.yaml`, pin 1, verbatim: *"cap to GND sets output
turn-on slew (inrush limit); **Size Cdvdt for the 5V_PROTECTED bulk**"*.

**MEASURED:** `C_DVDT` = **1 nF**. `5V_PROTECTED` carries CE1 220 µF + C_IN1
10 µF + C_LDOIN 10 µF + C_STOPR 10 µF (through the 0 Ω `R_STOPRAIL`) + C_IN2
0.1 µF ≈ **250 µF nominal, ~294 µF at the electrolytic's +20 %**.

SLVSE57C Eq. 2: `SR_ON [V/ms] = I_INRUSH [mA] / C_OUT [µF]`. The datasheet's own
table gives SR_ON at VIN = 5 V of 21.9 V/ms (CdVdt open) and 11.6 V/ms
(CdVdt = 3300 pF, the abs-max). **MEASURED (by me):** even at the *maximum
legal* CdVdt the demanded inrush is `11.6 × 294 = 3.41 A`; at 1 nF it is ~5 A.
Both are above the eFuse's ~1.7 A programmed limit, so **every cold start is a
current-limited ramp, not a dVdt ramp**, and no legal CdVdt value can change
that with 220 µF of bulk.

Benign in practice — SLVSE57C Table 2, read here, states overcurrent and short
circuit **do not** raise `FLT`, so no spurious fault reaches `EFUSE_FLT_N`, and
the charge event is ~0.9 ms / ~3.7 mJ. The finding is that a declared design
obligation was never discharged, and the resulting start-up behaviour is
undocumented for the bring-up engineer.

### P2-6 — Reverse-polarity crowbar / fuse coordination is unrecorded, and the brief's own supply sits in F1's indeterminate band
Under reverse hookup `D_REVCLAMP` (SS34, 3 A I_F(AV)) forward-conducts and the
fault current must trip F1. **MEASURED (INHERITED limits):** F1 I_hold
**2.00 A**, I_trip **3.50 A** (the part.yaml notes the ADR-0001 "4 A" was wrong
and that the table governs). BRIEF §3 specifies a *"5V SELV >=2A (pref 3A)"*
supply — so at the briefed supply the fault current is **3 A, between I_hold and
I_trip**, the band in which the Bourns table publishes no trip time, while SS34
carries it at 100 % of its average rating. No trip-time-versus-diode-rating
coordination exists anywhere in the tree.

### P2-7 — Over-current on `5V_PROTECTED` raises no flag, but the test point is named `TP_PGOOD`
**MEASURED:** `EFUSE_FLT_N` = `{U_EFUSE.6, R_PG (100 k to 3V3), TP_PGOOD.1,
U_EXP.1 (GPB0)}`. SLVSE57C Table 2, read here: FLT asserts on
**overtemperature** and on **OVLO shutdown**; it does **not** assert on
overcurrent or short circuit. A sustained current limit — which sags the rail —
is invisible to the board's only power-fault telemetry until the eFuse
eventually reaches OTP. BRIEF §3 asks for "power-good"; this signal is a fault
flag with two of the four interesting causes missing, and the test point's name
asserts otherwise.

### P2-8 — The fault latch has a reachable forbidden state; defence in depth holds
**MEASURED (netlist):** `FAULT = NAND(FAULT_SET_N, FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR)`
(`U_LATCHA`) and `FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR = NAND(REARM_PULSE_N, FAULT)` (`U_LATCHB`) —
a classic active-low SR latch; `FAULT_SET_N = WD_OK · ESTOP_OK · TEMP_OK`
(`U_FAULTAND`). The re-arm one-shot's clear input (`U_ONESHOT.11`, `R2_N`) is
tied to **`WD_OK` only**.

So a re-arm asserted during an active **E-stop** or **over-temperature** — which
`R2_N` does not guard — drives both `FAULT_SET_N` and `REARM_PULSE_N` low and
both latch outputs high simultaneously. Verified harmless: `KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED`
still requires `AND1 = MODE·WD·ESTOP` and `AND2 = TEMP·MCU_EN·HOST_AUTH`, and
`CTR_SAFE = WD·ESTOP·TEMP` still gates the contactor, so nothing can be
authorised from that state and it resolves when `REARM_N` releases. Recorded
because the guard is asymmetric and nothing says why.

Related, also P2: re-arm is an MCP23017 GPIO write (`U_EXP.26 → REARM_N`), i.e.
**software-issued**, against BRIEF §3's *"explicit manual re-arm"*. Bounded by
the same AND-chain (a spurious re-arm can only clear the latch once the
underlying fault has actually cleared), and consistent with the T6 waiver.
