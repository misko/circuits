### F1 — P0 — The 30 V NOT-SELV terminal's legend is planted on the sensor connector next to it

The board's SE corner holds two connectors 8 mm apart:

- `J_ISOLOOP` — `TerminalBlock_KF350_4P`, rot 90°, courtyard **191.55..199.25 x, 87.66..102.34 y**,
  four PTH poles at x=195.30, y = 100.25 / 96.75 / 93.25 / 89.75, nets
  `CONTACTOR_C` / `CONTACTOR_LOOP` / `CONTACTOR_LOOP` / `CONTACTOR_E`. This is the isolated 30 V
  contactor loop — the one terminal on the board that is explicitly not SELV.
- `J_RH_EXHAUST` — `JST_GH_SM05B-GHS-TB_1x05-1MP_P1.25mm_Horizontal`, courtyard
  **180.60..191.39 x, 93.75..100.24 y**, five pads at y=95.15 carrying
  `3V3_SW_RHE` / `GND` / `SDA_B` / `SCL_B` / `SHIELD_DRAIN`. Pure SELV; `SDA_B` and `SCL_B` run
  straight to `J_PI` pins 8 and 10.

Both of the strings that identify the 30 V terminal are nearer the sensor connector:

| silk string | → J_RH_EXHAUST | → J_ISOLOOP | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1C2L3L4E` (the pole legend) | courtyard **0.15 mm**, pads 0.69 mm | courtyard **7.93 mm**, pads 10.58 mm | lead **-7.78 mm** against the wrong part |
| `J_ISOLOOP` (the refdes) | courtyard **0.14 mm**, pads 1.22 mm | courtyard **2.72 mm**, pads 5.36 mm | lead **-2.58 mm** |

The text bboxes are `1C2L3L4E` = 178.88..183.62 x, 100.39..101.61 y and `J_ISOLOOP` =
183.76..188.84 x, 100.39..101.61 y. Both sit inside `J_RH_EXHAUST`'s x-span (180.60..191.39) and
0.14 mm below its courtyard's bottom edge. They read as one horizontal caption directly beneath the
connector body. `J_RH_EXHAUST`'s own refdes is a small **90°-rotated** string to its left — which
loses every legibility contest against a horizontal caption sitting under the part.

The actual 4-pole terminal has **no** adjacent legend and **no** pole numbering of any kind. Its
only identification is 8 mm away, under a different connector.

**Reached three independent ways:**
1. Pad-edge / courtyard distance metric on the board file (numbers above).
2. Centroid metric — `J_ISOLOOP` refdes centre is 10.90 mm from its own courtyard centre and
   4.01 mm from `J_RH_EXHAUST`'s.
3. Eye, on **two separate render paths**: `twin_top_hires.png` (with 3D bodies) and
   `bare_v20/render_top_bare.png` (no bodies at all, different renderer output). Both show
   `1C2L3L4E  J_ISOLOOP` as a single horizontal caption under the 5-pin JST pad row, and the
   4-pole PTH column at the right edge with nothing beside it.

**Why it matters.** A technician told to land the isolated 30 V contactor loop on `J_ISOLOOP`, poles
1=C 2=LOOP 3=LOOP 4=E, walks to the SE corner, finds the string `J_ISOLOOP` and the string
`1C2L3L4E` — and both are under the 5-pin JST. Landing 30 V there puts it on `SDA_B` / `SCL_B` /
`3V3_SW_RHE` / `GND`, i.e. on the SELV I²C bus that goes to the host Pi through `J_PI`. That is not
a damaged board; that is the isolation barrier defeated, on a safety-critical cooking appliance
controller, by a legend that is present and wrong. The board's own NW-corner banner says
`J_ISOLOOP (SE CORNER) = ISOLATED 30V CONTACTOR LOOP -- NOT SELV -- POLES 1=C 2=LOOP 3=LOOP 4=E`,
which does not disambiguate: *both* connectors are in the SE corner, 8 mm apart.

Secondary defect within F1: even for someone who finds `1C2L3L4E`, the mapping is undefined. The
legend is a horizontal left-to-right string; the poles are a **vertical** column running pole 1 at
y=100.25 (bottom) to pole 4 at y=89.75 (top). Nothing on the board says the string reads
bottom-to-top. Pole 1 is identifiable only from its rounded-rect pad shape versus the three round
ones.

### F2 — P0 — The door-interlock connector is unlabelled, and the words next to it say "ISO 30V"

`J_DOOR` — same `SM05B-GHS-TB` footprint, courtyard **193.75..200.25 x, 76.36..87.16 y**, pads at
x=195.15 carrying `3V3` / `DOOR_RAW` / `GND` / `DOOR_RAW` / `GND`. Pure SELV logic.

Its refdes text (bbox 185.65..189.35 x, 83.15..84.37 y) is:

| → | courtyard | pads |
|---|---|---|
| `U_CAND2` (a SOT-23-6) | **0.20 mm** | **0.70 mm** |
| `D_DOOR` | 0.61 mm | 1.38 mm |
| `J_ISOLOOP` | 3.95 mm | 6.47 mm |
| **`J_DOOR` itself** | **4.40 mm** | **4.95 mm** |

Pad-edge margin **+4.25 mm** the wrong way; centroid margin **+7.08 mm** the wrong way. The string
`J_DOOR` sits on a SOT-23-6 and labels it. Restricted to the J/F/TP family only — the scope
`P-SILK-OWN` uses — the lead is still **-0.45 mm**, because within that family the `J_DOOR` string is
nearer `J_ISOLOOP` (3.95 mm) than `J_DOOR` (4.40 mm).

Meanwhile the two free warning strings nearest `J_DOOR`'s actual location are the ones that belong
to `J_ISOLOOP`:

| string | bbox | → J_ISOLOOP | → J_DOOR | lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `NOT SELV` | 187.07..191.68, 84.51..85.74 | 1.92 mm | **2.07 mm** | **+0.15 mm** |
| `ISO 30V` | 188.78..192.72, 85.89..87.11 | 0.55 mm | **1.03 mm** | **+0.48 mm** |

`design-policies.md` P5 states the standard in its own words: *"a 0.1mm lead is a coin toss dressed
as a pass"*. `NOT SELV` leads by 0.15 mm. And neither string's nearest part of **any** kind is the
terminal — `NOT SELV`'s nearest is `U_OPTO` at 1.42 mm (then `U_CAND2` 1.57, `D_DOOR` 1.72,
`J_ISOLOOP` fifth at 1.92); `ISO 30V`'s nearest is `U_OPTO` at **0.05 mm**.

**Reached two independent ways:** the metrics above, and eye on both render paths. Reading down the
right-hand board edge in `crops/doorstop.png` and `crops/bare_se.png`, the connector stack is
J_MODE / J_ESTOP / **J_DOOR**, and the next text below J_DOOR is `NOT SELV` then `ISO 30V`. Nothing
between them. `J_DOOR` is the connector that visually owns the words "NOT SELV / ISO 30V".

**Why it matters.** This is F1 running in the opposite direction and it lands on the same barrier.
The one connector on the right edge that a wiring tech would read as the not-SELV 30 V terminal is
`J_DOOR`, which carries `3V3` on pin 1 and `DOOR_RAW` on pins 2 and 4. 30 V onto that pin 1 is 30 V
onto the 3V3 rail of the whole logic side. F1 and F2 together mean the SE corner has *two*
connectors mislabelled toward each other across the isolation barrier, either of which alone is an
order blocker.

### F3 — P1 — Four cross-mateable connectors, two of them safety inputs, and one legend tie at 0.00 mm

Four footprints on this board are the identical `JST_GH_SM05B-GHS-TB_1x05-1MP_P1.25mm_Horizontal`:

| ref | courtyard | pin 1..5 nets |
|---|---|---|
| `J_ESTOP` | 193.75..200.25 x, **65.48..76.27** y | 3V3, ESTOP_RAW, GND, GND, GND |
| `J_DOOR` | 193.75..200.25 x, **76.36..87.16** y | 3V3, DOOR_RAW, GND, DOOR_RAW, GND |
| `J_RH_AMBIENT` | 162.60..173.39 x, 93.75..100.24 y | 3V3_SW_RHA, GND, SDA_A, SCL_A, SHIELD_DRAIN |
| `J_RH_EXHAUST` | 180.60..191.39 x, 93.75..100.24 y | 3V3_SW_RHE, GND, SDA_B, SCL_B, SHIELD_DRAIN |

Any harness fits any of the four. `J_ESTOP` and `J_DOOR` are stacked with **0.09 mm** between their
courtyards, and the `J_ESTOP` refdes bbox (192.39..193.61 x, **73.36..77.40** y) physically straddles
the boundary: **0.14 mm to J_ESTOP's courtyard and 0.14 mm to J_DOOR's**. Family lead **+0.00 mm** —
a literal coin toss on which of the E-stop and the door interlock that legend names. With F2 having
moved `J_DOOR`'s own legend 4.40 mm away onto a SOT-23-6, the pair has one ambiguous label and one
absent one.

Worked mis-plug: the E-stop is a dry contact between pin 1 (`3V3`) and pin 2 (`ESTOP_RAW`). Plugged
into `J_RH_AMBIENT` it lands between `3V3_SW_RHA` and `GND` — it shorts a switched rail to ground and
`ESTOP_RAW` is never driven at all. The E-STOP goes **silently inert**: no fault, no indication, and
the board behaves exactly as if the E-stop were healthy and unpressed.

Also in this family: `R_COILENS` sits **0.13 mm** from `J_MODE` (own part 3.12 mm away) and
`R_CTRSAFEPD` sits **0.21 mm** from `J_RH_AMBIENT` (own part 8.41 mm away) — see F7.

### F4 — P1 — Silkscreen ink on solderable pads

Two silk texts overlap `F.Cu` pad extents:

- `ANALOG SENSE (3V3_ANALOG)` — bbox 27.97..44.03 x, 83.28..84.72 y, height 0.70 mm — overlaps
  **`Q_SWDRVA` pad 1 and pad 3**. Visible directly in `crops/bare_analog.png` (body-free render):
  the glyphs `A` and `L` of "ANALOG" are printed across the two magenta pads.
- `KEYPAD ISOLATION COMB   >=6mm creepage   GND_ISO ONLY   (contact columns face pockets)` —
  bbox 76.01..135.99 x, 20.18..21.82 y, height 0.80 mm — overlaps **`TP_RKEY` pad 1** at 0.00 mm.

Silk over a mask opening is resolved by the fab one of two ways: clip the silk (the warning text
loses characters) or print it (the pad does not wet). `Q_SWDRVA` is a live SOT-23 driver — a partly
inked pad is an intermittent joint on a switched sense rail. `TP_RKEY` is only a probe pad, so that
half is lower consequence, but it is the same defect and it lands on the one banner that carries the
6 mm-creepage instruction.

Reached two ways: pad-bbox intersection computed from the board file, and eyeball on the body-free
bare render (where nothing can be hiding under a 3D model).

### F5 — P1 — 71 silk texts below the fab's minimum silkscreen line width

Full histogram of the 251 F.Silkscreen texts:

| height (mm) | count | stroke (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| **0.45** | **71** | **0.112** |
| 0.60 | 174 | 0.150 |
| 0.70 | 3 | 0.175 |
| 0.80 | 2 | 0.200 |
| 0.90 | 1 | 0.225 |

JLCPCB's published silkscreen capability is **0.15 mm minimum line width** and **0.8 mm minimum
character height**. Against that: **72 of 251 texts are under the width floor** and **248 of 251 are
under the height floor**. The 0.45 mm / 0.112 mm set is not scattered — it is exactly the densest
region of the board (`R_REF0..7`, `R_SER0..7`, `C_FLT0..7`, the `TP_*` cluster around x=46..68,
y=68..92), which is where a legible refdes matters most. A 0.112 mm stroke is 75% of the fab floor;
the realistic outcome is those 71 designators arriving as unresolvable smudges or dropping out.

I did not find a silk floor declared anywhere in `cooksense.kicad_dru` — the file contains no rule
mentioning silkscreen, text, or a width minimum. So nothing in the gate stack is asserting this
number.

### F6 — P1 — `assembly.pdf` cannot be read, and it is the only drawing for 16 hand-fit parts

`assembly.pdf`: 1 page, A4 landscape (841.9 x 595.3 pt). Rendered at 200 dpi and measured:

- Dominant ink colour **RGB(175,175,175)** — relative luminance 175/255, **contrast ratio 1.43:1**
  against the white page.
- The refdes and value text is **RGB(242,237,161)** — pale yellow, luminance ≈232/255,
  **contrast ratio ≈1.2:1**. For reference, WCAG's floor for large text is 3:1. On paper this is
  blank.
- Ink bbox is 69% of the page width and **44% of the page height**. The drawing occupies roughly
  30% of the sheet; the entire lower half is empty. A free 2x scale-up is sitting unused, which
  would have been the one mitigation for F5's 0.45 mm text.
- Refdes and values overprint each other throughout the dense region (visible in the render).
- There is no frame, no title block, no scale, no revision.

This is not cosmetic, because of what is *not* in the CPL. `cpl_jlc.csv` has 208 placements and
contains **zero** rows matching `J_PI`, `J_LOADCELL`, `J_TC`, `J_ISOLOOP` or `K_*` — I grepped for
exactly that and got 0. So **16 designators are hand-assembly only**: `K_U1..K_U6`, `K_D1..K_D4`,
`K_PRESS`, `K_STOP` (12 Standex DIP reed relays), `J_TC`, `J_ISOLOOP`, `J_PI`, `J_LOADCELL`. Those
16 include the 30 V terminal and every relay in the keypad matrix. The person fitting them has
`assembly.pdf` at 1.2:1 contrast, and `B.Silkscreen` is **completely empty** (confirmed on
`twin_bottom.png`: no refdes, no outlines, nothing), so there is no designator anywhere on the side
they will be soldering from either.

`pcb_layers.pdf` is also 1 page for a 4-layer board — all copper layers composited onto one sheet,
with a single blue fill occupying 9.87% of the page. No individual layer can be inspected from it.
That is a documentation weakness only (the gerbers are the fab truth), so it is folded in here
rather than raised separately.

### F9 — P2 — Order-package integrity

`bom.csv` and `bom_jlc.csv` are byte-identical (60 rows). Three of those rows have **blank MPN and
blank LCSC**, covering 14 designators:

```
DIP05-1A72-13L, "K_D1,K_D2,K_D3,K_D4,K_PRESS,K_STOP,K_U1,K_U2,K_U3,K_U4,K_U5,K_U6", ,
KF350-3.5-4P,   J_ISOLOOP,   ,
PCC-SMP-K,      J_TC,        ,
```

None of those 14 designators appears in `cpl_jlc.csv`. Two further rows (`J_PI` = C35165,
`J_LOADCELL` = C157991) carry valid LCSC numbers but also have no CPL placement. Uploaded as-is,
JLC's BOM parser will raise "part not found" on 3 lines and "no placement" on 5 designators. It
does not endanger the board; it stalls the order and invites a substitution query on parts that
were deliberately self-supplied.

### F10 — P2 — The isolation comb is asymmetric and non-collinear at its ends

`Edge_Cuts` resolves to one outer outline with **12 holes**, in two staggered rows:

- row A (y **49.10..50.10**): 6 slots — `13.00..22.60` (9.60 mm long), then 8.44 mm slots at
  44.64, 75.12, 105.60, 136.08, 166.56
- row B (y **25.80..26.80**): 6 slots of 8.44 mm at 29.40, 59.88, 90.36, 120.84, 151.32, 181.80

Row A is completed at its east end not by a slot but by a **notch in the outer outline**:
`(200.00,48.80) → (191.50,48.80) → (191.50,49.80) → (200.00,49.80)`. Two consequences:

1. That notch spans y **48.80..49.80** while all 12 slots span y **49.10..50.10** — a **0.30 mm**
   y-offset in what is meant to be one continuous comb row.
2. The **west** end of row A is *not* a notch. Slot 0 stops at x=13.00 against a board edge at
   x=12.00, leaving a **1.00 mm** FR4 web. The east end breaks through; the west end does not. It
   is a 1.00 x 1.00 mm web in a 1.6 mm board — mechanically adequate, but the asymmetry looks
   unintended and it is the one place in the comb where the surface is continuous.

See R1 below: I checked whether this web undercuts the board's own ">=6mm creepage" claim. It does
not. This finding is geometry hygiene only.

### F11 — P2 — The mounting-hardware warning names the wrong failure mode first

All four mounting holes are NPTH (`attr 3`, 2.70 mm drill, no annulus) at H1 (24.00,14.00),
H2 (194.00,14.00), H3 (17.00,90.00), H4 (193.00,52.00). All four carry a `NYLON HW` legend and in
every case the hole is that legend's nearest footprint — H4 0.96 mm, H3 2.04 mm, H2 2.44 mm,
H1 3.96 mm. That part is well done and the warning is load-bearing.

But the warning text is `MOUNTING HW: NYLON M2.5 -- OR METAL IN A NON-CONDUCTIVE ENCLOSURE ONLY. /
A PLATE BONDING ANY 2 HOLES BREAKS THE 6mm KEYPAD ISOLATION. ADR-0012` — i.e. it permits a metal
screw and names *bonding two holes* as the failure mode. The nearer failure is a single screw:

- H2's hole wall is **0.50 mm** from `RSTOP_MID` copper on F.Cu — an isolated appliance-side relay
  contact net. Copper therefore starts at radius 1.35 + 0.50 = **1.85 mm** from the hole centre.
- H1's is **1.40 mm** → copper at radius **2.75 mm**.
- A DIN 125 M2.5 washer has 6.0 mm OD, i.e. radius **3.00 mm**. It overlaps that copper at both
  holes, separated only by soldermask.

So a single permitted metal screw with a washer at H2 sits over isolated appliance-side copper with
mask-only separation, and the silk does not say so. P2 because the escape ("nylon") is stated and is
the default reading, but the permitted alternative is more dangerous than the warning implies.

## Findings I raised against myself and then refuted

**R1 — "The 1.00 mm web at comb slot 0 breaks the >=6mm creepage claim."** Refuted. I measured every
F.Cu conductor on the isolated keypad side that lies above the comb row (344 items: pads, tracks,
zone fills on `KP_U1..U6`, `KP_D1..D4`, `U_SEL_BUS`, `D_SEL_BUS`, `RKEY_MID`, `RSTOP_MID`, `GND_ISO`)
against every non-isolated F.Cu conductor below it (4603 items), pairwise, restricted to pairs that
straddle y=49.10..50.10. Minimum straight-line surface gap is **6.53 mm**
(`K_D1` pad 4 / `D_SEL_BUS` at x 112.9..114.4, y 44.9..46.4 → the `COIL_D1_N` track at
x 114.1..121.0, y 52.9..59.8), with the next nine pairs all at **6.63 mm** (relay contact pads to the
F.Cu `GND` zone, whose fill starts at y=53.00). The silk's `>=6mm creepage` claim is **TRUE before
the slots are counted at all** — the slots are added margin on top of a compliant straight-line gap.
The web is a mechanical note (F10), not an isolation defect.

**R2 — "The 12 relay footprints in the top half of the board are unpopulated."** Refuted. This was
my first impression from `twin_top.png` — a row of twelve empty 6.50 x 19.30 mm silk outlines with
bare pads. `missing_models.txt` states 208/208 CPL bodies mounted, and the CPL genuinely has no
`K_*` rows: the relays are BOM-only, hand-fit parts, so there is no body to draw. Not a placement
defect. It does feed F6 (they are hand-assembly work with no usable drawing).

**R3 — "The relay pin-1 dot is 4.12 mm from pad 1, so it marks the wrong pin."** Refuted. For `K_U2`
(rot 0°) the silk circle is at (36.64, 28.80), which is the **top-left corner of the 14-pin DIP body
outline** (body 37.99..44.49 x, 28.35..47.65 y), and pad 1 (`5V_KEY_RELAY`) is the upper of the two
left-column pads at (37.43, 32.92). For the 180°-rotated units the dot flips correctly: `K_U1` dot
(30.60, 47.20) with pad 1 at (29.81, 43.08) — bottom-right corner, bottom-right pad. Same for
`K_D1` and `K_PRESS`. The dot marks the package pin-1 *corner*, which is the convention a hand
assembler uses. Correct in all five sampled units.

**R4 — "`J_DOOR`, `J_ESTOP` and `J_MODE` bodies overhang the board edge."** Refuted. Their F.CrtYd
bboxes do run past x=200.00 — `J_DOOR` +0.25, `J_ESTOP` +0.25, `J_MODE` +0.50 mm — and `J_ISOLOOP`
runs +0.34 mm past y=102.00. But every pad is inside the outline (max pad x = 198.35 on the
GH mounting pegs; `J_ISOLOOP`'s lowest pad is at y=100.25), and no silk bbox corner falls outside
the board or into a void (0 of 251, checked against the resolved Edge_Cuts polygon with all 12 holes).
These are horizontal edge-entry connectors whose courtyard deliberately includes the mating region.
Not a defect.

**R5 — Polarity, all nine diodes and the electrolytic: CORRECT, two ways each.** Every `D_*`
footprint carries the standard KiCad three-sided silk outline with the closed bar on the pad-1 side,
and pad 1 is the cathode in every case:

| ref | pad 1 net (cathode) | pad 2 net (anode) | sense |
|---|---|---|---|
| `D_ESD_IN` | 5V_IN | GND | reverse-biased clamp ✓ |
| `D_TVS` | 5V_PROTECTED | GND | ✓ |
| `D_REVCLAMP` | 5V_FUSED | GND | reverse-polarity catch, conducts and blows F1 on reversal ✓ |
| `D_KSTOP` | 5V_STOP | COIL_STOP_N | coil flyback, conducts coil-node→rail on turn-off ✓ |
| `D_COILEN` | COIL_EN_IN | GND | ✓ |
| `D_DOOR` | DOOR_RAW | GND | ✓ |
| `D_ESTOP` | ESTOP_RAW | GND | ✓ |
| `D_LCCLK` / `D_LCDAT` | LC_CLK / LC_DAT | GND | ✓ |

Second, independent route — the 3D bodies' own markings versus our silk, which is the one thing a
machine check cannot see: `D_REVCLAMP` (SMA) and `D_TVS` (SMB) both show the package band at the
**left** end, and both silk cathode bars are at the left (x=36.49 and x=59.34), pad 1 left.
`CE1` (CP_Elec_6.3x7.7) shows the modelled can's **black segment on the right** — negative — while
the silk `+` is at x≈45.96, i.e. the left, over pad 1 = `5V_PROTECTED` at x=47.30, with GND on pad 2

Verified visually in `crops/pol.png`.

**Also verified clean:** IC pin-1 markers. Every sampled SOIC/TSSOP carries a silk triangle at the
pin-1 corner and the modelled body's own pin-1 dot is at the same corner (`U_DECU`, `U_DECD`,
`U_SR1`, `U_EXP`, `U_ADC`, `U_ULNA`, `U_ULNB`, `U_OPTO`, `U_CAND2`). No silk in any milled void and
none off the board edge (0 of 251 on both tests). Single-sided assembly confirmed. `J_TC`, the Type-K
thermocouple socket, carries a dedicated 0.80 mm `+` glyph at (67.80, 96.00) against the square
`TC_POS_IN` pad at (70.04, 96.00) — the one place a polarity mark on a user-wired terminal was
needed and it is there and correct.

## Open pre-order obligation (not a package defect)

`fab/rotation_human_gate.txt` declares that 13 designators across 11 LCSC codes — `U_SR1`,
`U_ONESHOT`, `U_ULNA`, `U_ULNB`, `U_ADC`, `U_TC`, `U_EFUSE`, `U_EXP`, `U_DECD`, `U_DECU`, `U_SCHM`,
`U_COMP`, `U_COMP2` — have no numbering-free channel corroborating their rotation and **"EACH MUST
PASS THE JLC ORDER-PREVIEW HUMAN GATE BEFORE THE FIRST ORDER."** Nothing in this package can
discharge that; it requires a human at JLC's order preview. Recording it so it is not lost, not
counting it as a finding against the artefact.

## Assessment

Ten of the eleven findings are fixable in the silk generator without touching copper, and the two
that block are the same defect seen from both sides: at the SE corner, within a 15 mm square, the
30 V NOT-SELV terminal's identity has migrated onto an adjacent SELV sensor connector (F1) while the
SELV door-interlock connector has been left with "NOT SELV / ISO 30V" as its nearest words (F2).
Either one alone routes 30 V into the SELV domain by way of a legend that is present, legible and
wrong — which is worse than a missing one, because a missing legend makes a technician stop and ask.
Four of the connectors involved are physically cross-mateable (F3), so silk is the *only* barrier,
and on this board that barrier is inverted.

Everything a machine gate can see here is clean: no silk off-board, no silk in a void, no polarity
error, no flipped body, no unplaced part, courtyard overhangs all intentional, and the board's own
6 mm creepage claim measures 6.53 mm and holds. The defects are exactly the class that survives a
presence check and only a distance check or a human eye catches.

`04_kicad/` and `07_releases/` were not written to; nothing outside this review file was modified.

VERDICT: DO-NOT-ORDER
