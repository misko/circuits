## 1. The keypad isolation barrier — re-derived without DRC. **SOUND.**

I was warned that a pad-centre-only check once reported a false PASS here, so I
built the check to be incapable of that: every copper entity on the board is
turned into its real `SHAPE` (pads via `GetEffectiveShape(layer)`, **track
segments**, **via barrels on each layer they touch**, and **every filled-zone
sub-polygon with its holes**), and minimum separations are found by bisection on
`SHAPE::Collide`. This shares no method with `kicad-cli drc` (canon M1).

**MEASURED (by me), 12,791 copper entities** — 4,388 via-layer entities, 4,181
tracks, 4,076 pad-layer entities, 146 zone-fill polygons. 1,432 of them are on
the 14 `KEYPAD_ISO` nets (`KP_U1..KP_U6`, `KP_D1..KP_D4`, `U_SEL_BUS`,
`D_SEL_BUS`, `RKEY_MID`, `RSTOP_MID`; all 14 declared patterns resolve to a real
net — no ghosts).

**Same-layer result: 57 pairs closer than 6.000 mm, and every one of the 57 has
`J_KEY_MATRIX.MP` on the other side.** Worst 0.9965 mm (`KP_D1` track at
(17.80, 12.70) → MP). **Pairs outside the DRU's declared exemption: 0.**

The `.kicad_dru` exemption is `!B.memberOfFootprint('J_KEY_MATRIX')`, which is
broader than the MP tabs it argues for — so I checked whether anything else
could ride it. It cannot: **MEASURED**, the board's only unnetted copper
(excluding zero-annulus NPTH) is exactly the two `J_KEY_MATRIX.MP` SMD tabs,
1.000 × 2.700 mm, F.Cu only.

> **A false lead I chased and killed, recorded so nobody repeats it.** My first
> pass reported 92 additional sub-6 mm pairs against `H1.MP` / `H2.MP` /
> `H4.MP`, worst **1.6000 mm**. They are an artifact of my own tooling:
> `MountingHole_2.7mm_M2.5_ISO14580` pads are `PAD_ATTRIB_NPTH` with
> **size 2.700 = drill 2.700**, i.e. a zero-width annulus and no copper at all,
> but `GetEffectiveShape()` still returns the full disc. KiCad's DRC is right
> and my prefilter was wrong. **Any independent isolation check on this fleet
> must exclude NPTH pads whose size ≤ drill, or it will invent findings.**

**The floating-tab bridge question** — a floating conductor between two domains
divides the creepage path, so an exempted unnetted tab is only safe if it is not
*between* them. **MEASURED**: the second MP tab sits **1.0673 mm** from
`KEYPAD_ISO` copper (`KP_U1`) and **13.9891 mm** from the nearest netted
non-`KEYPAD_ISO` copper (`K_U1.2`, net `COIL_U1_N`); series creepage through the
tab is therefore **15.0563 mm against a 6.000 mm requirement**. The first MP tab
has no netted SELV copper within the 20 mm search radius at all. The exemption
is sound on the physics, not just on the rule text.

**And it holds in three dimensions.** A same-layer clearance rule says nothing
about a plane 0.21 mm below. **MEASURED, plan-view (layer-agnostic) distance
from `KEYPAD_ISO` copper to every plane fill:**

| plane | plan-view distance to nearest KEYPAD_ISO copper |
|---|---|
| `GND` @ F.Cu | **6.6300 mm** |
| `GND` @ In1.Cu | **6.6300 mm** |
| `3V3` @ In2.Cu | **6.6300 mm** |
| `GND` @ B.Cu | **6.6300 mm** |

All four planes are carved back to the same 6.63 mm envelope. There is no plane
under the keypad island on any layer. This is a genuine 3-D moat, and it is the
single most convincing thing I measured on this board.

## 2. Thermal — the AMS1117 path, independently derived

**The measured thermal path.** `U_LDO` pad 4 (tab, net `3V3`) is
**2.000 × 3.800 = 7.600 mm²**. **MEASURED**: there are **no F.Cu `3V3` tracks
within 41.68 mm of the tab** — the nearest F.Cu `3V3` copper of any kind is
other pads. The tab is an island in the F.Cu GND pour whose only metallic exit
is **2 vias inside the pad**, Ø0.250 mm / drill 0.150 mm, at (25.150, 68.900)
and (25.150, 70.000). Every other 3V3 pad in the cell (`U_LDO.2`, `C_LDOOUT.1`,
`C_3V3.1`, `FB1.1`) drops straight to the In2 plane on its own via. The
"flooded with 3V3 copper" claim is correctly refuted; the only F.Cu 3V3 zone
anywhere is **none** (the four filled zones are GND/F.Cu 2701.43 mm², GND/B.Cu
7385.83, GND/In1 8465.52, 3V3/In2 8420.03 — I re-enumerated them).

**Plane coverage under the part, re-measured by me** over `U_LDO`'s full pad
extent (8.300 × 6.100 mm), 3600-point grid: In1 `GND` **98.0 %**, In2 `3V3`
**99.3 %**, B.Cu `GND` **98.0 %**. ADR-0026 claims 98.6 / 98.9 / 98.6 over the
body; the two agree within the difference of window. **CONFIRMED.**

**θ_JA derived independently** (JLC 4-layer 1.6 mm stackup, 0.21 mm prepreg
F→In1, k_FR4 0.3 W/m·K, k_Cu 385 W/m·K, barrel plating at ADR-0027's own 18 µm
spec floor):

| term | value |
|---|---|
| tab → In1 through 0.21 mm prepreg over 7.600 mm² | 92.1 K/W |
| 2 × Ø0.150 mm barrels, 1.6 mm, 18 µm plating (∥) | 278.3 K/W |
| the two in parallel | **69.2 K/W** |
| + θ_JC (SOT-223) ≈ 15, + plane→ambient ≈ 1.9 | |
| **θ_JA (derived)** | **≈ 86 K/W** (≈ 81 K/W at 25 µm plating) |

The declared **90 °C/W** is therefore **conservative by 4–9 K/W** against my own
derivation. The declaration is honest and I do not challenge it.

**Junction temperature, my arithmetic:**
`P = (5.25 − 3.201) × 0.200 + 5.25 × 0.011 = 0.4098 + 0.0578 = 0.4676 W`;
`Tj = 75 + 0.4676 × 90 = ` **117.1 °C** against `Tj_max` 125 °C — **7.9 K
margin**, which reproduces the MANIFEST's stated figure exactly. Adding
ADR-0026's declared SHT4x coincident dual-pulse excursion of +3.8 K gives
**120.9 °C, 4.1 K margin**. Tight, declared, and passing.

**`R-THERM` holds independently**: `U_EFUSE` pad 9 is 0.900 × 1.600 = 1.440 mm²
(below the 4 mm² trigger) with 1 via in-pad + 1 at 0.40 mm; `U_LDO` pad 4 is
7.600 mm² with 2 in-pad vias.

**Board-level heat.** Summed at declared maxima the board dissipates
**≈ 1.3–1.7 W** (LDO 0.468 W, twelve 5 V reed coils ≈ 0.71 W, stop rail 0.10 W,
DMOS array ≈ 12 mW, eFuse ≈ 6 mW, F1 ≈ 11 mW, `Q_REV` ≈ 12 mW). Over
188.1 × 92.1 mm = 0.01732 m² (both faces 0.0346 m², h ≈ 12 W/m²K) that is
**≈ 3.6 K of self-heating** — an estimate, with its assumptions stated, not a
measurement. It is what makes **L5** worth writing down rather than ignoring:
at the enclosure STOP threshold of 65 °C the relay bodies sit at ≈ 68.6 °C
against a **+70 °C** rated ceiling — **≈ 1.4 K** — and at the 75 °C ambient
ADR-0026 grades the LDO at, they are **≈ 8.6 K over rating**. ADR-0018 already
names `DIP05-1A72-13L` at −20…+70 °C as *"the board's own binding envelope"*;
ADR-0026 chose 75 °C for the LDO, which is the conservative direction **for that
rail**, so its own analysis is not weakened by this. No document reconciles the
two, and the ORDER_README carries no operating-ambient statement at all.

## 3. The J_THERM tab land — I asked the question the other way round, and the framing collapses

I was given: *"the board's tab pads are 1.000 × 2.700 (matching the CLONE
`C42376901`); the genuine part's are 1.210 × 2.700; 'the board IS the genuine
part's land' holds on the eight SIGNAL pads only. Grade whether that is
acceptable to ship as UNRESOLVED."*

Half of that is right and the causal half is wrong.

**MEASURED (by me), board** (`J_THERM_A` / `J_THERM_B`, KiCad
`JST_GH_SM08B-GHS-TB_1x08-1MP_P1.25mm_Horizontal`): signal pads 0.600 × 1.700 at
±4.375 mm; MP tabs **1.000 × 2.700** at ±6.225 mm.

**MEASURED (by me), JLC's own cached CAD for the genuine `C265111`**
(`06_build/twin/easyeda/C265111/jlc.pretty/CONN-SMD_SM08B-GHS-TB-LF-SN.kicad_mod`):
signal 0.600 × 1.700 at ±4.380; tabs **1.210 × 2.700** at −6.22 / +6.23. Row
separation is 3.200 mm on both — the only other delta is a 0.250 mm datum
offset, which is why the twin fits at 0.01 mm.

**MEASURED (by me), live EasyEDA fetch of the SHOU HAN clone `C42376901`**
(units 10 mil, verified against the part's own 1.2500 mm pitch):

| | board | genuine `C265111` | clone `C42376901` |
|---|---|---|---|
| signal pad size | 0.600 × 1.700 | **0.600 × 1.700** | **0.700 × 1.800** |
| tab pad size | **1.000 × 2.700** | 1.210 × 2.700 | **1.000 × 2.500** |
| tab centre x | ±6.225 | −6.22 / +6.23 | ±6.2249 |

**The board is not the clone's land.** It disagrees with the clone on all eight
signal pads (0.600 × 1.700 vs 0.700 × 1.800) *and* on the tab **length**
(2.700 vs 2.500). It matches the clone on tab **width alone** — 1 of 10 pads'
full geometry.

**And the sweep says the outlier is JLC's model, not our library.** This board
fits three JST GH 1.25 mm side-entry parts. JLC's own models:

| part | JLC model tab pad | board tab pad |
|---|---|---|
| `C189896` SM05B-GHS-TB (`J_RH_AMBIENT`, `J_RH_EXHAUST`) | **1.000 × 2.700** | 1.000 × 2.700 ✔ |
| `C2683602` SM10B-GHS-TB (`J_KEY_MATRIX`) | **1.000 × 2.700** | 1.000 × 2.700 ✔ |
| `C265111` SM08B-GHS-TB (`J_THERM_A/B`) | **1.210 × 2.700** | 1.000 × 2.700 ✘ |

One JST series, one tab land in JST's own drawing, three vendor models — and
**two of the three agree with the board exactly**. The reading the evidence
supports is that `C265111`'s model carries a 0.210 mm tab-width outlier relative
to its own siblings, not that the KiCad library encodes a clone.

**Grade: acceptable to ship as UNRESOLVED — P2 — with the disposition text
changed.** The board's tab copper is a strict subset of `C265111`'s (inset
0.100 mm inboard, 0.110 mm outboard); the joint area is 2.700 mm² vs 3.267 mm²,
a **17.4 % retention derate** on a pad whose function is mechanical strain
relief, on a connector whose eight signal pads match the genuine part to
0.005 mm. It does not make the board wrong on either sourcing branch, and the
harness is hand-mated rather than shock-loaded. What should change is the
*sentence*: "we match the clone" is refuted, and carrying a refuted rationale
forward is the inherited-defect pattern this repo names.

**What artifact does the gate read, and could it be the wrong one?**
`jlc_twin` graded `C265111,J_THERM_A,OK,fit=0.01mm` — correctly, because
**PAD-GEOM compares pairwise pad-CENTRE distances**, and the centres agree to
0.005 mm. **`jlc_twin` has no pad-SIZE check at all**, for any part on any
board. That is why a 0.210 mm land-width disagreement reaches a release with a
green twin, and it is the gate-shaped gap behind this whole item. (I ran the
size comparison over every coded part: 214 pad-level disagreements > 0.100 mm,
but almost all are the benign direction — KiCad's IPC-7351 nominal lands are
*larger* than EasyEDA's minimum lands. The JST GH tab is one of the few where
ours is *smaller*, which is exactly why it is the one worth a sentence.)

**The 5 V chain has no plane** — I re-confirmed ADR-0027's premise
independently: the board's only four filled zones are `GND` on F.Cu/B.Cu/In1 and
`3V3` on In2. Every 5 V net is tracks and vias only, so ADR-0027's nodal solve
is measuring the right thing. **INHERITED (from ADR-0027, not re-solved by me):
the 137.79 mΩ copper term and the 328.29 mΩ `J_PWR.1 → U_LDO.3` total.** I
confirmed the geometry those rest on, not the solve.

**Ampacity.** `PWR_IN` class carries a 0.500 mm DRU floor on F.Cu 35 µm against
a whole-board worst case of 0.4024 A — roughly 4–5× headroom on IPC-2152 at a
10 K rise. `COIL_RAIL` / `STOP_RAIL` at 0.400 mm against 0.150 A / 0.020 A.
Not close to anything.

**Local decoupling / hot loops (L1).** `policy_audit` grades `P-ADJ` as WAIVED,
and I re-ran it. The gate reports **five** exceedances:

| net | measured pair | span | budget | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `5V_RPP` | `U_EFUSE.3 → C_EFIN.1` | **5.95 mm** | 3.0 | **1.98×** |
| `EF_OVLO` | `U_EFUSE.2 → R_OVT.2` | **7.56 mm** | 5.0 | 1.51× |

The waiver's `why` says: *"Three violations, all measured … (spans ~150mm)"*,
and its mechanism is *"the metric charges the ENTIRE rail net to the budget
declared for a LOCAL decoupling cap."* **Neither statement describes these
findings.** There are five, not three; the spans are 5.89–7.56 mm, not ~150 mm;
and the mechanism cannot apply at all to `EF_ILM` (**MEASURED: 2 pads on the
net**, `R_ILM.1` and `U_EFUSE.7` — the max-pairwise span *is* the local pair) or
`EF_OVLO` (**3 pads**). The gate has evidently learned to use anchor pins and
now measures local pairs; the waiver still argues against the old metric.
`waiver_provenance.py` reports this entry `OWED — 'why' leans on a typed
measurement and declares no evidence:`, so no machine has ever re-run it. This
is the canon-M4 class the skill documents — a waiver that reverses its own
conclusion when re-measured — and it is **P1** because it is discharging live
findings it does not describe.

On the physics, so the P1 is not read as bigger than it is: `EF_ILM` at 6.98 mm
of 0.2 mm track adds ≈ 17 mΩ against `R_ILM` = 1.2 kΩ (0.001 %) — the ILIM
budget's real subject is noise pickup, not resistance. `EF_OVLO` is a
high-impedance divider node run 7.56 mm. The one with genuine physics is
**`5V_RPP` — the eFuse input bypass at 1.98× its 3.0 mm budget**, i.e. ~6 mm of
extra IN→GND loop (~6 nH) on the part whose fast current-limit is the reason the
datasheet asks for the loop to be minimised *at the terminals*. It is a P2-scale
physical miss inside a P1-scale waiver defect.
