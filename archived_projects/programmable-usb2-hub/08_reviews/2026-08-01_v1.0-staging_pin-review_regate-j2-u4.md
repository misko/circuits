## J2 — TE Connectivity 292304-1

**VERDICT: FAIL**

Physical numbering and winding:

- The TE PC BOARD MOUNTING DIMENSIONS show the signal-hole matrix as pins `2, 1` on the upper row and `3, 4` on the lower row, with 2.50 mm horizontal pitch and 2.00 mm row spacing. The dossier shows exactly that matrix: pin 1 at `(+1.25,-2.00)`, pin 2 at `(-1.25,-2.00)`, pin 3 at `(-1.25,+0.00)`, and pin 4 at `(+1.25,+0.00)`. This is a rotation-equivalent, non-mirrored match and agrees with the dossier's **CCW (top view)** result.
- The drawing calls for four 0.92 ± 0.08 mm signal holes and two 2.30 ± 0.08 mm shell/board-lock holes on 12.04 mm centers. The dossier preserves four signal pads and two shell-land rows at x = ±6.02 mm. The pad count and land locations are physically consistent with the drawing.

Findings:

- J2 pin 1 (VBUS): expected USB Type-B contact 1 in the drawing's upper-right board-hole position, carrying host VBUS without board-to-host backfeed, vs the dossier's upper-right pad 1 on `USB_UP_VBUS`. **PASS** for pin identity, function, and dedicated upstream-VBUS net intent. The provided sources do not expose the wider topology, so the no-backfeed implementation itself is outside this review's evidence.
- J2 pin 2 (D-): expected USB Type-B contact 2 in the upper-left position on the upstream negative data conductor, vs the dossier's upper-left pad 2 on `UP_CONN_N`. **PASS**.
- J2 pin 3 (D+): expected USB Type-B contact 3 in the lower-left position on the upstream positive data conductor, vs the dossier's lower-left pad 3 on `UP_CONN_P`. **PASS**.
- J2 pin 4 (GND): expected USB Type-B contact 4 in the lower-right position on signal/power return, vs the dossier's lower-right pad 4 on `GND`. **PASS**.
- J2 pin SH / footprint pad 5 (SHIELD), left land: expected the left 2.30 mm board-lock/shell land at x = -6.02 mm to remain a distinct physical land while being electrically identified as part of the common metal shell, vs the dossier's left pad-5 row at `(-6.02,+2.71)` on `GND`. Physical geometry and intended shield net are sane, but the logical identity is collapsed to pad 5 without the required fused declaration. **FAIL**.
- J2 pin SH / footprint pad 5 (SHIELD), right land: expected the right 2.30 mm board-lock/shell land at x = +6.02 mm to remain a distinct physical land while being electrically identified as part of the common metal shell, vs the dossier's right pad-5 row at `(+6.02,+2.71)` on `GND`. Physical geometry and intended shield net are sane, but the logical identity is collapsed to pad 5 without the required fused declaration. **FAIL**.
- J2 alias/shell-land contract: expected a manufacturer-evidenced alias for the two physical shell lands collapsed into schematic/footprint identity 5, explicitly including `fused: true`, `why`, and evidence, vs `part.yaml` providing `SH -> schematic 5 / footprint 5`, `why`, and evidence but omitting `fused: true`. The drawing depicts both board locks as features of the common metal shell, so a fused mapping is supportable, but the release metadata does not actually declare it. **FAIL — BLOCKING unresolved contradiction**.

Required disposition before release: add an explicit, evidence-backed `fused: true` declaration for the two shell lands (or give the lands distinct physical identities and map them without collapse), regenerate the staged dossier, and repeat the fresh-context review. No signal-contact winding or D+/D- swap was found.

## U4 — Diodes Incorporated AP63203QWU-7

**VERDICT: PASS**

Physical numbering and winding:

- DS43698's top-view pin assignment is `1 FB`, `2 EN`, `3 VIN` down one side and `4 GND`, `5 SW`, `6 BST` back up the other. The marking figure shows the equivalent TSOT26 arrangement under a 90° rotation. The dossier positions reproduce that same sequence: pins 1/2/3 down the west side and 4/5/6 up the east side. This is non-mirrored and agrees with the dossier's **CCW (top view)** result.
- The exact orderable row identifies `AP63203QWU-7` as the fixed 3.3 V, 1100 kHz, PFM/PWM TSOT26 member. The package has six leads and no exposed pad. The dossier has exactly six pads and no EP; no physical identity is missing or collapsed.

Findings:

- U4 pin 1 (FB): expected the AP63203Q fixed-output feedback pin tied directly to VOUT, with no adjustable divider, vs the dossier's pad 1 on `3V3_LOGIC`. **PASS**; this matches DS43698's AP63203Q-specific application and layout instruction.
- U4 pin 2 (EN): expected a digital enable driven high, tied to VIN, or left floating for automatic startup, vs the dossier's pad 2 on `VIN_PROTECTED`. **PASS**; a VIN tie is explicitly permitted.
- U4 pin 3 (VIN): expected the 3.8 V to 32 V power input on the converter input rail, vs the dossier's pad 3 on `VIN_PROTECTED`. **PASS** for function-to-net intent.
- U4 pin 4 (GND): expected power ground, vs the dossier's pad 4 on `GND`. **PASS**.
- U4 pin 5 (SW): expected the power switching node feeding the output LC filter, vs the dossier's pad 5 on `SW_3V3`. **PASS**; the net is explicitly identified as the 3.3 V converter switch node.
- U4 pin 6 (BST): expected the high-side gate-drive boost node, with a 100 nF capacitor to SW, vs the dossier's pad 6 on `BST_3V3`. **PASS** for function-to-net intent; the named bootstrap node is distinct from VIN, GND, and the regulated output.

No winding error, mirrored pin map, missing pad, exposed-pad omission, pin-function mismatch, or contradictory connected-net intent was found for U4.
