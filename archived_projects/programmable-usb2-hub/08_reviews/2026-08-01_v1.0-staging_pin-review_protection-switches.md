# Fresh-context pin review — protection and power switches

Date: 2026-08-01  
Release: v1.0-staging  
Scope: Q1–Q6, U1, U9–U12

## Review boundary and method

This review used only the fresh-context pin-review protocol, the eleven named release pin-audit dossiers, and the five named manufacturer PDFs. No schematic, board source, prior review, or design-session history was consulted.

The manufacturer pin figures were rendered and read as top views before comparison with the dossier coordinates. Rotation is ignored; mirroring is not. Pin count, exposed/fused copper treatment, function-to-net kind, and pairwise symmetry were then checked independently.

Authority-derived package facts:

- Infineon BSC016N06NS, datasheet rev. 2.6 page 1: top-view numbering is 1–4 along the source/gate side and 5–8 back along the opposite side, hence CCW. Pins 1–3 are Source, pin 4 is Gate, and pins 5–8 are Drain. Package page 12 explicitly identifies the recommended copper as “TDSON-8 Fused Leads: Boardpads & Apertures” and shows one 4.41 mm by 4.55 mm fused drain boardpad.
- AOS AON6354, datasheet rev. 2.0 page 1: the top-view figure has pin 1 at the upper-left, pins 1–4 descending on the left, and pins 5–8 ascending on the right, hence CCW. Pins 1–3 are S, pin 4 is G, and pins 5–8 are D. The bottom-view package illustration shows the exposed drain metal and the four drain-side terminals; there is no separately numbered EP.
- TI LM74810-Q1, SNOSD98A Figure 5-1 and Table 5-1 page 3: 12-pin WSON top view, pin 1 upper-left, pins 1–6 descending left and 7–12 ascending right, hence CCW. The exposed thermal pad is RTN and must be left floating, not tied to GND. DRR0012E identifies it as pad 13 and shows all twelve perimeter pads plus pad 13.
- TI TPS25947xx, SLVSFC9C Figure 5-1 and Table 5-1 pages 5–6: 10-pin RPW top view, pin 1 upper-left, pins 1–4 descending left, power lands 5 and 6 through the middle, and pins 7–10 ascending right, hence CCW. RPW0010A pages 72–74 show ten terminals and two distinct long manufacturer-fused copper lands: pin 5 is IN and pin 6 is OUT. There is no EP. SLVUC01’s EVM schematic independently uses pins 5/6 as IN/OUT and the same control-pin numbering for TPS259470 devices.
