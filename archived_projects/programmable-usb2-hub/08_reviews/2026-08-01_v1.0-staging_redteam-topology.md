# Fresh-context red-team review — topology, protection, and ratings

## Scope and method

This was an adversarial review of the mutable pre-seal staging archive. Inputs
were limited to the release archive and the curated brief, architecture,
detailed design, decision records, part dossiers, and `03_src` configuration.
No journals, learnings, STATUS beacon, prior `08_reviews` records, or publication
manifest were read.

MEASURED (by this reviewer): the archived KiCad board and exported netlist were
traced from J1 through F1, Q1/Q2, D1, U2/U3/U4, U9-U12, and J3-J6; the archived
BOM was compared to those refs; threshold and margin arithmetic was recomputed
from the exact resistor values and manufacturer limit tables in the supplied
PDFs. The reviewed board SHA-256 is the value in the header.

## Findings

| finding | severity | evidence | disposition recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| RT-TOP-01 — The protected-side TVS does not protect all parts connected to `VIN_PROTECTED` within their absolute ratings. | P0 | MEASURED: the board connects D1 cathode to `VIN_PROTECTED` and anode to GND. The same rail directly feeds both LM5116 cells: Q3/Q5 drains are on `VIN_PROTECTED`, while Q4/Q6 see the corresponding switch node, and U4 pins 2/3 are on `VIN_PROTECTED`. The exact SMBJ26A PDF specifies 28.9 V minimum / 31.9 V maximum breakdown and 42.1 V maximum clamp at 14.3 A. The exact AON6354 PDF specifies 30 V absolute `VDS` and only a 36 V, 10 us spike rating. The exact AP63203Q PDF specifies 35 V DC and 40 V for 400 ms absolute maximum at VIN. Thus D1 can allow voltage above the AON6354 absolute rating before breakdown and its specified clamp exceeds both the MOSFET spike rating and U4's transient absolute maximum. The LM74810 OV path has up to 5.4 us deglitch before HGATE turnoff, so the OV disconnect is not an independent fast clamp for this pair. | Block the release. Re-select both LM5116 power-FET sets and the logic buck for ratings above the verified protected-rail transient, or redesign the surge clamp/disconnect so a source-impedance-specific worst-case waveform stays below every downstream absolute maximum. Recompute loss and compensation after any MOSFET change and bench-verify the transient at `VIN_PROTECTED` and both SW nodes. |
| RT-TOP-02 — The input OV threshold can disconnect a valid 24 V source. | P0 | MEASURED: actual board values are R1=88.7 kOhm from `VIN_FUSED` to `OV_TOP`, R2=0 Ohm to `OV_SENSE`, and R3=4.75 kOhm to GND. The LM74810 PDF gives rising OV threshold 1.195/1.231/1.267 V and OV leakage 200 nA maximum. Nominal trip is `1.231*(1+88.7/4.75)=24.218 V`. The guaranteed-low corner, using threshold minimum, R1 -1%, R3 +1%, and zero leakage, is `1.195*(1+87.813/4.7975)=23.068 V`. This is below the brief's permitted 24 V input. The design documents calculate only the high trip corner (about 25.5 V) and miss the acceptance corner. | Block the release. Redesign the OV divider against both inequalities: guaranteed ON at the full specified source maximum including its tolerance, and guaranteed OFF below the independently safe downstream/clamp limit. The present 24 V input / 26 V TVS window is too narrow to retune casually; resolve it together with RT-TOP-01. |
| RT-TOP-03 — E-MARGIN is a false pass: the declared resistance omits the mandatory connector and cable, and the 5 V window fails at worst case. | P0 | MEASURED: R102/R202 are 3.92 kOhm 0.1%, R103/R203 are 1.21 kOhm 1%, and LM5116 Vref tolerance is 1.5%. The recomputed low setpoint is 5.031704 V. Relative to the declared 4.75 V load threshold at 3 A, total un-derated resistance may be at most 93.901 mOhm; with the gate's stated 20% margin, actual total resistance may be at most 78.251 mOhm. `power_tree.yaml` spends 70 mOhm on eFuse plus PCB copper/joints and explicitly excludes the mating plug and cable, leaving only 8.251 mOhm. The exact USB1130 specification allows 30 mOhm per mated contact initially (40 mOhm after several qualification stresses), so even one contact exceeds the remaining budget; the VBUS and GND contacts together add up to 60 mOhm before any plug wiring or cable. At 70+60=130 mOhm, load voltage is 4.641704 V without the 20% margin and 4.563704 V with it. The high feedback corner is 5.272858 V, above the brief's 5.25 V no-load connector ceiling. No cable MPN, length, gauge, plug resistance, or routed-copper resistance derivation is present. The archived policy report nevertheless says E-MARGIN PASS because the config supplies the board-only 70 mOhm value as if it were total. | Block the release. Define the actual load brownout point and a bounded cable/plug assembly; derive or measure both supply and return PCB resistance on this board; include eFuse, solder joints, both connector contacts, cable conductors, and plug in `ir_budget_mohm`; then choose a regulation strategy/tolerance window that is <=5.25 V at no load and >=the load threshold at maximum current with margin. Re-run E-MARGIN with the total value and add four-port voltage-drop qualification. |
| RT-TOP-04 — MCU power-command inputs have no hardware reset default despite the source claiming they are low on reset. | P1 | MEASURED: each `P1_PWR_CMD` through `P4_PWR_CMD` net contains only one U7 GPIO and one U8 AND-gate input; there is no pull resistor. The 100 kOhm resistors R410/R420/R430/R440 are on the AND outputs `Pn_PWR_EN`, not on the command inputs. The STM32G0B1 exact datasheet states that on reset all I/Os are analog inputs unless otherwise specified. The archived TSX instead says, "On reset every MCU command is low," and ARCHITECTURE says hardware defaults hold the ports off. A floating 74LVC08 input does not establish that state; an output pulldown does not override a driven-high logic output. | Add a hardware pulldown to each MCU command input (and define hub PRTPWR reset behavior at U8), then assert those components/nets in `electrical_invariants.yaml`. Verify ports remain off during MCU reset, hub reset, brownout, debugger halt, and firmware crash. |
| RT-TOP-05 — The only sustained input overcurrent protection is not a completely specified part, and its assembly instructions contradict the exact holder dossier. | P1 | MEASURED: the board's F1 footprint is a Keystone 3568 holder connecting `VIN_RAW` to `VIN_FUSED`; F1 is absent from both BOM and CPL by assembly rule. No exact 10 A blade-fuse MPN, DC voltage rating, interrupt rating, or time-current curve appears in the release BOM or part dossiers. The supplied Keystone drawing labels Cat. No. 3568 as one complete MINI FUSE HOLDER with four leads. Its dossier likewise calls it a complete holder, but `assembly.yaml` and ORDER_README instruct the builder to procure/install "two exact Keystone 3568 clips." The LM74810 dossier explicitly says the controller is not a current limiter, so this unresolved manual item is the trunk's only sustained overcurrent protection. | Select and document one exact 10 A MINI fuse with adequate DC voltage, interrupt capacity, and time-current behavior for the 10 A copper/source envelope. Add it to procurement/assembly evidence. Correct the instruction to install one exact 3568 holder, or deliberately change the footprint and dossier to two separately identified clips. Add an assembled continuity/fuse-value inspection gate. |
| RT-TOP-06 — Release order dimensions disagree with the actual board. | P1 | MEASURED: `floorplan.yaml` defines x=20..150 mm and y=20..110 mm, nominally 130 x 90 mm. The archived board edge bounding box measures 130.1 x 90.1 mm including 0.1 mm edge strokes. MANIFEST and ORDER_README instead state 150 x 80 mm. | Correct all staging order paperwork to 130 x 90 mm nominal and regenerate any cost/mechanical claims based on 150 x 80 mm before seal. |
| RT-TOP-07 — The archived authoring source begins with an unrelated board's live-looking design narrative. | P2 | MEASURED: `source/programmable_usb2_hub.tsx` begins with hundreds of lines headed `usb-hub-3s-v3 (rev v1.6)` describing a 3S LiPo board, three USB-A charging ports, and a Raspberry Pi USB-C rail, including the statement "NOT a USB hub." The actual `export default` for this four-port programmable hub begins much later. These comments are not executed, but they directly contradict BRIEF/ARCHITECTURE and obscure which constraints govern the reusable helper code. | Remove the foreign historical narrative and dead board-specific material from this project's authoring source; retain only helpers actually used by the current export and current-board derivation comments. Rebuild and prove the netlist/board are unchanged. |

## Protection-chain trace

MEASURED: the implemented directionality itself is correct where present:

- J1 positive reaches F1, then Q1/Q2 in a common-drain back-to-back N-FET
  chain (`VIN_RAW -> F1 -> VIN_FUSED -> Q1 -> FET_MID -> Q2 ->
  VIN_PROTECTED`). Q1 sources are on `VIN_FUSED`, Q2 sources are on
  `VIN_PROTECTED`, and all physical drain pins join `FET_MID`.
- D1 is the unidirectional SMBJ26A with cathode/pad 1 on `VIN_PROTECTED` and
  anode/pad 2 on GND, after reverse blocking; reverse input therefore does not
  forward-bias it ahead of the controller.
- Each TPS259470A has IN on its assigned 5 V rail and OUT on its own port VBUS.
  D4-D7 are cathode-to-VBUS/anode-to-GND negative-transient clamps. Each
  USBLC6-2SC6 is in-line on D+/D- and has its VBUS pin on the corresponding
  post-eFuse rail.
- U2/U3 are correctly BUCK topologies for 12-24 V to 5 V, and external-feed
  removal is a real de-energization method for this non-battery board.

Those correct net connections do not cure the rating and corner failures in
RT-TOP-01 through RT-TOP-03.

## Verdict rationale

`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE` because RT-TOP-01, RT-TOP-02, and RT-TOP-03 are P0
findings. Each independently blocks seal: downstream parts are not protected
within absolute maximum, valid 24 V input operation is not guaranteed, and the
advertised output voltage/current envelope cannot be guaranteed with the real
connector path, even before a cable is added.

`order_verdict: BLOCKED-SOURCING` is intentionally independent of the design
verdict. MEASURED from the staged stock evidence: 59/59 coded BOM lines were
graded, 58/59 meet the five-board stock requirement, and C5248536 / exact
AP63203QWU-7 / U4 measures stock 0. The stock report's catalog verdict is FAIL
with a planned consignment disposition. Planning to consign does not convert
zero catalog stock or an unaccepted consignment into ORDER authority. Re-run
the measurement after exact-part procurement and JLC consignment acceptance;
until then the sealed measurement supports BLOCKED-SOURCING.

No P0 may be waived for this release. Fix upstream source/configuration, rebuild
the board and package, rerun all mechanical gates, and obtain a fresh-context
post-fix topology lens before seal.
