# Independent exact-artifact RF fabrication review

The supplied SHA-256 was independently reproduced from the exact ZIP. `unzip -t` reports all 13 members intact.

## Evidence and findings

### USB2-FAB-STACKUP — FAIL

The Gerber X2 headers do identify an unambiguous four-copper order: F.Cu = L1 Top, In1.Cu = L2 Inner, In2.Cu = L3 Inner, and B.Cu = L4 Bottom. That is not enough to bind the RF cross-section. The ZIP contains no Gerber job file, stackup drawing, impedance coupon/request, order-form capture, material/thickness declaration, or JLC04161H-7628 selection. The sealed board's `setup` block likewise contains no physical stackup table. Consequently the contract's 0.2104 mm L1-to-L2 dielectric height, 1.6 mm JLC04161H-7628 construction, controlled-impedance request, and pre-payment calculator confirmation are not preserved by this fabrication artifact.

### USB2-FAB-COPPER — FAIL

The plots preserve 0.25 mm USB trace apertures (`D73`, with only 0.2496 mm arc interpolation apertures where applicable). A representative long P1 hub run has 0.40 mm center spacing, hence the authored 0.15 mm edge gap with 0.25 mm traces.

However, the exact Gerbers and sealed board contradict the binding RF claim that every high-speed segment stays on F.Cu over In1.Cu with zero signal-layer transitions:

- B.Cu contains routed segments for P1_PORT_P/N; P2_HUB_P/N and P2_PORT_P/N; P3_PORT_P/N; and P4_HUB_P/N and P4_PORT_P/N.
- In2.Cu contains routed segments for MGMT_P/N.
- Those bottom-layer USB segments reference In2.Cu, not the contract-declared In1.Cu ground plane; the management pair is itself routed on the layer named as an inner reference/power layer.

This is not plot corruption: the same layer assignments are present in the sealed source board, and the Gerbers faithfully reproduce them. It is a board/package contradiction with `USB2-CLAIM-ZDIFF` and `USB2-CLAIM-RETURN`.

### USB2-FAB-MASK — PASS

Both negative-polarity top and bottom solder-mask plots are present and registered with `SameCoordinates,Original`. USB connector, switch, ESD, and hub pad openings remain discrete at the inspected launches. The sealed board enables front/back via tenting, and representative 0.25/0.15 mm USB signal-via coordinates have no corresponding top or bottom mask opening. No clipped mask boundary or unintended merged launch opening was found.

### USB2-FAB-DRILL — FAIL

The PTH drill file is syntactically complete and declares plated holes across layers 1-4. It includes a 0.150 mm via-drill tool. Cross-checking the exact source board finds 28 through signal vias on declared USB nets, each with 0.25 mm pad and 0.15 mm drill: two each on P2_HUB_P/N, P2_PORT_P/N, P4_HUB_P/N, MGMT_P/N, P3_PORT_P/N, P1_PORT_P/N, and P4_PORT_P/N. These coordinates are present in the plotted plated-drill data. They are deliberate signal transitions, not ground stitching, and directly violate the RF contract's zero-signal-via acceptance condition.

The separate NPTH file contains the four 3.2 mm mounting holes at (24,24), (24,106), (146,72), and (146,106), matching the sealed board.

### USB2-FAB-PLOT — PASS

The archive has the complete expected fabrication set: four copper layers, two masks, two pastes, two silkscreens, one non-plated profile, and separate PTH/NPTH drill files. All Gerbers use metric 4.6 absolute coordinates, positive copper polarity, common original coordinates, and valid `M02` termination; both drill files are metric absolute and end in `M30`. The edge profile is a closed 130 mm x 90 mm rectangle from (20,20) to (150,110). No missing layer, coordinate mismatch, truncated file, open outline, or plot clipping was found.

## Verdict

`fab_package_verdict: HOLD` because the stackup, copper, and drill requirements fail. Before prototype authorization, bind the JLC04161H-7628 stackup and impedance-control/coupon settings into reviewable order evidence, then either reroute every declared USB segment onto F.Cu without signal vias over a continuous In1.Cu ground reference or revise the RF design contract and independently re-review/solve every actually used layer transition and reference structure. Regenerate and hash a new Gerber/drill archive after correction.

Even after those failures are closed, production remains on hold until the first-article eye, TDR/coupon, packet/reconnect, and switch-node-coupling measurements in `rf.yaml` pass.
