A-POS — is the shipped CPL on JLC's PLACEMENT DATUM?  Measured 2026-07-25.

THE RULE (landed 2026-07-25, after this board's v1.0 seal): JLC's CPL
Mid X/Y is the bbox centre of PAD CENTRES, not the footprint anchor
(fp.GetPosition()). Over 228 cached JLC-native footprints on six boards:
  pad-CENTRE bbox   matches the model origin to <=0.01mm on 227/228 (99.6%)
  pad-OUTLINE bbox  213/228        centroid  198/228
It is not theoretical: crow-recorder-central-v2 v1.4 places its only
USB-C 1.3025mm off against 1.150mm contacts = 0.000mm pad overlap, and
usb-hub-3s-v3 v1.5 has 11 of 108 rows off, ALL FIVE connectors among them.

METHOD (canon M1): assembly_coverage.py's A-POS parses the board TEXT.
This re-derivation uses pcbnew instead — a different reader — and
reports BOTH candidate datums per row so the two cannot be confused.

RESULT: the shipped CPL is ALREADY on the pad-centre datum, exactly.
Re-exporting cannot change a single coordinate, so fab/cpl.csv stays
BYTE-IDENTICAL to v1.0 and the paperwork-only framing holds.

WHY THIS BOARD HAS ZERO EXPOSURE TO THE CLASS. On all 26 placements the
pad-centre datum and the footprint anchor COINCIDE to 0.00000mm, so the
two definitions cannot disagree here. The class bites footprints whose
ANCHOR is offset from the pad array — characteristically CONNECTORS,
with shield tabs, mounting lugs or body-referenced origins (all five of
usb-hub-3s-v3's offenders are connectors; crow-recorder-central-v2's is
a USB-C). This board's 26 machine-placed parts are all symmetric chip
(0603/0805/1210), SOIC-8, SOT-553 and a 4-pad symmetric transducer —
anchor-at-pad-centre by construction. Its only two connector-class
parts, J1 (RJ45) and MK1 (electret), are hand-solder and are NOT ON THE
CPL AT ALL. The exposure is structurally absent, not merely absent today.
