========================================================================
  `release_freshness_check.py --bom-only-supersede` asserts more than
  'it differs': the delta must be WHOLE ROWS REMOVED, for designators NOT
  on the CPL. A row added, a row edited, or a removal for a still-placed
  designator all FAIL. Measured this release:
      2 whole row(s) REMOVED (J1, MK1), 0 added, 0 edited;
      neither removed designator is on the CPL.          -> PASS
