### Finding AUD-2 (tool, P2) — C1's A-RENDER FAIL is a CLASSIFIER blind spot, not a render defect

`twin_overlay.extract_body()` classifies a body pixel as
**`saturation(p) < SAT_THRESHOLD` (0.12)** — it assumes an unsaturated
(grey/white/black) component body against the saturated green board.

**C1's JLC mesh (C49066, 1210 100 µF MLCC) renders OCHRE.** Measured colours
inside C1's expected box on `twin_top.png`:

| region | RGB | saturation |
|---|---|---|
| C1 body (expected-centre seed pixel) | `(196,154,0)` | **1.000** |
| C1 terminations | `(254,254,254)` / `(181,181,181)` | 0.000 |
| bare mask / copper (background) | `(76,81,55)` / `(29,67,48)` | 0.321 / 0.567 |

**771 of the 1209 pixels (63.8 %) inside C1's expected box are classified as
BACKGROUND by the gate.** The seed at the expected centre lands on a
saturation-1.000 pixel, so the flood falls back to the nearest admissible pixel
— a termination — and returns a **0.913 × 2.658 mm sliver, 208 px**. Every ref
that PASSES has an unsaturated body: U1 `(74,74,74)`, D2 `(84,84,84)`,
LS1 `(70,70,70)`, all saturation 0.000.

The signature that names it as under-capture rather than displacement:
**outward excursion 0.059 mm**. A body genuinely 1.62 mm out of position would
show outward ≈ centre delta. 0.059 mm means the measured blob sits essentially
INSIDE the expected box.

**Re-measured with a classifier that admits BOTH the ochre body and the
grey terminations** (`saturation < 0.12 OR > 0.90`; window, erosion 2, seed,
dilate-back and arithmetic otherwise identical to `twin_overlay`):

```
C1: component 1024 px  (the gate's classifier got 208)
  expected  58.900,42.750..62.100,45.250
  measured  58.475,42.652..62.547,45.314
  centre delta 0.020 mm   outward excursion 0.447 mm   (tol 1.00)
  edge deltas L,T,R,B: -0.425,-0.098,+0.447,+0.064
```

**0.020 mm centre delta, 0.447 mm outward — both inside tolerance**, and the
edge deltas sit in the same band as the passing refs (D2 −0.75/+0.72,
D3 −0.68/+0.59: the anti-aliased specular edge of a metal termination).

**VERDICT: A-RENDER PASS on the board.** The render IS faithful; every one of
the 5 measurable bodies is where the geometry says, C1 included. The gate's
exit-1 is a **real defect in `twin_overlay.py`** — a saturated body colour is
neither measured nor declared unresolvable, it is silently mis-measured and
fails — and it is filed as AUD-2 against the tool, not against this board.
Overlay + crops: `06_build/proof/audit-2026-07-27/overlay_top/`.
