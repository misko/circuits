# 0. LEAD ITEM — independent derivation of U1's rotation

**U1 (XU316-1024-TQ128-I24, C6938291, consigned) belongs at CPL rotation 270°.
The shipped `fab/cpl.csv` says 270.0. CONFIRMED.**

Derived from three independent channels, none of which is the project's own
`rot_remeasure.py` (canon M1 — checker and checked must not share a method):

**(a) Our own pad geometry, re-transformed by hand.** Parsed
`07_releases/.../source/crow_recorder_central_v2.kicad_pcb` as text; U1's
footprint placement reads `(at 90.0 102.0)` with **no angle token — the
footprint sits on the board at 0°**. Applied the KiCad footprint→board
transform myself (Y-down, CCW-positive: `x' = x·cosA + y·sinA`,
`y' = −x·sinA + y·cosA`) to all 129 numbered pads.

**(b) JLC/EasyEDA's own cached land pattern for C6938291**
(`easyeda2kicad:TQFP-128_L14.0-W14.0-P0.40-LS16.0-BL-EP4.7`, 129 pads). This is
the orientation JLC's placement machine calls 0°. Fitting the JLC model to our
placed pads, matched **by pad number**, centroid-aligned:

| θ | RMS error | max error |
|---|---|---|
| 0° | 11.9812 mm | 13.9392 mm |
| 90° | 16.9439 mm | 19.7114 mm |
| 180° | 11.9812 mm | 13.9392 mm |
| **270°** | **0.0025 mm** | **0.0025 mm** |

Separation from next-best: **4811×**. Not a close call.

Readable by eye, pad 1 relative to each footprint's own centroid:
JLC's model puts pad 1 at (−6.200, **+7.660**) — bottom edge, left end.
Ours puts pad 1 at (**−7.662**, −6.200) — left edge, top end. Rotating JLC's
(−6.200, +7.660) by 270° CCW gives (−7.660, −6.200) = ours. The sign convention
is fixed by the physics of the transform, not by agreement with the record:
`R = φ_ours + A − φ_jlc`, and A = 0 here, so R = 270°.

**(c) The datasheet figure, to rule out a mirror.** XU316-1024-TQ128 datasheet
XM-014532-PC v2.0.0, **§3 Fig. 2 "TQ128 pin configuration", p. 7** (rendered at
500 dpi and read directly). The pin-1 dot is at the **top-left**; pin 1 =
X0D06 sits at the **top of the LEFT edge** with numbering running **downward**
(1 X0D06, 2 X0D01, 3 X0D07, 4 X0D10, 5 VDD, 6 X0D00, 7 X0D11, 8 X0D14,
9 X0D16), and the top edge runs **right-to-left** (128 X0D05, 127 X0D04,
126 X0D33, 125 X0D34, 124 X0D31, 123 X0D32, 122 X0D30). Our footprint has pad 1
at left-edge-top and pads 97→128 running right-to-left along the top — **same
chirality, no mirror**. JLC's model traces the same counter-clockwise sense
(1–32 bottom L→R, 33–64 up the right edge). All three agree.

Every pin name I could read off Fig. 2 matches
`02_parts/XU316-1024-TQ128-I24/part.yaml` exactly.

**Corollary — the paste stencil under the EP is correct and nobody said so.**
U1's footprint carries 130 pads, not 129: pad `129` is the 4.70 mm EP on
`F.Cu F.Mask` with **no F.Paste**, plus **nine unnumbered 1.2 × 1.2 mm
F.Paste-only windows** on a 1.5 mm grid. Paste coverage = 9 × 1.44 / 4.70² =
**58.7 %** — inside the normal 50–80 % band for an exposed pad, and the reason
the 16 in-pad thermal vias won't wick the joint dry. (ORDER_README §1a already
buys epoxy-filled + capped vias.)

# P1 — answer before ordering

## P1-1 — The 0V9 core rail is declared at 0.8 A. The SoC's own published maximum is 830 mA, and the converter is a 1 A part.

`03_src/rules/power_tree.yaml` declares `0V9_CORE: iout_max_A: 0.8`. The XU316
datasheet, **§15.7 Power Consumption, p. 37**, publishes for the **I24** grade
this board buys:

| symbol | typ | max |
|---|---|---|
| I(VDD) Active VDD current (C24, **I24**) | 225 mA | **830 mA** |
| I(VDD) (hs) — VDD current in hs mode | 6 mA | **9 mA** |
| I(PLL_AVDD) | 5 mA | (no max) |

PLL_AVDD is on this same rail — `L_pll` bridges `N0V9` → `PLL_AVDD` — so the
0V9 net's datasheet worst case is **830 + 9 + 5 = 844 mA**, and this board is a
USB **high-speed** device so the `(hs)` adder applies.

The AP61102Z6-7 is **"1 A Continuous Output Current"** (DS42004 Rev 6-2, p. 1).
844 mA is **84 % of the part's continuous rating**, against a declaration that
says 80 % of it is the ceiling.

What is *not* wrong, so the question is narrow:
- Thermally it is fine. Fig. 41 (p. 16) is flat at 1.0 A to **92 °C ambient**;
  θJA(SOT563) = 141 °C/W, Tj max 125 °C (p. 15). At ~0.16 W of IC loss the rise
  is ~22 °C.
- It will not fold back: IPEAK_LIMIT min 1.7 A, IVALLEY_LIMIT typ 1.9 A (p. 6)
  against a peak inductor current of ~1.02 A.
- L2 = 1 µH is exactly Table 1's value and gives ΔIL ≈ 0.34 A = 34 %, inside
  the datasheet's own 30–50 % window.

**Ask:** is 16 % margin on the core rail of a consigned SoC the accepted
posture, or should `iout_max_A` be restated at 0.85 A and the decision recorded?
E-TOPO grades LINEAR rails on dissipation but grades a BUCK rail on topology
only — **no gate on this board compares a switching rail's declared current to
its converter's rating**, which is why this reached v1.7 unremarked.

Related and cheap to fix: `01_docs/DETAIL_DESIGN.md` §"3V3 digital buck" says
*"Inductor + Cout per datasheet design table for 1.5A / ~1MHz"* and the ledger
key is `buck-1a5-sot563`. The AP61102 is a **1 A / 2.2 MHz** part. A 1.5 A
belief about a 1 A part is the belief that makes an 844 mA rail feel roomy.

## P1-2 — Q2 hard-switches an off-board INDUCTIVE load at 4 kHz with no freewheel path, and no pad for one.

`Q2` (AO3400A) sinks `BEEP_RETURN`, which leaves the board on RJ45 contact 6 of
every port, crosses ~25 ft of Cat5e, and returns through the pods' magnetic
calibration transducers to `+5V_BEEP`. From the tsx netlist, `BEEP_RETURN`
has exactly three kinds of node: J3–J10 pin 6, Q2 pin 3 (drain), and TP11.
**There is no clamp diode, no snubber, and no footprint for either.**

The design's entire stated justification for Q2 is `01_docs/BRIEF.md` log entry
**D1**: *"6×150mA=900mA peak through one AO3400A (rated 5.7A) is comfortable."*
That is a **continuous DC current check applied to a hard-switched inductive
load.** `02_parts/AO3400A/part.yaml` records `rdson`, `vgs_th`, `qg`, `tj_max`
— and **no θJA and no avalanche (EAS) rating**, so neither the switching
dissipation nor the avalanche energy can be checked from anything in this tree.

The arithmetic I can do, with assumptions stated:

- Gate network: 3.3 V GPIO → `R_bg1` 1 kΩ → node with `C_bg` 4.7 nF and
  `R_bg2` 100 kΩ. τ ≈ 1 kΩ × (4.7 nF + Ciss) ≈ **5.3 µs**; linear-region
  transit ≈ 5.6 µs per edge. Final Vgs = 3.3 × 100/101 = 3.27 V (fine —
  AO3400A is specified to Vgs = 2.5 V).
- Switching loss at 8 ports cabled (1.2 A): ½·5 V·1.2 A·5.6 µs × 2 edges ×
  4 kHz ≈ **0.13 W**, plus ~17 mW conduction. At a pessimistic θJA = 250 °C/W
  that is a ~38 °C rise. **Thermally this is not the problem.**
- **The problem is the turn-off voltage.** A 5 V / 150 mA magnetic transducer
  presents |Z| ≈ 33 Ω at 4 kHz; if its DC coil resistance is ~16 Ω the
  inductive part is L ≈ 1.15 mH, and 8 in parallel is ≈ 145 µH. Opening
  1.2 A in 5.6 µs implies L·di/dt ≈ **31 V** at Q2's drain. **AO3400A
  V(DS) max = 30 V.** The FET's own linear-region turn-off is the only thing
  holding this down, and if the drain does reach BVdss the part avalanches
  **8000 times a second** during every calibration burst, dissipating
  ≈ ½LI² = 104 µJ per edge ≈ **0.42 W** on top of the switching loss.

**Ask, before ordering:** (a) the CMT-8504-100-SMT-TR's coil L and R (that part
has no dossier in this tree — it is the pod board's part, but the switch that
must survive it is on **this** board); (b) whether the pod provides a local
clamp across the transducer. If neither is known, the honest reading is that
this board's one beeper FET is unprotected against its own load. A single
0603 Schottky from BEEP_RETURN to +5V_BEEP would close it and there is nowhere
to put one.

Note this is *not* the already-recorded beeper item: the v1.1 fresh-lens P2
(item 11) covers the **aggregate DC current** through FB_BEEP and F_IN. The
inductive turn-off is a different failure mode and is uncovered.

## P1-3 — The beeper burst modulates the same unregulated 5 V node that powers every microphone preamp, at 4 kHz, with ~zero delay.

Topology, from the tsx: `FB_BEEP` (BLM21SP601SN1D, 600 Ω @ **100 MHz**,
60 mΩ DCR) bridges `N5V` → `PLUS5V_BEEP` with `C_BEEP` 47 µF; each port's PTC
`F1..F8` bridges the *same* `N5V` → `P5VA_p`; and `U10` (the quiet analog LDO
feeding both ADCs' AVDD) takes its VIN from the *same* `N5V`. **There is no
bulk capacitance anywhere on `P5VA_p`.**

At 4 kHz the ferrite is ~65 mΩ and `C_BEEP`'s reactance is
1/(2π·4 kHz·47 µF) = **0.85 Ω** — neither isolates anything at the beeper's
fundamental. The 4 kHz square-wave current (0.9 A at 6 pods, 1.2 A at 8) is
drawn straight out of `N5V`, whose only on-board reservoir is
C_5V1 22 µF + Cin_U7 10 µF + Cin_U8 10 µF + Cin_U10 1 µF + C_BEEP 47 µF ≈
**90 µF**. Charge arithmetic for the half-cycle the brick cannot follow:
ΔV = I·(T/2)/C = 0.45 A × 125 µs / 90 µF ≈ **0.6 V p-p**, reduced to whatever
the GST25A05's own output capacitance and the DC cord contribute (with a
plausible 470 µF in the brick, ~100 mV p-p).

Downstream that lands in two places at once:
1. **On +5V_AUDIO**, i.e. directly on the pods' OPA1678 supply and electret
   bias — an in-band 4 kHz tone injected **electrically**, arriving with zero
   propagation delay, while the acoustic beep it is supposed to measure arrives
   7.6 m / 343 m·s⁻¹ ≈ 22 ms later. The beeper exists to measure inter-channel
   and inter-ADC skew; a common-mode, zero-delay electrical copy of the beep on
   every channel is precisely the artefact that would make a skew measurement
   read zero.
2. **On U10's VIN.** The XC6227 has PSRR 65 dB @ 1 kHz; at ~60 dB, 100 mV of
   4 kHz becomes ~100 µV on 3V3A, and because VREF = 0.5·AVDD this appears as
   ADC full-scale (gain) modulation of ~60 ppm rather than additive noise —
   sidebands around −84 dBc, present only during bursts. Tolerable, but it is
   the second-order term of the same root cause.

For completeness, the coupling the ARCHITECTURE worries about is **not** the
problem: the ground-plane return does spread across the analog band (Q2 sits at
board (172, 46), the 5 V inlet J1 at (24, 102), U2/U3 at (62, 62)/(128, 62) —
so the return traverses the whole board, contradicting ARCHITECTURE.md's claim
that it "stays ENTIRELY in the north port band"), but with In1+In4 solid 1 oz
planes that is ~1.5 squares ⇒ ~0.4 mΩ ⇒ **~0.34 mV** at 0.9 A, rejected
further by the PCM1865's 56 dB differential CMRR. **The plane is fine; the 5 V
rail is the coupling path.**

**Ask:** the measured `N5V` ripple during a burst, at the +5V_AUDIO tap. If it
is not small, the fix is board-side and cheap in the next revision (bulk on
PLUS5V_BEEP so the burst current comes out of a local cap, and/or per-port bulk
on P5VA), and firmware-side free right now (never record while beeping).

## P1-4 — Both PCM1865s are decoupled below their own datasheet's Pin Functions table on three of five supply pins.

TI **SLAS831D, "Pin Functions: PCM1862, PCM1863, PCM1864, and PCM1865", p. 11**
states per pin, verbatim:

| pin | datasheet requires | board has (U2 / U3) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 AVDD | "Connect 0.1-µF **and 10-µF** capacitors" | C_avdd2b 100 nF + C_avdd2a 10 µF | ✓ |
| 6 VREF | "Connect 1-µF capacitor" | C_vref2b 100 nF + C_vref2a 10 µF | ✓ exceeds |
| **11 LDO** | "Connect 0.1-µF **and 10-µF** capacitors from this pin to DGND" | **C_ldo2 1 µF only** | ✗ |
| **13 DVDD** | "Connect 0.1-µF **and 10-µF** capacitors from this pin to DGND" | **C_dvdd2 100 nF only** | ✗ |
| **14 IOVDD** | (Table 25, p. 79: source for the internal LDO) | **C_iovdd2 100 nF only** | ✗ |

Pin 11 is not a spare decoupling point — per **Table 25, p. 79** and
**Fig. 68 "PCM186x Power Distribution Tree", p. 79**, LDO is the output of the
on-chip 1.8 V regulator that powers **the entire digital core (DSP, logic,
clock-halt detect)**. It is running with 1 µF where TI asks for 10.1 µF.
Pin 13 DVDD is described as *"the digital voltage supply that is used for the
**PLL and the oscillator circuit**"* — the ADC's own clock generation — and
carries no bulk at all; the nearest 10 µF on 3V3 (`C_b3v3`) is at U1, ~40 mm
away.

This is a product-function finding, not a cosmetic one: under-decoupling the
ADC's internal digital rail and its PLL supply is a direct SNR/THD and jitter
mechanism on an 8-channel recorder. It is also the cheapest thing in this
report to fix — 4 × 10 µF 0805 and 2 × 100 nF, all values already on the BOM.

## P1-5 — `02_parts/TLV70018DDCR/part.yaml` cites a datasheet that does not exist, and the one number it records is conditioned on a voltage this rail never sees.

The file records `datasheet: SBVS205` in two places — `layout.source:` ("TI
SBVS205 sec 10 … fig 10-1") and `verified:` ("TI TLV700 datasheet SBVS205, pin
functions table 5-1 + DDC package pinout figure (p.3)") — and one rating,
`dropout: "250 mV @200mA"`, which `03_src/rules/power_tree.yaml` then quotes as
"the TI part's published 250 mV @ 200 mA (SBVS205 …)".

**`ti.com/lit/pdf/sbvs205` returns 404. The TLV70018DDCR's datasheet is
SLVSA00E** ("TLV700 200-mA, Low-IQ, Low-Dropout Regulator for Portable
Devices", Sept 2009, rev. April 2015). In SLVSA00E §6.5, p. 6, dropout at
200 mA is 175 mV typ / **250 mV max** — the number is right — but **footnote (1)
on that same table reads "VDO is measured for devices with VOUT(nom) ≥ 2.35 V".
There is no dropout specification for the 1.8 V option at all.** So the value
is correct-by-luck, attributed to a document that cannot be opened, and carries
a condition the file does not state.

This is the one part in `02_parts/` with **no local PDF**, which is exactly why
nothing caught it. It is the same class of defect the tree just paid off for
XC6227 ("PAGE ANCHORS CORRECTED 2026-07-27 … a citation that does not resolve
is a defect") — applied to a doc ID rather than a page number.

**But the audit's actual question has a clean answer, and it is not a P0.**
SLVSA00E §6.4 Thermal Information, p. 5, gives **RθJA(DDC) = 235.9 °C/W** (a
2-layer 2 oz EVM number, not JESD51-7; the Q1 sibling SLVSB67D §5.4 gives
262.8 °C/W for the identical package). The rail's real load is derived, not
assumed: `N1V8` has exactly two active sinks — U1.35/56 (VDDIOB18) and, through
FB_u18, U1.62 (USB_VDD18) — and XU316 §15.7 p. 37 gives
**I(USB_VDD18)(hs) max = 36 mA**; VDDIOB18 drives only the crystal, JTAG (on an
unstuffed header) and RST_N, ≤ 10 mA. So:

| load | P = 1.5 V × I | ΔT @ 235.9 °C/W | Tj @ 70 °C | Tj @ 85 °C (I24 Ta max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **46 mA (as built)** | 69 mW | 16 °C | **86 °C** | **101 °C** |
| 126 mA (VDDIOB18 AMR, absurd) | 189 mW | 45 °C | 115 °C | 130 °C ✗ |

Envelope exit is at (125 − 70)/(235.9 × 1.5) = **155 mA at 70 °C ambient**, and
113 mA at 85 °C. The fitted part holds the rail with **~3× margin**. Also
checked: TLV700 needs no minimum load (§6.3, IOUT min = 0 mA), the 1 µF
CL05A105KA5NQNC clears the 0.1 µF *effective* stability floor by ~6× after
derating (§8.2.2.1, p. 13), max ESR 200 mΩ is met by ceramic, and pin 3 (EN)
tied to IN is sanctioned (VEN abs max = VIN + 0.3 V).

Two things worth recording while the file is open: SLVSA00E p. 4 notes pin 4
(NC) *"can be tied to ground to improve thermal dissipation"* — it is floating
here, a free improvement; and current limit is **220 mA min / 860 mA max**,
only 20 mA above the 200 mA rating, so nothing on this rail should ever be
sized assuming inrush headroom.

# P2 — next revision

**P2-6 — L1 = 2.2 µH on the 3V3 buck where the datasheet's table says 1 µH,
and it pushes the same direction as the missing C3.** DS42004 Rev 6-2 Table 1
(p. 16) specifies **L = 1 µH at every listed output voltage**, and §10 (p. 17)
says *"choose ΔIL to be 30 % to 50 % of the maximum load current of 1 A"*. With
L1 = 2.2 µH at VIN 5 V / VOUT 3.3 V / 2.2 MHz,
ΔIL = 3.3 × 1.7 / (5 × 2.2 µH × 2.2 MHz) = **0.23 A = 23 %** — below the
datasheet's own floor. Cout is 22 µF against Table 1's 10 µF (allowed: §12
says 10–22 µF). All three deviations reduce the FB ripple a constant-on-time
loop needs, on the rail that has no C3. L2 = 1 µH on the 0V9 rail is per table
(ΔIL = 34 %). `03_src/rules/electrical_invariants.yaml` pins R1/R2 to Table 1
but not L or C3 — the invariant reads two of the table's five columns.

**P2-7 — ARCHITECTURE.md's ground strategy is not what was built, and the
as-built is the better one.** `01_docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §"Ground strategy"
says *"Digital GND (GND) + analog GND (GND_A) joined at a single point / star at
the ADC AVSS region"*, and `02_parts/RJHSE-5384/part.yaml` names `GND_AUDIO` on
contacts 5 and 8. **The netlist has one GND net; `GND_A`/`GND_AUDIO` do not
exist.** The single plane is *correct* — SLAS831D §12.1.1, p. 84: *"Use the
same plane for analog and digital grounds to avoid any potential voltage
difference… On the PCM186x EVM, maximum SNR performance is achieved by using a
single ground plane"* — so this is a documentation defect only. Fix the doc to
say what was done and why, and drop the star-ground language; leaving it in
invites a future revision to "restore" a split plane the ADC vendor argues
against.

**P2-8 — Pin the flash to C131025.** `fab/bom.csv` line 48 pins **C82317**.
JLCPCB's own parts API returns `isBuyComponent: 0`, `noBuyReason: "This
product is no longer manufacture"`, and `componentAlternativesCode: C131025`
for it. Both codes carry the byte-identical MPN `W25Q16JVSSIQ`, Winbond,
SOIC-8-208mil, with a character-identical `describe` string; the only
difference is packaging (C82317 = tube of 90, `encapsulationNumber: 90`;
C131025 = reel of 2000) and stock (9,873 vs 72,886). **The team's ACCEPT
adjudication is correct and I verified it independently** — same `SS` package
code (SOIC-8 208-mil), same `IQ` suffix, same device ID 4015h, W25Q16JV
Rev D/F §11 Ordering Information. But C82317 will silently re-resolve on
**every** future run. Pinning the buyable SKU removes the substitution event
instead of re-adjudicating it each order.

**P2-9 — the crystal actually ordered has no dossier.** `fab/bom.csv` line 19
ships **NX3225SA-24MHZ-EXS00A-CS08583 (NDK, C2762192)** in a footprint named
`Crystal_SMD_SeikoEpson_FA238-4Pin_3.2x2.5mm`, but `02_parts/` holds only the
**FA-238** dossier and PDF. The part actually fitted has **no `part.yaml`, no
datasheet, and therefore no verified CL, ESR or drive level** — in the one net
that sets USB timing and every audio sample clock. XU316 part.yaml records the
vendor requirement as *"24 MHz crystal, ±500 ppm or better, 12 pF load,
ESR ≤ 60 Ω… CL1 = CL2 = 22 pF (datasheet lists FA-238 exactly)"*; the board runs
a **9 pF-load** crystal with **12 pF** caps. That self-consistency is fine on
its own terms — 2 × (9 − 3 pF stray) = 12 pF — and even a full 2 pF error in
the stray estimate is only ≈ 56 ppm of frequency pull (C1 ≈ 5 fF, C0 ≈ 1.5 pF),
well inside ±500 ppm. **The gap is provenance, not arithmetic**: nothing in the
tree proves the fitted crystal's ESR is ≤ 60 Ω or that Rd = 680 Ω holds its
drive level within spec.

**P2-10 — the input RC is an EMI filter, not an anti-alias filter, and it is
differential-only.** `01_docs/DETAIL_DESIGN.md` calls the 100 Ω + 1 nF cell
"RC anti-alias/EMI". With R in *both* legs the differential corner is
1/(2π · 200 Ω · 1 nF) = **796 kHz**, ~33× above the PCM1865's 24 kHz Nyquist —
it provides essentially no anti-aliasing (which is fine; the part is a
delta-sigma and does its own). The substantive point is that `Cd` is the
**only** capacitor in the cell and it sits differentially: there is **no
common-mode path to ground** on either leg, so RF picked up on 25 ft of
unshielded outdoor Cat5e reaches the PGA inputs as common mode with only the
TPD2E2U06's ~1.5 pF in the way. Two small caps to GND per channel (or splitting
Cd into a differential + two common-mode legs) is the standard closure. Rename
the cell in the docs either way.

# Verified clean — what I checked, and how

Recorded so the next reader knows what has and has not been looked at.

**Boot chain (the deepest thing I went after, and it is right).** XU316 §9
Boot Procedure, p. 20: boot source is X0D06/X0D05/X0D04, *"sampled shortly
after reset with the internal weak pull-downs enabled"*, 000 = QSPI flash.
Those nets carry no external pull-up (`R_cs` 10 k is on X0D01/CS_N, which is
not a strap). The flash cannot fight them either: **W25Q16JV §6.4 note 3 —
the only internal pull-up in the part is on the dedicated /RESET pin, which
exists only on SOIC-16 and TFBGA**; on SOIC-8 pins 3 and 7 are plain high-Z
(ILI/ILO ±2 µA max) and are driven only after /CS falls, i.e. strictly after
strap sampling. §9.1 requires the flash be *"in its power-up state, where
QSPI-mode accesses will succeed"* for its 0xEB Quad I/O READ — the **`IQ`
suffix is factory-fixed QE = 1** (W25Q16JV §7, Status Register-2 S9), so quad
mode is live at power-up, and the W25Q16JV die has **no QPI mode at all**, so
it cannot be left stuck in one. tVSL = 20 µs ≪ the 300 µs budget §9.1 allows.
Pin map 1:/CS 2:DO 3:/WP 4:GND 5:DI 6:CLK 7:/HOLD 8:VCC matches the tsx exactly,
and X0D01/04/05/06/07/10 are all on VDDIOL = 3V3, matching the flash's rail.

**Power sequencing, both directions.** XU316 §14, p. 30: *"for systems with a
3.3 V VDDIO supply, while the VDDIOB18 supply is off, the 3.3 V supply should
not rise above 1.98 V **unless the VDD supply is off***. On this board U8's EN
is U7's PG, and AP61102's PG is *"actively held low during the soft-start
period until the output voltage reaches 95 %"* with a 55 µs rising delay and
0.5 ms tSS (DS42004 p. 6, p. 19) — so **VDD is off for the whole window in
which 3V3 crosses 1.98 V with 1V8 not yet valid**, and the AMR condition never
bites. U9 (1V8) is fed from 3V3 with EN tied to its own VIN, so it is up
sub-millisecond, i.e. **VDDIOB18 is not last** — the datasheet's own
recommended remedy. VDDIOL/R/T are valid long before RST_N releases
(R_rst 10 k × C_rst 100 nF ≈ 1 ms after 1V8). On power-down 3V3 falls before
1V8 can matter, and the LDO's body diode lets 1V8 track it. **Correct.**

**Pin maps against the datasheet figure (canon S-VER).** XU316 Fig. 2 p. 7 read
directly (above). PCM1865 against SLAS831D's *PCM1862/63/64/65* table, p. 11 —
note the datasheet carries **two** pin tables and the other one (PCM1860/61) has
different functions on pins 19–26, so this is the mirror-number trap in a
different costume; the tsx uses the right one. Differential pairs confirmed
CH1 P=3/M=1, CH2 P=4/M=2, CH3 P=30/M=28, CH4 P=29/M=27 — all four match.
I²C straps: U2 pin 25 (MS/AD) → GND = 0x4A, U3 pin 25 → N3V3 (IOVDD) = 0x4B,
MD0 (26) → GND on both = I²C mode. **The clock trap is respected**: MCLK lands
on SCKI (15), XI (10) is tied to DGND, XO (9) open. No address collision with
SHT40 at 0x44. Also checked and correct: NC7NZ34 (1A/3Y/2A/GND/2Y/3A/1Y/VCC,
Fig. 2 p. 2), SHT40 (1 SDA / 2 SCL / 3 VDD / 4 VSS, no central pad soldered —
and the footprint chosen is the `NoCentralPad` variant), TPD2E2U06 (3 = IO1,
5 = IO2, 4 = GND, 1/2 floating is datasheet-sanctioned), XC6227 (1 CE / 2 VSS /
3 NC / 4 VIN / 5 VOUT, CE = 5 V against a 1.2 V threshold), TLV70018
(1 IN / 2 GND / 3 EN / 4 NC / 5 OUT).

**The audio input topology, including the TODO the DETAIL_DESIGN left open.**
`01_docs/DETAIL_DESIGN.md` still says *"exact input coupling (AC vs DC, VCM
bias) to be finalized against the PCM1865 datasheet's line-input application
circuit at authoring"*. It is finalized and it is right: **SLAS831D p. 28 —
*"DC blocking capacitors are required on the analog inputs"* and *"the input
pins are designed to bias to AVDD/2"***. So the AC-coupled node is internally
self-biased and the 2.2 µF series caps are mandatory, not optional. High-pass
corner with the PCM1865's **10 kΩ per-pin input impedance** (p. 13; note the
1865 is 10 kΩ where the 1860–63 are 20 kΩ) is
1/(2π · 10.1 kΩ · 2.2 µF) = **7.2 Hz** per leg. Full scale is **4.2 Vrms
differential** (p. 13), against a pod delivering ~3 V/V from a −24 dBV/Pa
electret — ≈ 47 mVrms differential from an 82 dB SPL caw at 7.6 m, i.e. −39 dBFS
with 106 dB of SNR underneath and up to +32 dB of on-chip PGA available in
differential mode. Gain staging is sound and the remaining choice is firmware.

**The 13 off-CPL designators, one at a time.** All are declared with evidence
in `03_src/rules/assembly.yaml` and A-POP passes. Checked what the audit asked:
**none is a pull-up, strap, or boot-mode selector.** J1 + J3–J10 are THT
hand-solder (populated at integration, just not by JLC — J1 is the *only* power
inlet and correctly leads the hand-solder list). J_DBG unstuffed leaves
TDI/TMS/TCK/TDO floating, which is safe because all four carry internal pulls
(XU316 §4: TDI PU, TMS PU, TCK PD) and TDO is an output. JP_INJ + R_inj1/R_inj2
DNP is not merely safe but **required**: populated, they tie ADC1P to ADC5P
through 2 kΩ, which against the ~99 Ω pod source is −26.5 dB of ch1→ch5
crosstalk on a product whose whole point is inter-channel isolation. I derived
that number independently and it matches ORDER_README's to 0.1 dB.

**Input protection orientation.** Q1 (AO3401A P-FET RPP): S = N5V (load side),
D = VIN_RAW (supply side), G = GND via RG1 10 k — body diode forward in the
normal direction, Vgs = −5 V against a ±12 V limit, off under reversal.
**Correct.** D1's cathode is on VIN_RAW and anode on GND in the netlist —
correct for a unidirectional TVS; only its *physical* rotation is unverifiable
(§0b). The TVS sits before the FET, which is where it protects it. F_IN is
upstream of both.

**G14 silk.** Eight per-jack `NOT ETH 5V!` labels at x = 25/45/65/85/105/125/
145/165, one full `NOT ETHERNET - CUSTOM 5V AUDIO PINOUT` banner at (95, 33),
and a pinout legend `1,2=AUDIO+/- 3,6=+5VBEEP/RTN 4,5&7,8=+5VAUD/GND` at
(30, 43). The per-port string is abbreviated rather than the brief's verbatim
sentence, which is the only way it fits at a 20 mm jack pitch. **Satisfied.**
