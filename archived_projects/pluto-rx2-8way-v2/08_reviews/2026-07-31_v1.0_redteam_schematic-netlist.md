## 3. THE CENTRAL QUESTION — label direction, measured in ink

**MEASURED, and it passes.** Method deliberately avoids the `justify` field: the
plate's body direction is taken from the centroid of the **6-point closed polygon
KiCad's own renderer emitted**, relative to the anchor. All 44 plates were located
in ink (44 six-point closed polygons found; 44 labels matched, 0 unplaced).

| class | n | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ANTI-PARALLEL (plate opposite its conductor — correct, discriminating) | **32** | worst dot = −1.000 on every one |
| PERPENDICULAR (sense test cannot discriminate) | **12** | declared vacuity |
| **SAME-SENSE (plate drawn over its own conductor — the defect)** | **0** | — |
| no plate in ink | **0** | — |

**Falsification control — the instrument can fail.** Mirroring every plate about
its anchor (the `948ef54d` defect) turns **36 of 44 into SAME-SENSE**. A test that
flips 36 is not vacuous.

**The 12 perpendicular labels are closed, not waived.** Where the sense test is
blind I ran a second, independent reading — **attribution**: intersect the plate
rectangle against every wire segment and every pin of a *different* net. A plate
lying over a foreign conductor is read as naming that conductor, which is the
actual "wrong pin" failure, and it discriminates on perpendicular labels.

    perpendicular labels: 12
    cleared by the attribution test (no foreign conductor under the plate): 12 of 12

The 12 are `3V3`, `3V3_MOD` ×2, `ANT5`, `RX1_MAIN`, `SEL_V1`, `SEL_V3`, `SEL_V4`,
`SW_V1`, `SW_V2`, `SW_V3`, `SW_V4`. Every net reaching an RF port or a power rail
is therefore direction-proven by one test or the other. The only labels the
attribution test flags are the three in finding 2 — and those are anti-parallel,
i.e. correctly oriented and lying across someone else's wire.

This reproduces the prior agent's 32 / 12 / 36 split exactly, by a different
construction (INHERITED claim, now independently MEASURED), and additionally
closes the 12 they left declared-vacuous.

**Why finding 1 blocks.** It is not copper, and I have said so plainly. It blocks
because of *when*: `07_releases/` is immutable from creation, the schematic is the
human-facing artifact a person debugs an RF control plane with, and the sheet is

Both need a RED-verified known-bad fixture — this sheet at md5 `a010edf8` is one,
ready-made: 6 cross-net T-events, 1 × 4.4450 mm overlap, 3 plate-over-wire hits.
