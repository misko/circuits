## Scope and method

Everything below is measured from the staged artifacts, not read out of the
design documents. The netlist (`source/pluto_rx2_8way_v2.net`) was parsed to a
node table and every topology/protection claim traced against it. Copper
numbers come from `pcbnew` over `source/pluto_rx2_8way_v2.kicad_pcb`. The
shipped fence instrument (`verification/fence_pitch.py`) was RE-RUN at two
bands the release did not use, to test whether its FAIL is an artifact.

## E-MARGIN — the 3V3 rail, computed rather than asserted

One regulated rail, one known load. `power_tree.yaml` declares
`vout_min 3.234` (RT9013-33, ±2 %), `load_uv_threshold 2.3`
(PE42482A-X VDD operating min, Table 2 PDF p3), `iout_max_A 0.10`,
`ir_budget_mohm 100`.

| term | value |
|---|---|
| headroom = Vout_min − Vbrownout | 3.234 − 2.300 = **0.934 V** |
| total IR budget at the gate's declared Imax (0.100 A) | **9340 mΩ** |
| total IR budget at the load's REAL Imax (200 µA, Table 2) | **4670 Ω** |
| **cable resistance in this path** | **0 mΩ — there is no cable.** The netlist has NO connector on any power net; the board's only power input is `U_MCU` pad 21 |
| **connector resistance in this path** | **0 mΩ — there is no connector.** One hand-soldered castellation, ~5 mΩ with the reflow joints |
| `3V3_MOD` copper, MEASURED | 5.0342 mm @ 0.400 mm = 12.6 sq × 0.4926 mΩ/sq = **6.2 mΩ** |
| `FB_3V3` DCR (Murata max, PDF p2) | **60.0 mΩ** |
| `3V3` copper, MEASURED | 47.3255 mm @ 0.400 mm = 118.3 sq = **58.3 mΩ** |
| 2 vias on `3V3` | **2.3 mΩ** |
| joints | **5 mΩ** |
| **TOTAL, as built** | **131.8 mΩ** |

**Judgment: E-MARGIN is MET with 71× of margin** on the gate's deliberately
conservative arrangement (whole rail charged across our copper: drop
13.2 mV of a 934 mV headroom, 1.41 % utilisation), and with ~35 000× on the
current that actually crosses this copper (200 µA → **26.4 µV**). This is not
a close call in any corner, and the absence of a cable and a connector is the
real reason.

**But the declared number is wrong against the as-built copper (P2-1).**
`power_tree.yaml` itemises "~20 mm of 0.4 mm trace ~25 mΩ" → "~90 mΩ →
declared 100". Measured, the `3V3` net alone is **47.33 mm** and the
`FB_3V3.2 → U_SW.8` pad-to-pad straight line is **41.34 mm**. The itemisation
understates the trace term by **2.3×** and the total by **32 %**. That field's
own comment says it must be "THE ACTUAL SERIES RESISTANCE of the delivery
path, not a ceiling", so the number is wrong on its own terms even though the
verdict does not move.

### The rail's real binding constraint is thermal, and it is on a part we do not own

`(Vin_max − Vout_min) × Iout` at the module's own worst case (WS2812 full
white, ~110 mA per the dossier) = **222 mW**. `power_tree.yaml` allows 400 mW
(Tj 125 °C / 250 °C·W⁻¹ / TA 25 °C); `02_parts/RP2040-Zero/part.yaml` allows
500 mW from the *same* part using Tj 150 °C, and 320 mW at TA 70 °C. Two
different Tj_max for one regulator in one board (P2-2). The 70 °C case at
power_tree's Tj is 220 mW, i.e. **222 mW would be over** — power_tree already
says the rail fails first at high ambient and says it fails on the module, not
on our copper. That is honestly stated; it is the arithmetic that is
inconsistent between the two files.

## E-OFF — declared, not netlist-verifiable, and correctly N-A

`power_tree.yaml`: `source_type: usb_bus_powered_5v`, `off_control: unplug`,
`quiescent_ua: 0`.

**The declared `off_control` is NOT in the netlist, and cannot be.** There is no
connector, no switch, no EN pin and no load switch anywhere in
`source/pluto_rx2_8way_v2.net` — the only power path is `U_MCU` pad 21
(`3V3_MOD`), and pad 23 (`5V` = VBUS) is a no-connect. The thing that gets
unplugged is the module's own USB-C, which is not a board part.

**Judgment: the N-A is CORRECT and the declaration is the right way to state
it.** Total stored energy on the board is C_BULK 4.7 µF + C_SW2 1 µF +
C_SW1 100 nF = 5.8 µF, or **31.6 µJ** at 3.3 V. There is nothing to
de-energize and nothing to gate. The `usb-hub-3s-v3` failure this field exists
to catch (both buck ENs strapped active, pack self-drained in storage) has no
analogue here. `quiescent_ua: 0` is true of the board in storage, which is what
the field means.

## Clamp-vs-protected-part rating pairs

**There is no clamp anywhere on this board.** No TVS, no ESD array, no PPTC, no
DC block, no reverse-polarity element. That is a recorded decision
(ADR-0002 §3) and the reasoning about the RF cost is sound. What is missing is
the other half of the pair.

| protected part | rating | clamp | source of the rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| PE42482A-X RF ports (RF1–RF8, RFC) | **ESD HBM 1000 V, CDM 1000 V, all pins** | none | Table 1, PDF p2 — **read by me from the datasheet, because `02_parts/PE42482A-X/part.yaml` `limits:` has no ESD entry at all** |
| PE42482A-X RF ports, DC | **v_rfdc_max 0 V** (Table 8 fn 1) | no DC block on any of 10 ports | dossier records it, and records the port-DC question as OWED |
| PE42482A-X V1–V4 | **digital abs-max 3.6 V** vs VDD abs-max 5.5 V | 47 Ω source termination | Table 1, PDF p2 |
| PE42482A-X VDD | 5.5 V abs-max / 2.3–5.5 V op | none needed — source is a 3.3 V ±2 % LDO output | Table 2 |

Recomputed at the worst corner the release itself declares:

* **Control-line overshoot.** `V_peak = 2·Vdd·Z_line/(Z_line + Z_drv + R_S)`.
  DETAIL_DESIGN uses Vdd = 3.3 → 3.181 V. At the rail's own declared HIGH
  corner (`vout_max 3.366`) it is **3.245 V**, leaving **355 mV (9.9 %)** to
  the 3.6 V abs-max. The margin reaches **zero at Z_drv = 11.3 Ω**, and
  DETAIL_DESIGN states plainly that Z_drv ≈ 25 Ω is *back-solved from v1's
  published figure and not sourced from the RP2040 datasheet*. So the one
  un-sourced number in the protection chain is the one the abs-max rests on
  (P2-6). Mitigating, and it is real: the SW_V* lines run 32–40 mm pad-to-pad
  with 1.7–8.6 mm of that on In2.Cu (lower Z0 than 67 Ω), so the lumped
  doubling model is conservative, and `Z_drv + R_S = 72 Ω` against a 67 Ω line
  is a good source match.
* **Cost of the clamp that was refused.** A 0.1 pF shunt on a 50 Ω line at
  6 GHz is `b = 0.1885` → **RL 20.55 dB, IL 0.038 dB**. Against a part whose
  own RFC-port return loss is 13–24 dB *typ with no min column*, that is a
  defensible refusal — but ADR-0002 makes it in one sentence with no
  arithmetic and without naming the 1 kV HBM the ports are being left on.
  Eight of the ten SMA ports (ANT1–ANT7, RX2_OUT) are bare copper from an
  operator-touchable connector to an RF pad with **no series element**; only
  RF8 has 440 Ω in front of it. That is normal practice for an RF switch
  carrier, and it is also the single most likely field failure on a
  ten-connector instrument that gets re-cabled every session. The finding is
  that the rating pair is *incomplete in the record*, not that the decision is
  wrong (P2-4).

## The RF property, graded against the release's own measured evidence

The board's published property is per-path phase/loss deltas over a radial
star. Two of its three declared bounds are met and one is failed.

**MET — arm length match.** Measured spread **0.5314 mm = 6.985°** at 6 GHz
against `max_spread_mm: 1.0` (= 13.2°, the part's own part-to-part window).
`no_vias: true` holds absolutely: 0 vias on all eight members. The eight arms
are 13.835–14.366 mm, so "equal-length arms" in ARCHITECTURE §7 is not
literally true — 53 % of the whole budget is spent on static mismatch before
drift gets any — but the declared bound is met and the residual is
manufacturing-repeatable, so the deltas stay CONSTANT, which is what G7 asks.

**FAILED — the via fence.** `verification/fence_pitch.txt` ships
`VERDICT: FAIL`: **12 of 21 arm-sides OVER**, worst interior aperture
**5.1071 mm** against a declared **1.35 mm** (`λg/20 = 1.3693` at eps_eff
3.3286). That is **3.78× the bound**, or **λg/5.36**.

I re-ran the shipped instrument at bands the release did not use, to test the
obvious excuse — that the ±2.5 mm band excludes each SMA's four ground posts,
which stand at exactly 2.540 mm from the arm centreline (`nets.yaml`):

| band | arm-sides OVER | worst aperture |
|---|---|---|
| ±2.5 mm (as shipped) | 12 / 21 | 5.1071 mm |
| ±2.6 mm | 12 / 21 | 5.1071 mm |
| ±3.0 mm | 12 / 21 | 5.1071 mm |

**The FAIL is not a band artifact.** Individual arms improve (ANT1 E
1.3435→0.9455, ANT7 W 1.3435→0.6718, ANT4 W 3.6200→1.9000 at ±3.0), but the
count and the worst case do not move.

**Is the gap acceptable?** My judgment: acceptable for a FIRST ARTICLE, and
only with the bench characterisation ARCHITECTURE §10 already owes. Reasoning,
with the numbers:

* A 5.1071 mm aperture reaches λg/2 at **16.09 GHz**, 2.7× above the band
  ceiling. There is **no in-band slot resonance**; the failure mode is graded
  leakage and arm-to-arm coupling, not a notch.
* The worst site is **`RX1_MAIN` east, s = 7.15–12.26 mm** — the RX1
  straight-through path, which is the channel every published phase delta is
  referenced against. That is the electrically worst place for it, and it is
  why the phase table must not be published from simulation.
* `verification/fence_apertures.txt` classifies every one of the twelve as a
  named site OCCUPANCY (SMA antipad `avoid` rings, the SSE control corridor,
  the star hub), not as a pitch that could be fixed by a finer lattice. That
  classification is correct and is the honest form of the finding.

**But ARCHITECTURE §6's headline sentence must not be read as met**: "A
ground-via fence flanks every RF arm at ≤ 1.35 mm" is false of the shipped
board on 12 of 21 arm-sides, and the qualifying paragraph that follows is the
only thing that makes the section truthful.

**NEW, and not in any release document — the arms are not electromagnetically
congruent.** `nets.yaml`'s `scoped_clearances` justification for not modelling
coplanar loading ends: *"a coplanar ground would pull Z0 down if it ran
alongside the line — **it does not**."* Measured, edge-to-edge on F.Cu:

| arm | closest DC/control copper | length within 0.5 mm | within 1.0 mm | within 2.0 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ANT5 | **0.265 mm** (`SW_V4`) | **1.936 mm** | 3.166 mm | 9.736 mm |
| ANT4 | **0.770 mm** (`3V3`) | 0.000 mm | **6.834 mm** | **14.366 mm (100 %)** |
| ANT6 | 1.397 mm (`SW_V4`) | 0 | 0 | 1.636 mm |
| ANT1, ANT2, ANT3, ANT7, RX2_OUT, RX1_MAIN, RX1_TAP | — | 0 | 0 | **0.000 mm** |

`g/h` against the 0.2104 mm top prepreg is **1.26** for ANT5/SW_V4 and 3.66
for ANT4/3V3. Six of the ten RF nets have nothing within 2 mm; two have an
aggressor for 13 % and 48 % of their length. The clause quoted above is
falsified by the board it describes. The phase consequence is small — a few
tenths of a degree over a 1.9–6.8 mm perturbed section against a 13.2°
budget — and the perturbation is geometric, so it is repeatable unit to unit
and does not break CONSTANCY. The defect is the **false statement in the
rules file that is the source of truth for the RF class** (P1-3).

## Verdict rationale

**`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE`** — on the two P0s, both of which are cheap
right now and expensive after the seal. Neither is an electrical defect: the
netlist, the protection topology and the ratings are sound, and the arm
phase-match, the LS ground, the EP stitching, the pull-down placement and the
DRC/parity results all hold under independent measurement. What is defective is
the **record**: the release ships no MANIFEST and no ORDER_README (and its own
A-POP gate says so, in the archive, in a FAIL line), and the governing ADR
plus half of ARCHITECTURE declare a population posture that is the opposite of
what is built. A board whose accepted decision record says "ship JLC modules to
place" while its CPL omits the part will be ordered wrong by the next person to
read it.

**`order_verdict: DO-NOT-ORDER`** — the CPL is marked NOT ORDERABLE by the
exporter's own A-ROT gate, and the three human gates that would otherwise
carry the order (through-hole process selection, hand-solder instruction,
rotation confirmation) have no ORDER_README to sit on. **This is NOT a sourcing
block**: `verification/stock_check.json` reports `verdict: PASS`, 11/11 coded
BOM lines clear 5 × their build quantity, tightest 1294 units against a qty-1
line — so `BLOCKED-SOURCING` would be a false statement about this release, and
the A-ROT wall is bookkeeping (three measured rows that exist in
`twin_report.csv` and are missing from a table under `skills/`), not scarcity.
Land the two P0s, add the three rotation rows with the C2286 trap disarmed, and
this becomes an ORDER.
