## What I VERIFIED (measured, first-hand)

**The C1/C2 polarity conclusion is CORRECT. CPL 90.0 is right; v1.4's 270.0 was
180° reversed.** Five independent confirmations, none of which reuses the tool
that produced the defect:

1. **JLC's own silk glyphs.** `06_build/twin/easyeda/C2982822/jlc.pretty/CAP-SMD_BD6.3-L6.5-W6.5-LS7.2-FD.kicad_mod`, read directly: two filled `fp_poly` bars sharing centre **(−2.7060, 1.5181)** — one horizontal (x −3.1020…−2.3100), one vertical (x −2.8050…−2.6070, y 0.8581…2.1781) — draw a **crossed "+" over pad 1** (at x = −2.67). A lone horizontal bar at centre **(+2.7060, 1.5181)** draws a **"−" over pad 2** (x = +2.67). JLC's library says **pad 1 = positive**.
2. **The netlist.** `source/usb_hub_3s_v2.net`: `C1.1 [POS_1] → VIN`, `C1.2 [NEG_2] → GND`; same for C2. Our pad 1 is the positive terminal.
3. **The board's own silk.** pcbnew: C1 carries two crossed F.Silkscreen shapes at ≈(24.65, 64.05) — a drawn "+" — which is on the pad-1 side (pad 1 at y 62.70, pad 2 at y 57.30). Board silk, JLC model silk and the net all agree.
4. **The rotation frame, derived from absolute copper.** C1 footprint origin (26.500, 60.000) orient 90; pad 1 (VIN) lands at **(26.500, 62.700)**, i.e. −Y in the CPL's Y-up frame (south). JLC's model has pad 1 natively at −X (west). West + **90° CCW** = south. **Required CPL rotation = 90.0.** At 270.0 the "+" would point north — the "+" glyph landing on the GND pad. Confirmed reversed.
5. **`02_parts/KNM2100UF35V149EC0055/part.yaml`**: `type: polymer_capacitor`, `pins.1: {name: "+"}`. It is genuinely polarized; the venting argument is not rhetorical.

**J1 and Q7 are also correct.**
