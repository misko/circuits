## What was confirmed, and how

**The changed item.** Two instruments that share no code (canon M1): a
purpose-written s-expression tokeniser over the raw `.kicad_mod` text compared
against hand-retyped drawing numbers, and `pcbnew.FootprintLoad` plus KiCad's own
shape engine. 127 checks, 0 failures. The gate was then made to FAIL twice on
demand — against the v1.11 footprint as sealed, and against the ⌀0.65 variant.

**The blast radius**, four ways: a pcbnew-level classification (Edge_Cuts
identical, 0 footprints moved, one footprint's pads changed, 0 tracks and 0 vias
outside the J5 corner); a `SHAPE_POLY_SET` XOR of the saved filled polygons
(28.1019 mm² moved, 0.000969 mm² of it outside the J5 corner and that remainder
zone-filler slivers tens of microns across); a gerber-text census; and a
before/after render pair of the J5 corner from the same plot command.

**The electricals did not move**: netlist parity 0 by three tools (shipped `.net`
vs v1.11's sealed `.net` — 122 components, 73 nets, 372 nodes; board-vs-board
372/372; DRC's own parity leg).
