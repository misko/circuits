# PCBA audit — usb-hub-3s-v3 v1.4-2026-07-23

**Scope.** v1.4 is the current head of the release chain and was about to be
ordered as a PCBA. This audit asks the question the earlier reviews did not:
*not "is the board right?" but "is what the MACHINE will build right?"* — CPL
rotations, the population set, the order paperwork, and the part-vs-rail
ratings at the assembly boundary. The board's electrical design was already
red-teamed twice (v1.0 topology + layout lenses, v1.3 fresh lens) and the
copper is not in question here.

**Method (canon M1 — checker and checked must not share a method).** Every
rotation claim below is re-derived from (a) the sealed `04_kicad` board via
`pcbnew`, and (b) JLC's OWN cached footprint for that LCSC code
(`06_build/twin/easyeda/<code>/jlc.pretty/*.kicad_mod`, which is EasyEDA's
data converted into the same KiCad frame our footprints use), fitting pad sets
with the operator that was verified against `pcbnew` itself
(`pad.GetFPRelativePosition()` vs `pad.GetPosition()`, max error 0.000000 mm
over 72 pads — repo commit `e0d735c`). **No number below is taken from
`jlc_twin`'s `jlc_offset`**, which was negated by a handedness bug until
2026-07-25 and is the reason three of these defects existed at all.

**Headline.** v1.4 ships **four wrong CPL rotation cells**, one of which
(**C1/C2**) destroys the board on first power-up. v1.4 must not be ordered.

## 1. Findings

### PCBA-1 — **P0** — C1 and C2 placed 180° REVERSED (polarized bulk caps across the pack)

`fab/cpl.csv` rows 2 and 13 of sealed v1.4:

    C1,C2982822,CP_Elec_6.3x7.7,26.5,-60.0,top,270.0
    C2,C2982822,CP_Elec_6.3x7.7,26.5,-72.0,top,270.0

C1/C2 are **C2982822 = KNM2100UF35V149EC0055, a 100 µF / 35 V POLARIZED
polymer aluminium electrolytic** (`02_parts/KNM2100UF35V149EC0055/`), wired
`pin1 = VIN (+)`, `pin2 = GND` — directly across the XT60 3S-LiPo input
(9.0–12.6 V) behind a 10 A blade fuse. A reverse-biased polymer electrolytic on
a near-zero-impedance pack heats, gasses and **vents**. It happens at first
power-up, upstream of every bench gate in the ORDER_README.

**Measurement (2026-07-25).**

| quantity | our board | JLC C2982822 model |
|---|---|---|
| pad 1 local x | **−2.700 mm** (net VIN) | **−2.670 mm** |
| pad 2 local x | +2.700 mm (net GND) | +2.670 mm |

Already aligned ⇒ required rotation offset **0**; fit rms **0.030 mm @0** vs
**5.370 mm @180** (179× separation). Board orientation of C1/C2 is 90.0°, so
the correct CPL rotation is **90.0**. v1.4 ships **270.0** = exactly 180° off.

**Polarity cross-check — the part that makes the pad fit trustworthy.** A pad
fit alone *cannot* orient a 2-pad symmetric footprint: only the pad NUMBERING
distinguishes the ends, and a mislabelled model would fool it. So the polarity
was checked against JLC's own silk primitives, independently of the fit. JLC's
`CAP-SMD_BD6.3-L6.5-W6.5-LS7.2-FD.kicad_mod` carries three filled silk polygons:

    (fp_poly (pts (xy -3.101975 1.419098) ... (xy -2.309978 1.419098)) …)   horizontal bar @ x ≈ −2.71
    (fp_poly (pts (xy -2.804973 0.858114) ... (xy -2.804973 2.178101)) …)   VERTICAL bar @ x ≈ −2.71
    (fp_poly (pts (xy  3.102026 1.419098) ... (xy  2.310028 1.419098)) …)   horizontal bar @ x ≈ +2.71

The first two share a centre at (−2.706, 1.518) and cross: they draw a **“+”
over JLC's pad 1**. The third is a lone horizontal bar: a **“−” over JLC's
pad 2**. JLC's library therefore states explicitly that **pad 1 is the positive
terminal**, and our footprint puts **pad 1 on VIN**. The two agree. (JLC's silk
outline is additionally chamfered on the −x/pad-1 end, `-3.30,2.03 →
-2.03,3.30` and `-2.03,-3.30 → -3.30,-2.03`, consistent with the same end being
marked.) This is stronger evidence than the chamfer argument used when the
rotation row was first written, and it independently confirms it.

**Root cause.** No per-LCSC rotation row existed for C2982822, so the exporter
fell through to the footprint-NAME database, which resolved `CP_Elec_6.3x7.7`
to a 180 offset — a per-part fact a name key cannot carry. Fixed at source by
repo commit `9078ad9` (adds the C2982822 row, offset 0). Resolver check,
reproducing the defect exactly:

    with the row:    ('CP_Elec_6.3x7.7', 90.0, 'C2982822') -> (90.0, 0.0, 'lcsc')
    without (v1.4):                                        -> (270.0, 180.0, 'name')

**Disposition: FIXED in v1.5-2026-07-25** (CPL cell C1/C2 270.0 → 90.0).
v1.4 is **DO-NOT-ORDER**.

### PCBA-2 — **P1** — J1 (XT60 battery connector) placed 90° off

v1.4 ships `J1,C98732,XT60PW-M_EdgeTrim,30.0,-44.0,top,90.0`; the measured
correct value is **0.0**.

**Measurement (2026-07-25), all four pads, in the common KiCad local frame:**

| | our `XT60PW-M_EdgeTrim` (local, mm) | JLC `CONN-TH_XT60PW-M` (local, mm) |
|---|---|---|
| blade pad 1 (GND, −) | (−3.60, 0.00) | (3.00, 3.60) |
| blade pad 2 (VBAT, +) | (+3.60, 0.00) | (3.00, −3.60) |
| anchor | (−6.75, −6.00) | (−3.00, 6.75) |
| anchor | (+6.75, −6.00) | (−3.00, −6.75) |

*(our coordinates re-centred on the blade midpoint; JLC's are as-published)*

Rotating JLC's pad set by **270°** with the pcbnew-verified operator
`(x,y) → (x·cosθ + y·sinθ, −x·sinθ + y·cosθ)` lands **all four** pads on ours
to **rms 0.0000 mm**. At 90° the two anchors land **12.0 mm** away. Board
orientation 90.0° + offset 270 ⇒ **CPL 0.0**.

**This strengthens the evidence recorded in the rotation table.** That row
carries the caveat that “a 2-pad COLLINEAR fit distinguishes 90 from 270 only
through pad NUMBERING”. Measured here: the footprint has **four** pads, and the
two anchor holes sit **6.0 mm perpendicular to the blade axis**, so the 90-vs-270
ambiguity is broken by GEOMETRY ALONE, with no reliance on JLC's pad numbering
being right. See PCBA-11 for the residual polarity question, which this does
*not* settle.

**Root cause — a third distinct failure mode of name-keyed rotation.** The name
DB's pattern `^AMASS_XT60PW-M` is START-ANCHORED and this board uses a VENDORED
footprint named `XT60PW-M_EdgeTrim`, so **no rule fired at all** and the offset
silently defaulted to 0. A miss is as dangerous as a wrong hit and is quieter.
Fixed at source by repo commit `95a8180`.

**Disposition: FIXED in v1.5** (CPL cell J1 90.0 → 0.0).

### PCBA-3 — **P1** — Q7 (BSS138 gate inverter) placed 90° off

v1.4 ships `Q7,C78284,SOT-23,104.0,-96.0,top,270.0`; correct is **180.0**.

**Measurement (2026-07-25):**

| pad | ours (local, mm) | JLC C78284 (local, mm) | JLC rotated 180° |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (G, ENKILL) | (−0.938, −0.950) | (1.00, 0.95) | (−1.00, −0.95) |
| 2 (S, GND) | (−0.938, +0.950) | (1.00, −0.95) | (−1.00, +0.95) |
| 3 (D, QG) | (+0.938, 0.000) | (−1.00, 0.00) | (+1.00, 0.00) |

rms **0.062 mm @180**; at 270 the pads are **1.95 mm** out — a 3-pad
asymmetric fit, decisive with no numbering assumption. Board orientation 0.0° +
offset 180 ⇒ **CPL 180.0**.

**Consequence had it shipped.** Q7 inverts ENKILL onto Q6's gate. A 90°-rotated
SOT-23 puts gate, source and drain on the wrong lands: the USB-C reverse-block
P-FET Q6 would not be enable-gated — the Pi rail would be dead, or the
master-off switch would not open Q6 (losing the documented reverse-feed
protection, red-team RT-T4). Not a fire, but a dead or silently unprotected
port.

**Root cause.** The name DB maps `^SOT-23` to −90 (=270). That is right for some
SOT-23 parts and wrong for this one; the per-LCSC row (C78284 = 180) exists
precisely because a footprint NAME cannot carry a per-part fact. The row was
present in v1.4's table but had been populated from the negated `jlc_twin`
output era and re-verified only on 2026-07-25 (`e0d735c`).

**Disposition: FIXED in v1.5** (CPL cell Q7 270.0 → 180.0).

### PCBA-4 — **P1** — U12 (USBLC6-2SC6) V_BUS runs above its characterized standoff on the C rail

U12's `VBUS` pin (pin 5) sits on **VBUSC**, the Pi rail. Measured setpoints
(v1.4 ORDER_README tolerance table, re-derived here from `03_src/rules/
power_tree.yaml` and the shipped divider parts):

| rail | parts | nominal | worst MIN | worst MAX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5VC** (U12) | R12 4.12 k ±0.1 % C2984354 / R13 1.21 k ±1 % C5126242, Vref 1.215 V ±1.5 % | **5.352 V** | 5.227 V | **5.479 V** |
| **5VA** (U8/U9/U10) | R3 3.92 k ±0.1 % C728591 / R4 1.21 k ±1 % C5126242 | 5.151 V | 5.032 V | **5.273 V** |

**Datasheet, READ not inherited** (USBLC6-2, ST Doc ID 11265 Rev 5, Oct 2011,
`02_parts/USBLC6-2SC6/USBLC6-2SC6_ST.pdf`, pp. 1-2):

- **Table 1 “Absolute ratings” contains NO V_BUS voltage limit at all** — only
  V_PP (15/15/25 kV), T_stg, T_j (−40…+125 °C) and T_L.
- **Table 2**: `I_RM` leakage current is specified **at the test condition
  V_RM = 5.25 V** — typ 10 nA, **max 150 nA**.
- **`V_BR`, breakdown between V_BUS and GND at I_R = 1 mA: MIN 6 V.**
- Figure 4: leakage rises with junction temperature by roughly **30×** from
  25 °C to 125 °C.

So the number `vbus_max: 5.25 V` recorded in `02_parts/USBLC6-2SC6/part.yaml`
is a **conservative transcription of the leakage TEST CONDITION, not an
absolute maximum** — a distinction that matters, because the honest exceedance
is much smaller than “over abs-max”:

| | value | vs V_RM 5.25 V | vs V_BR min 6.0 V |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5VC nominal | 5.352 V | **+102 mV** | −648 mV |
| 5VC worst static corner | 5.479 V | **+229 mV** | **−521 mV** |
| 5VA worst static corner (U8/U9/U10) | 5.273 V | +23 mV | −727 mV |

**Failure mode, stated precisely.** At 5.479 V the part is ≥521 mV below the
*minimum* 1 mA breakdown knee, so it does not clamp and does not conduct
meaningfully. The exposure is **elevated reverse leakage above the
characterized point**, aggravated by temperature — bounded at roughly
150 nA × 30 ≈ 4.5 µA, i.e. ~25 µW, which is negligible against a 5 A rail and
does not touch the E-OFF quiescent budget (VBUSC collapses when Q6 opens). The
realistic long-term risk is slow leakage degradation of the V_BUS transil, not
breakdown, not thermal runaway.

**A finding the task brief did not name: U8/U9/U10 are in the same class.** The
three USB-A ESD arrays are the same C7519 part with V_BUS on 5VA. Their nominal
5.151 V is comfortably under 5.25 V, but the **worst static corner 5.273 V is
+23 mV above it** — same mechanism, an order of magnitude smaller. Recorded so
the acceptance covers all four devices, not just the loud one.

**Disposition: ACCEPTED + MEASURED (user decision 2026-07-25).** R12 is NOT
changed — lowering 5VC would spend the E-MARGIN headroom the Pi rail needs
(low corner 5.227 V leaves only **69 mV** of slack once the E-MARGIN gate's own
1.20 derate is applied: 597 mV headroom vs 528 mV required. The un-derated
"157 mV" that v1.4's paperwork carried is not the gate's number — v1.5 fresh
lens FL15-5). Instead:
bench gate **Q1 now RECORDS measured VBUSC and VBUSA values** rather than only
pass/fail, and the derating is written into the v1.5 MANIFEST as an
**evidence-backed waiver** (canon M4: the measurement, not the rationale). An
accepted measured exceedance is a judgement; silence would have been the defect.

### PCBA-5 — **P1** — the population set had no machine-readable home (no `assembly.yaml`)

`not_assembled:` line. Nothing read it, and nothing compared it to the board's
`FP_EXCLUDE_FROM_POS_FILES` attributes or to `fab/cpl.csv`. This is the exact
structure that let cooksense v1.1 ship 13 CPL rows with blank LCSC codes while
its MANIFEST declared 12 of them not-assembled.

**Measured population set for this board (2026-07-25):** 114 footprints − 4
mounting holes (H1-H4) = **110 BOM parts = 108 CPL placements + 2 declared
not-assembled (F1, SW1)**; `excl_pos = {F1, H1, H2, H3, H4, SW1}` — declaration
and machine instruction agree. 108/108 CPL rows `top`; **zero** bottom-side
footprints.

**Disposition: FIXED in v1.5** — `03_src/rules/assembly.yaml` created (seeded
from the skill template), declaring service, sides, fiducials, build_quantity,
the two `not_assembled` entries with dated evidence, and the exempt prefixes.
The MANIFEST `not_assembled:` line is now generated from that one file.

### PCBA-11 — **P2** — J1 `MODEL-REG` twin finding: false alarm, but the XT60 polarity gate still stands

The 2026-07-25 twin run raises one `MODEL-REG` on J1:

    body center 14.3mm off courtyard, area ratio 0.87 -> DO NOT blind-flip:
    JLC's footprint mounts this model at rot_z=0 (authoritative); body
    asymmetric (4.1mm bbox-center offset) so this metric is unreliable.

**Verified as the tool instructs.** The XT60 body's own bbox centre sits
4.10 mm from its model origin (WRL bbox x −5.177…1.949 in 0.1-inch units =

bbox-centre-vs-courtyard metric is meaningless for this part. Leads-on-pads was
then checked directly: the render was calibrated against the four mounting
holes and J1's four pad centres overlaid on `twin_top.png`. **All four land on
the model's own features** — the two blade bores on pads 1/2, the two housing
posts on the two anchor pads.

**Disposition: RECORDED — false alarm, no rotation override.** The rotation
stands on the four-pad fit of PCBA-2 (rms 0.0000 mm).
**BUT:** geometry cannot check which blade is “+”. `02_parts/XT60PW-M/part.yaml`
records “PAD 1 IS NEGATIVE — polarity is a PART FACT”, and the fleet ledger
records a reversed XT60 shipping once. J1 therefore **MUST** pass the
order-preview human gate (PCBA-8) before payment. This is a gate, not a note.

### PCBA-15 — **P2** — U12's V_BUS transil breaks down BELOW D5's breakdown window

Found while writing `DETAIL_DESIGN.md` sec.5 (PCBA-7), from the two datasheets
side by side:

    U12 USBLC6-2SC6 V_BUS transil  V_BR = 6.00 V min        (ST Doc ID 11265 Rev 5, Table 2)
    D5  SMBJ6.0A                   V_BR = 6.67 - 8.15 V     (02_parts/SMBJ6.0A/part.yaml)

On a **slow** VBUSC rise, U12's V_BUS transil therefore enters breakdown
**before** D5 does. U12 is a SOT-23-6 ESD array characterized for 8/20 µs
surges, not for continuous conduction — in that window it is effectively a
sacrificial first-responder ahead of the part that is supposed to be the
over-voltage element.

**Bounded, and it does not reopen PCBA-4.** Normal operation is unaffected: the
5.479 V worst static corner is 521 mV below U12's V_BR *minimum*. The window
only opens in a buck-fail-high, which ADR-0002 already declares as protected on
a **best-effort** basis only (D5 clamps ~10.3 V and F2 must trip; not a
guaranteed cutoff, supervised prototype, replaceable Pi).

**Disposition: RECORDED** — written into `01_docs/DETAIL_DESIGN.md` sec.5.4 so
that (a) nobody reads “D5 protects VBUSC” as covering U12, and (b) a future
revision adding active OVP knows U12 is part of what the present design spends.
No change to v1.5.
