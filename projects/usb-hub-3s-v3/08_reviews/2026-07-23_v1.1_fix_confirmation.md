## Finding-by-finding resolution

### Blocker-2 — USB-C setpoint 4.97 V too low for the Pi  → RESOLVED
- v1.0: buck-C FB 3.74k/1.21k = **4.97 V**, sensed at the buck output; after the
  (then-absent) delivery drop the Pi could dip toward its ~4.63 V brownout.
- v1.1 Fix 2 (option a, **sense at the connector**): FB top R12 senses **VBUSC**
  (post-eFuse) and R12 = 3.92k, so the loop regulates the CONNECTOR to
  1.215*(1+3.92/1.21) = **5.151 V, load-independent** (worst-low 5.07 V over Vref
  +-1.5%). Verified on the board: **R12.1 = VBUSC** (pin review).
- **Gate:** `E-MARGIN PASS` — rail USB-C Vout 5.151 V vs Pi load_uv 4.63 V =
  **521 mV headroom = 104 mOhm IR budget at 5 A**, clears the 100 mOhm delivery floor.

### Protected VBUS (RT-T4: no port current-limit, no reverse blocking)  → RESOLVED
- v1.1 Fix 1: **TPS26631 eFuse (U13)** between 5VC and J5 VBUS, with the
  datasheet-mandated **two-FET reverse-current block** (Q6 AON6354 power FET +
  Q7 BSS138 fast gate-pulldown, SLVSE94G 8.3.5 / Fig 8-7).
  - Current limit **I_OL = 18000/3.09k = 5.83 A** (min 5.42 A > 5 A load).
  - Input-OV cutoff **OVP = 1.2*(47.5+12.1)/12.1 = 5.91 V** (hard-trips a buck
    HS-short toward 12.6 V before it reaches the Pi).
  - Soft-start (dVdT 10 nF ~1 ms), MODE->GND auto-retry, active-low SHDN via a
    100k/150k divider (ties eFuse-enable to buck-C presence; master-off kills it too).
- Verified on the board (pin review + E-INV series_chain): 5VC -> Q6(S=5VC,D=EFINC)
  -> U13(IN=EFINC,OUT=VBUSC) -> VBUSC; J5 VBUS pads on VBUSC; CC1/CC2 Rp to VBUSC.
- **Gate:** `E-INV 16/16` (the eFuse path + reverse-block gates + CC Rp are all
  positively asserted); twin land-patterns VALIDATED (U13 fit 0.01 mm, Q7 0.08 mm,
  Q6 = reused AON6354 merged-drain adjudication).

### Master-off switch (self-drain in storage)  → RESOLVED
- v1.1 Fix 3: **SW1 (SS12D07 slide)** grounds the merged **ENKILL** bus (both
  LM5116 EN pins U2.4 + U11.4). Slide to T1 -> both bucks to ~9 uA shutdown +
  5VC collapse -> eFuse SHDN off. Verified: SW1.COM(2)=ENKILL, both EN on ENKILL.
- **Gate:** `E-OFF PASS` — off_control (SW1/ENKILL) + quiescent_ua (270 uA: ~252 uA
  switch sink through R8||R17=50k + ~18 uA 2x LM5116 shutdown) both declared.

### Caps — voltage margin (RT-T2 input, RT-T5 output)  → RESOLVED
- v1.1 Fix 4: buck **INPUT** MLCC 10uF **25V -> 50V** (GRM32ER71H106KA12L, C77102)
  — clears the SMBJ15A 24.4 V worst-case clamp with margin. Buck **OUTPUT** MLCC
  100uF **6.3V -> 10V** (GRM32ER61A107ME20L, C84455) — ends the 6.3 V-on-5 V
  DC-bias derating. (C49/C50 USB-C bulk stay 10uF/25V, already 5x-derated at 5 V.)
