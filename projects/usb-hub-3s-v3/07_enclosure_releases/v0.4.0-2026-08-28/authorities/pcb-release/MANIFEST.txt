THE GATE VERDICT IN THIS FILE THAT IS NOT EVIDENCE

  jlc_twin is the gate whose entire job is comparing our footprint to JLC's. Its
  verdict on J5, on the DEFECTIVE board and on the CORRECTED board:

      v1.11/verification/twin_report.csv:  C5337088,J5,OK,fit=0.00mm
      v1.12/verification/twin_report.csv:  C5337088,J5,OK,fit=0.00mm

  Identical, on two boards that differ by 0.375 mm on the very datum the gate
  exists to check. It is not mistuned, it is STRUCTURALLY BLIND to this class,
  in THREE compounding ways. The first two were known; the third came out of the
  fresh-context lens and is the one that makes a partial fix useless:

    (1) pads_of() drops unnumbered pads, so the two NPTH alignment holes — the
        datum the whole defect is measured against — never enter the fit at all.
    (2) centered() removes the centroid from both point sets before comparing,
        making the comparison translation-invariant BY CONSTRUCTION. A land row
        displaced as a RIGID GROUP is exactly what survives that.
    (3) JLC NAMES ITS PADS DIFFERENTLY, so the by-name correspondence set is
        tiny AND DEGENERATE. Measured here rather than asserted: JLC's package
        names its lands {1, A1/B12, A4/B9, A5, A6, A7, A8, B1/A12, B4/A9, B5,
        B6, B7, B8}; ours are {A1, A12, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, B1, B12, B4,
        B5, B6, B7, B8, B9, SH}. The intersection is EIGHT names — A5..A8,
        B5..B8 — and **all eight are COLLINEAR** (their y = 2982.205 on JLC's
        side, -3.670 on ours, one value each). So after centered() removes the
        centroid, the fit is eight points on a line against eight points on a
        line: it carries NO information about where that line sits along the
        perpendicular axis, which is the only axis this defect lives on.
        (The lens read this as an EMPTY correspondence set; it is not empty, it
        is eight collinear points, which is worse than it sounds and better
        than empty. Recorded as measured.)

  A fix that only stops dropping unnumbered pads will therefore NOT close this.
  The gate needs a numbering-free landmark fit — which this repo already has, in
  export_jlc_package's placement_datum cross-check.

  It is run here and it passes, and its J5 verdict is not evidence about the
  datum. The gap is reported and queued; it is deliberately NOT patched inside a
  board fix — skills/ changes and board changes do not belong in the same commit,
  and a gate rewritten by the agent whose work it is about to grade is not a gate.

  Nothing else was positioned to catch this either. DRC grades copper against
  copper and the wrong pattern is internally consistent. escape_check grades
  pitch and escape geometry, which were right. The pin review grades the pad-name
  -> net map, which was right. A-POS grades the board against itself. THE LAND
  PATTERN ITSELF HAD NO CHECKER; verification/j5_geometry_gate.txt is the first
  thing in this repo that reads a vendor drawing's numbers and compares them to
