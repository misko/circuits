Canon M9. v1.6 is a COPPER revision, and its Stage 0 is "fix the instrument
first": nothing measured with `jlc_twin` was trustworthy until the render path
was corrected, because the tool that would verify v1.6 was built out of the
defect it was meant to catch.

  the plan names.
- result: confirmed by reading that `xform()` (fixed in 1b69760) was ONE of
  FIVE copies of the same rotation form. The other four are all on the RENDER

## 2026-07-25 — iterate 1 (measure the model frame)

  | rot_z | formA(+mrot) predicts | formB(+mrot) predicts | RENDERED |

- next: fix all five sites through one named operator each, then take the J1

- next: v1.5 is CONFIRMED CORRECT on J1 and on all six FETs. It is not

## 2026-07-25 — iterate 3 (LED rotations — a NEW P0-class finding)

- did: the plan requires the C2296/C2297 rotations to be MEASURED with formA
  plus a numbering-free channel before the status LEDs are authored. Fetched
  both JLC footprints and measured both channels.
- result: **the two channels DISAGREE by 180 degrees, and the pad fit is the
  one that is wrong.**

  | channel | C2296 | C2297 |
  |---|---|---|
  | pad-number fit (verified operator) | **180**, residual 0.1125 mm | **180**, residual 0.1125 mm |
  | next non-mirrored candidate | 1.9875 mm (17.7x margin) | 1.9875 mm (17.7x margin) |
  | numbering-free (physical cathode) | **0** | **0** |

  Both codes ship the same JLC footprint `LED0805-R-RD`, pad 1 at x=+1.05
  (EAST), pad 2 at x=-1.05 (WEST).
  - JLC's F.SilkS carries a diode glyph spanning x -0.30..+0.20 whose triangle
    APEX points WEST; a diode triangle points at its cathode. **JLC cathode =
    WEST = their pad 2**, i.e. JLC numbers pad 1 = ANODE.
  - Corroborated independently on the same footprint: JLC's silk body outline
    is CHAMFERED at the WEST end (45 deg corners at x -1.82..-2.22) and a
    plain rectangle at the EAST end. Chamfered end = cathode. Same answer.
  - Our side: KiCad `Device:LED` is **pin 1 = K, pin 2 = A** (read from
    `/usr/share/kicad/symbols/Device.kicad_sym`), and
    `LED_0805_2012Metric` chamfers its F.Fab outline at pin 1, which is at
    x=-0.9375 (WEST). **Our cathode pad = pad 1, WEST.**
  - Both libraries therefore already draw the cathode at the WEST end: the
    PHYSICAL parts align at **offset 0**. The pad-number fit demands 180
    only because the two libraries NUMBER the terminals oppositely.

  **Consequence if unresolved: a rotation row of 180 ships every indicator LED
  REVERSED** — dark, and on the bench indistinguishable from a bad solder
  joint. This is the same class that produced P0s on three boards.

- next: the per-LCSC rows for C2296 and C2297 must be **0**, NOT the fitted
  180. The rotation table is owned elsewhere and is deliberately NOT edited
  from here (canon M1: a table populated from a checker's output cannot then
  be used to grade it). Both codes go on the ORDER-PREVIEW HUMAN GATE. The
  durable fix landed as canon **A-POL** / `jlc_twin` POLARITY-FIT: the
  numbering-free channel is now MEASURED and BLOCKING, so this cannot be
  discovered by eye again.

## 2026-07-25 — measured v1.6 copper baseline (independent re-measure)

Every number below is my own measurement against
`04_kicad/usb_hub_3s_v2.kicad_pcb`, not inherited from the plan. Radial
census walks tracks, vias, pads AND filled zone polygons (the fill, not the
outline).

**Mounting-hole radial table** (drill r = 1.600 mm on all four):

| hole | F.Cu | B.Cu | In1.Cu | In2.Cu | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | GND @1.800 | GND @1.800 | GND @1.800 | **VIN @1.800** | outer PASS |
| H2 | GND @1.800 | GND @1.800 | GND @1.800 | **VIN @1.800** | outer PASS |
| H3 | **5VA @1.800** + GND @1.800 | **5VA @1.800** + GND @1.800 | GND @1.800 | **VIN @1.800** | **outer FAIL** |
| H4 | GND @1.800, VBUSA3 @**4.152** | GND @1.800, VBUSA3 @**4.152** | GND @1.800 | **VIN @1.800** | outer PASS at r<=4.00 |

- **H3 CONFIRMED**: F.Cu *and* B.Cu each carry BOTH `5VA` and `GND` at
  r = 1.800 mm against a 1.600 mm hole = **0.200 mm of bare laminate**. Any
  metal M3 fastener (cap head r 2.75, nut across-corners 3.18, DIN125A washer
  3.50) lands on both nets on both faces and shorts the 6 A USB-A rail to GND
  through solder mask alone.
- **H4**: `VBUSA3` at 4.152 mm — outside a DIN125A washer, reachable by a
  DIN 9021 (r 4.50).
- **All four holes**: In2.Cu `VIN` at 1.800 mm = 0.200 mm from an unplated
  drill to the 9-12.6 V plane, inside JLC's +/-0.13 mm NPTH position tolerance.
- SCOPING NOTE: the same census flags J5 and SW1, but those NPTHs are
  connector alignment/shell pegs, not fastener holes — no metal fastener
  enters them. The r<=4.00 mm one-net-and-that-net-GND rule is scoped to
  holes a FASTENER enters (H*).

## 2026-07-25 — BLOCKER for Stages 1-4 (honest stop)

- did: ran `03_src/rebuild_fast.sh` on the untouched tree to establish a green
  baseline before making any v1.6 source change.
- result: **the rebuild does NOT reproduce the sealed v1.5 board, and lands
  1 DRC violation.** Deterministic across two runs.
    sealed board: 791 tracks / 270 vias, DRC 0/0/0 (re-verified here)
    rebuilt:      892 tracks / 272 vias, DRC **1/0/0**
    the violation: `hole_to_hole`, two 5VA vias at (52.675, 44.25) and
    (52.175, 44.0) — 0.559 mm centre-to-centre, 0.259 mm hole gap against a
    0.4995 mm minimum.
  Root cause is NOT v1.5: the SHARED backend changed after v1.5 sealed —
  `bb2af90` (heal_islands seats copper by OVERLAP, not via-centre-in-poly —
  a fleet fix) and `8667452` (three stitch-tool defects). The sealed board is
  a faithful historical artifact built against the older backend; today's
  backend legitimately stitches more copper and, on this board, puts two 5VA
  link vias too close together.
- next: this is a normal grind item, but it means **v1.6 does not start from a
  green baseline** — every copper change downstream (VBUS 0.8 mm floor, the
  H3/H4 zone rework, the LED placement re-route) has to be measured against a
  board that is already red. That grind, plus the LED cell, the paste
  window-panes, the fiducials and the D5 reselect, is the remaining v1.6 work.
  Stopping here rather than seal-chasing: canon says a partial result honestly
  reported beats a passing claim, and the brief says STOP on a new P0 rather
  than seal. Two P0-class findings landed today (the five-copy render defect,
  and the LED polarity numbering) and both are now gated.
  **04_kicad/ was restored to the sealed board; the working tree carries no
  half-built copper.**
