# Hardware bring-up journal

## 2026-08-16 — iterate 1 (first assembled board failure)
- did: Investigated a physical `v1.12-2026-07-28` board after U5 (TPS2557, USB-A port 3 high-side switch) burned when input power was applied after the user installed U2; the same board had previously been powered with only U11 installed without visible burning. Reviewed close photographs, the released board/netlist, component markings, and measured continuity.
- result: MEASURED/OBSERVED U5 had a visible crater near its pin-4/EN corner and was permanently failed. U2 pin-1 orientation matched the PCB marker. The first U2 installation soldered the perimeter leads but not the required exposed ground pad. R3 was visually confirmed marked `58B`, the correct EIA-96 marking for the released 3.92 kOhm part—not `580`. C17 measured approximately 0 ohms from 5VA to GND before U5 removal and approximately 35 ohms after U5 removal. Removing U5 therefore removed the hard short, but the damaged board retained an abnormal residual load. The original initiating cause remains unproven; the damaged board is not cleared for further power.
- next: Keep the failed board unpowered. If diagnosis resumes, first compare the isolated VBUSA3 side to GND, then remove U2 and remeasure C17; approximately 1.5 kOhm is the now-established assembled-board reference. Do not replace U5 until the residual 35-ohm path and original failure mechanism are isolated.

## 2026-08-16 — iterate 2 (replacement-board no-load qualification)
- did: Assembled a new board with F1, U2, and U11 installed. SW1 was deliberately absent. Checked the 5VA rail resistance and powered from a current-limited bench supply at three input voltages while measuring C17.
- result: MEASURED C17 resistance was approximately 1.5 kOhm with U2, U11, and F1 installed. With no USB loads connected: 10.00 V input produced 0.017 A and 5.17 V at C17; 11.20 V produced 0.015 A and 5.17 V; 12.11 V produced 0.014 A and 5.17 V. Input power was consequently 170, 168, and 170 mW—consistent constant no-load power—and 5VA remained inside the released 5.03–5.28 V qualification window. SW1 absence is equivalent to its ON state because R8 and R17 pull ENKILL high; it does not explain the failed board's U5 short.
- next: Pause until the ordered adjustable USB electronic load arrives. Resume with a no-load warm soak and temperature observation, measure all three USB-A VBUS outputs, then load one port at a time at approximately 0.1 A, 0.5 A, and 1.0 A while recording input current, C17/5VA, receptacle VBUS, and temperature. Test ports 1 and 2 before port 3/U5. Stop on 5VA above 5.30 V, receptacle VBUS below 4.75 V, unexpected current limiting, oscillation, odor, or rapid heating. An oscilloscope startup/ripple check remains desirable because the DMM measurements establish steady-state regulation only. Do not connect a valuable USB device until dummy-load qualification passes.
