WAIVER W-U12 (EVIDENCE-BACKED, canon M4 -- the measurement, not a rationale):
  CLAIM WAIVED: the USBLC6-2SC6 (C7519) V_BUS pin on U12 runs above the voltage
  at which ST characterizes it, and this release ships anyway.
  MEASUREMENT, from the datasheet READ DIRECTLY (USBLC6-2, ST Doc ID 11265 Rev 5,
  02_parts/USBLC6-2SC6/USBLC6-2SC6_ST.pdf p.2, read 2026-07-25):
    - Table 1 "Absolute ratings" carries NO V_BUS voltage limit at all (only
      V_PP, T_stg, T_j, T_L). The "5.25 V" recorded in part.yaml as `vbus_max`
      is a CONSERVATIVE TRANSCRIPTION OF THE TEST CONDITION for Table 2's
      leakage spec I_RM (typ 10 nA / max 150 nA at V_RM = 5.25 V), not a maximum.
    - The real device limit is V_BR = 6.0 V MINIMUM at I_R = 1 mA.
  EXCEEDANCE, against this board's derived corners (DETAIL_DESIGN sec.4):
    U12 on 5VC        nominal 5.352 V  = +102 mV over V_RM, 648 mV BELOW V_BR min
    U12 on 5VC   worst corner 5.479 V  = +229 mV over V_RM, 521 mV BELOW V_BR min
    U8/U9/U10 on 5VA worst    5.273 V  =  +23 mV over V_RM, 727 mV BELOW V_BR min
  MODE: at 5.479 V the transil is >=521 mV below its MINIMUM 1 mA breakdown knee,
  so it does not clamp and does not conduct meaningfully. The exposure is
  elevated reverse leakage above the characterized point, aggravated by
  temperature (datasheet Fig.4: ~30x from Tj 25C to 125C) and BOUNDED at roughly
  150 nA x 30 ~ 4.5 uA ~ 25 uW. Negligible against a 5 A rail; it does not enter
  the E-OFF quiescent budget because VBUSC collapses when Q6 opens.
  DECISION (user, 2026-07-25): ACCEPT + MEASURE. R12 is deliberately NOT changed
  -- lowering 5VC would spend the E-MARGIN headroom the Pi rail needs (157 mV of
  slack over the 440 mV IR budget); trading a 229 mV leakage-regime exceedance
  for undervoltage on the load is the wrong trade.
  ONGOING EVIDENCE: ORDER_README bench gate Q1 now RECORDS the measured VBUSC and
  VBUSA values rather than only passing/failing them, and sets an explicit
  re-derive threshold (measured VBUSC > 5.55 V invalidates the 521 mV argument).
  See 01_docs/DETAIL_DESIGN.md sec.5.3, DISPOSITIONS PCBA-4.
  RELATED, RECORDED NOT WAIVED (PCBA-15, DETAIL_DESIGN sec.5.4): U12's V_BUS
  transil V_BR (6.0 V min) sits BELOW D5's V_BR window (6.67-8.15 V), so on a
  slow VBUSC rise U12 conducts before the TVS. Normal operation is unaffected;
  the window only opens in the buck-fail-high case that ADR-0002 already declares
  protected on a best-effort basis only.
