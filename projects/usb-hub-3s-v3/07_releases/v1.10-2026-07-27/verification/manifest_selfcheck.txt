THE BOOTSTRAP, STATED RATHER THAN HIDDEN.
This file is itself one of the 79 rows in the table it is reporting on, so its
own sha256 is necessarily stamped AFTER this text was written. Of the 79 rows,
78 were hashed and compared by the run that produced this text; this file's own
row was computed from these bytes immediately afterwards and cannot have been
verified by the run that wrote them. That is not a gap you can close by being
cleverer about the order — it is closed by the READER, in one command, which is
why the command is printed here rather than a claim being made on its behalf:

    cd 07_releases/v1.10-2026-07-27
    sha256sum -c <(sed -n '/^sha256:/,$p' MANIFEST.txt | tail -n +2 \
                   | awk 'NF==2 {print $1"  "$2}')

means this directory was mutated after the seal, and 07_releases/contracts.md
says what to do then: it is no longer evidence of anything — rename it
TAINTED-v1.10-2026-07-27 and re-release. Do not repair it in place.
