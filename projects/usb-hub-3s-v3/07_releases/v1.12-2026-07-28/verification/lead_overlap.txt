LEAD vs LAND — the contact tail MEASURED FROM THE PART, not assumed from the
recommended land.

CORRECTION, 2026-07-28, raised by the fresh-context lens (F-1) and confirmed here
by an independent measurement. The FIRST version of this file opened by DEFINING
the contact tail as the vendor's recommended land (-1.6400..-0.5000, length
1.140). That is a category error: a land is lead PLUS fillets, so every number
downstream of it was a land-vs-land ratio wearing a lead's name — including the
"0.220 mm inboard", "80.7 % overlap" and "0.530 mm past the lead" figures that
reached the MANIFEST, the CHANGELOG, part.yaml and the footprint descr. Its own
output should have given it away: it reported the CORRECTED land as "100.0 %
overlap, heel 0.0000, toe 0.0000", i.e. a land with no fillet at either end,
which would be a bad land pattern rather than the vendor's own.

THE LEAD IS DIMENSIONED. The sheet's top view gives the tail projection past the
housing as `0.40 +/- 0.10` beside the `7.35` housing depth, and JLC's 3D mesh for
C5337088 is 7.7500 deep — 7.35 + 0.40, exactly. Measured below from that mesh
(.easyeda_cache/8fd25e967d514fadb32489eb3c21393e.obj, 3379 vertices), anchored on
the part's OWN datum: the two moulded alignment posts.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT TRUE ABOUT THE v1.11 LAND PATTERN
  NOT true: contacts hanging off their lands. The tail is 100 % on the land in BOTH.
  TRUE    : the fillet balance is INVERTED. The vendor puts the surplus at the HEEL
            (0.570 vs 0.170); v1.11 put it at the TOE (0.350 vs 0.700). The heel is
            where the solder fillet carries the joint, and v1.11 starved it to 61 %
            of the intended length while doubling the toe.
  TRUE    : 27 % more paste per land than the vendor specifies, and 0.700 mm of every
            land and its stencil aperture beyond the tail tip, on a 0.5 mm-PITCH row,
            on a part that cannot self-align. That is the bridge exposure.
  So the defect is real and correctly signed. It is a FILLET-BALANCE AND PASTE-VOLUME
  defect, not an open-joint one, and the release's documents now say so.
