they are the DATUM and they are in the same place in both pictures. Compare the
twelve grey lands above them:

  BEFORE  the lands are long, and their lower edges stop well short of the
          alignment-hole line. The real contact tail (0.400 long, measured from
          JLC's mesh) sits fully on the land in this picture too - but it sits
          in the wrong PART of it: 0.350 mm of land on the heel side where the
          fillet works, and 0.700 mm of land and open stencil aperture past the
          tail tip with no lead over it. 27% more paste per land than the vendor
          specifies, on a 0.5 mm pitch.
  AFTER   the lands are 1.140 long instead of 1.450, and the row has moved
          0.375 mm DOWN (toward the connector mouth, at the bottom of the

          land edges now come down to 0.219 mm from the hole edges.

the escape channel north of the land row is correspondingly clearer — the six
