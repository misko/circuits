AMPACITY — the A-AMP judgement, with the measurements that justify it

  Method (canon M1: reads only zone copper, so it cannot agree with a declared
  track width by construction): sweep the perpendicular cross-section of the
  FILLED zone polygons along each power path with pcbnew and sum the copper
  over every layer the net pours on, because the layers are via-bonded and
  conduct in parallel. Take the MINIMUM along the path.

  POUR-FED (declared pour_fed: WITH the measurement in 03_src/rules/nets.yaml):
    PWR_IN       7 A    VBAT   8.750 mm F.Cu (J1->F1)          need 4.399 mm  1.99x
                        VBAT_F 14.650 mm (B.Cu 8.950+F.Cu 5.700)
                        VIN    38.400 / 43.150 mm on the In2 plane
    SWITCH_NODE  7 A    SW_A   6.050 mm (B.Cu 4.800+F.Cu 1.250) need 4.399 mm  1.38x
                        SW_C   9.600 / 7.200 mm
    PWR_RAIL   6/5 A    PMID   9.300 mm (Q6->F2)               need 2.765 mm  2.62x
