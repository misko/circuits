### A-POS (JLC placement datum) — checked, and the CPL does NOT change

A gate that landed after v1.0 sealed: JLC's CPL Mid X/Y is the bbox centre of
**pad centres**, not the footprint anchor. It is not theoretical —
crow-recorder-central-v2 v1.4 places its only USB-C 1.3025 mm off against
1.150 mm contacts (0.000 mm pad overlap) and usb-hub-3s-v3 v1.5 has 11 of 108
rows off, all five connectors among them.

**Re-checked here on all 26 rows, two independent readers** (A-POS in
`assembly_coverage.py`, which parses board text, and a fresh pcbnew
re-derivation — `verification/cpl_datum.txt`): worst residual **0.00000 mm**
against the pad-centre datum. `fab/cpl.csv` is therefore **byte-identical** to
v1.0 and the paperwork-only framing is unaffected.

The exposure is structurally absent rather than merely absent: on this board
the pad-centre datum and the footprint anchor **coincide to 0.00000 mm on all
26 placements**, because every machine-placed part is a symmetric chip
(0603/0805/1210), a SOIC-8, a SOT-553 or a 4-pad symmetric transducer. The
class bites footprints whose anchor is offset from the pad array — typically
**connectors** — and this board's only two connector-class parts, J1 and MK1,
are hand-solder and not on the CPL at all.

### A-ROT: 4 codes measured and REPORTED (the table is owned elsewhere)

`export_jlc_package.py` now exits 2 rather than guess a rotation. 22 of 26
placements resolve; 4 codes have no measured row: **C192421** (U1),
**C22359707** (LS1), **C2480** (D2), **C559105** (D3). All four are measured in
`verification/rotation_measurements.txt` with residual + next-best separation.
Every ADJUDICATED offset equals what the CPL already ships, so no CPL value
moves. **The machine channel did not agree on LS1** — see below; its
paste-ready `ROW: C22359707,90` is quarantined in the evidence file and must
not reach the fleet rotation table.

**LS1's two channels disagreed, and the numbering-free one was the false one.**
The tool read pad-number fit → 0 (shipped) but its pin-1-mark channel → 90,
advising "believe the numbering-free channel". Adjudicated: our vendored LS1
`F.Fab` is a plain **unchamfered square**, verified invariant under
90/180/270, so the channel matched an arbitrary corner of one symmetric square
to another and returned a spurious *exact* 0.0000 mm — and then labelled the
row `two-channel`, claiming corroboration it did not have. The real
numbering-free channel is the **'+' silk mark present in both libraries**:
rotating JLC's '+' onto ours, only **θ=0** puts it on our '+' pad. At 90° JLC's
+ drive terminal would land on our **NC dummy** pad — the datasheet's
"2-DUMMY PAD", not internally connected to the coil — so the transducer would
be mechanically attached and electrically dead. Corroborated by the whole-part
oriented fit (0.1414 mm @0, 50×) and by `jlc_twin` through pcbnew
(fit=0.14 mm, offset=0). **LS1 = 0, unchanged.**

### Corrected claims (each was measured, none inherited)

- **MK1 is NOT absent from the JLC catalog.** v1.0's dossier, twin adjudication
  and ORDER_README all said so; a live query returns the exact MPN as
  **C3273706**. The real wall is stock 0 across all three AOM-5024 variants
  plus through-hole on an SMT-only order.
- **The LS1 twin waiver was geometrically impossible.** It claimed "JLC pad 1 =
  our pad 2"; that mapping fits at **rms 7.1007 mm at all four rotations** —
  nowhere. It had read our `.kicad_mod` y-down and JLC's y-UP. True mapping is
  a **3↔4 swap** of two NC dummy pads (rms **0.1414 @0**, 50× separation),
  independently confirmed by `jlc_twin` at fit=0.14 mm offset=0 through a
  separate code path. **The shipped CPL rotation was always correct.**
  Its validation method — pairwise pad-pair distances — is invariant under
  reflection and so could never have detected the error.
- **`policy_audit.md` shipped corrupt and hash-verified.** A stdout line was
  spliced into the table body, deleting the P-TIER row; the file claimed
  PASS=23 over a table containing PASS=22.
- **Board size:** 80 × 45 mm is the Edge.Cuts outline (measured 80.0000 ×
  45.0000). v1.0's MANIFEST "80.1 × 45.1" was the bounding box including the
  0.1 mm stroke. Both right, different measurements; order against 80 × 45.
