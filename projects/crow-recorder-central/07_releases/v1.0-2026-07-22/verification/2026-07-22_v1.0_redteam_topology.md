## P1 — F1: the protection chain does not survive a wrong-voltage supply

I traced the full path from both the netlist and the PCB (guarding against a hand-edit); they agree exactly:

- `J9.1`(TIP)=`5V_IN` → `F1.1`; `F1.2`=`5V_P` (PTC, i_hold 2 A, v_max 16 V).
- `D9.1`(K/cathode)=`5V_P`, `D9.2`(A/anode)=`GND`.
- `Q9.3`(D)=`5V_P`, `Q9.2`(S)=`5V`, `Q9.1`(G)=`GATE9`; `R90` `GATE9`→`GND`.

This chain correctly handles **reverse polarity** (Q9 blocks, D9 forward-conducts and trips F1) and **ESD** (D9 clamps). It does **not** handle **over-voltage**. The 5.5×2.1 mm barrel is not voltage-keyed and is the single most common connector for 9 V and 12 V wall-warts. On such a miswire: F1 passes (16 V rating), D9 (VBR 6.4–9.2 V) conducts but **clamps at up to 9.2 V**, Q9's source rises with the input so Vgs stays strongly negative and the FET **remains fully enhanced**, delivering ~9 V to the `5V` rail. Every 5 V-rail load — AP61102 ×2 (abs-max **6.5 V**, AP61102/part.yaml:20), XC6227 3V3A LDO (abs-max **6.5 V**, XC6227/part.yaml:19), and the pod +5 V feeds — is over-abs-max for the seconds it takes a 2 A PTC to trip. The AP61102's internal 6.3 V OVP only *stops switching*; the VIN pin still sees the over-voltage, and the XC6227 has no OVP at all. ADR-0002 explicitly scopes only reversal and ESD ("keyed against reversal"; "No UVLO… 5V wall supply") and never addresses over-voltage, so this is an un-mitigated gap, not a documented waiver. Because a correctly-built board on the *specified* 5 V supply is unaffected, this is P1 (order-note / next-rev), not P0.

## P2 — F2/F3/F6 (the substantive rating notes)

- **F2**: SMBJ5.0A VRWM = 5.0 V on a 5.0 V rail is the minimum-margin case; the part is an ESD clamp only, not an OVP. Documented and thermally negligible on a 5 A regulated supply, but there is no standoff headroom.
- **F3**: `U10.5(EN)=5V=VIN` and `U11.5(EN)=BK1_PG` (pulled to 5 V by R12) both put the bucks in **PFM** (EN > VIN−200 mV, AP61102/part.yaml:17). part.yaml:47 recommends forced-PWM for audio. Acceptable here only because both bucks feed digital rails and all analog is LDO-buffered (3V3A via XC6227, 1V8 via TCR2LF18) — but it is contrary to the part's own audio guidance and worth an order-note.
- **F6**: D9's limits (`5V standoff, 9.2V clamp, 600W`) are numerically right for an SMBJ5.0A, but the file carries `sha256: PENDING-FETCH` and `verified: same as SMBJ16A` — a copy artifact citing the 16 V sibling. Per the repo's own evidence rule, confirm the SMBJ5.0A row before order.

## Verified-correct (checked, not defects — these are the traps this lens hunts for)

**V1 — Q9 reverse-FET is oriented RIGHT.** AO3401A body diode: anode=DRAIN, cathode=SOURCE, conducts D→S (AO3401A/part.yaml:15). Board wires DRAIN=`5V_P`(input), SOURCE=`5V`(load) → body diode conducts input→load at power-up, then Vgs=−5 V enhances and shorts it. ADR-0002's literal "drain toward load" prose was wrong; ADR-0007 corrected it and **the netlist AND the PCB both implement the corrected orientation** — no design-doc-vs-netlist drift remains. Vgs at steady state = −5 V, inside the ±12 V abs-max, so no gate divider is needed. This is exactly the self-consistent-but-wrong failure class the prompt flags, and it was avoided.

**V2 — D9 TVS is not a crowbar.** Unidirectional TVS, cathode on the positive rail (`5V_P`), anode on `GND`. Blocks normally, clamps positive transients, forward-conducts only on reverse. Correct.

**V3 — setpoints / sequencing / straps / interfaces:**
- Buck 3V3: 0.6·(1+68k/15k) = **3.32 V** (R10=68k,R11=15k). Buck 0V9: 0.6·(1+10k/20k) = **0.90 V** (R13=10k,R14=20k) = XU316 VDD nominal. Both in range.
- **Sequencing correct:** `U11.EN=BK1_PG` (3V3 power-good) gates the 0V9 core on *after* 3V3. The 1.8 V rail (VDDIOB18) comes up with the LDO right after 3V3, i.e. before the PG-gated + soft-started core — so VDDIOB18 is never "last," satisfying XU316/part.yaml:248.
- **IO-bank straps correct:** VDDIOB18 pins 35/56 and USB_VDD18 pin 62 on `1V8`; LV_L/T/R_N straps (pins 40/43/52) tied to `3V3` = high = 3.3 V mode, matching VDDIOL/R/T all on `3V3`. No 3.3 V-into-1.8 V-bank mis-strap. MIPI unused: MIPI_VDD18/VDD09 (24/27) tied `GND`, data pins NC.
- **USB-C:** R31/R32 = 5.1 k Rd on CC1/CC2 to GND (correct UFP/device). VBUS_DET = 5·330/(220+330) = **3.0 V** into a 3.3 V GPIO — safe. No series R on DP/DM (on-chip HS term), ESD array on both.
- **ADC clocking:** MCLK driven into **SCKI (pin 15, 3.3 V)**; **XI (pin 10) grounded** — avoids the XI 2.1 V abs-max violation (PCM1865/part.yaml:47,55). MD0=`GND`→I2C; AD strap `GND`/`3V3` → 0x4A/0x4B, no bus conflict. NC7NZ34 MCLK buffer: three inputs (pins 1/3/6) tied to `MCLK_SRC`, outputs Y1/Y2 to the two ADCs, unused Y3 (pin 2) left open with its input driven — correct. IOVDD=3V3 matches the 3.3 V XU316 audio banks (IOL/IOT/IOR) driving BCLK/LRCK/DATA.
- **Domain split:** 3V3 (digital buck) vs 3V3A (XC6227 LDO from 5 V) kept separate, joined only inside the ADC and at GND — correct.
