## P2 — N1: the PTC hierarchy has no selectivity; one port faults the whole array

This is the finding the v1.0 memo did not make — it checked every clamp
*rating* but never checked whether the **fuses coordinate**.

The tree is one 2 A main PTC (F1, SMD1812P200TF16: `i_hold 2.0A`, `i_trip 4.0A`,
derates to `1.45A @ 60C` — SMD1812…/part.yaml) feeding **sixteen** 0.5 A branch
PTCs (F11–F18 audio + F21–F28 beep, all MINISMDC050F-2: `i_hold 0.5A`,
`i_trip 1.0A` — confirmed 16 rows in bom.csv). A protection hierarchy only
"discriminates" if the upstream device can carry the sum of downstream *normal*
current plus one branch's *trip* current without itself tripping. Here it
cannot:

- ADR-0002 baseline draw ≈ **1.2 A** (digital-dominated; +5V_AUDIO is only
  ~6 mA/port, beeper 150 mA one-at-a-time).
- A single-port hard short pulls that branch PTC toward its 1.0 A trip. The
  **main** then carries ≈ baseline + fault ≈ 1.0 A + 1.0 A ≈ **2.0 A** — exactly
  its 25 °C hold, and **above** its 60 °C derated hold of 1.45 A.
- Result: inside a warm outdoor enclosure, a fault on ONE cable port trips the
  MAIN PTC and browns out **all six channels + the XU316**, instead of isolating
  the bad port. The branch PTC it was supposed to defer to may never win the
  race.

ADR-0002's headline "2A ≥ 1.7× worst-case draw so no nuisance trips" is a 25 °C
number; at the 60 °C the same datasheet derates to, it is only **1.2×** — and
that is before any branch fault current is added. The main PTC is numerically
smaller than the aggregate its branches can pass (16 × 0.5 A = 8 A), so
selectivity is structurally unachievable at this sizing. Next-rev: either raise
the main PTC (e.g. a 3–4 A hold part, still under the jack rating — see N5) or
accept, in the ORDER_README, that this is single-fault-drops-everything.

## P2 — N2: four of six live RJ45 contacts have no connector-local ESD clamp

ADR-0002 states the cable ports "leave the enclosure — ESD enters here too,"
and the pod cable is an outdoor ~35 ft run (ADR-0002:34). Yet the only
connector-local clamp per port is one TPD2E2U06 (D21–D28) across **AUD_P/N**.
The RJ45 carries four other externally-exposed contacts with **no** local TVS:

- +5V_AUDIO (pins 4,7) and +5V_BEEP (pin 3) → straight onto the 5 V rail /
  branch-PTC node; the only rail clamp is D9, physically at the far south-west
  power corner, reachable only back through the inductive branch PTC.
- BEEP_RET (pin 6) → **bare AO3400A drain** (Vds abs-max 30 V, no local clamp).
  The v1.0 memo's F5 flagged this drain for the *inductive*-kick case; the ESD
  case (a strike on the exposed RJ45 return contact) is a second, independent
  exposure on the same unprotected node.

The audio pair is the right thing to protect first, so this is P2, not a
blocker — but "ESD enters at the ports" is only two-thirds solved. A pod
plugging/unplugging event or a lightning-adjacent field transient couples onto
the power/beep/return contacts with only the IC's internal structures behind
them. Next-rev: an IEC-rated flow-through TVS (or the beep contact into a
clamped drain) on the RJ45 power/return contacts.

## P2 — N3: the skew-injection tap is wired into a POPULATED channel

ARCHITECTURE.md:51-54 describes the J10 injection as coupling "ONE test signal …
into ADC1's and ADC2's **spare-channel** input networks … series resistors keep
later J7/J8 population legal." The netlist disagrees for ADC1:

- `INJ` (J10.1) → C90i (1 µF) → `INJ_C` → **R80 (1k) → AIN_P4** and R81 (1k) →
  AIN_P8 (net codes 27/31/104/105).
- `AIN_P4` = ADC1 VIN4 = **port 4**, and J4 is **populated** (RJHSE-5384 covers
  J1–J6; only J7/J8 are DNP). ADC1 has no spare — all four channels are live
  ports — so the tap had to land on a live one.

Consequences the design docs do not state: (a) port 4's live mic input carries a
permanent 1k + 1µF stub to the injection header (and, through R80/R81/INJ_C, a
DC-blocked bridge toward AIN_P8) — this is also the physical reason AIN_P4
stretches 96 mm (redteam L3); (b) **you cannot record port 4 while running the
inter-chip-skew injection** — the injected signal contaminates it. The v1.1
source-compliance pass backfilled the P-ADJ span waiver for AIN_P4 but did not
reconcile the "spare-channel" claim with the implemented tap. Functional, not a
rating breach — but it is a doc-vs-netlist drift the intent gate (E-INV/E-ADR)
exists to catch, and an operator limitation that belongs in the ORDER_README.

## P2 — N4: the governing protection ADR still contradicts the netlist it governs

The whole premise of this repo's E-INV/E-ADR gates (electrical_invariants.yaml
preamble) is that the ADRs and the netlist must AGREE, because the usb-hub D1
reverse-polarity defect passed every self-consistency gate and only *intent*
disagreed. Yet ADR-0002:32 still reads **"Q9 AO3401A P-FET reverse-polarity
guard (drain toward load…)"** — the precise reversed-orientation prose that
ADR-0007 was written to correct. The netlist is correct (E-INV asserts
Q9.3/DRAIN = 5V_P = input), so this is not a build defect; the v1.0 memo already
noted it under V1. I re-raise it because the v1.1 "source-compliance" commit
claimed to reconcile source with intent and **left the primary protection ADR
literally stating the wrong FET orientation.** A future reader trusting ADR-0002
over ADR-0007 re-introduces the defect. One-line fix to the ADR body; no metal.

## P2 — N5: the barrel jack is the weakest link in a downstream fault

DC-005C-20A is rated `DC 24V 3A` (part.yaml). The named supply is 5 V / **5 A**
(ADR-0002:12) and the main PTC trips at **4 A** (i_trip). In normal operation the
PTC holds board draw ≤ 2 A < 3 A, so the jack is fine steady-state. But during a
downstream fault the PTC allows ~3–4 A to flow for the seconds it takes to trip
near its threshold — through a 3 A-rated connector. The jack, not the PTC, is
the current-rating floor of the entry. Low probability / transient, hence P2,
but it interacts with N1: raising the main PTC to fix selectivity must stay under
the jack's 3 A rating (or the jack must be upgraded too).

## Clamp-vs-protected rating pairs I checked and cleared (not defects)

- **TPD2E2U06 (audio ESD) vs PCM1865 VIN** (`vin_analog_abs −1.7..+5.0V`): the
  clamp sits at the connector *ahead of* the 1 µF AC-coupling caps, so the ADC
  pin is DC-isolated from the connector node; VRWM 5.5 V comfortably brackets the
  ~2.5 V-biased audio line. OK.
- **TPD4EUSB30 (USB ESD) vs XU316 USB_DP/DM**: standard USB-2.0 HS clamp
  (vclamp 8 V @1 A, cio 0.8 pF) on a 3.3 V PHY; VBUS sensed-only, C31 1 µF
  present per §11.1. OK.
- **1V8 / 3V3A single-source**: 1V8 driven only by U12 (VOUT), 3V3A only by U13
  — so TCR2LF18's "do not parallel another supply into VOUT" auto-discharge
  caveat is satisfied. OK.
- **Beeper inductive kick (prior F5)**: with the ADR-0005 ~100 µs slowed gate,
  di/dt on the ~17 µH cable is ~1.5 kA/s → V = L·di/dt ≈ 26 mV, negligible vs the
  30 V drain rating. The slowed edge genuinely de-fangs the inductive case
  (the ESD case remains — see N2). OK on inductance.

**VERDICT: ORDER** — no P0. The protection-chain orientations (reverse-FET,
TVS, center-positive jack, no VBUS/rail contention) are re-verified correct on
the current netlist, and the ESD/clamp rating pairs that route to sensitive ICs
are DC-isolated or standard-part. Nothing here blocks ordering the sealed board.
But this pass adds three items the v1.0 memo and the v1.1 source-compliance
commit both missed and that the ORDER_README/next-rev work order should carry:
**N1** (PTC hierarchy cannot discriminate — a single-port fault browns out the
whole array, worse at temperature), **N3** (skew injection sits on a live
channel — port 4 is unrecordable during injection, contra the "spare-channel"
docs), and **N2** (ESD covers only 2 of 6 exposed RJ45 contacts). N4/N5 are
one-line source/next-rev cleanups. The prior P1 (F1, no OVP) is unchanged and
remains a mandatory "5 V-ONLY supply" ORDER_README note.
