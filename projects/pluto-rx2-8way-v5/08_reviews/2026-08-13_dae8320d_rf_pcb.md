  meander, layer change or RF via. Current saved copper lengths are
  14.5039 mm (common), 22.4079 mm (ANT1/ANT8), 35.0974 mm (ANT2/ANT7),
  31.5010 mm (ANT3/ANT6), and 36.5926 mm (ANT4/ANT5). Exact mirror pairs
  remain equal; phase matching between independently selected paths is not a
  design requirement.

## Launches, return paths and fence

- J2-J10 reproduce the Amphenol 901-143-6RFX Rev-C launch: one 1.50-mm RF
  hole in a 2.40-mm pad and four 1.70-mm ground holes in 2.80-mm pads on the
  +/-2.54-mm grid. The 11.6-mm signal-pin-to-mating-face datum places every
  face on its intended north, west or east board edge with zero datum error.
- In1.Cu contains one filled GND zone and no routed track. Sampling every RF
  centreline against the exact filled polygon finds continuous reference
  copper after the expected 1.39-1.42-mm signal-PTH antipad at each SMA.
  The four connector ground posts, including the inward pair at 2.54 mm,
  provide the launch return; U1's alternating perimeter grounds feed pad 25
  and its protected 3x3 GND-via field.
- The saved-board route-aware fence audit grades all 18 RF flanks. All pass
  the <=1.4000-mm guided-wavelength aperture bound; the worst interval is
  1.3979 mm on the RF_ANT8 right flank. Each route uses the exact connector
  and U1 package-owned endpoint structures rather than crediting the coarse
  whole-board stitching grid.
