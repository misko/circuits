# True-final independent RF schematic review

This review starts from the exact commit-bound artifact and current local
manufacturer evidence. I reopened pSemi DOC-75785-4, official local ST
DS13866 Rev 5, the exact part dossiers, `rf.yaml`, control contracts, the
schematic and all four PDF pages. A fresh KiCad S-expression export passes
32/32 electrical invariants, 21/21 label survival and 131/131 physical pin-map
assertions. Schematic-to-saved-board parity covers 22 nets, 131 connected
nodes and 24 no-connects with zero discrepancy. Fresh ERC has zero errors;
the known 190-warning presentation/library baseline does not determine this
connectivity verdict.

requirement: RF-SCH-TOPOLOGY PASS

U1 is one PE42482A-X absorptive SP8T. `RF_COMMON` joins only J2.1 and
U1.22/RFC. `RF_ANT1` through `RF_ANT8` join J3.1 through J10.1 in order to U1
RF1-RF8. All SMA shells are grounded. There is no splitter, switch tree,
parallel RF branch or simultaneous output. With LS low, pSemi Table 5 selects
exactly one throw per approved code and defines `V4..V1=1000` as terminated
all-off.

requirement: RF-SCH-PINMAP PASS

The fresh netlist reproduces pSemi Table 8: RF2/3/4 are pins 2/4/6;
RF5/6/7/8 are pins 13/15/17/19; RFC is pin 22; RF1 is pin 24; VDD is pin 8;
V1-V4 are pins 9-12; LS is pin 1. Ground pins 3/5/7/14/16/18/21/23 and pad 25
are grounded; allowed NC pin 20 is explicit no-connect. Official local ST
Rev 5 confirms the exact TSSOP-20 sequence: PA0-PA3 are U2 pins 7-10 and
PA13/PA14 are pins 18/19. Thus PA0-PA3 own V1-V4 in order. The eight words
0000, 0100, 0010, 0110, 0001, 0101, 0011 and 0111 match RF1-RF8.

requirement: RF-SCH-DC PASS

U1 VDD is regulated 3V3 within its 2.3-5.5 V range, with C4 as local 100 nF
bypass. Each RF net has one SMA centre and one U1 RF pin, with no intentional
DC source. DOC-75785-4 requires all nine RF pins at 0 VDC. No DC blocks are
fitted, so biased antennas, bias tees and DC-bearing RF sources are outside
scope.

requirement: RF-SCH-DEFAULT PASS

LS is hard-low. R3 pulls V4 high with 10 kohm; R4-R6 pull V1-V3 low with
10 kohm. Official ST Rev 5 confirms the MCU pins and supply/BOR boundaries;
reset or tri-state therefore leaves the passive `1000` word while U1 is
validly powered. A 5 uA maximum U1 control current shifts a 10-kohm pull only
50 mV. The generated profile passes 8/8 states and 8/8 windows, preloads
all-off, writes approved words atomically and uses 5 ms all-off guards versus
1.4 us maximum switch settling. No RF state is claimed below 2.3 V U1 VDD.

not include board launches/traces; return-loss values are typical. Calibrated
first-article VNA testing remains mandatory for every path and required off
state.
