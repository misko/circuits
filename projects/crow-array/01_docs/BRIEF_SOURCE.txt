Why six microphones instead of four
-----------------------------------

Four microphones are enough to prove the concept, but six microphones create 15 unique pairs, provide
all-around geometry, and allow the solver to reject one weak, obstructed, or wind-contaminated channel
without falling back to the minimum viable geometry.

Unique microphone pairs = M(M - 1) / 2 = 6 x 5 / 2 = 15

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| DESIGN FREEZE                                                                                            |
| Proceed with an eight-channel-capable central board and six field pods. Populate four channels during    |
| bring-up, then add the final two after USB, analog noise, and cable crosstalk are proven.                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+


2 ARRAY GEOMETRY AND TIMING
===========================

A 25 ft radius is already a very large acoustic aperture.

[Figure 2 image omitted in this text edition; see its caption and the PDF.]

Figure 2. Nominal hexagonal geometry; software must use the measured x, y and z coordinates.

Adjacent spacing = 2R sin(30 deg) = 2 x 25 ft x 0.5 = 25 ft

Maximum acoustic delay = 50 ft / 1125 ft/s = 44.4 ms

Maximum delay at 48 kHz = 0.0444 s x 48,000 = about 2,133 samples

+-----------------------+---------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Metric                | Value         | Interpretation                            |
+=======================+===============+===========================================+
| Radius                | 25 ft         | Nominal center-to-pod distance            |
+-----------------------+---------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Diameter              | 50 ft         | Maximum microphone-to-microphone baseline |
+-----------------------+---------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Angular spacing       | 60 deg        | Six evenly distributed positions          |
+-----------------------+---------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Adjacent chord        | 25 ft         | Useful medium-length baselines            |
+-----------------------+---------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Maximum TDOA          | 44.4 ms       | Across opposite microphones               |
+-----------------------+---------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Maximum sample offset | 2,133 samples | Large, easily searchable delay window     |
+-----------------------+---------------+-------------------------------------------+

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| IMPORTANT                                                                                                |
| The microphones do not need to form a perfect hexagon. Localization accuracy depends on knowing their    |
| actual coordinates and fixed channel delays, not on physically forcing ideal spacing.                    |

3A POD MECHANICS AND CALIBRATION TRANSDUCER
===========================================

The acoustic opening is the highest-risk physical detail.


Low-cost built-in transducer
----------------------------

The CMT-8504-100-SMT-TR is driven from the central board through a dedicated twisted pair and an AO3400A
low-side MOSFET. Only one transducer operates at a time. A coded 4 kHz carrier is used instead of a
continuous tone, enabling matched filtering and absolute burst detection.

+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Item                 | Rev-A target                                                       |
+======================+====================================================================+
| Nominal drive        | 5 V, approximately 150 mA                                          |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Carrier              | Near 4 kHz resonance                                               |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Recommended envelope | 31-chip pseudorandom on/off code                                   |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Chip duration        | 5-10 ms                                                            |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Repeats              | 4 per pod                                                          |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Inter-pod quiet time | About 500 ms                                                       |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Local microphone     | Exclude during its own transducer burst if clipped or mechanically |
|                      | coupled                                                            |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Primary purpose      | Health check, channel identity, movement detection and geometry    |
|                      | refinement                                                         |
+----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+


Mechanical guidance
-------------------

- Mount the microphone and transducer in separate small acoustic cavities.

- Place them at opposite ends of the enclosure or move the transducer 100-150 mm down the stake if
  structural vibration is excessive.

- Use a downward-facing or side-facing port under a rain hood, with drainage and replaceable acoustic
  foam.

- Conformal-coat the PCB but exclude the microphone, transducer, connectors and test points.

- Treat IP ratings as invalid after custom acoustic holes unless the complete housing is tested.

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| FIELD-TEST GATE                                                                                          |
| Before freezing the mechanical design, prove that the coded transducer is detectable at 25 ft and 50 ft  |
| through the intended hood or membrane, in wind, and without a dominant zero-delay electrical artifact.   |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+


4 CABLING AND PINOUT
====================

One low-cost outdoor Cat5e star run carries audio, pod power and beeper control.

Use 24 AWG solid bare-copper outdoor Cat5e. Do not use copper-clad aluminum. Each pod receives its own
home-run cable to the central enclosure; the custom pinout is not Ethernet.

+--------------+-------------+--------------------------------+
| T568B pair   | RJ45 pins   | Assignment                     |
+==============+=============+================================+
| Orange       | 1, 2        | AUDIO+, AUDIO-                 |
+--------------+-------------+--------------------------------+
| Green        | 3, 6        | +5V_BEEP, BEEP_SWITCHED_RETURN |
+--------------+-------------+--------------------------------+
| Blue         | 4, 5        | +5V_AUDIO, GND_AUDIO           |
+--------------+-------------+--------------------------------+
| Brown        | 7, 8        | +5V_AUDIO, GND_AUDIO           |
+--------------+-------------+--------------------------------+


Cable-length and voltage-drop assumptions
-----------------------------------------

Allow approximately 30-35 ft per pod, including routing and a service loop. Six 35 ft runs consume about 210
ft of cable. A 250 ft reel is minimally sufficient; 500 ft gives useful installation and repair margin.

Approximate beeper drop = 0.15 A x 1.8 ohm = 0.27 V on a 35 ft loop

Approximate pod-power drop = 0.005 A x 0.9 ohm = 4.5 mV using two parallel power loops


Termination and safety
----------------------

- At the pod, pass the cable through a gland and terminate directly on a PCB terminal block or solder
  pads.

- At the center, use internal RJ45 jacks behind glands or a sealed bulkhead. Keep the custom connectors
  inaccessible to ordinary network equipment.

- Label every cable and enclosure: NOT ETHERNET - CUSTOM 5 V AUDIO PINOUT.

- Use ESD suppressors at cable entry. They do not replace lightning protection or a storm-disconnection
  policy.

- Start with unshielded cable; reserve shield-bonding pads for later EMI testing.

Clocking and USB choices
------------------------

- Use the XU316 application PLL for the lowest-cost fixed-48 kHz implementation.

- Distribute MCLK, BCLK, LRCK, reset and configuration timing to both PCM1865 devices.

- Carry one four-channel TDM stream from each ADC into the XU316.

- Expose six channels by default and reserve an eight-channel diagnostic mode.

- Use asynchronous USB Audio Class 2 so the recorder clock, not the host, is the timing authority.

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| REFERENCE-DESIGN STRATEGY                                                                                |
| The XMOS multichannel audio platform uses an XU316 with two PCM1865 devices for eight analog inputs.     |
| Copy its power sequencing, clocking and USB implementation closely rather than inventing these sections  |
| from scratch.                                                                                            |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Per-cable central interface
---------------------------

Each cable port should provide ESD protection, power-current limiting, differential AC coupling into the
ADC, and a separately returned low-side beeper driver. Slow beeper MOSFET edges modestly; fast edges provide
no acoustic benefit and increase crosstalk.


PCB and enclosure recommendations
---------------------------------

- Use four PCB layers minimum; six layers are preferred.

- Keep USB routing controlled and short, with a continuous ground reference.

- Place the ADCs and input RC networks in a quiet analog region away from switching regulators and beeper
  traces.

- Provide eight cable-port footprints, six populated in Rev-A.

- Add test points for every rail, MCLK/BCLK/LRCK, both TDM data lines and the beeper returns.

- Include a same-signal injection header so channels on different ADC chips can be checked for
  deterministic skew.

- Keep the Pi thermally separate unless a larger sealed enclosure has been tested under worst-case summer
  conditions.


6 CALIBRATION AND HEALTH CHECKS
===============================

Calibration separates physical geometry, fixed channel delay, gain response and sound-speed effects.

+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Stage                             | Procedure                                                             |
+===================================+=======================================================================+
| 1. Physical survey                | Measure all six radii, all six adjacent distances, three opposite     |
|                                   | distances, and microphone heights. Fit actual coordinates with least  |
|                                   | squares.                                                              |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Colocated timing test          | Temporarily place microphones close together and play repeated        |
|                                   | broadband chirps to estimate fixed electronic and transducer delay    |
|                                   | offsets.                                                              |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. In-place broadband calibration | Use a portable speaker at 8-12 surveyed locations inside and around   |
|                                   | the ring. Refine coordinates, fixed delays and effective sound speed. |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. Built-in maintenance cycle     | Operate the six pod transducers one at a time and compare matched-    |
|                                   | filter TDOAs and received levels with the saved baseline.             |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5. Long-term trend checks         | Track noise floor, gain, calibration residuals, temperature and       |
|                                   | channel dropouts over time.                                           |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+

Measured TDOA(i,j,k) = (distance(source k, mic i) - distance(source k, mic j)) / c + delay_i - delay_j

Pairwise TDOA removes the unknown acoustic launch time of the inexpensive transducer. Six emissions and 15
microphone pairs provide as many as 90 raw pairwise constraints, although they are not statistically
independent.

+------------------+-------------------------------+--------------+
| Position error   | Equivalent propagation time   | At 48 kHz    |
+==================+===============================+==============+
| 1 mm             | 2.9 us                        | 0.14 sample  |
+------------------+-------------------------------+--------------+
| 1 cm             | 29 us                         | 1.40 samples |
+------------------+-------------------------------+--------------+
| 1 inch / 2.54 cm | 74 us                         | 3.55 samples |
+------------------+-------------------------------+--------------+
| 10 cm            | 292 us                        | 14.0 samples |
+------------------+-------------------------------+--------------+
| 1 ft / 30.5 cm   | 0.89 ms                       | 42.7 samples |
+------------------+-------------------------------+--------------+

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PRACTICAL TARGET                                                                                         |
| Measure physical coordinates to roughly 1-2 inches, then use acoustic calibration to improve the         |
| effective model. Recalibrate whenever a stand moves, a pod is serviced, or the daily residuals shift     |
| materially.                                                                                              |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

GCC-PHAT workload estimate
--------------------------

With 4096-sample windows, 50% overlap and six microphones, the system processes about 23.4 windows per
second. Each window requires six forward FFTs and 15 inverse FFTs, or roughly 492 total 4096-point FFT
operations per second. This is a modest workload for optimized native libraries on a Raspberry Pi 5.

Windows/s = 48,000 / 2,048 = 23.4

FFT operations/s = 23.4 x (6 forward + 15 inverse) = about 492

+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Task                         | Pi 5 suitability                     |
+==============================+======================================+
| Acquisition and USB capture  | Easy                                 |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Band-pass filtering and call | Easy                                 |
| detection                    |                                      |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| 15-pair GCC-PHAT             | Easy                                 |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Robust 2D nonlinear solve    | Easy                                 |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Spectrogram generation       | Easy                                 |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Small quantized classifier   | Likely real time                     |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Large audio transformer      | Possible but not the design baseline |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
| Model training               | Use a desktop or GPU                 |
+------------------------------+--------------------------------------+


Recommended Pi subsystem
------------------------

- Raspberry Pi 5, 8 GB

- Active cooler

- Separate high-quality 5 V / 5 A supply

- 500 GB minimum NVMe; 1 TB preferred

- Wired Ethernet when practical

- Hardware watchdog and hardened or read-only system partition

- Event-triggered clip storage rather than indefinite raw recording

9 CONSTRAINTS AND OPEN RISKS
============================

The central questions to answer before a production revision.

+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Risk                 | Consequence                     | Mitigation / test                                 |
+======================+=================================+===================================================+
| Acoustic             | The membrane, hood and foam can | Build and compare several pod openings outdoors.  |
| weatherproofing      | change sensitivity and phase.   |                                                   |
+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Wind and foliage     | Signals may correlate poorly    | Use wind protection, band limiting and robust     |
|                      | across the full aperture.       | pair rejection.                                   |
+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Beeper electrical    | PWM edges may appear as a zero- | Separate return pair, slow edges, differential    |
| crosstalk            | delay artifact.                 | audio, reject zero-delay peak.                    |
+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Mechanical beeper    | The local microphone may        | Isolate the transducer and exclude the local      |
| coupling             | receive enclosure vibration.    | channel.                                          |
+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Two ADC devices      | A fixed inter-chip latency may  | Share clocks/reset and verify with same-signal    |
|                      | exist.                          | injection.                                        |
+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Coplanar geometry    | Height is weakly constrained.   | Reserve channel 7 or 8 for an elevated            |
|                      |                                 | microphone.                                       |
+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Custom RJ45 pinout   | Accidental network connection   | Internalize connectors and apply conspicuous      |
|                      | can damage equipment.           | labeling.                                         |
+----------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
| Outdoor conductors   | ESD and lightning exposure      | Add TVS/PTC, disconnect during storms, engineer   |

=============================

A staged plan that isolates electrical, acoustic and software risk.

+--------+-------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+

|        |                               | beeper crosstalk.                                 |

+--------+-------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+

|        |                               | central prototype to establish stable Pi capture. |

| transducer; one custom outdoor Cat5e run per pod; two shared-clock PCM1865 ADCs; an industrial XU316 USB |

- Beeper flyback diode versus zener/TVS clamp
