# Red-team review A — usb-hub-3s v1.0-2026-07-21 (topology / protection / ratings)

pin-to-net nodes, not prose), `fab/bom.csv` + `fab/cpl.csv`, `02_parts/*/part.yaml`,

## What I verified as CORRECT (evidence-checked, not assumed)

- **Reverse-polarity FET orientation**: Q1 (AON6403, P-ch, −30 V/±20 V Vgs,
  3.1 mΩ@−10 V) drain=VBAT_F, source=VIN (net `VBAT_F: Q1.5(D)`, `VIN: Q1.1-3(S)`).
  Body diode conducts battery→load at first contact; gate to GND via R13 100 k
  (`RPP_G`); D5 BZT52C12 K=VIN(source)/A=gate clamps |Vgs|≤~12 V < 20 V abs.
  Without D5 a 24.4 V input clamp event would put Vgs at −24 V; the zener is
  present and correctly oriented. Under reversal Q1 stays off (Vgs ≥ 0),
  |Vds| ≤ 12.6 V < 30 V.
- **Buck-boost H-bridge wiring** matches IP6559 Fig. 8 exactly: VIN→RS2(5 mΩ)→
  VIN_S→Q4(HG2)→LX2→L1→LX1→Q6(HG1)→VOUT_PDS→RS3(5 mΩ)→VOUT_PD; Q5/Q7 low-side
  to GND; BST caps, 2 Ω+1 nF LX snubbers, 10 Ω+1 µF Kelvin sense filters
  (CSP2/CSN2 pins 26/25, CSP1/CSN1 pins 14/13), PCIN(24)=VIN_S, PCON(15)=VOUT_PDS,
  VOUTI(10)+VIO(12)=VOUT_PD, VOUT2(48) via R20 10 Ω to VBUSC, path FET Q8
  D=VOUT_PD/S=VBUSC/G=VOUT2G(1). P-taps on the current-entry ends of both shunts.
- **LM5116 buck**: boot diode D4 (1N4148WS) A=VCC_A/K=BOOT_A (present — no
  internal boot diode), VCCX(17)=GND, DEMB(11)=GND, source-shunt CS via RS1
  10 mΩ with R7/R8 0 Ω Kelvin taps, FB divider 3.74 k/1.21 k → 4.97 V, RT 12.4 k,
  RAMP 330 pF, SS 10 nF — all match SNVS499I worked design; recomputed values agree.
- **UVLO divider math (typicals)**: 49.9 k/6.98 k, K=8.149, Rth=6.12 k. With DS
  values (1.215 V, 5.4 µA pull-up, 0.1 V hyst — SNVS499I EC table): rising 9.63 V,
  falling 8.82 V. Docs claim 9.65/8.84 (used 5.0 µA) — agreement within 20 mV.
- **IP6559 EN gating**: `EN_PD: R21(10k from 5VA) R22(10k to GND) U1.5` — 2.5 V
  when 5VA up, hard pull-down when down; ADR-0001's variant-semantics risk is
  documented with a bench-first ritual.
- **PDO strap discipline**: R25 (GPIO0→GND slot) is absent from bom.csv and
  cpl.csv (110 placements, R25/F1 excluded; R7-the-CS-filter correctly placed).
  ADR-0004's DNP decision is implemented in the fab package.
- **TPS2557 ports**: EN active-high tied to IN=5VA (correct variant), RILIM
  36.5 k → IOS 2.72–3.29 A (recomputed from SLVS931B formulas: 2.71–3.28 A ✓),
  FAULT floated, 22 µF 25 V + 100 nF per port. TPS2513A (the A variant, C473910)
  channel mapping U6→ports 1+2, U7→port 3 verified pin-by-pin against SLVSBY8D.
- **ESD partitioning**: USBLC6-2SC6 (VBUS pin 5.25 V max) used ONLY on the 5 V
  A ports, pass-through pairs 1-6/3-4 correct; C-port DPC/DMC use rail-independent
  LESD5D5.0 (D8/D9); DM/DP abs max 6 V respected; CC1/CC2 (A5/B5 only, not
  cross-tied) go direct to U1 per DS Fig. 8. Vconn chain (5VA→SS210→VCONN5V→
  AO3401A switches, 2N7002 drivers, GPIO22→CC1 / GPIO21→CC2) matches Fig. 9.
- **Backfeed on VBUSC**: Q8's body diode (S=VBUSC) conducts external VBUS into
  VOUT_PD when off — cannot block back-drive. Checked consequences: 20 V
  back-drive charges VOUT_PD to ~19.3 V; VOUTI/VIO abs max 25 V, caps 25 V — no
  damage path. This is the DS reference topology (adjudicated in
  verification_report.md); ACCEPT.
- **Ratings that pass**: XT60PW-M 30 A ≥ 15.6 A worst; TYPE-C-31-M-12A 5 A/20 V
  rating = port max; SMBJ15A clamp 24.4 V < AON6403/AON6354 30 V, < IP6559 VIN
  abs 34 V, ≤ 25 V VIN ceramics (GRM32DR71E106KA12L, transient), < 35 V bulk;
  L2 buck inductor 7 A rms / 8.4 A pk vs 10–12 A rms / 15.2 A sat; RS2 0.71 W /
  RS1 0.25 W vs 4 W / 1 W; Q1 0.76 W at 15.6 A; fuse-holder 20 A/32 V; buck
  output caps are 16 V (EMK325ABJ107MM), better than the 6.3 V the doc claims.

## FINDINGS

### P1-1 — TVS D1 is on VBAT_F (before Q1), contradicting DETAIL_DESIGN/ARCHITECTURE, and its reverse-battery behavior is sacrificial and unanalyzed
**Evidence**: netlist `VBAT_F: D1.1(K) F1.2 Q1.5(D)`; tsx line 72–76 comments
"D1 input TVS on VBAT_F (before Q1)"; `02_parts/SMBJ15A/part.yaml` records
"cathode(1) → VBAT_F". But DETAIL_DESIGN.md §1: "**D1** SMBJ15A across VIN
**after Q1**"; ARCHITECTURE.md power tree draws D1 on VIN after Q1; ADR-0001's
decision line reads "fuse → P-FET → TVS + bulk → VIN" (its options text says
"after the fuse" — the ADR is internally inconsistent).
**Consequence (computed)**: with a reversed pack, the unidirectional SMBJ15A is
FORWARD-biased through F1. Loop is pack ESR + fuse + wiring ≈ 20–50 mΩ →
~250–600 A until F1 (20 A MINI) melts (~1–10 ms at that overdrive). SMBJ forward
surge rating is ~100 A/8.3 ms — D1 will likely die (typically short, which still
crowbars). Downstream stays safe either way (Q1 blocks), but every reversal
costs a fuse and probably D1. In the documented position (VIN, behind Q1) a
reversal is entirely benign — no parts consumed.
**Correction**: pick one intent and make the artifacts agree. Either (a) move
D1 to VIN in the next spin and keep reversal non-sacrificial (matches
DETAIL_DESIGN; hot-plug clamping is equivalent through the enhanced Q1), or
(b) keep the crowbar position and document the sacrificial behavior + fuse/TVS
replacement in ADR-0001, DETAIL_DESIGN §1 and ORDER_README. No fab change is
strictly required — the board is protected in both topologies.

### P1-2 — Below-UVLO residual drain is understated ~6–10×: LM5116 standby current is missing from the budget
**Evidence**: DETAIL_DESIGN §2 and ADR-0001 claim "residual ≈ 0.5 mA (IP6559
200 µA + UVLO divider 221 µA + LM5116 standby)". Netlist: `EN_A: R9(100k)`
from VIN — EN is always HIGH, so the LM5116 never enters its 10 µA shutdown
(ISTDBY spec requires EN=0 V, SNVS499I). Below UVLO it sits in STANDBY with the
**VCC regulator running** (SNVS499I §6.3.3 / UVLO pin description). The DS has
no standby-current spec for this state; the nearest bound is IBIAS = 5–7 mA
(operating, no gate load). Realistic standby draw is ~1–5 mA, not "~µA".
**Consequence**: total post-cutoff drain is plausibly 1.5–5 mA, not 0.5 mA. A
2 Ah 3S pack cut off at 8.8 V (~10–15 % SoC) reaches deep over-discharge in
days, not months. "Do not store the pack connected" (ORDER_README) becomes a
hard rule, and the ADR's "acceptable" disposition rests on a wrong number.
**Correction**: measure the actual below-UVLO input current in the first-power
ritual and write it into ORDER_README. For v2 consider driving EN (not just
UVLO) from the divider so the part reaches true 10 µA shutdown, or a hard
P-FET cutoff.

### P1-3 — L1 operates at ~100 % of its RMS rating at the low-battery/full-power corner
**Evidence**: L1 = MWSA1707S-100MT: Irms_40C = 12 A, Isat 16.5 A, DCR 9.9 mΩ
(part.yaml). Computed at Vout 20 V/5 A, Vin = 8.84 V (UVLO floor), stage
eff 0.95: I_avg = 11.9 A, ripple 2.0 App (250 kHz per IP6559 EC table, 10 µH),
I_RMS ≈ 11.9 A, peak 12.9 A. That is 99 % of the 40 °C-rise rating (≈1.4 W in
the winding), sitting next to the four H-bridge FETs; with L at −20 % tolerance
and the worst-corner UVLO floor (8.3 V, see P2-4) it slightly exceeds rating.
The part.yaml gotcha admits "runs at rating". Saturation margin is fine
(12.9 A < 16.5 A).
**Correction**: before advertising sustained 100 W at low battery, thermal-test
the inductor at 9.0 V in / 20 V/5 A out. Mitigations if hot: derate PD power at
low VIN (the unused GPIO0/NTC derating hook), or a 12 A+ rms inductor in v2.

### P2-1 — SMAJ24A on VBUSC cannot protect the 25 V-abs-max devices behind it
**Evidence**: `VBUSC: D3.1(K)`; SMAJ24A Vbr 26.7–29.5 V, Vc(max) 38.9 V.
Protected devices: IP6559 VOUT1/VOUT2/VOUTI abs max 25 V (IP6559_V1.4 §6, via
Q8 body diode minus ~0.7 V), C26/C28/C29 rated 25 V. Any event that makes the
TVS conduct already exceeds 25 V. Only the 30 V FETs are truly under its
umbrella. Note: no SMA TVS can fit a 21 V-working/25 V-abs window — real OVP is
the chip's internal loop + 4 kV pin ESD; the TVS only tames large cable surges.
**Correction**: none practical at part level; document that D3 is surge-grade
(not abs-max-grade) protection so nobody later "relies" on it.

### P2-2 — SMAJ30A LX clamps break down above the FETs' 30 V Vds rating
**Evidence**: D6/D7 on LX2/LX1 (`LX1: D7.1(K)`, `LX2: D6.1(K)`); SMAJ30A
Vbr 33.3–36.8 V, Vc up to 48.4 V. Q4–Q7 = AON6354: Vds 30 V, vspike_10us 36 V
(part.yaml). At the TVS's high breakdown corner (36.8 V) the FET's 10 µs spike
rating is already exceeded before the TVS conducts; at any surge current the
clamp is far above it. The FETs protect the TVS, not vice versa. The DS Fig. 7/8
calls for this part, and ns-scale ringing is really handled by the 2 Ω/1 nF
snubbers + FET avalanche, so this is coordination-in-name-only, not a defect.
**Correction**: accept (DS-conformant) but strike any claim that the LX TVS
"protects the FETs"; the true spike defenses are the snubbers and Vds margin.

### P2-3 — Fuse coordination is loose: 20 A fuse over 16 A-rated copper, no fuse MPN in the release, 78 % continuous loading
**Evidence**: worst continuous input 15.6 A at 8.84 V (recomputed: (100/0.95 +
30/0.9)/8.84). F1 = 20 A MINI blade — 78 % of rating (blade-fuse practice is
≤75 % continuous). DETAIL_DESIGN §1: "wiring/pours sized 16 A continuous" — a
sustained 17–26 A overload (below the ~135 % min-melt) would cook 16 A copper
without opening the fuse. Realistic exposure is low (both converters
current-limit; true shorts are >>27 A), but the classic "fuse protects the
wire" rule is not met. ORDER_README's hand-solder list says only "20 A MINI
blade fuse" — no MPN, voltage rating, or speed class (the 3568 part.yaml gotcha
names Littelfuse 0297020 but the release doc does not).
**Correction**: put an exact fuse MPN (e.g. Littelfuse 0297020.WXNV, 32 V) in
ORDER_README, and note the 75 %-rule tension (or move to 25 A copper floors /
a 17.5 A fuse decision in v2).

### P2-4 — UVLO at datasheet corners reaches 2.77 V/cell, and cell imbalance is invisible
**Evidence**: recomputed with SNVS499I limits (threshold 1.170–1.262 V; hyst
0.1 V and pull-up 5.4 µA are typ-only specs) and 1 % resistors: falling
threshold spans **8.30–9.36 V** (2.77–3.12 V/cell); adding ±30 % on the
unspecified hyst/pull-up widens it to 7.98–9.70 V (2.66 V/cell floor). Rising
spans 9.0–10.3 V. The docs state only typicals (9.65/8.84). Additionally the
cutoff is pack-level: a 150 mV-weak cell in an unbalanced pack sits at ~2.6 V
at the low corner while the board still runs. Combined with P1-2's drain this
is the board's real over-discharge exposure.
**Correction**: document the corner band (not just typicals) in DETAIL_DESIGN
§2; state in ORDER_README that packs must have their own BMS/balance protection
(this board protects the pack average only).

### P2-5 — Stale/contradictory design docs (three concrete instances)
1. **ARCHITECTURE.md power tree still describes a deleted UVLO circuit**:
   "UVLO: U10 TLV431 + Q13 sense VIN, gate U9 EN (GPIO18)". No TLV431/Q13
   exists in the netlist (U10 is a USBLC6), and ADR-0001 explicitly REJECTED
   the TLV431. Also "≥9.4 V rising" vs the derived 9.65 V.
2. **Refdes drift between DETAIL_DESIGN and the build**: the doc's R100/R101/
   R102/R103–106 are the netlist's R13/R21/R22/R23–27; the doc's "R7 (GPIO0)
   … DNP" is the netlist's **R25**, while the netlist's R7 is a POPULATED 0 Ω
   CS-filter resistor (`CSF_A: R7.2 U2.12`). Anyone applying DETAIL_DESIGN §5's
   "R7 DNP" to the fab BOM would depopulate the LM5116 current-sense path.
   ORDER_README carries the correct "R25 is DNP" — the release doc is right,
   the design doc is a trap.
3. **ARCHITECTURE refdes for the converters/ICs** (U9=IP6559, U3/U5/U7=TPS2557)
   do not match the netlist (U1=IP6559, U3/U4/U5=TPS2557).
**Correction**: one editing pass over ARCHITECTURE.md/DETAIL_DESIGN.md to the
as-built refdes and the single-mechanism UVLO; this is exactly the class of
drift the repo's own canon warns about.

### P2-6 — CC-pin fault tolerance is unverifiable from project records
**Evidence**: CC1/CC2 run connector-direct to U1.41/38 (plus AO3401A drains).
IP6559_V1.4 abs-max table lists no CC voltage rating (only 4 kV HBM). A
defective cable shorting CC to a 20 V VBUS puts 20 V on U1's CC pin and, via
Q9/Q11 body diodes, ~19 V on VCONN5V (C32 is 25 V ✓, AO3401A Vds −30 V ✓; its
±12 V Vgs is exceeded only if the chip drives an e-marker read during the
fault). This is the DS reference wiring, but the chip's survival of the
industry-standard CC-short-to-VBUS test is an article of faith.
**Correction**: none required for order (reference-conformant); optionally add
it to the first-power/abuse test list, or 5.1 V-standoff-…-24 V-clamp CC ESD
parts in v2 would not help (they'd clamp below 20 V — the exposure is inherent).

### P2-7 — Sustained port overload runs 1.5 A-class USB-A contacts at up to 3.3 A
**Evidence**: TPS2557 holds IOS (2.72–3.29 A) continuously into a resistive
overload (thermal-cycling aside); KH-AF90DIP-112 has no vendor current rating
and is a 1.5 A-class contact system (ADR-0006's own honest disposition;
0.33 W in a 30 mΩ contact at IOS max). Normal 2 A/2.5 A use is the dispositioned
industry practice — this note is about the fault corner only.
**Correction**: accepted per ADR-0006; no action beyond keeping the 2 A
continuous silk/spec (already done).

## VERDICT: **ORDER** (no P0 found)

The netlist is topologically sound: every protection element I traced is
present, correctly oriented, and pin-accurate against the cached datasheets;
the two fresh-context review catches (USB-A plug→receptacle, TPS2513→A variant)
are genuinely fixed in the release artifacts; the DNP strap and fab package are
consistent. Nothing in the fab outputs must change before ordering.

Prioritized fix list:
1. **P1-2** (before trusting storage-with-pack): measure real below-UVLO drain
   at first power; correct ADR-0001/DETAIL_DESIGN/ORDER_README numbers; plan
   EN-based shutdown or hard cutoff for v2.
2. **P1-1** (before user-facing docs): pick the D1 position story — as-built
   crowbar (document sacrificial fuse+TVS on reversal) or move D1 to VIN next
   spin; fix DETAIL_DESIGN §1 / ARCHITECTURE / ADR-0001 to match one intent.
3. **P1-3** (before sustained 100 W at low battery): thermal-validate L1 at
   9 V in / 20 V 5 A out; consider NTC/derating hook if marginal.
4. **P2-5** doc pass (refdes + stale TLV431 paragraph — the "R7 DNP" trap is
   the sharp edge).
5. **P2-3** name the fuse MPN in ORDER_README.
6. **P2-4** record UVLO corner band + BMS-required note.
7. **P2-1/2/6/7**: record as accepted limitations (one paragraph each) so the
   next reviewer doesn't re-litigate them.
