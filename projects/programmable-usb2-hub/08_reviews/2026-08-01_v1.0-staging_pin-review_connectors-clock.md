# Fresh-context pin review — connectors and clock

Date: 2026-08-01  
Release: v1.0-staging

Scope was restricted to the fresh-context protocol, dossiers `F1`, `J2`–`J7`, and `Y1`, and the specifically supplied manufacturer/USB PDFs. No schematic, board source, prior review, or session history was consulted.

## F1 — Keystone 3568 mini-blade fuse holder

**VERDICT: PASS**

- F1 pins 1/2 (fuse terminals): the Keystone 3568 mounting detail requires four holes in a 9.9 mm by 3.4 mm rectangle, with the two holes at each end belonging to one conductive fuse clip; the dossier shows duplicated pad 1 at x=0 and duplicated pad 2 at x=9.92, each separated 3.40 mm in y. This preserves all four physical tails and the two fused electrical poles.
- F1 pins 1/2 (fuse terminals): a fuse is non-polar; `VIN_RAW` on one clip and `VIN_FUSED` on the other is the required series function. The stated winding is not electrically meaningful for this two-pole symmetric part, and there is no mirror-numbering trap.

## J2 — TE 292304-1 USB Type-B receptacle

**VERDICT: FAIL**

- J2 pins 1–4 (VBUS, D-, D+, GND): the TE PC-board mounting figure and numbered mating view place pin 2 upper-left, pin 1 upper-right, pin 3 lower-left, and pin 4 lower-right. The dossier coordinates match those physical contacts, and the nets `USB_UP_VBUS`, `UP_CONN_N`, `UP_CONN_P`, and `GND` match the USB 2.0 assignments 1=VBUS, 2=D-, 3=D+, 4=GND.
- J2 pins 1–4 (package winding): the authoritative top/component-side arrangement winds 1→2→3→4 **CCW** (NE→NW→SW→SE), but the dossier declares **CW**. This is a direct winding contradiction and is blocking under the protocol even though the listed coordinates themselves match the drawing.
- J2 pin SH (shell/shield): the TE drawing has two physical shell mounting tails and USB 2.0 identifies the shell as `Shield`; the dossier reports `part.yaml pins with NO pad on the footprint: ['SH']` and instead gives both physical shell lands the unrelated numeric identity 5. Without an explicit fused/alias mapping from `SH` to those two lands, the physical shell identity is not preserved across artifacts. Both lands being on `GND` does not repair the missing identity mapping.

## J3 — GCT USB1130 Type-A receptacle, port 1

**VERDICT: PASS**

- J3 pins 1–4 (VBUS, D-, D+, GND): the GCT recommended PCB layout has four 2.00 mm-pitch contact holes centered 2.71 mm behind the shell stakes; the USB 2.0 Type-A receptacle reference drawing numbers them left-to-right 1, 2, 3, 4 in the component/front view. The dossier matches that order and dimensions, with `P1_VBUS`, `P1_CONN_N`, `P1_CONN_P`, and `GND` on the required contacts.
- J3 pin 5 (shell/shield): the two x=±3.50 mm shell lands match the GCT drawing's two 7.00 mm-spaced shell stakes and both are on a ground-class net. The manufacturer's single metal shell explicitly fuses those two mounting tails. A perimeter winding is not defined for the four collinear signal contacts, so the dossier's computed `CW` label is not used as physical evidence; the left-to-right contact identity is unambiguous.

## J7 — Samtec FTSH-105-01-L-D-K 2×5 header

**VERDICT: PASS**

- J7 pins 1–10 (physical identities): the Samtec top view numbers the first double-row position 01/02, then advances by pairs to 09/10 for a five-position part. Rotating that authoritative view onto the dossier frame gives odd pins in the x=0 row and even pins in the x=1.27 mm row, with each successive pair 1.27 mm farther in y, exactly as shown. All ten physical contacts are present.
- J7 pins 1–10 (package/keying): `105` requires five positions per row, `D` requires two rows, and `-K` is a housing keying feature for mating with FFSD, not an omitted electrical contact. The dossier correctly retains pin 7 as a physical but unconnected `KEY_NC` pad. The zig-zag pair numbering is not a perimeter package winding; the dossier's `CCW` summary does not alter the correct odd/even physical map.
- J7 pins 1–10 (electrical sanity): `VTREF` is on `3V3_LOGIC`; SWDIO/SWCLK/NRST go to their corresponding signal nets; GND and GND_DETECT are on `GND`; and the spare/NC/key pins are explicitly unconnected. No power, ground, or NC contradiction is present.

## Y1 — Kyocera CX3225SB24000H0FLJCC crystal

**VERDICT: PASS**

- Y1 pins 1–4 (package winding and pin-1 corner): the Kyocera connection top view places pin 1 at bottom-left, pin 2 bottom-right, pin 3 top-right, and pin 4 top-left, winding CCW. The dossier has the same corner, winding, four-pad count, and 3.2×2.5 mm-family geometry.
- Y1 pins 1/3 (HOT crystal electrodes): the authority identifies pins 1 and 3 as HOT; the dossier connects them to `XTAL1` and `XTAL2`, respectively.
- Y1 pins 2/4 (GND): the authority requires both pins 2 and 4 on GND; the dossier places both on `GND`.
