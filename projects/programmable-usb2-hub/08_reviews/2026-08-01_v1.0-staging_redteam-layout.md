# Adversarial layout / thermal / power-integrity review

## Scope and method

This is a fresh-context review of the mutable pre-seal archive
`07_releases/v1.0-2026-08-01` plus the curated design documents, decisions,
part dossiers, and `03_src` configuration. Journals, learnings, STATUS,
PUBLISH_MANIFEST, and prior files in `08_reviews` were not read.

The exact archived board was loaded read-only with KiCad 10.0.4 `pcbnew`.
Track centerline lengths, widths, layers, via sizes, pad coordinates, pad/drill
sizes, zone layers, courtyard polygon intersections, and component positions
were measured from the saved `.kicad_pcb`. Distances below are Euclidean pad or
feature distances unless described as routed centerline length. Copper-area
figures for switch nodes are track-ribbon sums (`length x width`), so they are
conservative approximations where track ribbons overlap; zone counts and layer
assignments are exact.

## Detailed measurements

### High-current copper and via paths

The archived board has only three copper zones, all GND: F.Cu, B.Cu, and
In1.Cu. Power distribution is routed track copper rather than dedicated power
zones. Exact saved-board totals include:

| net | routed centerline | widths present | layers | ordinary vias |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| VIN_RAW | 14.314 mm | 3.0 mm | F.Cu | 0 |
| VIN_FUSED | 37.345 mm | 1.5, 3.0 mm | F.Cu/B.Cu/In2.Cu | 9 |
| FET_MID | 50.964 mm | 0.25, 3.0 mm | F.Cu/B.Cu/In2.Cu | 4 |
| VIN_PROTECTED | 208.295 mm | 0.25, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 3.0 mm | F.Cu/B.Cu/In2.Cu | 29 |
| 5V_A | 185.891 mm | 0.25, 2.5 mm | F.Cu/B.Cu/In2.Cu | 18 |
| 5V_B | 174.866 mm | 0.25, 2.5 mm | F.Cu/B.Cu/In2.Cu | 17 |
| P1/P2/P3/P4 VBUS | 46.689 / 59.436 / 63.132 / 69.241 mm | 0.25, 1.5 mm | F.Cu/In2.Cu | 6 / 4 / 3 / 5 |

The entry mechanics are otherwise plausible: J1 is a 5.00 mm-pitch terminal
with 3.00 mm lands and 1.50 mm drills; F1 has paired same-number pads with
2.78 mm lands and 1.78 mm drills; J3-J6 contact holes measure 0.92 mm in
1.75 mm lands at 2.00 mm pitch. The weakness is not the connector land itself;
it is the long narrow launch and single small layer transition behind it.

### Switch-node containment and adjacency

There are no SW_A, SW_B, or SW_3V3 zones. The two high-power switch nets stay
on F.Cu without vias, which avoids capacitively driving an inner layer, and the
nearest measured USB-copper edge separations are 12.572 mm (SW_A), 20.742 mm
(SW_B), and 24.448 mm (SW_3V3). Thus direct switch-node adjacency to a USB pair
is not the defect. The defect is the very large local switch/hot-loop geometry:
SW_A/SW_B span 37.622/36.517 mm diagonally and the FET-to-inductor distances are
19.550/22.549 mm. The 3.3 V cell is materially better: U4 SW-to-L3 is 4.762 mm
and U4 BST-to-C22 is 2.146 mm, although the SW span still exceeds its dossier's
3.5 mm budget and must be rechecked after the larger power stages are moved.

### USB differential geometry and return continuity

All USB tracks measure 0.2496-0.2500 mm wide. Where the router holds a coupled
corridor, the intended 0.15 mm edge gap is present, but width/gap alone is not
an impedance result. The layer and length audit is:

| path | P length | N length | absolute delta | routed layer/vias |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| upstream connector segment | 8.462 mm | 13.715 mm | 5.253 mm | F.Cu, no vias |
| upstream hub segment | 86.742 mm | 86.917 mm | 0.176 mm | F.Cu, no vias |
| management path | 27.709 mm | 20.092 mm | 7.617 mm | In2.Cu, two vias/member |
| port 1 end-to-end | 80.746 mm | 79.535 mm | 1.211 mm | F.Cu + B.Cu, two vias/member in switch segment |
| port 2 end-to-end | 54.692 mm | 53.253 mm | 1.439 mm | B.Cu hub + switch segments, four vias/member |
| port 3 end-to-end | 79.706 mm | 81.197 mm | 1.491 mm | F.Cu + B.Cu, two vias/member in switch segment |
| port 4 end-to-end | 93.971 mm | 95.411 mm | 1.440 mm | B.Cu hub + switch segments, four vias/member |

The external per-segment mismatch is also noncompliant: hub deltas are
1.308/1.537/1.394/1.344 mm for ports 1-4, switch deltas are approximately
2.824 mm on every port, and connector deltas are approximately 2.92 mm on
every port. These measurements exclude vertical barrel length; symmetric
barrels would not repair the mismatch.

F.Cu routes have the intended adjacent In1.Cu GND reference. B.Cu routes do
not: the adjacent In2.Cu layer has power/signal tracks and no GND zone. The
management pair on In2.Cu can reference In1.Cu, but it is not the documented
0.20 mm outer-prepreg geometry and is not length matched. Therefore the board
does not substantiate 90 ohm differential impedance or high-speed operation.

### Thermal paths, courtyards, and package mechanics

MEASURED positive observations:

- 206/206 footprint courtyard polygons were present and zero same-side
  courtyard intersections were found. The staged DRC also reports zero copper,
  clearance, unconnected, and courtyard violations.
- U6's exposed ground pad contains sixteen 0.20 mm plated vias. U9-U12 each
  contain eight 0.15 mm plated IN/OUT vias. U2/U3 have four/two GND vias in the
  exposed-pad region.
- The custom BSC016N06NS copper preserves source pins 1-3, gate pin 4, and the
  four fused drain lands 5-8; the AON6354 copper preserves source 1-3, gate 4,
  and drain 5-8 around the large exposed drain. Their measured pad envelopes
  agree with the dimensions recorded in the corresponding part dossiers.
- The four USB-A footprints measure 0.92 mm signal drills at 2.00 mm pitch and
  2.26 mm shell drills in 3.30 mm lands at 7.00 mm spacing, matching the cited
  GCT drawing dimensions. No courtyard or pad-clearance defect was found at
  the connector row.

Those positives do not mitigate the P0 current-path and switching-loop
defects. In particular, DRC 0/0/0 proves spacing/connectivity, not ampacity,
return-plane continuity, impedance, or stable 7 A switching behavior.

## Verdict rationale

`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE` because LAY-P0-01 through LAY-P0-03 are confirmed
on the exact archived board. Any one P0 blocks the seal; all three require
source/config changes and a fresh board build before re-review.

`order_verdict: BLOCKED-SOURCING` independently because the staged catalog
measurement has one zero-stock coded line, U4/C5248536, whose consignment is
planned but not accepted. Even if sourcing clears, the design P0s still make
this staging archive a do-not-fabricate state.
