## Required human policy grades

### S5 design-math spot check: FAIL

The sealed archive contains values but no derivations. Spot check 1, the TPS259470 port-1 current-limit resistor R414 = 976 ohm, cannot be re-derived because the intended current, governing equation, tolerance, and margin are absent. Spot check 2, the port-1 OVLO network R410/R411 = 100 kohm/36.5 kohm, likewise has no published threshold calculation or tolerance corners. The identical values repeated on ports 2-4 do not substitute for a derivation.

### S6 schematic readability: EFFORTFUL

Local blocks are legible when zoomed, but the story-critical path is not traceable as one drawn circuit. For example, the power path from J1/F1 through U1, the protected rails, U4, and the two LM5116 branches requires following separated net-label hops; each port similarly changes among `P1_HUB_*`, `P1_PORT_*`, and `P1_CONN_*` labels between U6, U13, U17, and J3. Page-fit text is very small because the whole circuit is compressed onto one whitespace-heavy sheet.

### S7 decoupling adjacency: PARTIAL / FAIL

The four repeated port blocks do place their local capacitors beside U9-U12 and U13-U16, and U4's input/bootstrap/output capacitors are grouped with its regulator block. The USB2517 hub core is the counterexample: C32-C41 are drawn as rail-oriented capacitor banks/vertical farms displaced from U6 rather than immediately at the power pins they serve. The schematic therefore does not consistently teach decoupling placement and fails S7 as a whole.

## Visual/mechanical disposition

- No populated bottom-side bodies are shown or expected; `twin_bottom.png` agrees with `render_bottom_bare.png`.
- Among bodies that are actually rendered, no obvious body-to-body collision is visible in the top, isometric, or edge views. J1 screw access and the J2 upstream USB-B mating face appear accessible at the west edge.
- The calibrated twin overlay reports orthographic anisotropy 0.9990, 57/195 expected bodies measured, zero resolvable-but-unmeasured bodies, and all measured center/outward deltas within its 1.00 mm gate. That supports the rendered SMD population but does not cure the J3-J6 denominator gap or the F1 fallback.
- Functional silkscreen for input polarity, fuse intent, host, ports, per-port 5 V / 3 A, and the proprietary USB-A power-mode warning is visible. Dense reference text is frequently obscured by installed bodies, so the assembly PDF remains necessary for servicing.

## Verdict rationale

`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE` is mandatory because RR-P0-01 and RR-P0-02 make the mechanical render untrusted for five important through-hole parts. This verdict does not assert that the four USB-A footprints or the Keystone holder land are electrically wrong; it states that the required visual/mechanical proof is absent or contradictory.

`order_verdict: BLOCKED-SOURCING` is independent of the design verdict. The archived stock check grades 59/59 coded BOM lines and records exactly one shortfall: C5248536 / AP63203QWU-7 / U4 at stock 0, with planned exact-MPN consignment. That is a sourcing/order blocker, not the cause of the design verdict.
