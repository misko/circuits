# Independent RF PCB review

## Scope and method

The exact declared PCB artifact was loaded read-only with KiCad 10.0.4
`pcbnew`. The review measured every declared USB net's layers, track widths,
routed centerline lengths, signal vias, adjacent reference zones, and proximity
to the switching-power nets. It also inspected the board-embedded layer and
stackup declarations, the USB netclass/DRU constraints, connector/switch/ESD
pad geometry, and the exact-board pad-separation result. No design-author
conclusions or prior RF review verdicts were used.

## Requirement evidence

### USB2-PCB-STACKUP — FAIL

The contract declares `USB2_L1_OVER_L2` as F.Cu over In1.Cu with 0.2104 mm
dielectric height, Dk 4.4, 0.25 mm width and 0.15 mm gap. The exact board file
records only total thickness 1.6 mm and four signal copper layers; it contains
no `(stackup ...)` tuple binding dielectric materials/thicknesses, copper
thicknesses, solder-mask state, or the claimed JLC04161H-7628 construction.
The contract itself says the JLC order calculator confirmation remains due, and
no field-solver result is bound to this artifact. Consequently the realized
cross-section cannot be reproduced from the board and solver evidence.

### USB2-PCB-GEOMETRY — FAIL

All declared USB traces measure 0.2496-0.2500 mm wide. Coupled portions use the
authored 0.15 mm nominal gap and normal solder-mask coverage. That geometry is
only declared for F.Cu over In1.Cu, however. The exact board routes P2/P4 hub
segments and every P1-P4 switch-to-ESD segment on B.Cu, and routes MGMT_P/N on
In2.Cu. No B.Cu-over-In2.Cu or In2.Cu-over-In1.Cu impedance cross-section was
declared or solved. Therefore much of the realized copper does not use the
claimed geometry even when its planar width/gap numerically matches.

### USB2-PCB-RETURN — FAIL

The board has GND zones on F.Cu, B.Cu and In1.Cu only. F.Cu USB segments have
the intended adjacent In1.Cu reference. B.Cu USB segments do not: the adjacent
In2.Cu layer has no GND plane and instead carries power and signals. The saved
board contains 24 external USB signal vias: two per conductor on every
switch-to-ESD segment plus two per conductor on the P2 and P4 hub segments.
MGMT_P/N add four more signal vias. This directly violates the contract's
zero-transition, F.Cu-over-In1.Cu return claim. No local return-via structure
can repair the absence of an adjacent B.Cu ground plane.

### USB2-PCB-UNCOUPLED — FAIL

The DRU's 15.0 mm uncoupled-length check reports no DRC violation, but the RF
protocol also requires measured routed length/spread and symmetry. Centerline
measurements from the exact copper give:

| path | P | N | delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| upstream connector segment | 8.462 mm | 13.715 mm | 5.253 mm |
| upstream hub segment | 86.742 mm | 86.917 mm | 0.176 mm |
| management path | 27.709 mm | 20.092 mm | 7.617 mm |
| port 1 end-to-end | 80.746 mm | 79.535 mm | 1.211 mm |
| port 2 end-to-end | 54.692 mm | 53.253 mm | 1.439 mm |
| port 3 end-to-end | 79.706 mm | 81.197 mm | 1.491 mm |
| port 4 end-to-end | 93.971 mm | 95.411 mm | 1.440 mm |

Within ports 1-4, hub-segment deltas are 1.308/1.537/1.394/1.344 mm,
switch-segment deltas are about 2.824 mm each, and connector-segment deltas are
about 2.92 mm each. These exceed the design's <=0.50 mm per-segment and
<=1.00 mm end-to-end limits. `nets.yaml` contains no machine-read
`length_match` groups, so the copper-length gate reports the subject N-A rather
than protecting it. The via barrels are paired symmetrically and omitting their
vertical length does not explain these planar deltas.

### USB2-PCB-NOISE — FAIL

Direct switch-node spacing is good in isolation: the minimum measured
switch-copper-to-USB-copper edge separations are 12.572 mm for SW_A, 20.742 mm
for SW_B, and 24.448 mm for SW_3V3, comfortably above a 3W spacing heuristic.
But the B.Cu USB routing sits immediately adjacent to routed In2.Cu power and
logic rather than a ground plane. For example, the P2 hub pair traverses the
same x/y region as the 2.5 mm 5V_A inner-layer trunk around x=85.5-87.2 mm,
y=47.0-48.7 mm. The broad adjacent-layer conductor and its switching-current
return replace the claimed reference plane and create uncontrolled common-mode
coupling. The large LM5116 switch copper (SW_A/SW_B track-ribbon sums about
56.4/55.2 mm2 over 37.6/36.5 mm bounding diagonals) increases the need for the
missing return discipline. Noise isolation therefore fails despite adequate
planar distance from the switch nodes themselves.

### USB2-PCB-PADSEP — PASS

The exact-artifact pad-separation gate measured 784 pads and 299,899
inter-footprint pad pairs with zero overlap, zero-distance, or foreign-paste
intrusion findings. Independent geometry inspection found 206 courtyard
polygons and zero same-side intersections. The USB-A footprints measure 0.92 mm
signal drills at 2.00 mm pitch and 2.26 mm shell drills in 3.30 mm lands at
7.00 mm spacing, matching the cited GCT dimensions. The USB-B, FSUSB42, and
USBLC6 launches have no separate-footprint copper touch. This pass is strictly
mechanical and does not mitigate the failed cross-section, return, or symmetry
requirements.

## Findings and disposition

| id | severity | finding | required disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| RFPCB-P0-01 | P0 | The board implements undeclared B.Cu and In2.Cu USB cross-sections, with B.Cu lacking an adjacent ground plane and 28 total USB signal vias. | Re-route every declared USB net on F.Cu over continuous In1.Cu GND with zero signal-layer transitions, then bind the exact board stackup and order-calculator/solver result. |
| RFPCB-P0-02 | P0 | All four external downstream end-to-end pairs exceed the 1.00 mm limit; the upstream and management mismatches are 5.429 mm and 7.617 mm. | Add non-vacuous length-match groups and reroute to <=0.50 mm per segment and <=1.00 mm end-to-end; remeasure exact copper. |
| RFPCB-P0-03 | P0 | B.Cu USB pairs use an In2.Cu power/signal neighborhood as their adjacent electromagnetic environment, including overlap with the 5V_A trunk region. | Restore a ground reference between USB and power routing; repeat coupling review after the return plane is continuous. |
| RFPCB-P1-04 | P1 | The 1.6 mm board does not embed the declared dielectric/copper/mask tuple, and the JLC impedance-calculator result is still outstanding. | Bind the selected JLC04161H-7628 tuple and calculator result to the release; preserve exact width/gap in fab output and require coupon/TDR plus USB eye qualification. |

## Verdict

`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE`. Four of six declared PCB requirements fail on the
exact artifact, including three confirmed P0 findings. RF layout seal is
blocked until the board is rerouted and this phase is repeated on the new hash.
