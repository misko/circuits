Date: 2026-08-01  
Release: v1.0-staging  
Scope: U2, U3, U4, U5, U6, U7, U8, U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U18, U19, U20

This review used only the fresh-context pin-review protocol, the named release pin-audit dossiers, and the seven supplied manufacturer PDFs. Package figures were read as manufacturer top views and compared allowing rotation but not reflection. Net judgments below are limited to electrical kind and polarity visible in the dossiers.

## U2 — LM5116MHX/NOPB

- U2 pin 1 (VIN) and package winding: LM5116 Figure 4-1 (SNVS499I PDF p.3) puts pin 1 at the upper-left of the top view and numbers 1→10 down the left side, then 11→20 up the right side (CCW); the dossier has the same pin-1 corner, CCW winding, and 10 pins per side.

- U2 pin 21 (EP): the datasheet requires the exposed pad on the ground plane; the dossier shows GND.
- U2 pins 1–10 (VIN through VOUT): expected input rail, threshold/timing/control nodes, analog ground, feedback/compensation nodes, and regulated-output monitor respectively vs VIN_PROTECTED, UVLO_A, RT_A, EN_A, RAMP_A, GND, SS_A, FB_A, COMP_A, and 5V_A. These are the expected net kinds.
- U2 pins 11–20 (DEMB through SW): expected diode-emulation reference, differential current sense, PGND, low-side gate, VCC, optional VCCX, bootstrap supply, high-side gate, and switch node vs GND, CSF_A, CSGF_A, GND, LO_A, VCC_A, GND, BOOT_A, HO_A, and SW_A. Grounding unused VCCX is explicitly permitted; the gate, bootstrap, sense, and switch nets have the required kinds.

## U4 — AP63203QWU-7

VERDICT: QUESTION

- U4 authority (exact orderable): the supplied AP63200/AP63201/AP63203/AP63205 PDF explicitly says the automotive AP63203Q is covered by a separate datasheet, and its ordering table lists AP63203WU-7 but not AP63203QWU-7. Therefore the exact Q-qualified device's pinout and package cannot be proven from the supplied authority. An exact AP63203Q datasheet is required to close this item.
- U4 pin 1 (FB) and package winding, conditional on the non-Q family map: the supplied top-view TSOT26 figure puts pin 1 upper-left and winds CCW with three pins per side; the dossier matches without reflection.
- U4 pins 1–6 (conditional electrical comparison): the supplied non-Q map expects FB, EN, VIN, GND, SW, BST vs 3V3_LOGIC, VIN_PROTECTED, VIN_PROTECTED, GND, SW_3V3, BST_3V3. This is electrically coherent for a fixed 3.3 V buck, and all six leads are present with no EP, but it does not resolve the exact-orderable authority gap.

## U6 — USB2517I-JZX

VERDICT: PASS

- U6 pin 1 (USBDN1_DM) and package winding: USB2517I Figure 3-1 (DS00001598C PDF p.7) places pin 1 at the lower-left of its top view and winds CCW with 16 pins per side. Rotating that figure 90° clockwise gives the dossier's upper-left pin 1 and W→S→E→N side progression exactly; this is a rotation, not a mirror.
- U6 pins 1–64 (count/functions): the dossier's complete numbered map agrees with Figure 3-1 and Table 5-1: downstream pairs at 1–12 and 53–56, LED/strap/power-control pins at 13–23 and 26–51, power pins at 5, 10, 24, 25, 46, 52, 57, 62, 64, serial/reset/VBUS-detect pins at 40–45, upstream pair at 58/59, crystal at 60/61, RBIAS at 63, and TEST at 19.
- U6 thermal slug (VSS): the datasheet requires the QFN thermal slug connected to VSS. The dossier realizes this single slug identity as pad 65 across the central land and thermal-via features, all on GND; no signal-pin identity is collapsed.
- U6 USB data pins: every DM pin maps to an N net and every DP pin maps to a P net. Ports 1–4 map to P1–P4 hub pairs, port 5 maps to MGMT_N/P, unused ports 6–7 are unconnected, and upstream pins 58/59 map to UP_HUB_N/P.
- U6 power/bias/timing pins: all 3.3 V pins map to 3V3_LOGIC; VDD18 and VDD18PLL have distinct 1.8 V/filter nets; VBUS_DET, XTAL1/2, and RBIAS have dedicated sense/timing/bias nets. These are the expected net kinds.
- U6 power-control/configuration pins: PRTPWR1–4 map to HUB_PP1–4 and OCS1_N–4 to P1_FLT_N–P4_FLT_N. Unused OCS5_N/6_N/7_N are held inactive at 3V3_LOGIC, unused PRTPWR5–7 are unconnected, CFG_SEL2 and CFG_SEL1 are low, and CFG_SEL0 shares HUB_SCL as permitted by the serial-configuration strap scheme. TEST is an internal-pulldown input and is unconnected. No power-pin or polarity mismatch was found.
