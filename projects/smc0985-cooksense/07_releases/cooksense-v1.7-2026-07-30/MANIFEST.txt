  FORM was incomplete. theta_JA is a junction-to-AMBIENT-AIR figure measured with

  says "additional heat sources mounted near the device must be considered". This
  board's other 0.958 W — twelve reed relay coils at 0.705 W dominate — raises the

  margin moves -0.31 mV/K (copper is 42 % of the series sum), so 75 -> 65 buys it

  worst case with the CITED 2 x 10 mOhm Micro-Fit contact maximum, +3.4 mV at the aged
  2 x 30 mOhm allowance, and +497 mV typical.
