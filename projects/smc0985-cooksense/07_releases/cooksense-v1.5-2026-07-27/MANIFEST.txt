*** E-TOPO FAILS, AND IT IS A SUPPLY SPECIFICATION, NOT A COPPER DEFECT ***
  power_topology.py learned LINEAR on 2026-07-27. v1.4 declared `rails: []` with its envelopes in a
  `linear_rails:` key the checker ignores by design, so E-TOPO graded 0 of 1 converters — an LDO-only
  board reaching a green gate by showing it nothing. v1.5 declares the AMS1117-3.3 rail properly:

    rail '3V3' LINEAR (AMS1117-3.3): headroom 1101 mV (Vin_min 4.5 - Vout_max 3.399) vs dropout
    1300 mV; PD 690 mW ((Vin_max 5.5 - Vout_min 3.201) x 0.3 A) vs rating 1200 mW (57%)
    -> FAIL DROPOUT: only 1101 mV of headroom against a 1300 mV dropout

  Both numbers are CITED to Advanced Monolithic Systems ds1117 (2009-08 RoHS): dropout p.3 "Dropout
  Voltage (VIN-VOUT)" MAX at IOUT = 0.8 A (Note 4); PD p.3 Note 2 "1.2 W for SOT-223"; Vout p.2
  AMS1117-3.3 at VIN 4.8 V, boldface full-temperature limits.

  THIS RAIL DRAWS 0.3 A AND THE DATASHEET PUBLISHES NO DROPOUT FIGURE THERE. p.1 says only that
  dropout is "guaranteed maximum 1.3V, decreasing at lower load currents"; Note 4 bounds the HIGH
  side; p.6's six TYPICAL PERFORMANCE curves include no dropout-vs-load. So the 0.3 A dropout is
  OWED, the graded number is the only CITED one at 2.67x this rail's load, and vin_min was left at
  4.5 rather than raised to the 4.75 that makes the gate pass — that would be fitting a number to a
  gate. WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE ORDER: the LDO input needs >= 3.399 + 1.300 = 4.699 V, so "5 V SELV"
  (BRIEF section 3.5, no tolerance stated) under-specifies this board's supply. Buy a supply that
  holds >= 4.85 V at J_PWR under load, and MEASURE VIN-VOUT at U_LDO at bring-up — that single
  measurement retires the OWED fact. ORDER_README section 0. NOTHING HERE BLOCKS FABRICATION OR
  ASSEMBLY; the boards are exactly as orderable as v1.4's were.
