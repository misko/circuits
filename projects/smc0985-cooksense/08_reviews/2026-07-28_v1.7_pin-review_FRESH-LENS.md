## FAIL — the 12 DIP05-1A72-12L reed relays. VERIFIED INDEPENDENTLY BY THE MAIN LOOP.

`K_U1..U6, K_D1..D4, K_PRESS, K_STOP`.

**Citation:** Standex *DIP Series Reed Relays*, Version 03 / 01 Aug 2025,
`02_parts/DIP05-1A72-12L/datasheet-reed-relay-series-dip.pdf`, **p.3 "Pin-Out
(Top View) (2.54mm pitch grid)", sub-figure "12"**. Re-rendered at 300 dpi and
read by the main loop as an independent channel.

**What sub-figure 12 shows: EIGHT leads, four per row** — bottom 1, 2, 6, 7 and
top 14, 13, 9, 8 — with a continuous vertical conductor tying **1 <-> 14** into
ONE contact node and **7 <-> 8** into the OTHER, and the COIL on the INNER pins.
(Sub-figure **13**, on the same page, is the 4-lead variant at 14/8/2/6 — which
is what `part.yaml`'s prose actually describes. The wrong sub-figure was read.)

**What the project has:** `03_src/lib/cooksense.pretty/
Relay_StandexDIP_1A_pinout12.kicad_mod` carries **4 pads** at (+-3.810, +-7.620)
= DIP-14 corners 1/7/8/14, and `part.yaml` asserts `1: COIL_A, 7: COIL_B,
8: CONTACT_B, 14: CONTACT_A`.

**Confirmed against the netlist by the main loop:**

| pad | DIP pos | datasheet node | net |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | **contact A** | `5V_KEY_RELAY` |
| 4 | 14 | **contact A — SAME NODE** | `U_SEL_BUS` |
| 2 | 7 | **contact B** | `COIL_U1_N` |
| 3 | 8 | **contact B — SAME NODE** | `KP_U1` |

**Four real consequences:**
a. **No holes exist for the coil leads** (DIP 2/6/9/13). The part cannot be seated.
b. **`5V_KEY_RELAY` is hard-shorted to `U_SEL_BUS`.** On `K_STOP` this shorts
   `5V_STOP` to `KP_U6`, a membrane-matrix line going OFF-BOARD.
c. **Each ULN2803 output is shorted to its keypad line** (`COIL_*_N` to `KP_*`).
d. **The coil is never energised** — the entire key-matrix relay array is
   non-functional.

**Collateral, and it is not small:** the declared 1.5 kVDC coil/contact isolation
boundary — "between the columns", cited to ADR-0002 and used to justify the
pour-relief and routing rules in the `layout:` block — **does not exist in pinout
12**. The coil/contact split runs along the package's long axis, not between the
rows. Every downstream creepage/clearance argument built on that geometry is
unsupported.

**Scope beyond v1.7:** these relays are `not_on_assembly_bom` (hand-soldered), so
JLC never places them — but the PCB HOLES and the NETLIST are wrong regardless,
and this footprint predates v1.7. It is present in the sealed v1.0-v1.6 fab sets.

## Second blocking finding (electrical)
**`U_EXP` pin 1 (GPB0) is driven above absolute maximum.** `EFUSE_FLT_N` =
{U_EFUSE.6 open-drain, R_PG.1, TP_PGOOD, U_EXP.1} with `R_PG.2 = 5V_PROTECTED`
(100k). With no fault the node idles at **5.0 V** into an MCP23017 at VDD 3.3 V
(abs-max V_IN = VDD+0.3 = 3.6 V, DS20001952C S1.0). 100k limits clamp current to
~14 uA so it will not fail immediately, but it is out of spec and continuously
injects into 3V3. Every other 5 V-domain signal on this board is level-referenced;
this one is not. Needs a divider or a 3V3 pull-up.

## QUESTIONS (8) — not cleared, not failed
U_TC MAX31856 **cannot be verified — no local PDF and analog.com unreachable**;
part.yaml itself says "PENDING datasheet-figure confirmation in pin review", and
this review CANNOT close it. U_ULNB IN5-IN8 float while IN4 is grounded.
KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED leaves the board on J_MODE.3 with no series R and no ESD.
J_MODE is copper-symmetric: a 180 deg rotation is invisible to DRC and AOI and
would put `3V3` onto `COIL_EN_IN` — i.e. arm the coil rail. J_ISOLOOP pins 2-3
are a bare two-node link. Pi bus-B on GPIO14/15 has no native I2C alt. The
Manual/Auto pole mapping differs from the BRIEF's wording. **The "one pull-up of
eleven" count is off by one — there are TWO (`R_FAULTPU` and `R_REARMPU`), and
R_REARMPU being a pull-up is what closes the CD74HC221 R=rising re-trigger
hazard. Do not let anyone "normalise" it to a pull-down.**
