# P1 — ORDER_README + next revision

## P1-1 — H4 mounting hole is four defects at one feature: 0.850 mm web, slot overlap, 0.200 mm plane clearance, blocked fastener

Measured, all from the board file:

| fact | measured |
|---|---|
| H4 = NPTH Ø2.700 mm at (193.000, 52.000) (drill file `T2C2.700`) | wall at y = 50.650 |
| slot `S_e*` (edge-open notch) = x 191.500–200.000, y 48.800–49.800 | edge at y = 49.800 |
| **web between H4 wall and the slot** | **0.850 mm** (FR-4, full 1.6 mm thickness) |
| H4 courtyard (190.470,49.480)–(195.530,54.520) vs the slot | **overlaps by 4.025 × 0.325 mm** — the *only* footprint on the board whose courtyard intersects a milled slot |
| copper clearance, H4 wall → nearest copper | **F.Cu 0.200 / In1.Cu 0.200 / In2.Cu 0.200 / B.Cu 0.200 mm** |
| what that copper is | F.Cu **GND** pour, In1 **GND** plane, B.Cu **GND** pour, **In2 = the 3V3 plane** |
| nearest part to H4 | `J_MODE` courtyard at **2.73 mm**, `D_COILEN` 3.08, `U_ULNB` 3.19 |

Three consequences:

1. **Structural.** A 1.0 mm routing bit cutting `S_e*` leaves a 0.850 × 1.6 mm glass-epoxy web
   immediately beside a load-bearing screw hole. M2.5 fastener torque acts directly on it. (It is not
   catastrophic — the board is fully continuous for x < 191.5 at that y, so a crack extends the slot
   rather than severing anything — which is why this is P1 and not P0.)
2. **Fastener fouling.** An M2.5 pan head is Ø4.7 mm (r 2.35) — clears `J_MODE` at 2.73 mm by 0.38 mm.
   A DIN 125 washer (Ø6, r 3.00) or a standard M2.5 hex standoff (AF 5 mm, r ≈ 2.9) **does not clear
   `J_MODE`**, and its far edge reaches y = 49.0, i.e. **overhanging the slot** with no material to
   bear on.
3. **Electrical.** 0.200 mm is the board's own `min_hole_clearance` — so DRC *cannot* flag it. On a
   2.700 mm NPTH a metal M2.5 shank leaves a 0.100 mm radial air gap; the In2 **3V3** plane is
   0.200 mm from the wall on one side and the GND planes are 0.200 mm on the other. Drill smear or
   0.05 mm of positional error on both counts puts a metal fastener between 3V3 and GND.
   JLCPCB publishes **0.254 mm** NPTH-to-copper; 0.200 mm is below it (confirm against the fab's
   current capability table before ordering).

`H3` at (17.000, 90.000) carries the same **0.200 mm on all four layers** (F.Cu GND / In1 GND /
In2 **3V3** / B.Cu GND) but has no slot or part conflict. `H1` and `H2` are clear (1.600 / 3.238 mm)
because they sit in the plane-free keypad band.

**ORDER_README:** do not fit a fastener in H4 on this revision; if H3 is used, use a nylon screw or a
shouldered nylon washer.
**Next revision:** move H4 clear of `S_e*` (or shorten the notch), and give every mounting hole an
explicit ≥0.5 mm annular copper keepout on all four layers.

## P1-4 — the K-type thermocouple pair is not a pair: 132.79 mm² of differential loop, split across layers

This is the measured consequence of P1-3 on the board's most sensitive net (MAX31856, ~41 µV/°C,
0.0078 °C LSB ≈ 0.32 µV) — its three `keep_short` budgets are `T_PLUS`/`T_MINUS`/`BIAS`, all
non-resolving, so nothing graded it.

| fact | measured |
|---|---|
| path graded | `J_TC` → `R_TCP`/`R_TCN` (100 Ω) → `U_TC` (`TC_POS_IN`+`TC_POS` vs `TC_NEG_IN`+`TC_NEG`) |
| sampled path length (0.2 mm steps) | 40.37 mm |
| **mean separation between the two conductors** | **3.289 mm** |
| **worst separation** | **8.984 mm** at (69.400, 93.300) on F.Cu |

The two halves of the differential pair spend different fractions of their length on opposite sides of

# P2 — recorded

## P2-1 — the PWR_IN ampacity floor carries a current but no temperature rise
`nets.yaml`: `current: "2A worst-case"`, `min_width: 0.5mm`, **no ΔT**. IPC-2221, 1 oz external:

| width | 10 °C | 20 °C | 30 °C |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2500 mm | 0.875 A | 1.188 A | 1.419 A |
| 0.4498 mm | 1.340 A | 1.818 A | 2.173 A |
| 0.5000 mm | **1.447 A** | **1.963 A** | 2.346 A |

2.00 A at ΔT 10 °C needs **0.782 mm**; the 0.5 mm floor is the ΔT ≈ 20.8 °C width. At the real
whole-board worst case (0.50 A, `power_tree.yaml`) the rise is ~1 °C — physically fine. The finding is
that the floor's own justification is absent, and it matters upstream of the eFuse: `5V_IN`,
`5V_FUSED`, `5V_RPP` are limited only by **F1 (I_trip 3.50 A at 23 °C)**, and at 3.50 A a 0.5 mm 1 oz
trace runs **ΔT ≈ 74.5 °C**. Downstream of the eFuse the limit is ~1.79 A ⇒ ΔT ≈ 16 °C.

## P2-2 — the eFuse pad-entry neck feeds one IN pin in series (verified inside its waived area)
Two PWR_IN segments measure below the 0.5 mm floor; **both are inside the `efuse_padentry` rule area
(57.000,55.500)–(63.000,59.500); zero sub-floor segments lie outside it** — the scoped DRU exception
`scoped_efuse_padentry` (min 0.25 mm) covers exactly what exists.
Topology measured at the pads: the 0.5 mm trunk lands on **pad 4** only
((59.100,58.000)→(60.300,59.200) then →(59.100,57.900)→(59.000,57.800)→pad 4); **pad 3 is reached only
through the 0.250 × 0.500 mm link** (59.050,57.750)–(59.050,57.250). Pins 3 and 4 are the same die
node, so the two entries are in parallel and the neck is not the series bottleneck — but it is the
reason pad 3 is not an equal entry, and it is worth naming rather than assuming.

## P2-3 — `ISO_CONTACTOR` clears its own rule by exactly zero
Independently measured minimum from any `ISO_CONTACTOR` item to any other item, same layer:
**2.0000 mm** — `J_ISOLOOP` pad 1 (`CONTACTOR_C`) @ (195.300,100.250) to the F.Cu GND pour.
The `opto_isolation_2mm` DRU rule requires 2.0 mm. Zero manufacturing margin; 1 oz etch tolerance is
typically ±0.05 mm. Against IEC 60664-1 itself (30 V working, PD3, MG IIIa ⇒ 1.7 mm creepage for basic
insulation) there is still ~0.3 mm of real margin after tolerance, so this is recorded, not escalated.

## P2-4 — every slot is exactly at JLCPCB's published 1.0 mm minimum
All 13 slots measure **1.000 mm** wide — none under, none over. `S_W`'s web to the left board edge is
**1.0000 mm** exactly. Zero margin on both against the fab's stated floor; any tightening in a future
revision has nowhere to go.

## P2-5 — 1097 vias, all 0.250/0.150, annular ring exactly at the board's floor
Every via on the board is 0.250 mm pad / 0.150 mm drill ⇒ **0.050 mm annular ring**, which is exactly
`min_via_annular_width: 0.05`. Within JLC's 4-layer via spec (0.15/0.25) but with no margin for drill
wander.

## P2-6 — 273 in-pad vias sit under solder paste, and the release explicitly disables plugging
273 vias fall inside the paste aperture of 270 pads, including **2 inside the `U_LDO` 7.600 mm² tab**
(at (25.150,70.000) and (25.150,68.900)), 1 in the `U_EFUSE` EP window, 1 in `D_TVS` pad 2 (5.750 mm²),
2 in each `J_PWR` mount pad. `.kicad_pro` sets `tenting front/back yes` but the pad's own mask opening
overrides tenting inside the pad, and `plugging` is **`front no / back no`**. At 0.15 mm the wicking
risk is at the low end, but the release contains no fill/plug instruction and 273 is not a small number.

## P2-7 — reference-plane change on every F.Cu↔B.Cu transition, because In2 is 3V3 and not GND
663 signal vias in the plane region; detour to the nearest 3V3/GND bypass cap median **7.56 mm**, max
**29.02 mm** (`ADC_CS_N`), 36.3 % over 10 mm. 31 caps carry the whole In2→In1 return. 1804.1 mm of
B.Cu signal copper references the 3V3 plane, longest `SCL_B` 81.05, `SDA_B` 68.24, `SDA_A` 63.14 mm.
At 400 kHz I²C / ~1 MHz SPI this is not a functional risk; it is an immunity note for the cavity
environment.

## P2-8 — relay flyback loops are large and have no return plane at all
Worst loop `K_U1`: driver COM (`U_ULNA` pin 10) → coil pin 1 = 72.93 mm, coil pin 2 → `U_ULNA` pin 18 =
72.93 mm ⇒ **145.86 mm perimeter**, enclosing roughly 740 mm². Measured **0.00 mm of GND track and 0
GND pads anywhere in y < 53** — the plane-free band is required by the isolation architecture, so this
is structural, not fixable by routing. `5V_KEY_RELAY` runs 198.45 mm and `5V_STOP` 21.44 mm of
unreferenced copper in that band. Flyback is clamped inside the TBD62083 (COM = pin 10 = `5V_KEY_RELAY`
on both drivers, verified), and the stored energy is µJ-class, so this is an EMI/immunity note.
**Bring-up:** at G8/G10, look for coil-loop pickup with the magnetron running.

## P2-9 — the analog rail's ferrite split is defeated capacitively
`FB1` splits `3V3` (pad 1 @ 31.938,70.000) → `3V3_ANALOG` (pad 2 @ 34.062,70.000). `3V3_ANALOG` then
runs **99.35 mm as 0.5 mm track directly over the In2 plane, whose net is the *digital* 3V3** — the two
rails are broadside-coupled along the analog rail's entire length. `TCAM_THRESH` (76.59 mm),
`TH_CAM_A` (69.69), `TH_CAM_B` (69.85) are long for µA-level sense lines and each hops F.Cu↔B.Cu
repeatedly (35/17 and 41/5 segments).

## Verdict rationale

Nothing measured blocks fabrication or assembly, and the board's headline safety property — the 6 mm
keypad-contact barrier — was re-derived from raw copper geometry with an engine that shares no code
path with the DRC rule, and it **holds at 6.3100 mm with zero domain crossings**. The exemption in the
custom rule was checked rather than trusted, and the exempted set is finite (2 items), enumerated, and
14 mm away from anything it could bridge to.

The six P1s are all next-revision + bring-up items: one mechanical feature that must not take a screw
(H4), one thermal path that must be measured because its governing constant is not written down
(U_LDO), one gate that structurally cannot fail (21 unreachable budgets) plus the one physical defect it
let through (the thermocouple pair), one missing declaration (copper weight), and one under-provisioned
exposed pad (U_EFUSE). None of them stops five prototype boards from being useful, and every one of them
is closed by an ORDER_README line plus a change in the next revision.

VERDICT: ORDER
