## 0. Calibration you need before reading the findings

**The "206/206 bodies mounted, none missing" manifest does not cover the whole board.**
`missing_models.txt` states its own denominator: *"source of the population set: 206 CPL
placements"*. The BOM carries **222 designators**; the CPL carries **206**. The 16-part
difference is hand-populated and therefore outside the manifest:

    J_ISOLOOP  J_LOADCELL  J_PI  J_TC
    K_U1 K_U2 K_U3 K_U4 K_U5 K_U6  K_D1 K_D2 K_D3 K_D4  K_PRESS  K_STOP

Of those, `J_PI` and `J_LOADCELL` **do** carry a body in the twin (`twin_report.csv` rows
`C35165,J_PI,MIRRORED` and `C157991,J_LOADCELL,OK`). The remaining **14** — the twelve
DIP reed relays, the isolated terminal block and the thermocouple socket — appear as bare
pads in the modeled render. **That is correct and expected, not a finding.** A reviewer
taking the brief's premise ("a footprint with no visible body would be a real finding") at
face value would have raised fourteen false P0s. Recorded below as P2-6.

**Render resolution bounds what any render review can say.** At 5.1383 px/mm a SOT-23
plastic body is ~15 × 7 px and an 0402 is ~5 × 3 px. I can confirm the board *looks*
populated and can see SOIC-and-larger bodies clearly; I **cannot** independently confirm
body presence or rotation for the 0402/0603 population from these images. The A-RENDER
gate itself measured only **51 of 208** refs with an expected body (24.5 %). Everything I
did not verify is stated as UNVERIFIED, not passed.

bottom silk line reads, left to right, `J_RH_EXHAUST` then `1C2L3L4E`, both at the same

connector's legend. Measured: 0.69 mm from `J_RH_EXHAUST`'s pads, 2.71 mm from
`J_ISOLOOP`'s.

Electrically the barrier is sound — see §4.

### Keypad-contact isolation barrier

Caption `KEYPAD ISOLATION COMB   >=6mm creepage   NO GND BOND   (contact columns face
pockets)` at 0.80 mm / 0.20 mm, x 76.03–135.97, y 20.18–21.82. Legible in both renders.
I checked its two claims:

* **"NO GND BOND"** — every filled zone (`GND` on F.Cu, B.Cu, In1.Cu; `3V3` on In2.Cu)
  is bounded to **y ≥ 53.000**, and a four-layer keepout covers y 10.0–53.0 plus a pocket
  per relay. No plane enters the keypad band. Visible directly in `twin_bottom.png`: the
  band above py ≈ 475 (board y ≈ 52.7) is bare laminate, the band below is poured.
  **Claim honest.**
* **"≥6 mm creepage"** — minimum **straight-line** copper gap between the isolated keypad
  domain (`KP_U1…U6`, `KP_D1…D4`, `U_SEL_BUS`, `D_SEL_BUS`, `RKEY_MID`, `RSTOP_MID`) and
  the SELV coil domain (`5V_KEY_RELAY`, `5V_STOP`, `COIL_*_N`, `GND`, `3V3`) is
  **6.310 mm** — `D_SEL_BUS` track (105.60, 37.60)–(105.60, 32.40) to `K_U6` pad 1
  (`5V_KEY_RELAY`) at (98.39, 32.92). Second-closest 6.310 mm, then 6.329, then a cluster
  at 6.532 mm (the relay's own pad-to-pad column spacing). Creepage ≥ clearance, and the
  slots lengthen it further. **Claim honest, with 0.31 mm of margin.**

| `PCC-SMP-K` | `J_TC` | unique |

Different pin counts and different JST families cannot inter-mate, so the only real
confusion pairs are **A↔B within each identical pair**:

* **`J_RH_AMBIENT` ↔ `J_RH_EXHAUST`** — distinguished by plain English words on silk,
  22 mm apart, unmistakable. ✅ *(But see P1-2: the nearest legend to `J_RH_EXHAUST` is
  the terminal block's pole map, tied at 0.69 mm.)*
* **`J_THERM_A` ↔ `J_THERM_B`** — distinguished **only by the trailing character** of a
  0.60 mm-tall refdes, 22 mm apart. There is no functional caption anywhere near them
  saying which zone or camera each serves. Swapping them transposes
  `3V3_SW_A`/`3V3_SW_B`, `SDA_A`/`SDA_B`, `SCL_A`/`SCL_B` and `TH_CAM_A`/`TH_CAM_B` — no
  voltage mismatch and, because TEMP_OK is the OR of two identically-thresholded
  comparators, **no loss of the safety function**; but the Pi's camera-to-thermistor
  attribution silently inverts. Recorded as P2-2.

**Answer to the mandated question:** yes, every cross-mateable pair on this board *can* be
told apart from the silk alone, provided the 0.60 mm refdes prints. The weakest case is
`J_THERM_A`/`J_THERM_B`, which hangs on one character.

### S6 — schematic readability: **EFFORTFUL**

`pdf/schematic.pdf` is one 900 × 450 pt page (317.5 × 158.75 mm). The ink occupies
x 266.2–633.4, y 6.3–442.6 pt = **129.5 × 153.9 mm — 41 % of the page width, 59 % of the
sheet blank**. Median word glyph-box height **2.10 pt = 0.740 mm**; minimum 1.75 pt =
0.617 mm. At 1:1 on paper that is at the edge of legibility, and there is no reason for
it: the drawing could have been 2.4× larger on the same sheet.

Wires **are** drawn — this is not the 0-drawn-wire fleet baseline. Real multi-segment
polylines with junction dots connect e.g. `J_PWR` pin 2 → junction → `D_ESD_IN` → GND, and
`Q_COILDRV` → `Q_COIL` → `R_HSG`. **But the primary connectivity mechanism is still stub
net labels.** Concrete example, the mandated power-entry trace:

> `J_PWR` pin 1 (`V5IN`) terminates in a stub label **`N5V_IN`** at (277.5, 234.9) pt.
> There is **no drawn wire** to the fuse. To continue you must find the *second*
> occurrence of the string `N5V_IN`, at `F1` pin 1 (303.5, 212.6) pt — 33 pt away, with
> six other symbols between them. `F1` pin 2 → label `N5V_FUSED`; find it again at
> `Q_REV`. `Q_REV` → `U_EFUSE`; `U_EFUSE` OUT → label `N5V_PROTECTED`, which occurs
> **6 times** on the sheet. So `J_PWR → F1 → Q_REV → U_EFUSE → N5V_PROTECTED → U_LDO`
> is **four string-matching hops**, not one traced path.

Ground is not a network at all: **`GND` appears 104 times** as an independent stub label;
`N3V3` 33 times; `VCC` 21 times. There is no sheet organisation — no power section, no
hierarchy, no titleblock content. The underlying `cooksense.kicad_sch` is 1133 symbol
instances, **0 wires**, 605 global labels, confirming the tscircuit render is the only
place any wire is drawn at all.

Grade **EFFORTFUL**. I could trace the chain, but only by string-search, and only on
screen at ≥ 400 % zoom.

### My independent view of the two A-RENDER items

**`U_LDO`, centre delta 1.248 mm, outward excursion 0.116 mm — pixel-extraction artifact,
NOT a mount error.** Crop of `twin_top.png` at x 17–28, y 65–76 shows a correctly seated

pad exposed to the east. A SOT-223 3D model's *plastic* is deliberately narrower than the

deliver for SOT-23. I concur.

### P1-1 · `J_PWR` — the 5 V input connector owns no legend

* **Evidence.** `5V SELV IN` (0.70 mm, x 35.87–42.13, y 53.58–55.02) is **7.80 mm** from

* **Why P1.** This is the canon P-SILK-OWN class verbatim, and the same shape as the
  precedent the policy already records (`USB 5V` 7.40 mm from `J_USB`, 6.01 mm from `F1`).
  Not P0 because the Micro-Fit is keyed and is the only 4-pad connector in that corner, so
  no wrong cable can physically go in — the cost is a technician reading "U_LDO" next to
  the power inlet.

### P1-2 · The NOT-SELV pole map `1C2L3L4E` is adjacent to the wrong connector

* **Evidence.** Text bbox x 186.76–191.49, y 100.39–101.61. Distance to
  `J_RH_EXHAUST` pads **0.69 mm**; to `J_ISOLOOP` pads **2.71 mm** — a 3.9× inversion. In
  the connector-nearest-legend test, `1C2L3L4E` **ties** `J_RH_EXHAUST`'s own refdes at

  pole assignment. Both are 0.60 mm, on the **same baseline**, 5.27 mm apart, so the
  bottom silk line reads `J_RH_EXHAUST   1C2L3L4E` as one legend (see bare render
  x 176–200, y 90–102).
* **Why P1 and not P0.** The block itself is correctly and unambiguously marked `NOT SELV`
  (1.84 mm, rank 1) and `ISO 30V`, the board-level note names the SE corner and repeats
  the pole map in full, and pad 1 is square — so nobody wires 30 V into the humidity
  connector. The defect is that the pole map, the one piece of information a person needs
  with a screwdriver in hand, is 2.71 mm from the screws and 0.69 mm from something else.
  Move it to y ≈ 87 (north of the block, beside `ISO 30V`/`NOT SELV`) or under the poles.

### P1-3 · Four component refdes print nowhere — no silk, and no F.Fab duplicate

* **Evidence.** `R_REF4` (58.80, 83.40), `R_SER2` (51.20, 78.80), `R_SER3` (49.50, 82.00),
  `R_MODEPD` (191.21, 63.90) all have `Reference` **invisible** on `F.Silkscreen`. Their

* **Why P1.** Canon P4 requires both "F.SilkS, visible, de-collided" **and** "F.Fab

  the DETAIL_DESIGN §4 / ADR-0019 deterministic safety-chain pulls — the resistor that
  holds `MODE_RAW` in its restrictive default. If it is ever suspect in the field there is
  no way to point at it on the board or on any drawing; only the CSVs name it. Making the
  refdes visible costs nothing.

### P1-4 · The whole PDF package is unidentified — blank Title, Sheet, Date and Rev on all 13 pages, and no page of `pcb_layers.pdf` names its layer
