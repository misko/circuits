## Summary of what was traced, and what it says

The protection topology is **correct**. I hunted specifically for the
consistently-wrong-together artifact set and did not find one: every diode, FET and
gate polarity is right in the netlist *and* right in the board *and* right against the
part dossiers' own pin tables, and where the three could have agreed with each other
while being wrong (TVS direction, RPP body-diode direction, comparator sense,
restrictive-default direction) I re-derived the physics rather than reading the intent.

What I did find is a class of defect one level up: **numbers that are declared,
graded, and load-bearing, but that omit a term.** The v1.7 release exists because
ADR-0021 turned an E-TOPO dropout FAIL into a 55 mV PASS. That 55 mV is derived from
three device resistances and **zero board copper**. I measured the copper. It is
102.24 mΩ, and it takes the margin to 16.3 mV at 20 °C and 8.6 mV at 70 °C.

## 1. Reverse-polarity / input protection — traced from the netlist

Chain, pad by pad (`cooksense.net`, confirmed identical in `cooksense.kicad_pcb`):

```
J_PWR.1 (V5IN) ─ 5V_IN ─┬─ D_ESD_IN.1  (PESD5V0S1BA, pin2→GND)
                        └─ F1.1  (MF-MSMF200L-2 polyfuse)
F1.2 ─ 5V_FUSED ─┬─ D_REVCLAMP.1 = K   (SS34, pad2 = A → GND)
                 └─ Q_REV.3 = DRAIN    (AO3401A, SOT-23 1=G 2=S 3=D)
Q_REV.1 = GATE → GND      Q_REV.2 = SOURCE → 5V_RPP
5V_RPP ─ U_EFUSE.3 + .4 (IN)  ·  R_OVT.1  ·  C_EFIN.1
U_EFUSE.5 (OUT) ─ 5V_PROTECTED ─ D_TVS.1 (SMBJ5.0A) · CE1.1 · U_LDO.3 · Q_COIL.2 · R_STOPRAIL.1 · J_LOADCELL.1
D_TVS.2 → GND
```

**TVS directionality — VERIFIED CORRECT, from pads not prose.** `D_TVS` pad 1 =
`5V_PROTECTED`, pad 2 = `GND` in both `.net` and `.kicad_pcb`. The footprint is
`Diode_SMD:D_SMB`; pad 1 sits at x = −2.15 mm and the F.Fab glyph puts the cathode bar
at x = −0.649 with the triangle apex on it and its base at x = +0.501, i.e. **pad 1 is
the cathode**. `02_parts/SMBJ5.0A/part.yaml` declares `pins: {1: K, 2: A}` and
`type: tvs_unidir_5v`. Cathode on the rail, anode on GND — the only correct
orientation for a unidirectional TVS. Same check on `D_REVCLAMP` (pad1 = K =
`5V_FUSED`, pad2 = A = GND — correct for a reverse-polarity shunt crowbar upstream of
nothing and *downstream of F1*, so a reverse hookup drives the trip current through the
polyfuse) and on `D_KSTOP` (pad1 = K = `5V_STOP`, pad2 = A = `COIL_STOP_N` — correct
flyback across the K_STOP coil).

**Reverse-polarity FET orientation — VERIFIED CORRECT.** `Q_REV` is an AO3401A P-channel
with **DRAIN on the supply side (`5V_FUSED`) and SOURCE on the load side (`5V_RPP`)**,
gate to GND. That is the orientation in which the intrinsic body diode (anode = drain
for a P-channel) conducts in the *normal* direction and the channel then shorts it, and
in which a reversed input reverse-biases the body diode while forcing V_GS = 0. The
opposite (source-to-supply) wiring — which is what a hurried reader writes — would let
the body diode conduct in the reverse-polarity case and protect nothing. It is not
wired that way.

**Load-switch orientation — VERIFIED CORRECT and note it is the OPPOSITE of Q_REV, as
it must be.** `Q_COIL` (same AO3401A part) has SOURCE on `5V_PROTECTED` and DRAIN on
`5V_KEY_RELAY`, so its body diode *blocks* when the gate is released — a load switch
that cannot be back-fed through its own diode. Gate held at the source by `R_HSG` 100 k
(off by default), pulled to GND by `Q_COILDRV` (2N7002) when `COIL_EN` is asserted. The
two AO3401As are deliberately opposite-handed and both are right.

**Ordering of the protection elements — correct.** The ESD device is *upstream* of the
fuse (so a strike does not have to pass the PTC), the reverse crowbar is *downstream* of
the fuse (so it can trip it), the eFuse is behind the RPP FET, and the 600 W TVS is on
the eFuse output. See P2-4 for what that last placement costs.

## 3. Thresholds recomputed at worst-case corners

### 3a. E-stop Schmitt input, `J_ESTOP` UNFITTED — RESTRICTIVE, confirmed

`ESTOP_RAW_IN` = {`J_ESTOP.3`, `R_ESTOPPD` 470 Ω→GND, `R_ESTOPS` 680 Ω→`ESTOP_RAW`,
`D_ESTOP`}. With no plug the only currents are `D_ESTOP` leakage (≤1 µA) and the HC14
input current (±1 µA):

```
V(ESTOP_RAW_IN) ≤ (1 µA + 1 µA) × 470 Ω               = 0.94 mV
V(ESTOP_RAW)    ≤ 1 µA × (680 + 470) + 1 µA × 470     = 1.62 mV
```

(ADR-0025 states 1.15 mV; it charges the HC14 current through 680 Ω and 470 Ω
separately rather than in series and omits the diode-leakage term. The difference is
0.47 mV and changes nothing.) Against the SN74HC14 V_T−(min) at the V_CC = 2.0 V row,
which bounds the 3.3 V figure from below, this is **two to three orders below the
threshold**. `U_SCHM` is a *double* inverter per channel (1A→1Y→2A→2Y), so polarity is
preserved: `ESTOP_RAW` LOW ⇒ **`ESTOP_OK` LOW**.

Propagation verified in the netlist, four independent stops (ADR-0025 claims three):

1. `U_AND1(MODE_AUTO_HW, WD_OK, ESTOP_OK)` → `AND1` LOW → `U_AND3` → `KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED` LOW → coil rail never authorised.
2. `U_FAULTAND(WD_OK, ESTOP_OK, TEMP_OK)` → `FAULT_SET_N` LOW → NAND latch sets `FAULT`, holds `FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR` LOW → a **second** term of `U_AND3` is LOW, and no `REARM_PULSE_N` can clear it while the set input is held.
3. `U_OSCLR(ESTOP_OK, STOP_REQ_N, 3V3)` → `OS_CLR_N` LOW → `U_ONESHOT.3` (R1_N) held asserted → `PRESS_TIMED` can never rise.
4. `U_CAND1(WD_OK, ESTOP_OK, TEMP_OK)` → `CTR_SAFE` LOW → `U_CAND2` → `CONTACTOR_DRV` LOW → the isolated contactor loop is open.

**No node in the chain floats.** Every net in it carries a deterministic pull in the
restrictive direction: `R_ESTOPPD` 470 Ω↓, `R_ESTOPOKPD` 100 k↓, `R_AND1PD`/`R_AND2PD`
100 k↓, `R_FSETNPD` 100 k↓, `R_FLCPD` 100 k↓, `R_CTRSAFEPD` 100 k↓, `R_MODEHWPD`
100 k↓, `R_HOSTAUTHPD` 100 k↓, `R_MCUENPD` 100 k↓, `R_STOPPD` 100 k↓,
`R_STOPREQNPD` 100 k↓, `R_COILENPD` 680 Ω↓, `R_DECUPD`/`R_DECDPD` 100 k↓,
`R_KRSTPD` 100 k↓, `R_RAEN*PD` 100 k↓ — and the two that must be pull-**ups** to be
safe are pull-ups: `R_REARMPU` 100 k↑ (a floating re-arm cannot clear the latch) and
`R_OE` 10 k↑ (`SR_OE_N` high = '595 outputs Hi-Z). `R_FAULTPU` 100 k↑ on `FAULT` gives
"fault asserted" if `U_LATCHA` is dead. I checked every one against its own direction
and found no inversion.

With the plug fitted (bridging 2–3), `ESTOP_RAW_IN` = 3V3 through a wire short and
`R_ESTOPPD` carries 3.399/470 = **7.23 mA**, dissipating 24.6 mW in an 0402 rated
62.5 mW (39 %). Acceptable, and well above the ~1 mA dry-circuit wetting threshold.

### 3b. Camera over-temperature comparator — the published trip point is WRONG by 2.1 °C

`DETAIL_DESIGN.md` §1 and ADR-0011 §1 both compute the hard-stop from a bare
`3V3_ANALOG → R_REF0 10 k → TH_CAM_A → NTC → GND` divider: node 1.650 V at 25 °C,
trip at **74.9 °C**. **The netlist has a third element on that node that the arithmetic
omits: `R_CLMPA` / `R_CLMPB`, 22 kΩ from `TH_CAM_A` / `TH_CAM_B` to GND**, fitted, in
the BOM (`0402WGF2202TCE`, C25768), and carrying its own `pin_on_net` invariants
(`electrical_invariants.yaml` lines 637–652, `adr: "0011"` — the *same* revision).

Re-deriving with the clamp in circuit, node V = 3.3 · X/(10 k + X) where X = R_NTC ∥ 22 k:

| quantity | DETAIL_DESIGN §1 | recomputed **with** `R_CLMPA` |
|---|---|---|
| node V at 25 °C | 1.650 V | **1.344 V** |
| R_NTC at trip (V_TH = 3.3·10/78 = 0.4231 V) | 1470.7 Ω | **1575.9 Ω** |
| **default trip (R_TH2 = 10 k)** | **74.9 °C** | **72.79 °C** |
| R_TH2 = 8.2 k | 81.0 °C | 79.26 °C |
| R_TH2 = 12 k | 69.4 °C | 66.99 °C |
| R_TH2 = 15 k | 63.0 °C | 60.03 °C |

(B25/85 = 3987 K, R25 = 10 kΩ, per `02_parts/KNTC0603-10KF3950`.) The **entire
solder-select field is optimistic by 1.7–3.0 °C.** The default is still inside the
brief's 70 (stop) / 75 (hard) band and errs *early*, so it fails safe — but with
DETAIL_DESIGN's own ±2.5 °C tolerance stack the low corner is now **70.3 °C**, i.e. the
hardware hard-stop can fire essentially on top of the software stop setpoint, which is
not what the band was designed to do. A field engineer picking `R_TH2` = 12 k from the
published table to get 69.4 °C actually gets 67.0 °C.

The hysteresis figure is wrong for the same reason: DETAIL_DESIGN says "R_HYS 1 M
against the 10 k source impedance ≈ 33 mV ≈ ~2 °C". The source impedance at trip is
10 k ∥ 22 k ∥ 1576 Ω = **1282 Ω**, so the hysteresis is (3.3 − 0.4231)/1 MΩ × 1282 Ω =
**3.7 mV ≈ 0.3 °C**, not 33 mV / 2 °C. Not load-bearing (the fault latch holds anyway),
but it is the second number on the same node computed without the clamp.

### 3c. Open-thermistor detect — correct, and the clamp is what makes it correct

`TCAM_OPEN` = 3.3 × 100/(62 + 100) = **2.0370 V** into `U_COMP2` IN+; `TH_CAM_*` into
IN−, so the output pulls LOW (TEMP_OK false) whenever `TH_CAM > 2.0370 V`.

- Harness **unplugged**: node = 3.3 × 22/(10 + 22) = **2.269 V** > 2.037 V ⇒ trips. At
  the low-rail corner 3.201 × 22/32 = 2.201 V, still trips.
- The clamp also keeps the maximum node reading **inside** the LMV393's common-mode
  ceiling: 2.269 V vs V_CC − 0.7 = 2.501 V at V_CC = 3.201 V, **+232 mV**. Without
  `R_CLMPA` the open node would sit at the bare 3.3 V rail, 0.8 V *outside* V_ICR, and
  the open-detect would never compare. The invariant file says exactly this and it is
  correct.
- **Cold false-trip corner**: with the clamp, the detect trips at R_NTC = 60.43 kΩ =
  **−10.4 °C**. The board's own declared corner (ADR-0021's OVLO worst case, and the
  DIP05 T_op) is **−20 °C**, where the node is 2.133 V — above the 2.037 V threshold. So
  between −20 °C and −10.4 °C the board declares a false open-thermistor fault and
  latches. It fails *safe*, and −20 °C is not a kitchen, but the declared envelope and
  the detect window do not agree over their bottom 10 °C. Recorded as P2-5.

Over-temp comparator polarity re-derived, not read: `U_COMP` IN+ = `TH_CAM_A` (falls
with rising temperature), IN− = `TCAM_THRESH`, open-drain out → LOW when hot.
**Correct.** `U_COMP2` IN+ = `TCAM_OPEN`, IN− = `TH_CAM_A` → LOW when open/cold.
**Correct.** Both wired-AND onto `TEMP_OK` through one 10 k pull-up; four open-drain
leakages (1 µA max each) plus two 1 MΩ hysteresis legs droop V_OH by ~80 mV to 3.32 V,
far above the LVC1G11 V_IH of 2.0 V. **OK.**

`R_TEMPOKSER` 10 k contention check (the direction that matters): a mis-written
MCP23017 GPB4 driving HIGH against a comparator pulling LOW gives
0.1 V + (3.3−0.1)×10/20 ≈ **0.5–0.7 V**, under the LVC1G11 V_IL of 0.8 V ⇒ the forced
node **loses** and TEMP_OK stays restrictive. Confirmed as claimed.

### 3d. Coil-enable gate threshold

`KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED` (LVC1G11, V_OH ≈ 3.2 V at 4.9 mA) leaves the board at `J_MODE.3`
and returns at `J_MODE.4` = `COIL_EN_IN`, loaded by `R_COILENPD` 680 Ω to GND, then

### Rail-by-rail

**3V3 (AMS1117-3.3).** Setpoint 3.201–3.399 V (cited full-temperature limits). Brownout
= the supervisor: TPS3823-33 V_IT− max **3.00 V**. Headroom **201 mV** at I_max 0.30 A ⇒
total IR budget **670 mΩ**. 3V3 rides an In2.Cu plane through 76 vias, so the real
distribution resistance is single-digit mΩ. **PASS, wide.**

**5V_PROTECTED → AMS1117 input.** This is the binding one and it is where the omission
is.

`power_tree.yaml` derives `vin_min` as `4.850 − 0.0952 = 4.7548 V`, from

three is a cited datasheet maximum and every one is right. **What is missing is the
board.** The four measured copper legs above sum to **102.24 mΩ** in the same series
path — 54 % more resistance on top of the term that was counted.

Recomputing at the same corner (shared legs at 0.50 A, the LDO-only leg at 0.30 A):

```
shared J_PWR→eFuse OUT:  (70.0 + 21.75 + 73.5 + 5.93 + 47.0 + 16.24) mΩ = 234.42 mΩ

eFuse OUT → U_LDO.3:       58.32 mΩ × 0.30 A                            =  17.50 mV

V_required = V_OUT(max) 3.399 + dropout 1.300 = 4.699 V

**margin +8.6 mV**. Add ~10 mΩ of Micro-Fit contact resistance (5 mV at 0.5 A) and it
is ~+3.6 mV. See P1-1.

**5V_KEY_RELAY → reed coils.** Rail floor recomputed with copper:
4.850 − 0.1172 (input chain) − 0.0270 (`5V_PROTECTED` copper to Q_COIL at 0.15 A)
− 0.0110 (Q_COIL R_DS(on) 73.5 mΩ × 0.15 A) − 0.0011 (branch copper at 10 mA)
= **4.6937 V** (power_tree declares 4.740 V). Brownout = the coil's must-operate at
+70 °C = 3.500 × (1 + 0.004 × 50) = **4.200 V**. Headroom **494 mV** against a driver
drop of 50.7 mV ⇒ **PASS, +443 mV.**

**5V_STOP → K_STOP.** 4.850 − 0.1172 − 0.0038 (copper) − 0.050 (an 0 Ω 0603 jumper's
typical 50 mΩ max, uncounted in `power_tree`) − 0.0007 = **4.678 V**; minus the 2N7002's
estimated 0.10 V ⇒ **PASS, +378 mV** at 70 °C.

## 5. E-OFF — de-energization and stored quiescent drain

`power_tree.yaml` declares `source_type: external_5v_selv` and **no `off_control`**.
That is honest rather than a gap: I confirmed against the netlist that there is no
battery, cell, supercap or coin cell (no such part in 239 components), and that the only
stored energy is `CE1` 220 µF plus ~90 µF of ceramics on `5V_PROTECTED` and `3V3`.

- Stored energy: ½ × 220 µF × 5.25² = **3.03 mJ**. Not a hazard, not a shock risk.
- Bleed path: the AMS1117's own quiescent (~5 mA class) plus the 3V3 logic discharges
  the bulk in single-digit milliseconds. There is no dedicated bleed resistor and none
  is needed at 5 V.
- **De-energization is by unplugging `J_PWR`, and nothing on the board can do it.**
  There is no switch in series with any rail, the eFuse's only enable pin
  (`U_EFUSE.2`) is committed to the OVLO divider and is not brought out, and `J_PI`'s
  5 V/3V3 pins are NC (`unconnected-(J_PI-pin1/2/4/17)`), so the Pi cannot back-power
  the board either.
- **Nothing asserted but absent.** The one thing that *would* have been an
  asserted-but-not-implemented off control — a declared `off_control` key — is not
  declared. Verified by grep across `03_src/rules/`: the only occurrence is in
  `contracts.md` describing the schema.

Quiescent drain while powered, worth recording because it is a design choice and not
free: with the `J_ESTOP` shorting plug fitted, `R_ESTOPPD` draws **7.23 mA** from 3V3
continuously — 2.4 % of the rail's 0.30 A budget, permanently, for a static logic level.

## 8. The safety AND-chain and the `J_ESTOP` shorting plug

**(a) No `DOOR_*` net or door part survives anywhere.** Measured:
`grep -ci door` → `cooksense.net` **0**, `cooksense.kicad_sch` **0**, `fab/bom.csv` **0**,
`fab/cpl.csv` **0**. The `.kicad_pcb` has exactly **1** hit and it is inside the
`TerminalBlock_KF350_4P` footprint's `descr` prose ("the ISO_CONTACTOR 2.0 mm moat to
J_DOOR's GND shell tab") — a historical string in a footprint description, no copper, no
pad, no net. The `.tsx` has 30 hits and **all 30 are comments** documenting the deletion.
`J_DOOR`, `R_DOORPD`, `R_DOORS`, `D_DOOR`, `R_DOOROKPD` are absent; `R_DOOROKSER` is
present as the renamed `R_GPB3PD` with `GPB3_SPARE` pulled down through 10 k, exactly as
ADR-0025 D4 specifies. **Clean.**

**(b) With `J_ESTOP` unfitted the chain is genuinely INERT** — computed in §3a: 1.62 mV
on `ESTOP_RAW` against a V_T− floor of hundreds of mV, four independent stops, all
restrictive. **Not permissive.**

**(c) Nothing in the chain floats** — every net enumerated in §3a carries a resistor to
a rail in the restrictive direction.

**The AND-chain as built** (verified term by term against the netlist, against
`BRIEF.md`:82):

```
KEY_RELAY_ALLOWED = U_AND3( AND1, AND2, FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR )
  AND1 = U_AND1( MODE_AUTO_HW, WD_OK, ESTOP_OK )
  AND2 = U_AND2( TEMP_OK, MCU_RELAY_ENABLE, HOST_AUTH )
```

Seven terms, exactly the brief's seven, no substitutions, no permissive tie-off. The
output then leaves the board at `J_MODE.3` and returns at `J_MODE.4` — the Manual/Auto
pole physically breaks the chain, which is the brief's "MANUAL = relay power physically
disabled". Contactor gate `CTR_SAFE = U_CAND1(WD_OK, ESTOP_OK, TEMP_OK)` →
`U_CAND2(CTR_SAFE, FAULT_LATCH_CLEAR, CONTACTOR_REQ)`. Fault latch is a proper
cross-coupled NAND SR (`U_LATCHA`/`U_LATCHB`) that cannot be cleared while the set
condition persists. All correct.

**One structural consequence ADR-0025 names but does not close.** After the door's
removal, `ESTOP_OK` is the *only* field permission left, and all four of its stops
originate in `U_SCHM` stages 1–2 — one package, one die. `MODE_AUTO_HW` originates in
stages 3–4 of the **same package**. And `MODE_RAW` is the one field-fed input on this
board with **neither a series element nor an ESD device**: `J_MODE.2` → `MODE_RAW` →
`U_SCHM.5` directly, with only `R_MODEPD` 10 kΩ to GND (compare `ESTOP_RAW_IN`, which
gets `R_ESTOPS` 680 Ω *and* `D_ESTOP`, and `COIL_EN_IN`, which gets `R_COILENS` 680 Ω
*and* `D_COILEN`). A metre of panel-switch harness lands on that pin unprotected. If a
strike takes `U_SCHM`'s stage-2 output driver to a shorted-high failure, `ESTOP_OK` is
stuck **permissive**, and the expander readback (`R_ESTOPOKSER` → GPB2) samples the
*same net*, so software has no independent way to see it. → **P1-11**

Pitch census taken from the BOM and the placed footprints, not from the ADR:

| connector | part | family / pitch | circuits | how many of this family on the board |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `J_ESTOP` | SM03B-SRSS-TB | **JST SH, 1.00 mm** | 3 | **1 — unique** |
| `J_RH_AMBIENT`, `J_RH_EXHAUST` | SM05B-GHS-TB | JST GH, 1.25 mm | 5 | 5 (GH) |
| `J_THERM_A`, `J_THERM_B` | SM08B-GHS-TB | JST GH, 1.25 mm | 8 | 5 (GH) |
| `J_KEY_MATRIX` | SM10B-GHS-TB | JST GH, 1.25 mm | 10 | 5 (GH) |
| `J_MODE` | S4B-ZR-SM4A-TF | JST ZH, 1.50 mm | 4 | 1 |
| `J_LOADCELL` | B5B-XH-A | JST XH, 2.50 mm | 5 | 1 |
| `J_PWR` | 43650-0224 | Micro-Fit 3.0, 3.00 mm, keyed peg | 2 | 1 |

**Does the SH-3 keying actually work at 1.00 vs 1.25 mm?** Checked in both directions.

- *Into* `J_ESTOP`: the SH-3 shroud cavity is 5.0 × 2.9 mm; a GHR-05V-S plug is
  7.50 × 4.15, ZHR-4 is 7.50 × 3.40, XHP-5 is far larger. **Hard interference — nothing
  on this board can enter it.**
- *Out of* `J_ESTOP`: an SH-3 plug (6.0 × 2.8) can be pushed loosely into a GH or ZH
  shroud, so the claim has to rest on **contact alignment**, and it does. SH circuits sit
  at 0.00 / 1.00 / 2.00 mm; GH posts at 0.00 / 1.25 / 2.50; ZH at 0.00 / 1.50 / 3.00.
  Circuit-3 offsets are **0.50 mm (GH)** and **1.00 mm (ZH)** against a ~0.30 mm post.
  **Circuit 3 cannot engage in any foreign housing on this board** — and circuit 3 is
  where `ESTOP_RAW_IN` lives, with the plug bridging 2–3. The short cannot be
  completed elsewhere. Circuit 1 (offset 0.00, i.e. the one that *can* engage) is GND,
  which is the restrictive landing in every cell. **The keying is real and correctly
  reasoned.**

**What a mis-plug DRIVES onto a safety node — every GH cell, worked:**

| mis-plug | what lands where | board survives? | permission |
|---|---|---|---|
| `J_RH_AMBIENT` ↔ `J_RH_EXHAUST` (identical GH-5) | bus A pod on bus B | yes | none touched — data-labelling error only |
| `J_THERM_A` ↔ `J_THERM_B` (identical GH-8) | camera A thermistors read as B | yes | both feed `TEMP_OK` identically |
| GH-5 pod into GH-8 at circuits 1–5 | pod shield → `TH_CAM_A` | yes | shield is grounded at the pod ⇒ node → 0 V ⇒ "very hot" ⇒ **TEMP_OK LOW, restrictive** |
| GH-5 pod into GH-8 at circuits 2–6 | pod 3V3 rail → GND | yes — AMS1117 current-limits and thermal-shuts; `Q_SWRHA` is a 4 A part; the rail droops ⇒ TPS3823 resets ⇒ **restrictive** | pod SDA → `TH_CAM_A` pulled toward 3.3 V ⇒ **open-detect trips** |
| GH-5 pod into GH-8 at circuits 3–7 | pod GND → `SCL_A` (stuck bus, recoverable); pod SDA → `TH_CAM_A` | yes | **open-detect trips, restrictive** |
| GH-5 pod into GH-8 at circuits 4–8 | pod GND → `TH_CAM_A` | yes | node → 0 V ⇒ **TEMP_OK LOW, restrictive** |

**Zero cells reach a permission in the permissive direction.** The `R_CLMPA`/`R_CLMPB`
22 kΩ bleed is what keeps every one of those injected readings inside the LMV393's
common-mode range while still tripping a detect.

**The residual the ADR did not analyse: the plug's own crimp.** `J_ESTOP` is
1 = GND, 2 = 3V3, 3 = `ESTOP_RAW_IN`, and the shorting plug bridges **2–3**. A plug
crimped one position off bridges **1–2 = 3V3 directly to GND with zero series
impedance.** ADR-0025 D5c analyses the plug in *foreign* shrouds thoroughly and does not
consider the plug mis-built in its *own* shroud. The outcome is benign (the AMS1117
current-limits and the board simply does not come up — restrictive), but it is a
1-position error on a hand-crimped object that ships loose. The 4-circuit
`SM04B-SRSS-TB` (C160404) the ADR already costed and recorded would let a spare circuit
sit between 3V3 and GND. → **P2-10**

`J_PWR` cannot be mated reversed (Micro-Fit polarizing peg, per the dossier).
`J_ISOLOOP` is a screw terminal, not pluggable — but see P1-4.

### P1-1 — the LDO dropout margin that unblocked this release omits 102.24 mΩ of the board's own copper; +55 mV becomes +16.3 mV (20 °C) / +8.6 mV (70 °C)

```
J_PWR.1  → F1.1        21.75 mΩ
F1.2     → Q_REV.3      5.93 mΩ
Q_REV.2  → U_EFUSE.3   16.24 mΩ
U_EFUSE.5→ U_LDO.3     58.32 mΩ
                       ────────
                      102.24 mΩ  omitted

**134.71 mV**, so V_IN(LDO) = **4.7153 V** against a requirement of 3.399 + 1.300 =
4.699 V ⇒ **+16.3 mV**. At 70 °C (copper +19.65 %) ⇒ **+8.6 mV**. Add ~10 mΩ of
Micro-Fit contact resistance and it is ~+3.6 mV.

itself: the 1.300 V dropout is the datasheet's **0.8 A** figure applied at this rail's

between it and zero is an admittedly unquantified conservatism.

**Evidence**: `03_src/rules/power_tree.yaml` lines 85–130 and 176 (`dropout_mv: 1300`);

`KEY_RESET_N` (.33), `SPI_*`, `I2C_*`, `INT_ALERT` all connect Pi silicon to board
silicon directly.

The power side of the rule *is* honoured — `J_PI` pins 1, 2, 4, 17 are
`unconnected-(J_PI-pinN-PadN)`, so neither board can power the other through the header
rails. But with one side unpowered, the signal pins phantom-power the other through the
receiving devices' ESD clamps: board-off/Pi-on injects through `U_SCHM`, `U_AND*`,
`Q_STOPDRV`; board-on/Pi-off injects into the Pi's GPIO clamps from `U_ADC.12`/`U_TC.11`
(`SPI_MISO`) and `U_EXP.20` (`INT_ALERT`). No ADR withdraws the requirement.
**Evidence**: netlist nets `HOST_AUTH`, `MCU_RELAY_ENABLE`, `STOP_REQ`, `SPI_MISO`,
`INT_ALERT`; `BRIEF.md`:74; `ARCHITECTURE.md`:58.
**Why P1**: a commissioned mitigation is claimed as present in ARCHITECTURE and is
absent. ORDER_README must at minimum state the power-sequencing constraint.

### P1-4 — the isolated contactor loop is declared ≤30 V / ≤50 mA; the opto can guarantee 2.66 mA and there is no freewheel path for an inductive load

`nets.yaml` ISO_CONTACTOR: `current: "signal (<=30V / <=50mA dry contact; the opto
collector is the limit)"`. The drive chain is
`U_CAND2.4` → `R_OPTOLED` **330 Ω** → `U_OPTO.1` (LTV-817S-TA1 LED anode), cathode to
GND. Worst case for minimum LED current: 3V3 at 3.201 V, LVC1G11 V_OH drop ~0.15 V at
this current, LTV-817 V_F max ~1.3 V:

```
I_F  = (3.201 − 0.15 − 1.3)/330 = 5.31 mA
CTR  = 50 % min  (02_parts/LTV-817S-TA1: "ctr: 50-600% (TA1 bin)")
I_C  = 5.31 × 0.50 = 2.66 mA guaranteed
```

**2.66 mA, not 50 mA — 19× short of the declared class figure**, before any allowance
for CTR degradation with LED age (routinely −50 % over life) or low temperature.
Raising it is not available: 50 mA of I_C at 50 % CTR needs 100 mA of I_F, above the
LED's own continuous rating and far above what an SN74LVC1G11 can source.

Second, and independent: `J_ISOLOOP` puts the opto's collector and emitter in series
with an external E-stop pole and an external **contactor circuit**, and there is **no
diode, no snubber and no TVS anywhere on the isolated side** — `CONTACTOR_C`,
`CONTACTOR_E` and `CONTACTOR_LOOP` connect only the opto and the four screw terminals.
Interrupting a 30 V contactor coil with a bare phototransistor produces an inductive
kick that will exceed the LTV-817's **V_CEO of 35 V** on the first operation. Nothing in
`02_parts/`, `nets.yaml`, ADR-0013 or ADR-0011 requires the installer to fit an external
freewheel diode.
**Evidence**: `03_src/rules/nets.yaml` ISO_CONTACTOR block; nets `CONTACTOR_DRV`,
`OPTO_LED_A`, `CONTACTOR_C`, `CONTACTOR_E`, `CONTACTOR_LOOP`;
`02_parts/LTV-817S-TA1/part.yaml` (`ctr: 50-600%`, `vceo: 35V`);
`02_parts/SN74LVC1G11DBVR/part.yaml`.
**Why P1**: the declared interface capability is unachievable by 19×, and the obvious
intended load will destroy the isolating device on first use. Both belong in
ORDER_README before anything is wired to that terminal block.

### P1-6 — the published camera over-temperature trip point is wrong by 2.1 °C because the fitted 22 kΩ bleed is not in the arithmetic

**Evidence**: `DETAIL_DESIGN.md` §1 tables; `01_docs/decisions/0011-…` lines 21–25;

### P1-11 — the last remaining field permission is a single-die single point of failure, fed by the one input with neither a series element nor a clamp, with no independent readback

After ADR-0025, all four stops in the safety chain originate in `U_SCHM` stages 1–2

## P2

### P2-1 — the TVS's 9.2 V clamp is above the DIP05 coil's 7.5 V maximum
`D_TVS` clamps `5V_PROTECTED` at 9.2 V (at 65.3 A, 10/1000 µs); `5V_STOP` is that rail
through a 0 Ω link and carries `K_STOP`'s coil, rated **7.5 V max**. For a 10/1000 µs
transient this is not a hazard (the coil's rating is thermal/insulation), and the
*sustained* case is bounded by the OVLO at 6.2394 V < 7.5 V. Recorded because it is the
one pair in the table that goes negative and because the eFuse dossier already tracks
the 7.5 V coil limit for the OVLO argument but not for the clamp.

### P2-2 — 5 V and 3V3 leave the board on `J_LOADCELL` with no clamp, to a module whose ratings are outside this tree
`J_LOADCELL` = 1:`5V_PROTECTED`, 2:`3V3`, 3:GND, 4:`LC_DAT`, 5:`LC_CLK`. The two signals
get 33 Ω + PESD5V0S1BA; **the two power pins get nothing**. `U_EFUSE.5` → `J_LOADCELL.1`
measures **136.59 mΩ** of copper. A 9.2 V TVS clamp event propagates down that cable to
an HX711-class part (abs-max V_SUP typically 6 V). Out of scope to verify (cook-loadcell
is a separate sealed board) — recorded so it is not silently assumed safe.

### P2-3 — the OVLO-to-TVS margin is 42.5 mV at the cold corner
Latest OVLO trip **6.2394 V** vs `D_TVS` V_BR(min) at −20 °C = 6.40 × (1 − 0.00041 × 45)
= **6.2819 V**. Both corners can occur together. 0.68 % of margin. It is positive and it
is the declared design (ADR-0021 states both numbers); recorded because at −40 °C the
TVS floor is 6.0975 V and the ordering **inverts** — the board must never be qualified
below its declared −20 °C.

### P2-4 — the 600 W TVS is on the eFuse OUTPUT; the input side has only a SOD-323 ESD diode with no published ratings
`D_TVS` (SMBJ5.0A, 600 W) sits on `5V_PROTECTED`. Everything upstream — `J_PWR`, `F1`,
`D_REVCLAMP`, `Q_REV`, `U_EFUSE`'s IN pins — is defended against a cable-borne surge only
by `D_ESD_IN`, a PESD5V0S1BA in SOD-323 whose surge rating is not in its dossier
(P1-5). The parts behind it are robust (eFuse IN abs-max 20 V, AO3401A V_DS 30 V), so
this is probably fine; it is unverifiable from the permitted inputs.

### P2-5 — the open-thermistor detect false-trips over the bottom 10 °C of the declared temperature envelope
With `R_CLMPA` in circuit the detect trips at R_NTC = 60.43 kΩ = **−10.4 °C**. The
board's declared corner (ADR-0021's OVLO worst case; the DIP05 T_op) is **−20 °C**, where
`TH_CAM` = 2.133 V > the 2.037 V threshold ⇒ false open fault, latched. Fails safe;
−20 °C is not a kitchen; recorded because the two declarations do not agree.

### P2-6 — the STOP relay's driver has no datasheet, no limits, and no committed R_DS(on)
`02_parts/2N7002/part.yaml` is four lines of substance: mpn, type, three pin names, an
LCSC code. No `datasheet:`, no `limits:`, no `electrical:`. This part is `Q_STOPDRV`
(the K_STOP coil sink — the actuator that must work when the AND-chain has killed
everything else), `Q_COILDRV` (the coil-rail enable) and the four sensor-rail drivers.
ADR-0023 flags it honestly and I confirm the verdict is insensitive (§7). Owed: a
committed datasheet and an R_DS(on) at V_GS = 3.3 V.

### P2-7 — `02_parts/SN74HC14DR/part.yaml` still describes the deleted door channel, and it is the file the threshold argument reads from
`electrical.pins.11`: *"5A = DOOR_RAW. Field-fed via J_DOOR.2 → R_DOORS 680R
(ADR-0024)"*, plus a gotcha describing the DOOR double-inverter and `R_DOOROKPD`. The
netlist has `U_SCHM.11` and `.13` on GND and `5Y`/`6Y` unconnected (ADR-0025 D3, correctly
implemented). The stale text sits in the same `electrical:` block as the `v_il_max: 0.700`
that ADR-0024's pod-mismate bound is computed against, so it is not inert prose.

### P2-8 — two cells of ADR-0023's coil-margin table are arithmetically wrong and the table is non-monotonic
V_PI(+55) = 3.990 V not 4.140; V_PI(+65) = 4.130 V not 4.320 (§7). Both tables in the
ADR carry the same two bad cells, and 4.140 → 4.320 → 4.200 across rows 55/65/70 cannot
be a monotonic function of temperature. Errors are conservative; the binding rows
(+70, +75) are right; the verdict is unaffected.

### P2-9 — `i_coil_design_mA: 7.0` is mislabelled in two dossiers
Both `TBD62083AFWG` and `ULN2803ADWR` declare `i_coil_design_mA: 7.0` as "one
DIP05-1A72-13L 5 V coil, 500R nom, at 20 °C". 5 V / 500 Ω = **10.0 mA**; 7.0 mA is
3.5 V / 500 Ω, the current at the *must-operate threshold*. Understates the driver drop
by ~30 % (45.5 vs 50.7 mV at 70 °C). Immaterial to the verdict, wrong as a label, and it
is the number the `node_level` invariant is built on.

### P2-10 — a one-position crimp error on the `J_ESTOP` shorting plug shorts 3V3 to GND
Pinout is 1 = GND, 2 = 3V3, 3 = `ESTOP_RAW_IN`, plug bridges 2–3. A plug crimped one
position off bridges 1–2 = **3V3 to GND with zero series impedance**. Outcome is benign
(AMS1117 current-limit + thermal shutdown; the board does not come up = restrictive) but
the object ships loose and is hand-crimped. ADR-0025 D5c analyses the plug exhaustively
in *foreign* shrouds and not in its own. The `SM04B-SRSS-TB` (C160404) the ADR already
costed and rejected on 1.0 mm of column slack would allow a spare circuit between the
rail and ground.

### P2-11 — `board_netlist_parity.py` crashed in this archive; the board-vs-netlist parity claim has no result
`verification/board_parity.txt` ends in
`AttributeError: 'NoneType' object has no attribute 'GetFootprints'` at
`board_netlist_parity.py:24` (loading the sealed board). The property is covered from
another direction — S-COUNT passes 4/4 over 239 refdes, and the DRC gate reports
`Found 0 schematic parity issues` — and I independently confirmed pad→net agreement
between `.net` and `.kicad_pcb` on every protection ref I traced. Recorded because a
gate that produced a traceback is not a gate that passed.

### P2-12 — `R_STOPRAIL`'s 0 Ω jumper resistance is uncounted
`power_tree.yaml` treats `5V_STOP` as `vout_min = vin_min` ("0R link"). A 0 Ω 0603
(`0603WAF0000T5E`) is typically specified at **≤50 mΩ**, which at the 20 mA rail costs
1 mV — genuinely negligible, but it is the third uncounted series term after the copper
of P1-1 and it is on the always-available STOP rail.

3. **All five PESD5V0S1BA clamp pairs.** No ratings published (P1-5).
4. **eFuse inrush.** `C_DVDT` = 1 nF against ~280 µF of output capacitance; the dV/dt
   relation is not in the dossier, so I cannot confirm the ramp stays under the current
   limit. First-order estimate says yes by ~2.5×.
5. **Copper weight.** The `.kicad_pro` declares no stackup; every resistance in this
   review assumes 1 oz outer. At 2 oz, P1-1's omitted term halves to 51 mΩ and the
   margin becomes +36 mV / +30 mV — still under the declared 55 mV, so the finding
   stands either way, but the magnitude does not.
6. **`J_THERM_*` plug-mate compatibility with the SHOU HAN substitute** — `assembly.yaml`
   says it is unverified and I have no way to verify it here.

## Bottom line

The circuit is right. I set out to find a coherently-wrong protection chain and there
isn't one: the TVS points the right way in the netlist, in the board and against its
dossier's own pin table; the reverse-polarity FET is drain-to-source in the orientation
that actually blocks; the coil-rail switch is deliberately the opposite handedness and
that is also right; the comparator senses are right; every restrictive default pulls the
restrictive way; the door is genuinely and completely gone; the unfitted E-stop measures
1.6 mV and stops the board four independent ways; and ADR-0023's coil-driver verdict
survives my recomputation with the board's real copper in it.

What blocks is one sourcing fact (P0-1: 5 pieces in stock against 10 required) and a
set of *declared numbers with a term missing* — of which P1-1 is the important one,
because the 55 mV that turned this release's E-TOPO gate from FAIL to PASS is 16 mV once
the board's own 102 mΩ of copper is counted, and 9 mV hot.
