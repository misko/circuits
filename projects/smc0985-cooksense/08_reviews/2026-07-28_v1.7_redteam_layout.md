## P0 — blocks the order

### **P0-1 — The eFuse's three programmed setpoints are set by four components and NOT ONE of them is computed anywhere in this archive.**

`U_EFUSE` (TPS259573DSGR) is the board's only over-voltage cutoff, only current limit and only inrush control — BRIEF §78 commissions it as "OV cutoff/eFuse".

Measured from the board, cross-checked to `fab/bom.csv` LCSC codes:

| component | net | measured value | what it programs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `R_OVT` | 5V_RPP → EF_OVLO | **100 kΩ** (C25741 = 0402WGF1003TCE) | OV cutoff, top leg |
| `R_OVB` | EF_OVLO → GND | **15 kΩ** (C25756 = 0402WGF1502TCE) | OV cutoff, bottom leg |
| `R_ILM` | EF_ILM → GND | **1.2 kΩ** | current limit |
| `C_DVDT` | EF_DVDT → GND | **1 nF** | inrush slew into ≈ **240 µF** of `5V_PROTECTED` bulk (CE1 220 µF + C_IN1 10 µF + C_LDOIN 10 µF + C_IN2 100 nF) |

Divider ratio = 15/(100+15) = **0.13043**, so `EF_OVLO` sits at **0.652 V at 5.00 V in**. The cutoff therefore lands at:

- **5.50 V** only if V_OVLO = 0.717 V
- **6.13 V** only if V_OVLO = 0.800 V
- **9.20 V** if V_OVLO = 1.20 V ← the value common across TI's eFuse family
- **10.35 V** if V_OVLO = 1.35 V

`02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/part.yaml` states the intent — *"For a 5V nominal rail, set the OVLO divider for ~5.5-6V cutoff… **verify divider math + inrush (Cdvdt) in power review**"* — and **no such verification ships**. I grepped ORDER_README.md, every `verification/*.md`, every `verification/*.txt`, `electrical_invariants.yaml` and DETAIL_DESIGN.md: the only artifact touching it is `net_has_part` on `EF_OVLO` asserting *"the divider EXISTS"*. That is precisely the failure this board's own E-INV doctrine names — an invariant that pins existence does not pin the number — except worse, because even `part_value` would not close it: **nothing anywhere converts 100 k / 15 k into a trip voltage and compares it to a requirement.**

Why it blocks rather than being a P1: if the threshold is the 1.20 V figure, the cutoff is at **9.20 V**, and `5V_PROTECTED` feeds `U_ULNA.10` / `U_ULNB.10` COM and, through `Q_COIL` and `R_STOPRAIL`, twelve **DIP05-1A72-12L coils whose datasheet maximum is 7.5 V** (`02_parts/DIP05-1A72-12L/part.yaml` `limits.coil`). That is a hardware-damage path on the relay set that presses keys on a cooking appliance.

**I am NOT claiming the setpoint is wrong.** SLVSE57C does not ship in this archive and I cannot open it. I am claiming the setpoint is **unverified**, that the part dossier explicitly demanded it be verified, and that the arithmetic which *can* be done from the shipped values does not obviously land on the stated 5.5–6 V intent. This is a ten-minute datasheet lookup that either clears or reveals a P0. On a board whose whole thesis is "report measured numbers", shipping the only OV protection with no computed trip point is not a P2.

**What should happen:** open SLVSE57C §7.5, record V_OVLO(rising) with a page cite, compute the cutoff, compare to the 7.5 V coil ceiling and the 15 V AMS1117 V_IN(max), and do the same for R_ILM → I_LIM and C_DVDT → dV/dt against 240 µF (an auto-retry hiccup at power-up is the failure mode there — the part is TSD-auto-retry, not latch-off, per its own gotcha). Emit all three as `part_value`-backed invariants with the arithmetic in DETAIL_DESIGN.

## P1 — must be documented or mitigated before first power

### **P1-1 — The AMS1117 SOT-223 tab is NOT flooded. `power_tree.yaml` says it is, and that sentence is the entire basis for not derating the dissipation.**

`03_src/cooksense/rules/power_tree.yaml`, `rails: 3V3`:

> *"Not derated further: the tab is VOUT and is flooded with 3V3 copper (that part.yaml's layout `notes`), which is the mounting Table 1 (p.5) characterises at 55-65 C/W. PD = (5.500 − 3.201) × 0.3 = 690 mW vs 1200 mW → 57%, PASS"*

MEASURED:

| quantity | measured |
|---|---|
| `U_LDO.4` tab pad (VOUT/3V3) | 2.000 × 3.800 = **7.600 mm²** |
| connected F.Cu 3V3 island containing the tab | **7.365 mm²** — bbox x[24.15, 26.15] y[68.10, 71.90], i.e. **the pad and nothing else** |
| all 3V3 copper on F.Cu within ±10 mm of `U_LDO` | **13.01 mm²** of a 400 mm² window (3.3 %) |
| all 3V3 copper on F.Cu, whole board | **59.3 mm²** (B.Cu: 6.0 mm²) — the rail *is* the In2 plane (8416.8 mm²), reached by **5.97 mm** of track |
| thermal vias in the tab | **2**, 0.250 mm OD / 0.150 mm drill |

There is no spreader. The only heat path off the tab is two 0.15 mm-drill barrels down through **both** dielectrics to In2 (≈ 1.275 mm on JLC's 4-layer 1.6 mm stackup). With 25 µm barrel plating, k_Cu = 385 W/m·K, k_FR4 = 0.3 W/m·K:

- per via **241 K/W**, two in parallel **120 K/W**; FR-4 through the 7.6 mm² pad **559 K/W**
- combined **≈ 99 K/W tab → In2 plane**
- at the declared PD of **0.690 W**: the tab sits **≈ 68 K above the plane**, before junction-to-tab and before plane-to-ambient

The ds1117 45–65 °C/W mounting requires hundreds of mm² of tab-connected copper. This board provides **7.365 mm²** — the minimum-pad case, where published SOT-223 θ_JA is 110–160 °C/W. At 0.690 W that is ΔT = 76–110 K; in a cooking appliance at 40–50 °C ambient, T_J = 116–160 °C against the AMS1117's 125 °C ceiling.

Honest counterweight: the **realistic** 3V3 load is far under the declared 0.3 A (two MLX90640 pods ≈ 46 mA + MCP23017 ≈ 1 mA + MAX31856 ≈ 2 mA + MCP3208 ≈ 0.5 mA + ~20 CMOS logic ICs at µA + opto LED 5 mA ≈ 60–100 mA). At 100 mA and 5.0 V in, PD = 0.170 W → ΔT = 19–27 K, comfortable. The failure direction is also benign: AMS1117 thermal foldback drops 3V3, which drops the eleven restrictive pulls → coil rail off.

So the defect is not "it will burn". The defect is that **an envelope was declared (0.3 A) and the mounting was never measured against it, and the justification for not measuring is a copper flood that does not exist on the shipped board.** Fix either end: pour 3V3 on F.Cu around the tab (there are 276.2 mm² of GND pour in the 20 × 20 mm window that could be re-partitioned) and add 4–6 more tab vias, **or** measure the real 3V3 draw at bring-up and re-declare `iout_max_A` with the measurement beside it. Escalates to P0 if the measured load exceeds ~0.2 A.

### **P1-2 — The eFuse has NO input capacitor, and the gate that should have caught it graded nothing because the budget names a net that does not exist.**

`02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/part.yaml` `layout.source` quotes SLVSE57C §11 / Fig. 68: *"0.01uF+ Cin between IN and GND with MINIMAL loop area at the IN/GND terminals"*, and declares `keep_short: {net: 5V_SELV, max_span_mm: 3, …}`.

MEASURED: the eFuse's IN net is **`5V_RPP`** (`U_EFUSE.3`, `U_EFUSE.4`). Capacitors on `5V_RPP`: **zero**. On `5V_FUSED`: zero. On `5V_IN`: zero. The whole input chain `5V_IN → D_ESD_IN → F1 → 5V_FUSED → D_REVCLAMP → Q_REV → 5V_RPP → U_EFUSE` carries **no capacitance at all**; the nearest is `C_IN2` 100 nF / `C_IN1` 10 µF on the **output** side, and the TVS `D_TVS` (SMBJ5.0A) is also on the output. `D_ESD_IN` (PESD5V0S1BA, an ESD diode, not a surge TVS) is **26.1 mm** of 0.5 mm track from `U_EFUSE.3`.

The four eFuse budgets measured against the board's **real** net names:

| datasheet budget | real net | budget | measured span | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cin at IN/GND, minimal loop | `5V_RPP` | 3 mm | **15.581 mm** (`Q_REV.2`↔`R_OVT.1`), **and no capacitor on the net** | **FAIL** |
| OVLO divider local to pin 2 | `EF_OVLO` | 5 mm | **8.473 mm** | **FAIL** |
| Rilm local to pin 7 | `EF_ILM` | 5 mm | **6.982 mm** | **FAIL** |
| Cdvdt local to pin 1 | `EF_DVDT` | 5 mm | **4.524 mm** | PASS |

And the shipped `verification/padj_unreached_census.md` records **all four** as `UNREACHED — 0 pads`, because the budgets name the **datasheet pin names** (`5V_SELV`, `EN_OVLO_N`, `ILM`, `dVdt`) and the board's nets are called something else. This is a dead gate sitting on the board's only protection IC: three genuine violations, graded by nothing, and recorded as "unreached" rather than as a naming defect. `EF_ILM` at 6.982 mm matters directly — the dossier says *"trace resistance adds directly to current-limit error"*.

**What should happen:** add a 0.1 µF 0402 within 3 mm of `U_EFUSE.3/4` with its GND return at the pin; rename the four `keep_short` nets to the board's net names so P-ADJ can fire; re-run and record the three spans.

## Why DO-NOT-ORDER

The isolation holds — 6.1200 mm, 2.0000 mm on all four layers, keepouts clean, no floating conductor splitting either gap. Nothing hangs off the board, no courtyards overlap, no copper is stranded, no analog net runs near a coil return, the ULN banks and the eFuse pad are thermally trivial at their real currents, and the eleven new v1.7 pulls landed correctly with their directions right. That is a lot of good work and none of it is what stops the order.

What stops it is that **the board's only over-voltage protection has no computed setpoint anywhere in the archive**, on a rail that feeds twelve coils rated 7.5 V; that **the LDO's dissipation was cleared against a copper flood that does not exist**; that **the eFuse's mandated input capacitor is absent and the gate that should have caught it was dead on a net-name mismatch**; and that the archive ships a `FAIL` gate plus two contradictory values for a safety-barrier number.

None of it is unfixable. P0-1 is a datasheet lookup and three lines of arithmetic. P1-2 is one 0402. P1-1 is a re-pour and four vias, or a bring-up measurement and an honest re-declaration of `iout_max_A`. P1-3/4/6 are paperwork. Close those and I would sign it.
