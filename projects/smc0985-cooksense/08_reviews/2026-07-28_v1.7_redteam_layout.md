## P1-a — the tightest point in the moat is invisible to every gate on this board
**KiCad DRC does not test zone-fill-vs-pad clearance.** Proved rather than
asserted: with opto_isolation raised 2 -> 8 mm and keypad_isolation 6 -> 12 mm,
DRC returned 514 clearance violations and the item-pair histogram is
(Track,pad) 213, (Via,pad) 156, (Track,Track) 51, (Track,Zone) 19, (Via,Zone) 8
— **(Zone,pad) = 0**, while K_STOP pad 3 sits 7.38 mm from the pour edge and
must have violated a 12 mm rule. So the 2.0000 mm figure — the minimum in the
whole moat, and the only number sitting exactly at its limit — is produced by a
hand-typed rectangle and CONFIRMED BY NOTHING.

## P1-b (BLOCKING) — J_ISOLOOP's silk label is printed on J_RH_EXHAUST
Of 13 connectors, exactly one inverts:
  J_ISOLOOP refdes bbox x[187.398,191.202] y[100.539,101.461]
  its OWN courtyard starts x=191.555 -> the label ends **0.353 mm outside its
    own part, on the wrong side**
  J_RH_EXHAUST courtyard x[180.605,191.395] **fully contains the label's x-span**
  text centre -> own pads **4.900 mm**; -> J_RH_EXHAUST pads **1.412 mm** (3.5x inversion)
Every other connector label is nearest its own body. This is the ONE connector
where miswiring crosses the safety barrier: J_RH_EXHAUST carries 3V3_SW_RHE /
SDA_B / SCL_B / SHIELD_DRAIN, and the moat's 2.000 mm supports only ~30-63 V
(IEC 60664-1 PD3 IIIa). Silk-only fix.
[INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED by the render lens the same day, which measured the
same inversion from the other direction: 0.314 mm to J_RH_EXHAUST vs 0.373 mm
to its own courtyard. Two lenses, no shared method, same defect.]

U_LDO SOT-223 tab [3V3]: total 3V3 F.Cu within +-10 mm = 13.74 mm2, of which
the tab pad itself is 7.60 -> ~6 mm2 of spreading copper. **2 vias in the tab**

not reachable on this copper. Failure direction is SAFE (thermal shutdown ->

vias only; no thermal simulation run.

## P2
a. DIN 125 A2.7 washer rule was derived at H4 only; at H1 the disc overlaps
   KP_U2 keypad copper by 0.050 mm (only solder mask separates a metal washer
   from it). No two-net bridge even at the 6.3 mm hard limit. H2 clean.
b. Staged copper AND mask gerbers carry plotted drill marks (1047x C,0.150 +
   105x C,0.350). Every mark verified concentric with, and no larger than, its
   hole, so drilling removes it — cosmetic, but non-standard package content and
   the reason staging does not byte-reproduce with default options.
c. 14 GND pads bond to the F.Cu pour through a single 0.5 mm thermal spoke.
   Twelve have a via/track doing the real bonding; **J_LOADCELL pad 3** (nearest
   GND via 2.906 mm, no GND track) and **J_PI pad 39** (1.487 mm) do not.
d. Input protection is not at the inlet: D_ESD_IN 14.05 mm from J_PWR.1, F1
   18.11 mm. D_TVS at 40.64 mm is BY DESIGN (on 5V_PROTECTED) — refutation
   recorded, not a finding.
e. No bypass capacitor on the eFuse INPUT node (5V_RPP has no capacitor).
f. Analog victim spacings: TC_POS 0.1743 mm from SPI_SCLK; TCAM_THRESH
   0.1524 from SDA_A/B. All above the 0.12 floor; RC filters at ~160 kHz.
g. Decoupling spans: U_SCHM VCC -> C_SCHM **9.96 mm** (the Schmitt conditioning
   DOOR/ESTOP/MODE); U_EXP 9.57; best on board 1.80.
h. All 1047 vias 0.25/0.15 -> 0.050 mm annular ring. Min hole-to-copper
   **0.2005 mm** vs a configured 0.200 — 0.5 um on a hole a metal screw passes
   through. Min copper-to-edge 0.3000 mm. Both need FAB-capability confirmation.
i. Longest sensitive run with no inner plane beneath: COIL_STOP_N 7.076 mm,
   inside the deliberate keypad_iso plane-free band.
