### U_ONESHOT (CD74HC221M96) — VERDICT: QUESTION
Pinout read from: **SCHS166F p.1, "Pinout — CD54HC221 / CD74HC221 (PDIP, SOIC, SOP, TSSOP) TOP VIEW"**; topology and triggering from **p.2 Functional Diagram + Truth Table**.

Datasheet top view: 1 1Ā, 2 1B, 3 1R̄, 4 1Q̄, 5 2Q, 6 2Cx, 7 2CxRx, 8 GND ↓ left; 9 2Ā, 10 2B, 11 2R̄, 12 2Q̄, 13 1Q, 14 1Cx, 15 1CxRx, 16 Vcc ↑ right. Dossier matches pin-for-pin. **Not mirrored.**

Both sections are wired to the datasheet's own Functional Diagram topology (Cx between nCx and nCxRx; Rx from nCxRx to Vcc):

| | Rext | Cext | trigger | static input | /CLR | outputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sec 1 | R_OS **510 kΩ** → 3V3 | C_OS **1 µF** (pin14↔pin15) | 1B rising = PRESS_REQ (U_SR1 QH) | 1Ā = GND ✓ (truth table row L/↑/H) | OS_CLR_N | 1Q→ULNB IN3→K_PRESS; 1Q̄→U_LATCHG B |
| Sec 2 | R_OS2 **10 kΩ** → 3V3 | C_OS2 **1 µF** (pin6↔pin7) | 2Ā falling = REARM_N (U_EXP GPA5, idle-high via R_REARMPU 100k) | 2B = 3V3 ✓ (truth table row ↓/H/H) | WD_OK | 2Q̄→U_LATCHB A; 2Q(5) open |

Computed: **tW1 = 0.7·510k·1µF ≈ 357 ms** (press timer), **tW2 = 0.7·10k·1µF ≈ 7 ms** (re-arm pulse into the fault latch). Both Rx are far above the 500 Ω minimum (p.1). Both sections are complete — this is not a half-wired second section.

Findings:
- `U_ONESHOT pin 2 (1B / PRESS_REQ)`: expected a defined level at all times — vs the net has exactly two pads, `U_SR1 pad 7 (QH)` and this pin, **with no pull resistor**. U_SR1's /OE is SR_OE_N, held high by R_OE 10k whenever MCU_RELAY_ENABLE is low (its default, R_MCUENPD 100k) — so **at power-on the 595 outputs are Hi-Z and this HC input floats**. It is only saved when OS_CLR_N happens to be low; with the door closed (DOOR_OK high) and STOP_REQ_N high, OS_CLR_N is high and the section is armed on a floating trigger. Consequence is bounded (the coil rail is separately gated by COIL_EN), but a floating CMOS trigger on the press timer is a real defect. The '221's B input is Schmitt-triggered (p.1 features) — that mitigates slow edges, it does not define a floating node.
- `U_ONESHOT pin 5 (2Q)`: unconnected. Correct and allowed — it is an output, not an input. No finding.
- Accuracy note (not a blocker): the datasheet quotes tW = 0.7·Rx·Cx **at Vcc = 4.5 V**; this runs at 3.3 V, and C_OS/C_OS2 are 0603 class-II ceramics whose DC-bias/temperature derating cuts effective capacitance. Both effects shorten the real pulse. Immaterial for the 7 ms latch reset (the LVC1G00 needs nanoseconds); worth measuring for the 357 ms press timer.

### U_EXP (MCP23017-E/SS) — VERDICT: QUESTION
Pinout read from: **DS20001952C p.1, "Package Types" figure, MCP23017 SOIC/SPDIP/SSOP column.** 1–8 GPB0–7, 9 VDD, 10 VSS, 11 NC, 12 SCL, 13 SDA, 14 NC, 15–17 A0–A2, 18 RESET̄, 19 INTB, 20 INTA, 21–28 GPA0–7. Board: pad 1 top-left → pad 14 bottom-left → pad 15 bottom-right → pad 28 top-right. **Exact match, not mirrored.** 28 pads, no EP (SSOP has none) ✓.

Net sanity, pin by pin, is correct throughout: VDD=3V3, VSS=GND, both NC pins genuinely unconnected, SCL/SDA to J_PI pads 5/3 (Pi I2C1, whose 1.8k pull-ups live on the Pi), A0/A1/A2 all GND → address 0x20, GPB0–7 all read-only status nets, GPA0–5 all outputs with 100k defaults (RAIL_EN_×4 pull-down, CONTACTOR_REQ pull-down, REARM_N **pull-up** — correct, it is active-low), GPA6/7 board-ID straps (R_BID0 to GND, R_BID1 to 3V3).

The re-targeted reset pin: `U_EXP pin 18 (RESET̄) = WD_OK`. Electrically sound — the datasheet calls it "External Reset Input", it has no internal tie and must be driven, and the TPS3823 push-pull drive plus R_WDOKPD satisfies that. The consequence chain is coherent: watchdog timeout → expander held in reset → all GPIO revert to inputs → every RAIL_EN, CONTACTOR_REQ and REARM_N falls to its safe default via its own pull. Nothing floats during reset. That part is right.

The finding is the *readback*:
- `U_EXP pin 8 (GPB7 / WD_OK)`: expected a status input that can report both states — vs pin 8 and pin 18 (RESET̄) are on **the same net**. Whenever WD_OK is low, the device is in reset and cannot be read; whenever it can be read, GPB7 necessarily reads 1. **The watchdog readback is degenerate — it can never report "not OK."** Software has no way to distinguish "watchdog healthy" from "watchdog readback meaningless", and the expander's other seven status bits (ESTOP_OK, DOOR_OK, TEMP_OK, FAULT, …) share the same reset gate, so the whole diagnostic port goes dark exactly when a fault is most interesting. Worth a deliberate decision, not an accident.
- pin 19 (INTB / EXP_INTB) terminates on the expander alone — a dangling output. Harmless, but the net exists with one pad.

**Process finding on this part's dossier:** `U_EXP.md` was emitted with `datasheet: (none)`, `verification note: (none)` and `(not in yaml)` on **all 28 pins**. The part.yaml is fine — it has a complete, annotated pin map including `18: {name: RESET_N, …}`. The cause is in `skills/kicad-pcb/scripts/pin_audit.py`:

```python
ypath = parts / mpn / "part.yaml"
if mpn and ypath.exists() and yaml:
```

a literal MPN→directory join with a silent fall-through. `MCP23017-E/SS` cannot be a directory name (the folder is `MCP23017-E-SS`), so the lookup misses and the dossier is written with an empty function column — no warning, no non-zero exit. **16 of 56 dossiers on this board are affected**, in four different ways: `/` in the MPN (U_EXP), `*` vs `x` (J_PI, `2.54-2*20PPC104`), a `(LF)(SN)` suffix (J_LOADCELL), and an **empty MPN column** for self-supplied parts (the twelve `DIP05-1A72-12L` relays and `KF350-3.5-4P`). The one part whose reset pin this revision re-targeted is one of them. A reviewer trusting the dossier's function column would have had nothing at all to check pin 18 against.

## Summary of what I would do before ordering

Blocking (per the protocol's FAIL criterion — winding or a wrong power/gate/enable net): **none**. Nothing here stops the order.

Open QUESTIONs, in the order I would resolve them:
1. **KEY_LATCH has no default** (U_LATCHG pin 1) — the only input in the reviewed set without one, on a net that clocks the relay shift register's storage latch. One 100 kΩ pull-down makes it match the other three Pi-sourced safety inputs.
2. **PRESS_REQ floats at power-on** (U_ONESHOT pin 2) — U_SR1's outputs are Hi-Z by default via R_OE, and the one-shot is not held in reset when the door is OK.
3. **The watchdog readback is degenerate** (U_EXP pin 8 sharing a net with pin 18) — GPB7 can never read 0.
4. **J_MODE pins 1 and 3 leave the enclosure unprotected** while pins 2 and 4 do not.

Process findings:
- `pin_audit.py` silently drops the part.yaml function map and datasheet path whenever the BOM MPN is not a legal directory name or is blank — **16 of 56 dossiers on this board, including U_EXP, the part this revision touched**. The fix is a hard failure (or a `note_dirname`/sanitizing lookup) rather than a fall-through; the MCP23017 part.yaml already records `note_dirname` for exactly this reason and nothing reads it.
- **4 of the 10 MPNs in scope have no archived datasheet PDF** in `02_parts/`: SN74LVC1G00DCKR, SN74HC14DR, ULN2803ADWR, LTV-817S-TA1. Worse, two of the cited URLs no longer resolve — TI's `sn74lvc1g00`/`sn74hc14` still work, but **`ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/uln2803a.pdf` now returns 404** and Lite-On's own host rejects the request. I completed the review by fetching SLRS049E and the LTV-817 series sheet from independent mirrors; a future reviewer with a stricter network cannot.
- Two documentation defects: `SN74HC14DR/part.yaml` pin 12 named `6A_Y` where the datasheet says `6Y`; `S4B-ZR-SM4A-TF/part.yaml` cites eZH p.4 for a table that is on p.5 (p.4 gives B = 7.5 for the through-hole S4B-ZR, contradicting the correct B = 9.0 it records).
