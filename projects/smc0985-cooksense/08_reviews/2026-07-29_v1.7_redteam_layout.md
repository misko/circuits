## Method

All geometry was measured with `/usr/bin/python3` + `pcbnew` (KiCad 10.0.4) on the
staged board, **with `ZONE_FILLER` run first so every pour is filled**. Distances are
true polygon-to-polygon minima (segment-to-segment over the filled outlines with
their holes), not bounding boxes.

Resistances come from a nodal-analysis solver written for this review: every track
segment becomes `rho*L/(w*t)`, every via barrel becomes `rho*L/(pi*d*t_plate)` per
layer transition, segments are **split at every junction point that lies on them**
(so no segment resistance is shorted out by the connectivity step), and each filled
plane is collapsed to one equipotential node (stated approximation — it flatters the
plane-fed rails and is noted where it matters). Constants: `rho` 1.72e-5 ohm*mm,
outer copper 35 um, inner copper 17.5 um (JLC 4L 0.5 oz inner), barrel plating 25 um,
dielectrics 0.2104 / 1.065 / 0.2104 mm.

**Solver validated** against a hand-computable net: `5V_IN` has 8 segments whose
individual resistances sum to 21.741 mOhm in series; the solver returns
**21.549 mOhm** for `J_PWR.1 -> F1.1`, the difference being the parallel stub to
`D_ESD_IN`. Every power number below comes from that validated solver.

**DRC reproduced independently:** `kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-all --refill-zones
--schematic-parity` on a scratch copy of the staged source returns **0 real
violations / 0 unconnected / 0 schematic-parity issues** (the only 14 hits are
`lib_footprint_issues` "library 'cooksense' is not enabled", an artifact of my
sandbox's fp-lib-table, not the board). The gate claim holds. **Every finding below
is invisible to that gate**, which is the point of this review.

## Findings

### P1-PI-1 — `power_tree.yaml`'s series-resistance budget contains no PCB copper at all; the declared dropout margin is 7.6x optimistic and the file's own stress case FAILS

`power_tree.yaml` derives the LDO's input envelope from the ADR-0021 J_PWR spec by
subtracting three **cited device** resistances, and totals them explicitly:

```
F1      MF-MSMF200/16X   70.0 mOhm   (R1Max)
Q_REV   AO3401A          73.5 mOhm   (hot-interpolated to 70 C)
U_EFUSE TPS259573        47.0 mOhm   (RON MAX)
                        ----------
                        190.5 mOhm
   Drop = 0.50 x 0.1905 = 95.2 mV  ->  vin_min = 4.850 - 0.0952 = 4.754
   headroom = 4.754 - 3.399 = 1355 mV  vs  dropout 1300 -> PASS +55 mV
```

Every term is a device. **There is no copper in the budget.** Measured on the board,
the same series path carries **96.939 mOhm of copper** — 50.9% of the true total, and
101.5% of the declared budget:

| series element | R (mOhm) | source | I (A) | drop (mV) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `J_PWR.1 -> F1.1` copper | **21.549** | measured | 0.50 | 10.77 |
| F1 element | 70.0 | cited | 0.50 | 35.00 |
| `F1.2 -> Q_REV.3` copper | **5.823** | measured | 0.50 | 2.91 |
| Q_REV element | 73.5 | cited | 0.50 | 36.75 |
| `Q_REV.2 -> U_EFUSE.3` copper | **12.312** | measured | 0.50 | 6.16 |
| eFuse element | 47.0 | cited | 0.50 | 23.50 |
| `U_EFUSE.5 -> U_LDO.3` copper | **57.255** | measured | 0.30 | 17.18 |
| | | | **total** | **132.27** |

(The last row is charged at 0.30 A, not 0.50 A — only the LDO's own branch current
flows there. This is the *charitable* reading; charging it at 0.50 A gives 143.7 mV.)

Corrected numbers, using the file's own 4.850 V floor, its own 3.399 V `vout_max`
and its own 1300 mV `dropout_mv`:

| case | vin at LDO pin | headroom | vs dropout 1300 mV | file claims |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 A, copper at 20 C | 4.7177 V | 1318.7 mV | **+18.7 mV** | +55 mV |
| 0.50 A, copper at 70 C (x1.196) | 4.7105 V | 1311.5 mV | **+11.5 mV** | — |
| 0.60 A (the file's own stress case) | 4.6947 V | 1295.7 mV | **-4.3 mV FAIL** | "+37 mV" |

The declared `vin_min: 4.754` is optimistic by **36.3 mV** at the node it claims to
describe. **66% of the declared margin is consumed by copper the budget never
counted**, and the sensitivity check the file offers as reassurance ("It still passes
at 0.60 A") inverts to a fail.

**Why it matters.** This is the exact defect class ADR-0021 was written to close —
"'hold >= 4.85 V' is written at the wrong node; the answer is to DERIVE the LDO input
from the J_PWR spec with CITED series resistances rather than assert it." The
derivation was done, carefully and with real citations, and then stopped one element
short: it derived through the *parts* and not through the *board*. A 55 mV margin is
small enough that the omitted term decides the verdict.

Mitigating, and stated so the severity is not overplayed: the 1300 mV `dropout_mv` is
itself a 0.8 A figure applied at 0.3 A, which the file flags honestly as OWED and
conservative by an unquantified margin. The physical rail very probably works. What
does not work is the *claim*: the number that will be quoted as the margin is not the
board's margin. **P1, not P0, for that reason.**

### P1-PI-2 — there is no capacitor anywhere on the eFuse input side, and the layout rule that would have caught it names a net that does not exist

`02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/part.yaml` carries, as its first `keep_short` entry:

```
- {net: 5V_SELV, max_span_mm: 3, why: "Cin 0.01-0.1uF IN->GND loop minimized at the
   terminals (Fig.68); controls the short-circuit interrupt transient (Eq.18)"}
```

Measured membership of every net on the input side of the eFuse:

| net | pads | capacitors |
|---|---|---|
| `5V_IN` | `D_ESD_IN.1, J_PWR.1, F1.1` | **NONE** |
| `5V_FUSED` | `Q_REV.3, D_REVCLAMP.1, F1.2` | **NONE** |
| `5V_RPP` | `U_EFUSE.3, U_EFUSE.4, Q_REV.2, R_OVT.1` | **NONE** |
| `5V_PROTECTED` (the OUTPUT) | 12 pads | `C_IN2, CE1, C_IN1, C_LDOIN` |

Nearest capacitor of any kind to the eFuse IN pins (3/4 centroid 59.050, 57.500):
`C_DVDT` at **3.233 mm** — but that is the dV/dt cap on pin 1, not an input bypass.
The nearest bypass is `C_IN1` (10 uF) at **4.416 mm**, and it is on `5V_PROTECTED`.

Two independent defects, one measurement:

1. **The component is absent.** `C_IN1` / `C_IN2` are named like input capacitors and
   are not: both sit on the eFuse **output**. During a short-circuit interrupt the
   eFuse FET is OFF, so the output capacitance does not clamp the input node at all.
   The input node's only energy sink is `D_ESD_IN` (a SOD-323 ESD diode on `5V_IN`,
   two elements upstream) and `D_REVCLAMP` (SS34 on `5V_FUSED`, one upstream, and
   reached only back through Q_REV). SLVSE57C Sec.10 / Fig.67 asks for a local Cin
   between IN and GND precisely because harness inductance rings against the input on
   interrupt; the IN pin's abs-max is 20 V against a 2.7-18 V operating range.
2. **The gate cannot fail.** `5V_SELV` is not a net on this board — the input net is
   `5V_IN`. A span rule whose net does not exist has no members, measures nothing,
   and reports success. This is the `jlc_twin`-exited-0-on-11-unverified-parts pattern
   in `keep_short` form.

**Why it matters.** This is the input protection stage of a mains-adjacent board — the
one block whose entire job is surviving a fault. It is also the finding on this list
that **cannot be fixed after fabrication**: it is a component addition, i.e. a
schematic and netlist change.

### P1-TH-1 — the LDO's claimed theta_JA cites a copper condition that does not exist on the board, and no ambient is declared anywhere in the tree, so Tj cannot be closed

`power_tree.yaml`, on the one graded rail:

> Not derated further: the tab is VOUT and is **flooded with 3V3 copper** (that
> part.yaml's layout `notes`), which is the mounting Table 1 (p.5) characterises at
> **55-65 C/W**.

and `02_parts/AMS1117-3.3/part.yaml`:

> SOT-223 TAB = VOUT (pin 2) and is the ONLY heat path: **flood copper on the
> 3V3/VOUT net under and around the tab** to pull theta_JA toward the 45C/W floor.

**Measured at U_LDO (22.000, 70.000), pours filled.** Tab = pad 4, 2.00 x 3.80 mm =
7.600 mm^2; pads 2+4 together 10.600 mm^2. Copper inside the pad-box footprint
(x 17.850..26.150, y 68.100..71.900), by layer and net, area-sampled on a 0.05 mm grid:

| layer | what is actually there |
|---|---|
| **F.Cu** | `GND` 12.413 mm^2 (40% of box); `3V3` 9.975 mm^2 (32%); `5V_PROTECTED` 3.015 mm^2 (10%) |
| In1.Cu | `GND` 30.360 mm^2 (**97% coverage**) |
| In2.Cu | `3V3` 31.230 mm^2 (**100% coverage**) |
| B.Cu | `GND` 30.360 mm^2 (97%) |

**There is no 3V3 flood on the tab's own layer.** The 32% of the pad box that is 3V3
on F.Cu *is the two pads themselves*. Widening the window to +/-12 mm around the LDO,
total F.Cu 3V3 copper is **13.702 mm^2**, of which 10.600 mm^2 is pad — leaving
**~3.1 mm^2** of top-layer 3V3 track in total. The top layer at the LDO belongs to the
GND pour, which closes to **0.2005 mm** from the tab (witness 24.650,68.100 ->
24.650,67.900 — exactly the 0.2 mm zone clearance). There was no room for the flood
the dossier describes, and none was made.

The `3V3` pour is on **In2.Cu** — the third copper layer — and the tab reaches it
through **2 vias, both 0.15 mm drill** (at 25.150,70.000 and 25.150,68.900; a third,
at 18.850,70.000, is in pad 2).

Substitute heat path, computed from that measured geometry:

| path | computation | R |
|---|---|---|
| 2 x 0.15 mm via, F.Cu -> In2.Cu, L = 0.2104+0.035+1.065 = 1.3104 mm, A = pi*0.15*0.025 = 0.011781 mm^2, k = 400 W/mK | 1.3104e-3 / (400 * 1.1781e-8) = 278.1 K/W each | **139.0 K/W** |
| tab -> In1.Cu GND plane through prepreg, t = 0.2104 mm, A = 10.600 mm^2, k_FR4 perp ~ 0.3 W/mK | 0.2104e-3 / (0.3 * 10.6e-6) | **66.2 K/W** |
| the two in parallel (tab -> plane) | | **44.8 K/W** |

So theta_JA >= theta_JC(SOT-223, ~15 K/W) + 44.8 + (plane -> ambient). Even granting
an optimistic 15 K/W for a 8476 mm^2 inner plane in still air, theta_JA lands near
**75 K/W**, above the cited 55-65 K/W band. *The plane-to-ambient term is my estimate,
not a measurement — that is exactly why the claim needs a bench number and not a
citation.*

**And the calculation cannot be closed at all, because no ambient is declared.** I
grepped `BRIEF.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `DETAIL_DESIGN.md` and every
`03_src/cooksense/rules/*.yaml` for an ambient / Ta / operating-temperature
specification: **there is none.** The board's own parts imply a ceiling — `DIP05-1A72`
`t_op: -20..+70C`, and `power_tree.yaml` itself interpolates Q_REV "to a 70 C
junction". At PD = 615 mW (the file's own figure):

| Ta | theta_JA 65 | theta_JA 75 | theta_JA 85 | vs AMS Tj(max) 125 C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 C | 65 C | 71 C | 77 C | comfortable |
| 60 C | 100 C | 106 C | 112 C | ok, but the theta_JA claim is load-bearing |
| **70 C** (the DIP05's own ceiling) | 110 C | 116 C | **122 C** | **3-15 C margin** |

**Why it matters.** A cooking-appliance sidecar is the one application where "assume
25 C" is not available, and the dossier's PASS ("615 mW vs 1200 mW -> 51%") grades
*package dissipation*, which is not the binding constraint — junction temperature is,
and it needs theta_JA and Ta, one of which is mis-cited and the other of which does not
exist in the tree.

Refuted while here, and worth recording: PD itself is **conservative**. At the high
corner (J_PWR 5.25 V) the measured series drop puts the LDO input at 5.118 V, so
PD = (5.118 - 3.201) * 0.3 = **575 mW**, not 615 mW. The 615 mW bound stands.

### P1-PI-3 — the LDO's frequency-compensation capacitor is 9.200 mm away and reaches the tab only through three 0.15 mm vias and a plane

`02_parts/AMS1117-3.3/part.yaml` budget: `{net: 3V3, max_span_mm: 5, why: "COUT
(>=22uF-class) hard against VOUT (pin2 / tab) - it IS the LDO's
frequency-compensation cap; loop stability depends on it (ds1117 Stability)"}`.

Measured `C_LDOOUT` pad 1 -> `U_LDO` pad 2: **9.200 mm** — 184% of a 5.0 mm budget.

The span understates it, because of what the 3V3 rail turns out to be. Measured over
the whole board, net `3V3` has **5 track segments totalling 4.18 mm** and **76 vias**.
There is no top-layer 3V3 distribution at all; every consumer drops straight into the
In2.Cu plane through a single via-in-pad. `C_LDOOUT`'s 3V3 pad
(x 27.550..28.550, y 69.275..70.725) contains exactly **1 via, 0.15 mm**.

So the compensation loop measured end to end is:

```
U_LDO tab (pad 4) --2 x 0.15mm via--> In2.Cu 3V3 plane --9.2 mm--> 1 x 0.15mm via --> C_LDOOUT
```

Three 0.15 mm barrel transitions and a plane traverse inside the loop the datasheet
says stability depends on. DC resistance is a non-issue (**3.724 mOhm** LDO-tab-to-plane,
1.1 mV at 0.3 A) — the loop *inductance* is the concern, ~1 nH per 1.31 mm barrel plus
the traverse. Compounding it: `C_LDOOUT` is a **22 uF 0805 ceramic**, i.e. very low
ESR, where ds1117's Stability hint asks for 22 uF *solid tantalum* whose ESR is part of
the compensation. Low ESR plus added series inductance is the standard ringing recipe
for this LDO family.

### P1-MECH-1 — the declared maximum conductive fastener OD lands on keypad copper at H1 and H2; the limit was derived at H4 only

ADR-0015 fixes the fastener spec from the H4 creepage geodesic: "**Maximum conductive
OD 6.0 mm**, hard limit 6.3 mm — see ORDER_README §1 REQUIRED FASTENER SPEC clause (2)."

Measured, per mounting hole, nearest copper to the hole centre on F.Cu (drill dia
2.700 mm, so the disc radius under test is 3.000 mm):

| hole | at | nearest F.Cu copper | net | domain | 6.000 mm washer (r 3.000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | 24.000, 14.000 | **2.950 mm** | `KP_U2` | KEYPAD | **OVERLAPS by 0.050 mm** |
| **H2** | 194.000, 14.000 | **2.950 mm** | `KP_U6` | KEYPAD | **OVERLAPS by 0.050 mm** |
| H3 | 17.000, 90.000 | 1.550 mm | `GND` pour | SELV | overlaps 1.450 mm (own-net, benign) |
| H4 | 193.000, 52.000 | 1.550 mm | `GND` pour | SELV | overlaps 1.450 mm (own-net, benign) |

**Maximum safe conductive washer OD at H1 and H2 = 2 x 2.950 = 5.900 mm**, against a
declared 6.000 mm. Both holes return the identical 2.950 mm, so this is a systematic
router band, not an accident of one net.

At H1 a compliant 6.000 mm conductive washer sits on top of the `KP_U2` track; at H2,
on `KP_U6`. The only thing between them is solder mask (~15-25 um), which is not an
insulation barrier for a safety-relevant isolated net. `KP_U2` and `KP_U6` are two
different **U-select rows of the emulated key matrix**: bridging them through
interconnected conductive standoffs makes any key in row 2 also register in row 6 —
a phantom key press on a keypad emulator whose entire safety argument is "the Pi can
at most press *valid* keys".

Second-order mitigation exists (ADR-0012, non-conductive enclosure is load-bearing),
and that is precisely why this is P1 and not P0. But ADR-0015 explicitly *permits* a
conductive washer up to 6.000 mm, and at 6.000 mm it lands on keypad copper. The two
documents contradict each other by 0.100 mm, measured.

**Why it matters as a process point:** ADR-0015 is a careful, correct, three-times-raced
derivation — of H4. The OD limit it produced was then written as a *board-wide*
fastener spec without being re-derived at the other three holes. H4 is not the binding
hole for this constraint; H1 and H2 are.

### P1-MECH-2 — the west end-pocket slot leaves a 1.000 mm web to the board edge, contradicting the same file's stated reason for skipping the east one

Reconstructed from `Edge_Cuts` (56 line segments -> 1 outer loop of 8 points, which
confirms the H4 notch is merged into the outline as ADR-0012/0015 claim, plus 12
internal cutouts):

All twelve comb slots measure **exactly 1.000 mm wide** — the P0-SLOT widening landed
correctly and meets JLC's published 1.0 mm non-plated-slot floor. Webs to the outer
outline:

| slot | span | nearest outer edge | web |
|---|---|---|---|
| **west end pocket** | x 13.000..22.600, y 49.100..50.100 | **W edge** | **1.000 mm** |
| coil gap r11r12 | x 181.800..190.240, y 25.800..26.800 | E edge | 9.760 mm |
| all others | — | — | 15.800 - 39.100 mm |

Slot-to-slot: no pair closer than 6 mm.

`floorplan.yaml` justifies skipping the **east** end pocket because it is "<3mm to the
board edge, web too fragile". The **west** end pocket it kept leaves **1.000 mm** — a
1.000 mm FR-4 rib, 9.600 mm long, between a routed slot and the board edge. The file's
own stated rule and its own geometry disagree, by measurement.

The file records this and declines to fix it, on the grounds that JLC publishes no
minimum remaining-wall figure. That reasoning is sound for *choosing not to guess a
number*; it is not sound for shipping. A 1.000 mm x 9.600 mm rib is where this panel
breaks in depaneling or handling, and if it breaks it breaks **the west end pocket,
which is where `J_KEY_MATRIX` lives** — the isolated keypad ribbon entry. This wants a
DFM answer from the fabricator before the order, not after.

### P1-PI-4 — `EF_OVLO`, the node that sets the over-voltage trip, is 8.473 mm from pin 2 against a 5.0 mm datasheet budget

`02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/part.yaml`: `{net: EN_OVLO_N, max_span_mm: 5, why: "OVLO
divider (IN/pin2/GND) local to pin 2, short GND return, no switching coupling — this
divider SETS the OV trip point"}`.

Measured members and worst pad-pair span:

```
U_EFUSE.2 (59.0, 56.8)   R_OVB.1 (67.5, 56.0)   R_OVT.2 (66.5, 58.0)
worst span U_EFUSE.2 -> R_OVB.1 = 8.473 mm   (budget 5.0, over by 3.473)
```

The divider is 100k / 26.1k, i.e. a ~20.7 kOhm source impedance feeding a comparator
with a 5 us typ fast-overvoltage response, on an 8.5 mm run. This is the node ADR-0021
rebuilt as the v1.7 P0 (9.200 V -> 5.798 V nominal trip, worst case bounded to
5.3682 / 6.2394 V). The *setpoint* was fixed with great care; the *layout* of the node
that realises it is 70% over its datasheet budget. A nuisance trip here removes
`5V_PROTECTED` and with it every rail on the board.

## Findings — P2

### P2-PI-1 — no gate in the stack measures `keep_short`; 7 of 11 measured budgets are violated in the shipping board

The `02_parts/*/part.yaml` `layout:` blocks are the richest datasheet-derived layout
constraints in this tree, and **nothing checks them**. The `.kicad_dru` contains 8
`track_width` rules, 2 clearance rules and 1 scoped width exception — and no span
constraint of any kind (KiCad DRU has no such primitive). Measured against the
budgets the part dossiers themselves declare:

| net | budget | measured worst span | verdict | physical consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `3V3` (COUT->tab) | 5.0 | **9.200** | over 4.200 | **real** — see P1-PI-3 |
| `EF_OVLO` | 5.0 | **8.473** | over 3.473 | **real** — see P1-PI-4 |
| `HS_GATE_COIL` | 6.0 | **10.169** | over 4.169 | refuted below |
| `EF_ILM` | 5.0 | **6.982** | over 1.982 | refuted below |
| `SWG_RHE` | 6.0 | **7.429** | over 1.429 | negligible |
| `SWG_B` | 6.0 | **6.445** | over 0.445 | negligible |
| `SWG_RHA` | 6.0 | **6.445** | over 0.445 | negligible |
| `3V3` (C_3V3->pin2) | 5.0 | 5.894 | over 0.894 | negligible |
| `EF_DVDT` | 5.0 | 4.524 | OK | — |
| `SWG_A` | 6.0 | 4.128 | OK | — |
| `5V_PROTECTED` (CIN->VIN) | 8.0 | 3.008 | OK | — |

Two of the eight misses have consequences (promoted to P1 above). The structural
finding is the other six plus the pattern: a body of carefully sourced,
datasheet-cited layout rules that no gate has ever been able to fail. Per this
project's own canon ("Test the checkers, not just with them" / M1), an unmeasured
constraint is not a constraint.

### P2-MECH-1 — every netclass declares 0.60/0.30 vias; all 1052 physical vias are 0.25/0.15, and the DRC floors were set to the values used

Measured: **1052 vias, 100% of them 0.25 mm pad / 0.15 mm drill** — confirmed not only
in the board but in the shipping drill file (`fab/cooksense-PTH.drl` tool table:
`T1C0.150` tagged `ViaDrill`, the only via tool). Annular ring = (0.25-0.15)/2 =
**0.050 mm**.

Meanwhile all eight netclasses in `cooksense.kicad_pro` declare
`via_diameter: 0.6, via_drill: 0.3` — including `PWR_IN`, the whole-board 2 A input
class. The board and its own netclass declaration disagree on every via.

This is deliberate (`route.yaml`: "KEEP advanced via-in-pad size (0.25/0.15) ... KRT
already emits 0.25/0.15", under `fab_tier: jlc_4layer_advanced`), and it is
`normalize_vias` applied board-wide rather than only at via-in-pad sites. Two
consequences worth numbers:

- **The DRC cannot object.** `design_settings.rules` carries
  `min_via_annular_width: 0.05`, `min_via_diameter: 0.25`,
  `min_through_hole_diameter: 0.15` — each set to exactly the value in use. The
  constraint was fitted to the artifact, so a 0.050 mm ring reports clean.
- **Barrel cross-section halves.** At 25 um plating, a 0.15 mm barrel is
  **0.01178 mm^2** against **0.02356 mm^2** for 0.30 mm — equivalent to a 0.337 mm vs
  0.673 mm trace in 35 um copper. Via counts on the power nets are nonetheless
  adequate: `5V_RPP` 4, `5V_PROTECTED` 8, `5V_KEY_RELAY` 35, `3V3` 76, and `5V_IN` /
  `5V_FUSED` use **0** vias (single-layer runs). No power via carries more than
  ~0.13 A. **Ampacity is not a finding** — the resistance already appears in P1-PI-1's
  measured copper, and the thermal consequence appears in P1-TH-1.

Recorded as P2 rather than higher because the tier decision is documented and the
electrical effect is bounded and measured. What I could **not** verify inside my
curated inputs is whether the tier's capability quote sanctions a 0.25 mm via
*diameter* (as distinct from a 0.15 mm *hole*) — `fab_tiers.yaml` lives outside the
input set. Flagging it as a DFM cross-check, not asserting a violation.

### P2-TH-1 — the eFuse EP is specified "flooded with thermal vias" and has one

`02_parts/TPS259573DSGR/part.yaml`: "EP(9)=GND flooded with thermal vias."

Measured: EP pad box x 59.550..60.450, y 56.200..57.800 = 0.90 x 1.60 mm = 1.440 mm^2,
containing **1 via** (60.000, 56.600, 0.15 mm drill). Copper under the EP: In1.Cu `GND`
1.355 mm^2 (97% coverage), B.Cu `GND` 1.175 mm^2 (84%), F.Cu `GND` 0.570 mm^2 (41%).

**Thermally non-binding, and I want to be explicit that this is a refutation of my own
first guess.** PD = I^2 * Ron = 0.50^2 * 0.047 = **11.75 mW** at RON MAX. At any
plausible WSON-8 theta_JA that is under 2 C of rise. The 97%-coverage In1.Cu plane
0.2104 mm below the EP is a better heat path than a via array would add. Recorded only
because the cited rule says "vias" plural and the board has one.

### P2-EMI-1 — relay coil freewheel loops up to 72.93 mm span / ~156 mm perimeter, with no local flyback on 11 of 12 relays

`02_parts/TBD62083AFWG/part.yaml`: "keep COM and each OUTn->coil freewheel loop area
SMALL so the internal clamp diode sees low loop inductance. p.7 names the COMMON line
explicitly as one of the three whose design can destroy the part."

Measured, driver COM pad to relay coil+ pad:

| driver | COM at | worst relay | span | loop perimeter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `U_ULNA` | 99.65, 64.08 | **K_U1** | **72.93 mm** | ~156.0 mm |
| `U_ULNA` | | K_U2 | 69.59 mm | ~149.3 mm |
| `U_ULNB` | 188.65, 64.08 | K_D4 | 42.77 mm | ~95.7 mm |

The coil tracks cross y=53, and the pours only exist for y >= 53, so in the relay row
there is **no image plane under the loop** — the loop is genuinely open.

**Refuted as a hazard, by calculation.** Loop inductance at ~1 nH/mm is ~156 nH for the
worst loop. Coil current is ~7-10 mA into a clamp; even at a 10 ns diode turn-on,
V = L*di/dt = 156e-9 * (0.010/10e-9) = **0.16 V**. Coil stored energy is
0.5*L*I^2 ~ 2.5 uJ. `K_STOP` additionally has a discrete `D_KSTOP` (SMA) flyback; the
other eleven rely on the driver's internal clamp at the far end of the loop, and that
is sufficient at this current. Recorded as a measured deviation from a cited rule with
quantified non-consequence.

## Refuted hypotheses — measurements that contradicted my own first guess

These are results. Each one is a defect I went looking for and did not find.

**1. Isolated 30 V contactor loop — the boundary holds on all four layers with pours
filled.** I expected the SE-corner pours to eat the moat. Measured minimum copper-to-
copper from {`CONTACTOR_C`, `CONTACTOR_E`, `CONTACTOR_LOOP`} to all non-isolated copper:

| layer | min | witness | binding neighbour |
|---|---|---|---|
| F.Cu | **2.0005 mm** | (194.200,101.350)-(192.200,101.350) | `zone:GND` |
| In1.Cu | **2.0005 mm** | same | `zone:GND` |
| In2.Cu | **2.0005 mm** | same | `zone:3V3` |
| B.Cu | **2.0005 mm** | same | `zone:GND` |

Against `opto_isolation_2mm` (min 2.0 mm): **PASS by 0.0005 mm on all four layers** —
tight, but the `iso_moat_block` / `iso_moat_opto` keepouts are doing exactly the job
ADR-0013 says they were added for ("the moat has to be pour GEOMETRY, not just a
rule"). Next tightest non-pour neighbour: `U_OPTO.3` to `J_RH_EXHAUST.5`
(`SHIELD_DRAIN`) at **2.1709 mm**.

**Which quantity is this?** It is a **clearance** (straight line through air). It is
*also* the **creepage** here, and that needs saying: no slot, groove or notch crosses
this boundary anywhere, so the surface path and the straight line coincide. At a 30 V
working voltage this satisfies both requirements. Both figures reported, per ADR-0015
Decision 2.

**2. The opto's own PCB barrier is generous.** `U_OPTO` (SMDIP-4_W9.53mm): pad 2 (`GND`,
primary) east edge x = 181.235; pad 4 (`CONTACTOR_C`, secondary) west edge x = 188.765.
**Pad-to-pad = 7.530 mm**, and the `opto_barrier` keepout (181.400..188.600) spans the
gap on all four layers, denying pours inside it. No finding.

**3. The keypad comb survives filled pours — 6.2344 mm against a 6.000 mm rule.** My
strongest prior was that filling the pours would collapse the 6 mm keypad isolation
somewhere across 12 relays and 199 nets. Measured, `KEYPAD_ISO` to all non-keypad
copper, per layer:

| layer | min | witness pair |
|---|---|---|
| **F.Cu** | **6.2344 mm** | `K_D1.4` <-> `trk:COIL_D1_N` (114.366,45.766)-(120.600,45.766) |
| In1.Cu | 6.3508 mm | `K_D1.4` <-> `K_D1.1` |
| In2.Cu | 6.3508 mm | same |
| B.Cu | 6.3508 mm | same |

**PASS by +0.234 mm at the worst point, on the worst layer, with every pour filled.**
And the tightest paths (the intra-relay column gaps at y ~ 45.7) are pure surface with
no slot crossing them — so **6.2344 mm is simultaneously the clearance and the
creepage** there, and the milled slots only lengthen the paths that do cross them. The
comb works.

**4. H4's creepage geodesic reproduces independently.** ADR-0015 reports the H4
clearance as 4.0286 mm by a construction I did not read the details of before
measuring. I measured centre-to-copper distance from H4 (193.000, 52.000) to the
nearest KEYPAD copper (`K_STOP.3`) as **7.029 mm**, so disc-boundary-to-keypad =
7.029 - 3.000 = **4.029 mm**. Agrees with ADR-0015's 4.0286 mm to 0.0004 mm by a
different method. The ADR is confirmed, not contradicted.

**5. Ground return integrity is excellent — the plane is one piece.** I expected the
1052 vias and dense south-band routing to have shredded the return path. Measured
island counts on the filled pours:

| pour | layer | islands | largest |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GND` | **In1.Cu** | **2** | **8476.6 mm^2** (+ one 0.3 mm^2 orphan) |
| `GND` | B.Cu | 24 | 7069.2 mm^2 |
| `3V3` | In2.Cu | **1** | 8430.6 mm^2 |
| `GND` | F.Cu | 113 | 1053.1 mm^2 |

F.Cu GND is heavily chopped (113 pieces, largest 1053 mm^2), but **In1.Cu carries a
single continuous 8476.6 mm^2 ground plane** and In2.Cu a single 8430.6 mm^2 3V3
plane. The return path is intact. No finding.

**6. No starved thermal reliefs on the THT ground pads.** `min_resolved_spokes: 1`
worried me on a 40-pin header. Measured pad-to-pour gap on `J_PI` pads 6/9/14/20/25/30/
34/39, `J_LOADCELL.3`, `J_PWR.2`, `CE1.2`: **In1.Cu gap 0.000 mm on every one** (i.e.
directly merged into the plane, not relieved), B.Cu 0.000 on all but `CE1.2`. My
earlier connectivity model split these into separate components; that was a **model
artifact**, corrected. No finding.

**7. eFuse and coil-driver thermals are non-events.** `U_EFUSE` PD = 0.50^2 * 0.047 =
**11.75 mW**. `U_ULNA`/`U_ULNB` (TBD62083AFWG) PD = 8 * 7 mA * 45.5 mV = **2.5 mW**
against a 1.31 W board rating. Neither part's theta_JA-vs-copper table is binding. The
ADR-0023 driver change removed the only thermal case on this board that ever was.

**8. Reed pull-in has 1.15 V of margin — the long coil rail does not threaten it.**
Worst case is the westmost relay. Measured resistances: shared input path 230.184 mOhm
at 0.50 A = 115.09 mV; `U_EFUSE.5 -> Q_COIL.2` **170.761 mOhm** at 0.15 A = 25.61 mV;
Q_COIL element 73.5 mOhm at 0.15 A = 11.03 mV; `Q_COIL.3 -> K_U1.1` **312.082 mOhm** at
0.010 A = 3.12 mV. Total 154.85 mV, so **V at `K_U1.1` = 4.695 V**; less the TBD62083's
45.5 mV on-state drop the coil sees **4.649 V** against a DIP05 `pull-in <= 3.5 V`.
**Margin 1.149 V.** The 312 mOhm coil-rail trunk looks alarming and is irrelevant at
10 mA.

**9. `HS_GATE_COIL` at 10.169 mm is not a crosstalk hazard, despite being the most
safety-critical net on the board.** This was my best candidate for a P0 and it does not
survive arithmetic. The node is a 100 kOhm pull-up into an AO3401A gate (Ciss ~500 pF),
so its own time constant is 100k * 500p = **50 us** — it is a low-pass filter, not an
antenna. Flipping it needs V_GS(th) min 0.5 V, i.e. Q = C*V = 500 pF * 0.5 V =
**250 pC**. A 10 mm run couples ~1 pF to an adjacent aggressor, so a 3.3 V logic edge
injects ~**3.3 pC** — nearly two orders short. Over budget, not a hazard. Demoted to
P2-PI-1.

**10. `EF_ILM` at 6.982 mm: the datasheet's own stated reason does not apply.** The
part.yaml says "trace resistance adds directly to current-limit error". Measured trace
resistance on that node is single-digit mOhm against R_ILM = 1200 Ohm — an error of
**0.001%**. The span is over budget and the cited mechanism is negligible. (Noise
coupling would be the real concern on ILM; the dossier does not claim it.)

**11. The 0.25 mm segment on the 2 A input path is legitimately scoped, not a slip.**
Measured: `5V_RPP` has 1 segment at 0.250 mm (0.50 mm long) against a `PWR_IN_width`
floor of 0.5 mm; `5V_PROTECTED` has 1 at 0.450 mm (0.07 mm long). The DRU carries
`scoped_efuse_padentry`, which relaxes the floor to 0.25 mm for exactly these two nets
inside the `efuse_padentry` keepout. At 0.50 A a 0.25 x 0.035 mm trace is well inside
IPC-2221 ampacity. Deliberate, documented, bounded. No finding.

**12. Slot widths and copper-to-edge are clean.** All 12 comb slots measure **exactly
1.000 mm** (the P0-SLOT widening is correctly applied and meets JLC's stated 1.0 mm
non-plated-slot floor). Minimum copper-to-board-edge over all four layers with pours
filled is **0.3000 mm** (`J_DOOR.MP` at 199.700,77.160 and `J_ESTOP.MP` at
199.700,66.280), with the pours at 0.3005 mm — exactly at the 0.300 rule, nothing
under it. `J_ISOLOOP.1` sits 0.650 mm from the south edge as ADR-0013 states.

**13. No switching node exists to have a hot loop.** Item 4 of my brief is largely N/A
by construction: `power_tree.yaml`'s `source_type: external_5v_selv` and the all-linear
topology are borne out by the board — one LDO, six load switches, no converter. There
is no SW node, no ripple loop and no layer-adjacency budget to violate.

## Assessment

The isolation work on this board is genuinely good and I could not break it: the
keypad comb holds **6.2344 mm** on its worst layer with every pour filled, the
30 V moat holds **2.0005 mm** on all four, the opto barrier is **7.530 mm** pad-to-pad,
H4's geodesic reproduces to 0.0004 mm by an independent construction, and the ground
plane is one continuous 8476.6 mm^2 island. DRC, unconnected and schematic parity all
reproduce at zero. The things this project has been measuring, it has measured right.

What it has not measured is the **board's contribution to its own electrical budgets**.
Three of the four P1s are the same defect wearing different clothes: a rail envelope
derived through the *parts* and not through the *copper* (P1-PI-1), a theta_JA cited
from a datasheet mounting condition that the layout could not and did not provide
(P1-TH-1), and a compensation loop whose datasheet span budget nothing in the gate
stack can measure (P1-PI-3, P2-PI-1). None of these is caught by DRC and none ever
could be. The fourth (P1-MECH-1) is a correct derivation at H4 generalised into a
board-wide fastener spec that two other holes contradict by 0.050 mm of copper.

**The order-blocking item is P1-PI-2.** Every other finding on this list is either a
document fix (P1-PI-1's arithmetic, P1-MECH-1's OD clause, P2-MECH-3's wire-entry
note), a fabricator question (P1-MECH-2's web), or a bounded risk that can be
characterised at bring-up (P1-TH-1, once an ambient is declared; P1-PI-3, on a scope).
The absent eFuse input capacitor is none of those: it is a component that the
controlling datasheet requires on the input-protection stage of a mains-adjacent
board, it cannot be added to a fabricated panel, and the layout rule written to
protect it has been silently passing on a net name that does not exist. Ordering now
means ordering a board whose protection stage is known to deviate from its part's own
Sec.10 requirement, to save one respin of a board that already needs a fastener-spec
correction and an ambient declaration before it can be thermally qualified at all.

Also owed before any thermal claim on this board can be graded: **declare the
operating ambient.** It is absent from `BRIEF.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`,
`DETAIL_DESIGN.md` and every `rules/*.yaml`, and no junction temperature on a cooking
appliance sidecar can be closed without it.

VERDICT: DO-NOT-ORDER
