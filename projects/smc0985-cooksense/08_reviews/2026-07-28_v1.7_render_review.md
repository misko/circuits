## P0-A (BLOCKING) — the two safety interlocks are inter-mateable and their labels do not attach
J_ESTOP and J_DOOR are both C189896 / SM05B-GHS-TB, same edge, same
orientation, 10.9 mm apart, **fully inter-mateable with each other AND with
J_RH_AMBIENT / J_RH_EXHAUST (same part number)**.

| silk text | h | -> J_ESTOP | -> J_DOOR | -> its own part |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J_DOOR | 0.45 | 0.133 | 0.064 | — |
| J_ESTOP | 0.45 | 0.258 | 0.835 | — |
| D_DOOR | 0.60 | 0.353 | 2.002 | **6.411** |
| D_ESTOP | 0.60 | 0.281 | 9.175 | 0.611 |

**The "J_DOOR" label discriminates its own connector from the E-STOP connector
by 0.069 mm.** And the LARGEST "DOOR" text on that edge is D_DOOR at h=0.60 —
33% taller than the real J_DOOR label — printed 0.353 mm from the E-STOP
connector and 6.411 mm from the diode it names. The single-character D_/J_
prefix is the only discriminator, at the smallest type size on the board.
Door harness into J_ESTOP puts DOOR_RAW onto J_ESTOP pin 4 = GND: a 3V3-to-GND
short through the door switch, with the E-STOP left unmonitored.
Fixable ONLY by a silk respin — there is no post-fab remedy.

## P1
- **P1-A 142 of 229 refdes (62%) are nearer another part than their own**, no
  leader lines anywhere (board F.SilkS holds only 12 PCB_TEXT, none a leader).
  107 nearer another part by >1 mm, 57 by >3 mm, 36 by >5 mm. Worst offset
  12.73 mm. R_SER6's label is 0.864 mm from J_THERM_A and 11.707 mm from
  R_SER6. Not an SMT risk (JLC uses the CPL) but the printed board is actively
  misleading for rework and bring-up on a safety interlock.
- **P1-B J_ISOLOOP — the NOT-SELV connector — is labelled nearer a SELV
  connector (0.314 mm) than itself (0.373 mm)**, and has NO pole legend: four
  silk lines, no pin-1 tick, no C/E marking, and the KF350 body covers all four
  pads once soldered. A 180 deg harness reversal maps CONTACTOR_C <-> _E, which
  go directly to U_OPTO.4/.3 with no series or reverse-blocking element —
  30 V backwards across an LTV-817S output (V_ECO ~6 V). Destructive, and a
  shorted opto is not fail-safe. Hand-wired part: silk is its only instruction.
- **P1-C J_THERM_A/B are pin-for-pin identical, 22 mm apart, unkeyed** — a swap
  is undetectable and transposes both temperature channels. Labelling is good
  here; the entire defence is one trailing character at 0.60 mm.
- **P1-D 34 refdes plot below the project's own silk stroke floor, and the floor
  is unsatisfiable at that height.** Measured in the SHIPPED gerber: D11 =
  0.1125 mm (27 selections), D12 = 0.1200 (14), D10 = 0.1500 (42). KiCad clamps
  stroke to <= 0.25 x height, so every h=0.45 refdes plots at 0.1125 — 25%
  under the 0.15 floor, structurally. Affected: J_DOOR, J_ESTOP, J_MODE,
  J_ISOLOOP. Separately 723 footprint silk outline segments plot at 0.120 —
  and those segments ARE the polarity marks.
