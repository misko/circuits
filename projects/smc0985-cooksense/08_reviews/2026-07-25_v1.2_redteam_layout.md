# THE TWO P0s (verbatim finding text, then the lead's independent re-measurement)

## P0-1 — Mounting hardware bridges the keypad↔SELV isolation; H4 breaches 6 mm on geometry alone

Reviewer's evidence: H4 is an NPTH Ø2.7 mm at (193.000, 52.000). From the hole
wall on EVERY layer: **0.200 mm** to GND (F.Cu/In1/B.Cu) and to 3V3 (In2); the
same wall is **5.679 mm** from `RSTOP_MID` (K_STOP pad 3). Keypad → screw → SELV
= 5.679 + 0.200 = **5.879 mm < 6.000 mm** before any hardware is fitted. With an
M2.5 ISO14580 head (Ø4.5): head edge **4.779 mm** from RSTOP_MID and **0.000 mm**
over the GND pour; with a Ø5.5 washer, 4.279 / 0.000. H1 (24,14) and H2 (194,14)
sit inside the keypad domain; H3 (17,90) is 0.200 mm from GND/3V3 on all four
layers. DRC cannot see this — an NPTH is a hole, not a conductor — and no silk
or copper feature prevents it.

**LEAD RE-MEASUREMENT (independent script, hole-wall to nearest keypad copper and
to nearest SELV pour, all four layers):**

    H1 @(24.0,14.0)  drill 2.70 : keypad  3.961mm (J_KEY_MATRIX.1 KP_U1) | SELV 37.650mm
    H2 @(194.0,14.0) drill 2.70 : keypad  4.612mm (R_STOP.2 KP_D1)       | SELV 37.651mm
    H3 @(17.0,90.0)  drill 2.70 : keypad 42.582mm                        | SELV  0.200mm
    H4 @(193.0,52.0) drill 2.70 : keypad  5.679mm (K_STOP.3 RSTOP_MID)   | SELV  0.200mm
        -> keypad->screw->SELV total 5.879mm ; M2.5 head edge 4.779mm from keypad copper

**CONFIRMED.** The decisive number reproduces exactly: H4 is **5.879 mm**, below
the board's own 6.000 mm requirement, with no hardware fitted. My H1/H2 figures
are larger than the reviewer's (it measured to keypad TRACKS, I measured to keypad
PADS) but that does not soften the finding: H1/H2 are keypad-side and 37 mm from
any SELV copper, H3/H4 are SELV-side at 0.200 mm, so any chassis plate or metal
standoff set spanning them is a galvanic keypad-to-SELV short.

## P0-2 — The opto-isolated contactor barrier is nullified by the pour, a via, tracks and the connector pinout

Reviewer's evidence: on F.Cu, `CONTACTOR_C` (192.60,86.60)–(194.00,85.20) is
**0.175 mm** from a `DOOR_RAW` via at (194.40,85.20); `CONTACTOR_LOOP` is
0.175 mm from `DOOR_RAW` and 0.199 mm from `ESTOP_RAW`. GND fill (F.Cu, In1,
B.Cu), 3V3 fill (In2), a GND via at (188.00,90.00) and an `SDA_B` track all pass
BENEATH the U_OPTO body. `J_ESTOP` (1.25 mm pitch) carries both domains in one
5-way connector: pad2 `ESTOP_RAW` adjacent to pad3 `CONTACTOR_C`, gap 0.650 mm.
`02_parts/LTV-817S-TA1/part.yaml` states the rule verbatim: *"Keep INPUT copper
(1/2) and OUTPUT copper (3/4) on opposite package edges with NO ground pour or
trace crossing beneath the body… preserve that full creepage/clearance across
the barrier."*

**LEAD RE-MEASUREMENT (exact-shape binary search, every CONTACTOR_C/E/LOOP item
vs every other net, layer-aware):**

    MIN isolated-to-SELV copper gap = 0.175 mm
       CONTACTOR_C (track)  <->  DOOR_RAW (via)

    Foreign copper beneath the U_OPTO body:
       ZONE FILL GND on F.Cu / In1.Cu / B.Cu ; ZONE FILL 3V3 on In2.Cu
       VIA GND at (188.00,90.00)  + 4 more GND vias
       tracks SDA_B, SCL_B, SHIELD_DRAIN, 3V3_SW_RHE

**CONFIRMED, exactly.** 0.175 mm where the part's own dossier demands the full
body creepage, with all four planes filled straight under the package. A 5 kVrms
barrier reduced to 0.175 mm is not a barrier.

# The reviewer's remaining findings (P1/P2), recorded
