Load brownout, from `02_parts/DIP05-1A72-12L/part.yaml` (DS p.2): **pull-in ≤ 3.500 V**, coil 500 Ω ±10 % (10.0–11.1 mA). ### 1b. `3V3` (AMS1117-3.3) — **PASSES on IR by >4×; the binding term is dropout, already declared failing**

Setpoint min, cited: **3.201 V**. Binding brownout: **3.000 V** — TPS3823-33 V_IT−(max) = 3.00 V and MAX31856 V_DD(min) = 3.0 V. ⇒ **Total IR budget = 201 mV at 0.30 A = 670 mΩ.** 3V3 is a plane net on a 0.3 mm floor; even a pessimistic 100 mΩ is 15 % of budget. **Fits.** The real constraint is already recorded honestly: headroom 1101 mV vs 1300 mV → **short 199 mV, declared FAIL, dropout at 0.3 A declared M-OWED.** I concur with that treatment; it must not be quietly closed by an invented number.

## MANDATORY CHECK 2 — E-OFF

`power_tree.yaml` declares `source_type: external_5v_selv` and no `off_control`. **Verified against the netlist and the claim holds structurally:** no battery, cell, pack, supercap or coin cell anywhere in 235 components.

Two corrections and one gap:
- **The stored-energy figure is wrong by three decades.** C on `5V_PROTECTED` = 240.1 µF ⇒ **E = 3.00 mJ at 5 V**, not "µJ".
- **The one element capable of being a master disconnect is hard-wired.** `U_EFUSE.2` (EN/OVLO_N) sits on a fixed divider — no GPIO, no jumper, no switch. **De-energization is unplugging J_PWR.**
- Discharge: `Q_REV`'s body diode and the eFuse both block reverse conduction, so the 3.0 mJ bleeds only through LDO quiescent draw.

**E-OFF verdict: the declaration matches the netlist. Corrections are P2 (RT-19).**

## P0 — BLOCKS THE ORDER

### RT-01 (P0) — one I²C register write forces `WD_OK` HIGH and permanently defeats the watchdog, including ADR-0020's headline claim

`WD_OK :: R_WDOKPD.1, TP_WDOK.1, U_AND1.3(B), U_CAND1.1(A), U_EXP.8(GPB7), U_EXP.18(RESET_N), U_FAULTAND.1(A), U_OENAND.2(B), U_ONESHOT.11(R2_N), U_WD.1(RESET_N)`.

`U_EXP.8` is **GPB7, a bidirectional MCP23017 I/O**, on the same node as the TPS3823's supervisor output *and* the expander's own `RESET_N`. Ratings: MCP23017 **25 mA sink/source per I/O**, V_OH = V_DD − 0.7 = **2.60 V while sourcing 3.0 mA**; TPS3823 RESET **V_OL ≤ 0.4 V only at I_OL = 1.2 mA**, output capped at ±5 mA.

Set `IODIRB.7 = 0, OLATB.7 = 1` and the expander drives `WD_OK` against a pull-down specified to 1.2 mA. **Neither datasheet bounds the resulting node voltage**, and the thresholds to recover are MCP23017 `RESET_N` V_IL = **0.66 V** and SN74LVC1G11 V_IL(max) = **0.800 V**. If the node does not fall below 0.66 V the expander never enters reset, never releases GPB7, and the contention is **self-sustaining**.

Consequences, all from the netlist:
1. `U_AND1.3` = 1 → the watchdog term in the coil-rail chain is gone.
2. `U_CAND1.1` = 1 → the watchdog term in the contactor chain is gone.
3. `U_FAULTAND.1` = 1 → a watchdog timeout no longer sets the fault latch.
4. `U_OENAND.2` = 1 → the '595 outputs stay enabled through a supervisor reset.
5. `U_EXP.18` never resets → **ADR-0020 Decision B's central claim, "the expander's outputs cannot persist across a watchdog timeout or a brown-out", is false.**
6. The TPS3823's brown-out assertion is overridden down to the MCP23017's 1.8 V floor.

GPB7-on-WD_OK predates v1.7; **v1.7 is what turned it from "defeats the AND-chain term" into "defeats the AND-chain term *and* the reset that was added to fix that class of defeat."**

**Fix (one 0402, existing BOM line):** ≥2.2 kΩ in series between `WD_OK` and `U_EXP.8` **only**. Use **10 kΩ (C60490, already on the BOM)**: contention current 0.28 mA, node ≈0.1 V. `U_EXP.18` and the five gate inputs stay on the raw net.

### RT-02 (P0) — the eFuse over-voltage cutoff is set at **9.20 V**, above the TVS breakdown and above the relay coil's absolute maximum; three documents say 5.5–6 V

`EF_OVLO :: R_OVB.1, R_OVT.2, U_EFUSE.2`; BOM: **R_OVT = 100 kΩ, R_OVB = 15 kΩ.** SLVSE57C §7.5: `V_OVLO(R)` = **1.13 / 1.20 / 1.27 V**; the '-73 die turns the FET **OFF** above it.

⇒ V_IN(cutoff) = **9.200 V nominal**, **8.492 … 9.933 V** worst case including ±1 % resistors.

| what it should protect | limit | margin |
|---|---|---|
| `D_TVS` SMBJ5.0A on `5V_PROTECTED` | V_BR = **6.40–7.07 V** @1 mA | cutoff is **1.4–3.5 V above breakdown** — on any sustained OV the 600 W/10-1000 µs TVS becomes the DC regulator and burns |
| 12× DIP05-1A72-12L coil | **7.5 V max** | exceeded from 7.5 V up to the cutoff |
| whatever hangs on `J_LOADCELL.1` (HX711-class) | 6 V abs max | destroyed |
| AMS1117-3.3 V_IN | 15 V | survives |

Every archived document contradicts the netlist: the part.yaml gotcha, `power_tree.yaml:130`, `ARCHITECTURE.md:41`, `BRIEF.md:77`.

**And E-INV cannot see it.** `electrical_invariants.yaml:54-58` asserts only `net_has_part EF_OVLO resistor min: 2`. There are exactly **7 `part_value` asserts** in the whole file and **none pins R_OVT or R_OVB**. This is verbatim the hole ADR-0018 wrote down for R_COILENPD, left open on the board's over-voltage cutoff.

**Fix.** The window is tight: a 5 V +5 % supply tops out at 5.250 V and the SMBJ5.0A breaks down from 6.400 V. **R_OVT = 57.6 kΩ (E96) with R_OVB = 15 kΩ** ⇒ nominal 5.80 V, corner window **5.383 … 6.245 V**. One new BOM line. Then add `part_value` asserts on both refs.

*(Coordination that does check out: `R_ILM` = 1.2 kΩ ⇒ I_LIMIT ≈ **1.7 A**, below F1's I_hold 2.0 A — correct ordering. `C_DVDT` = 1 nF ≤ the 3300 pF ceiling.)*

## P1

**RT-03 — `ESTOP_RAW`, `DOOR_RAW` and `MODE_RAW` have no series element, and a machine-read invariant says they do.** `electrical_invariants.yaml:612` states *"J_DOOR and J_ESTOP have one"* — **the parenthetical is false**; the netlist shows connector pin, TVS, pull-down and 74HC14 input all on one node, and `MODE_RAW` has no ESD device either. PESD5V0S1BA clamps at **9.8 V @1 A** against an SN74HC14 input abs max of **3.8 V**. **Fix:** 680 Ω (the same new BOM line ADR-0018 just created) between the connector node and `U_SCHM.1/.11/.5`, keeping TVS and pull-down at the pin. **Do NOT apply it to `J_MODE.3`** — that pin carries DC into `R_COILENPD` and 680 Ω would give 1.543 V, below V_GS(th) max, bricking the coil rail. Use ≤47 Ω there.

**RT-04 — the 100 kΩ restrictive defaults do not win against the MCP23017's 100 kΩ GPPU.** With the driver absent **and** GPPU set: `3.3 × 100k/200k = **1.650 V**` against SN74LVC1G11 V_IL(max) 0.800 / V_IH(min) 2.000 — neither. The attack is blunted from "deterministic permissive" to "deterministic indeterminate", which is not what ADR-0019 claims. **Fix:** 10 kΩ (C60490, already a BOM line) on the four expander-readback permissions ⇒ 0.300 V, guaranteed LOW.

**RT-05 — `TEMP_OK`'s undriven default is PERMISSIVE and it is not in ADR-0019's eleven.** All four comparator outputs are open-drain wire-ORed onto one 10 kΩ pull-up — a pull toward permissive. If both comparators are absent, `TEMP_OK` reads OK forever into `U_AND2.1`, `U_CAND1.6` and `U_FAULTAND.6`, and `U_EXP.5` samples the same net so software has no cross-check.

**RT-06 — over-temperature trip is 72.8 °C, not the published 74.9 °C.** `DETAIL_DESIGN.md` §1 omits `R_CLMPA`/`R_CLMPB` 22 kΩ from the sensing chain. As documented: 74.89 °C (reproduces the doc exactly). As built (R_NTC ∥ 22 k): **72.81 °C**. Δ = −2.08 °C. **Hysteresis is over-stated ~8×**: the claimed "≈33 mV ≈ 2 °C" uses R_REF alone; at the trip point the node impedance is **1.281 kΩ** ⇒ ΔV = **3.98 mV** ⇒ **0.35 °C** — smaller than the LMV393's own V_IO (±7 mV @25 °C, ±9 mV over temp). Total worst-case band **69.5 … 76.1 °C** against BRIEF's 70/75 °C. **Both edges breached.**

**RT-07 — `R_PG` pulls a 3.3 V expander input up to 5 V.** `R_PG.2` is on `5V_PROTECTED`; MCP23017 abs max on any I/O = **3.6 V**. `U_EXP.1`'s upper ESD clamp conducts continuously: **11 µA**. Permanent absolute-maximum violation on a safety-status input. **Fix:** move `R_PG.2` to `3V3`.

**RT-08 — E-MARGIN: the declared input envelope is 217 mV short of relay pull-in (§1a).** Structural cause: 3V3 is in `rails:` (graded) and `5V_KEY_RELAY` — the only rail whose load has a hard brownout threshold — is in `linear_rails:`, the bucket the checker ignores.

**RT-09 — LDO dissipation: `power_tree.yaml`'s own numbers, summed consistently, exceed the cited maximum.** `iout_max_A: 0.3` is scoped to logic only, but the same file declares four switched rails at 0.1 A each and `3V3_ANALOG` at 0.05 A, and the netlist confirms all five are fed **from 3V3**. Summed: **0.75 A ⇒ PD = 1.724 W = 144 %** of 1.2 W. The realistic load is fine; the defect is that the graded envelope excludes its own children.

**RT-10 — the 600 W TVS is on the wrong side of everything it should protect.** Order: `J_PWR → D_ESD_IN → F1 → D_REVCLAMP + Q_REV → U_EFUSE(IN) → U_EFUSE(OUT) → D_TVS`. The input side carries `Q_REV`'s **±12 V V_GS** limit and the eFuse's 20 V, behind only a SOD-323 ESD clamp. **Move `D_TVS` (or add a second) to `5V_FUSED`.**

**RT-11 — the reverse-polarity crowbar is not guaranteed to trip its own fuse, and `Q_REV` already made it unnecessary.** `Q_REV` alone blocks reverse polarity. The crowbar converts a benign no-op into a high-current event; F1 is I_hold 2.0 A / I_trip 4.0 A and BRIEF specifies a **≥2 A (pref 3 A)** supply, so at 3 A the polyfuse is **below I_trip** while the SS34 dissipates **1.5 W**. At exactly 2 A: 0.9 W indefinitely, no trip, no indication. **Either delete `D_REVCLAMP`, or size F1 so the crowbar current is ≥2× I_hold.**

**RT-12 — the isolated contactor output has no clamp, no snubber and no series element.** `CONTACTOR_C/E` carry nothing but `J_ISOLOOP` and `U_OPTO`. No clamp across C–E on a bare phototransistor rated 35 V. Drive margin: I_F ≈ **6.1 mA**, at the TA1 bin's **CTR minimum 50 %** ⇒ I_C available **3.0 mA**, halving with LED ageing. **Nothing in the release states the loop's required current.**

**RT-13 — the watchdog window is 0.9–2.5 s against a brief requirement of 300–500 ms.** The TPS3823's own dossier says *"Reconcile the watchdog architecture in pin/power review"* and points at an SN74LVC1G123 one-shot. **The BOM contains no SN74LVC1G123**, there is no reconciling ADR, and **ADR-0020 consumed the CD74HC221's second section**, closing the cheapest path to implementing it.

**RT-14 — a hard short of `COIL_EN_IN` to 3V3 still arms the coil rail, and 3V3 is three pins away on the same hand-crimped harness.** ADR-0018 §D is arithmetically correct and I verified it (+534 mV margin; rejection bound R ≥ 1564 Ω holds). It states the residual plainly. **What it does not say is that 3V3 is on pin 1 of the same connector** — a single mis-crimp or chafe from pin 1 to pin 4 arms the rail with all seven AND-chain terms and the Manual rail cut bypassed, and the mechanical key does nothing because it is a same-connector fault. **Fix, one 0402:** 2.2 kΩ in series with the 3V3 feed to `J_MODE.1` ⇒ a pin1→pin4 short gives **0.779 V**, below V_GS(th) MIN — literally row 3 of ADR-0018's own rejection table. Mode sense still works at 2.705 V vs V_T+(max) 2.31 V.

**RT-15 — the four remaining SM05B-GHS-TB housings still cross-mate, and the two that matter falsify each other.** Door harness → `J_ESTOP` shorts 3V3→ESTOP_RAW **and** 3V3→GND on pin 4. E-stop harness → `J_DOOR`: door reads closed. **The two safety interlocks can falsify each other with a plug swap.** ADR-0018's closure applied only to the ☠ class on `J_MODE`.

**RT-16 — brief requirements absent on the four Pi-native sensor-bus lines.** No 22–33 Ω damping (BRIEF:99), no low-C ESD (BRIEF:100), pull-ups hard-fitted at 2.2 kΩ where the brief wants selectable/default-OFF, no test points. And `ARCHITECTURE.md:49` claims *"Ioff buffers"* — **there are no buffers of any kind in the netlist**; back-power is 4 × (3.3−0.7)/2.2 k = **4.7 mA** into the Pi's clamps.

**RT-17 — `TH_ENCLOSURE` is a commissioned sensing channel with no sensor and no connector pin.** `TH_ENCLOSURE :: R_REF6.2, R_SER6.1, TP_ENCL.1` — ADC CH6 reads full-scale permanently. Also **none of the eight thermistor channels has an ESD device** where BRIEF:105 requires one.

**RT-18 — the supervised door loop the brief specifies is not implemented.** BRIEF:92 wants an EOL resistor. As built there is no supervision: a cut reads "door open" (safe) but a short from the adjacent 3V3 pin reads "door closed" and is undetectable.

## What I checked and found CORRECT

All six AO3401A orientations including the deliberate `Q_REV` difference; diode polarity throughout including the five symmetric PESD5V0S1BA; **decoder polarity** ('238 active-HIGH — with a '138 the same condition would have turned every relay ON); fault-latch power-on state (forced SET for the TPS3823 delay, `R_FAULTPU` up and `R_FLCPD` down mutually consistent); **the forbidden latch state is not an unsafe state** — I traced both chains and no permissive window exists; eFuse/polyfuse coordination (1.7 A < 2.0 A); `5V_STOP` ungated so K_STOP survives; `R_WDPETPD` = 1 kΩ with its `part_value` assert — **the pattern RT-02 needs**; thermistor channel map matches BRIEF one for one; CPL correctly excludes the 16 hand-solder refs; and ADR-0018's COIL_EN arithmetic recomputed independently.
