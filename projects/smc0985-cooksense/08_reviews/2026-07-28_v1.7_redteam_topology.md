## P0-1 (BLOCKING) — one I2C write defeats FOUR safety terms; only WD_OK was fixed

v1.7's P0-B added `R_WDOKSER` to `U_EXP.8` (GPB7 / WD_OK). **GPB1-GPB5 sit
DIRECTLY on their nets**, and ADR-0020 computed the mechanism, then applied the
remedy to exactly one of six port-B readback pins:

| pad | bit | net | opposing driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | GPB1 | MODE_AUTO_HW | U_SCHM.8 (HC14 push-pull) |
| 3 | GPB2 | ESTOP_OK | U_SCHM.4 (HC14 push-pull) |
| 4 | GPB3 | DOOR_OK | U_SCHM.12 (HC14 push-pull) |
| 5 | GPB4 | TEMP_OK | LMV393 OPEN-DRAIN + R_TEMPOK 10k pull-UP |
| 6 | GPB5 | FAULT | U_LATCHA.4 |
| 8 | GPB7 | WD_OK | **isolated by R_WDOKSER (v1.7)** |

`IODIRB=0x00, OLATB=0xFF` is ONE I2C transaction. MCP23017 V_OH >= V_DD-0.7 at
-3.0 mA (DS20001952C D090) => weakest source 233 ohm, realistic ~50 ohm; HC14
sink <= 82.5 ohm. Contention: **0.863 V weakest / 2.055 V realistic** against
SN74LVC1G11 V_IL max 0.8 V, V_IH min 2.0 V. **No corner of the two datasheets
makes this node a guaranteed LOW.** TEMP_OK is worse: open-drain wired-AND
behind a 10k pull-UP gives 2.48 V.

Consequence traced to copper: TEMP_OK feeds U_AND2.1 (coil rail), U_CAND1.6
(contactor) and U_FAULTAND.6 (fault-latch SET) — forcing it high defeats the
over-temperature interlock in ALL THREE at once, and **TEMP_OK is the one
safety term with no independent physical backup on this board.** Nothing
self-clears while a healthy Pi pets the watchdog.

This falsifies ARCHITECTURE.md's "All enforcement is hardware" for 4 of 7
AND-chain terms. REMEDY: 10k (C60490, existing BOM line) in series into
U_EXP.2/.3/.4/.5/.6, consumers staying on the raw nets — the R_WDOKSER pattern,
plus the matching pin_on_net asserts.

## P1
- **P1-1 the power tree does not balance.** 3V3 declares iout_max 0.3 A, then
  declares children summing to 0.45 A, and omits U_ADC/U_TC/U_COMP/U_COMP2 and
  the 3V3 field pins. At the file's OWN numbers both E-TOPO criteria invert:
  PD 1.537 W vs 1200 mW = 128% FAIL; headroom 1275 mV vs 1300 = short 24.5 mV.
- **P1-2 vin_min omits every ohm of PCB copper.** Measured by Dijkstra over the
  segment graph: J_PWR->LDO forward path = **89.9 mOhm** (5V_PROTECTED is
  track-only; the only zones are GND and 3V3). At 0.50 A that is 45.0 mV = 82%
  of the declared 55 mV margin; at 70 C, 53.8 mV leaves 1.2 mV. The +55 mV
  dropout PASS that ADR-0021 was made to obtain does not survive the board's
  own copper. The rail probably works (dropout_mv 1300 is a 0.8 A figure at a
  0.3 A load) but the DECLARED MARGIN IS NOT A MARGIN, and the owed bench
  measurement is the only thing that settles it.
- **P1-3 TEMP_OK's default is PERMISSIVE and ADR-0019 does not name it.** All
  eleven ADR-0019 directions re-derived and all eleven are CORRECT, including
  the two counter-intuitive ones (FAULT_SET_N down => latch SET; FAULT up =>
  FLC 0). But TEMP_OK is a twelfth permission input, an open-drain wired-AND
  that structurally cannot carry a restrictive pull-down: one dead comparator
  silently vanishes from the AND and it reads OK.
- **P1-4 the opto's published 50 mA rating is ~18x the LED drive.** IF =
  5.53-5.92 mA, CTR >= 50% => Ic guaranteed >= 2.8 mA. Fail-safe direction, but
  nets.yaml and the silk publish "<=30 V / <=50 mA".
