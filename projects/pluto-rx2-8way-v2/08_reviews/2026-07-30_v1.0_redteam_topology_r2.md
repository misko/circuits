### Method note — a self-correction, recorded

My first netlist extraction used a regex requiring `(pintype …)` to follow
`(pin …)` directly. That silently dropped every node carrying a
`(pinfunction …)` field and produced a netlist that appeared catastrophically
disconnected (GND with 7 nodes, `SEL_V1..V4` dangling, `ANT8` absent). **That
reading was an artifact of my tool, not a property of the board.** Re-parsed
with a real s-expression parser the netlist is complete and coherent: GND
carries 60 nodes, every select line reaches the module, and `RX1_MAIN` carries
`J_ANT8.1 + J_RX1.1 + R_T1.1` as designed. Recorded because a red-team report
that had shipped the first reading would have been a fabricated P0.

## What I verified as CORRECT (stated so the findings are not read as the whole picture)

These were checked against the netlist and the part limits, not against the prose.

- **Protection chain — traced from the netlist, and it is deliberately empty.**
  The BOM has no TVS, no PPTC, no reverse-polarity block, no ESD array; the only
  series element is `FB_3V3`. This is not an omission: `U_MCU.23` (5V / VBUS) is
  **unconnected**, so the board has no power entry to defend. `ADR-0002` Decision 3
  re-derives each v1 element and disposes of it against the new boundary.
  **Reverse polarity is structurally unreachable** — the sole power input is
  `U_MCU.21`, an LDO output on a soldered module. No TVS exists, so TVS
  directionality is N-A rather than wrong.
- **Every clamp-vs-protected-part rating pair.** There are no clamps. The rating
  pairs that do exist all hold: `PE42482A-X` `v_digital_abs_max` 3.6 V vs the
  worst-case driven control level; `vdd_abs_max` 5.5 V vs a 3.234–3.366 V rail;
  `vdd_op` min 2.3 V vs the same rail.
- **Control levels, recomputed at corners.** DC driven level
  `3.3 × 10000/10047 = 3.2846 V`; at the rail's `vout_max` 3.366 V it is
  **3.3502 V**, still 250 mV under the 3.6 V digital abs-max. Undriven level
  `5 µA × 10 k = 50 mV`, far below `v_il` 0.6 V. Against RP2040 `VOH_min` 2.62 V
  the switch pin still sees 2.608 V vs `v_ih` 1.17 V. All pass.
- **The 47 Ω series termination arithmetic reproduces exactly.**
  `2×3.3×67/(67+25+0) = 4.807 V` unterminated (exceeds 3.6 V abs-max by 1.2 V);
  `2×3.3×67/(67+25+47) = 3.181 V` with 47 Ω. Matches DETAIL_DESIGN §3 to the
  digit. `Z_drv ≈ 25 Ω` is honestly flagged there as back-solved from v1, not
  re-sourced.
- **Power-on default is safe.** `R_PD1..R_PD4` (10 k) sit on the **switch** side
  of `R_S1..R_S4` (netlist: `SW_V1 = {R_PD1.1, R_S1.2, U_SW.9}`), so V1..V4 are
  held at 0 through reset and supply ramp. Per Table 5 that is `RF1` selected, a
  real antenna — **not** the `V4=1` all-ports-terminated state that would
  silently mute the receiver. This is the correct position and the correct
  default.
- **`LS` (pin 1) and `NC` (pin 20) are both on GND** in the netlist, satisfying
  the 1 MΩ-internal-pull-up trap (a float would silently select the
  complemented truth table) and closing the fence at pin 20.
- **RF port assignment matches the invariants and the isolation argument.**
  `RF1(24)=ANT1`, `RF2(2)=ANT2` … `RF7(17)=ANT7`, `RF8(19)=RX1_TAP`,
  `RFC(22)=RX2_OUT`. The tap sits on RF8, one of the two lowest-IL ports
  (1.9 dB max at 4–6 GHz vs 2.2–2.3 dB elsewhere) — the correct port for the
  arm that is already 20 dB down.
- **The RX1 pickoff is arithmetically correct.** I recomputed the 3-port from
  the topology alone: `Z_node = 50 ‖ 490 = 45.3704 Ω`; `S11 = −0.048543` →
  **RL 26.277 dB**; `|S21|² = 4×0.475746² = 0.9053` → **IL 0.4319 dB**;
  `|S31|² = 4×(0.475746×50/490)²` → **coupling −20.256 dB**. DETAIL_DESIGN §2
  publishes 26.2773 / 0.4322 / −20.2567 dB. Independent agreement.
- **2-pad polarized parts.** `LED_ST` is the only one. Netlist has
  `LED_ST.1 (K_1)` on GND and `LED_ST.2 (A_2)` on the driven `LED_STAT_A`.
  Verified against the footprint's **own** marker rather than the dossier's
  claim: `LED_0603_1608Metric.kicad_mod` places pad 1 at x = −0.7875 and the
  F.SilkS cathode bar at x = −1.485 — same side. **Polarity is correct.** All
  other 2-pad parts (4 caps incl. the ferrite, 11 resistors) are non-polarized.
- **DRC / ERC / parity / stock.** `drc.json`: violations 0, unconnected_items 0,
  schematic_parity 0 at severity-all. `erc_errors.rpt`: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
  `stock_check.txt`: 11/11 coded lines ≥ 5× qty (tightest `C5121458` at 1294 for
  qty 1). `bom_source_check.txt`: PASS, every BOM LCSC == source.
- **E-INV graded the shipped artifact.** `06_build/netlists/…net` and
  `07_releases/…/source/…net` are md5-identical (`8350f0e4…`), so the 20/20
  E-INV pass is valid for this release. Same for the `.kicad_pcb`
  (`50116c4f…`).

### E-MARGIN (mandatory, by construction)

Single regulated rail, `3V3`, feeding one known load (`U_SW` VDD).

| term | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Vout setpoint (min) | 3.234 V | `power_tree.yaml` (RT9013-33 −2 %) |
| V brownout | 2.300 V | `PE42482A-X` `limits.vdd_op` min, Table 2 PDF p3 |
| headroom | **0.934 V** | difference |
| Imax **actually crossing our copper** | 200 µA | `PE42482A-X` `limits.idd` max |
| ⇒ total IR budget at real Imax | **4 670 Ω** | 0.934 / 200 µA |
| Imax charged by the gate (whole rail) | 0.100 A | `power_tree.yaml iout_max_A` |
| ⇒ total IR budget at gate Imax | **9.34 Ω** | 0.934 / 0.1 |
| **real series resistance** | **~90 mΩ** | FB_3V3 DCR 60 mΩ (Murata max) + ~25 mΩ of ~20 mm 0.4 mm 1 oz trace + ~5 mΩ joints |
| declared | 100 mΩ | `power_tree.yaml ir_budget_mohm` |

**Judgement: fits with enormous margin — 104× even on the gate's deliberately
over-stated 0.1 A, and ~52 000× on the 200 µA that actually flows.** Real drop
is 18 µV at 200 µA, 9 mV at 0.1 A. **There is no cable and no connector in this
path** — the module is soldered and the board has no power connector — so the
two terms that dominate a USB-powered board's IR budget are genuinely absent,
not merely omitted. The upstream leg also clears: USB device-end min 4.75 V vs
RT9013 needing 3.3 V + dropout ≈ 3.4 V at the module's own ~100 mA, leaving
~1.35 V. `ir_budget_mohm` is declared as the **actual** resistance rather than a
ceiling, which is the correct construction.

### E-OFF (mandatory, by construction)

`power_tree.yaml` declares `source_type: usb_bus_powered_5v`,
`off_control: unplug`, `quiescent_ua: 0`.

**The declaration is honest and it genuinely matches the netlist.** I looked for
the failure mode where a declared `off_control` names hardware that does not
exist: there is none to find, because `unplug` names no hardware. Corroborating
netlist facts — there is **no** EN pin, load switch, MOSFET, or power connector
anywhere in the 28-component BOM; `U_MCU.23` (5V/VBUS) is **unconnected**, so
the board has no power path other than the module's own USB-C; and the only
energy storage is 5.8 µF of ceramic. De-energization for storage is unplugging
the module's USB-C, which is real and is the only path. **E-OFF is correctly
N-A, and correctly *stated* rather than inferred from silence.** No finding.

One declared debit that is not a defect: the module's WS2812B is hard-wired to
3V3 with no power-enable (`RP2040-Zero/part.yaml` gotcha 5), so it free-runs
whenever the board is powered. Recorded as a debit in ARCHITECTURE §10 rather
than argued away.

## Assessment

The **circuit** is in good shape. I traced every protection chain from the
netlist rather than the prose and found a deliberately empty one that is correct
for the boundary this board actually has; every rating pair I could form holds
with margin; the control plane's default state is safe in the direction that
matters (a floating V4 would mute the receiver, and the pull-downs are on the
correct side of the series resistors to prevent it); the pickoff's three
published RF numbers reproduce from the topology alone; the one polarized part
is right against the footprint's own marker; and E-MARGIN and E-OFF both pass
honestly, the latter because the board genuinely has nothing to gate.

The defects that block are not in the schematic. Two are release-integrity: the
package omits the order paperwork that three of its own documents repeatedly
reference, and its headline audit was generated before the artifacts it claims
to grade — which masks the A-POP failure that the package's *other* verification
file reports plainly. Ordering as staged risks a board arriving with all ten RF
ports unsoldered, which is the exact outcome `assembly.yaml` was written to
prevent.

The one substantive electrical finding is finding 3: the sole supply-noise
mitigation peaks by ~+24 dB at ~100 kHz with no damping, and the dossier text
that waves this away was written for v1's topology, where the bead sat upstream
of an LDO whose PSRR did the work. On v2 the bead is downstream and nothing
covers that band. It does not stop the board working; it degrades the quantity
this board exists to measure, and it should be either damped or accepted on the
record with v2's own arithmetic.

Sourcing is not a constraint: 11/11 BOM lines carry stock at ≥ 5× the build,
and the module is a broadly available retail part.
