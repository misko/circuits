### 1.2 The parallel-plate mode and the bound (DERIVED)

`lambda_pp = lambda_0/sqrt(er) = 23.820074 mm`; `lambda_pp/20 = 1.191004 mm`.
The board publishes 23.8201 / 1.1910. **The parallel-plate bound is genuinely the tightest
of the four candidates** — I ranked them myself at 6 GHz:

| candidate | mine | board |
|---|---|---|
| microstrip `lambda_g/20` | 1.3753 mm | 1.3693 mm |
| CBCPW guided `lambda_g/20` | 1.4063 mm | 1.4063 mm |
| **parallel-plate `lambda_pp/20`** | **1.1910 mm** | **1.1910 mm** |
| free space `lambda_0/20` | 2.4983 mm | 2.4983 mm |

ADR-0004's central honest claim — *correcting the line type makes the requirement HARDER,
not easier* — is **TRUE**, and true across the whole declared Dk window (er 4.2 → 1.2190,
4.4 → 1.1910, 4.6 → 1.1648, reproduced exactly).

Supporting physics also reproduced: TM₁ parallel-plate cutoff **339.6 GHz**; conductor-backed
leakage phase-match angle **32.126°** (ADR-0005 says 32.13°); the half-wave resonance of a
cell at `lambda_pp/20` sits at **60.000 GHz = 10.00×** the band edge, which is the "10×
resonance margin" ADR-0005's whole argument turns on.

*Honest non-reproduction, immaterial:* plain Hammerstad–Jensen gives me microstrip
`eps_eff = 3.2999`, not ADR-0003's **3.3286**. ADR-0003 is superseded and the comparison is
only used to establish the *direction* of the correction, which holds either way
(−4.4 % vs −5.19 %). Flagged rather than smoothed over.

### F2 — P1, not blocking — `fence_pitch.py` grades interior gaps only, so both arm ends are ungraded

The gate drops any fence element whose perpendicular foot clamps beyond an outer end of the
arm polyline (`outer`), then measures gaps only *between* the survivors. It **prints**
`lead-in` and `run-out` and **never compares them to the bound**. Worst printed-but-ungraded
edge run on this board: **3.8000 mm at ANT4 side W**.

Two conventions produce that number, and I separated them:

1. **The ±2.5 mm band excludes the SMA ground posts on axis-aligned arms by 0.04 mm.** The
   posts sit at exactly **2.5400 mm** perpendicular. This is the same script whose own comment
   adds PTH posts to the element list because *"counting only PCB_VIA reports an aperture at
   every launch that is in fact the densest ground in the board"* — and then the band throws
   two of the four away again. Widening the band to 2.60 mm collapses ANT4 W 3.8000 → 2.8600,
   ANT6 E 1.9000 → 0.8800, RX2_OUT E 1.9000 → 0.8800, ANT3 E 2.2274 → 1.4394.
2. **Part of each end aperture sits over a launch antipad**, where In1.Cu is voided and no
   parallel-plate region exists for the missing short to short.

Correcting for both — band 2.60 mm, arm ends included, and restricted to arclength where
In1.Cu is actually present under the flank (sampled at 1.2 mm lateral offset, 0.02 mm steps):

| arm/side | In1-referenced aperture | vs `lambda_pp/20` | at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ANT7 W** | **2.1800 mm** | **1.83×** | `U_SW.17`, the RF-fan launch |
| ANT5 E | 1.9200 mm | 1.61× | `U_SW.13` |
| ANT4 W | 1.8600 mm | 1.56× | `J_ANT4.1` |
| ANT3 E | 1.4200 mm | 1.19× | `J_ANT3.1` |
| RX1_TAP W | 1.2200 mm | 1.02× | `R_T2.2` |

**5 of 22 arm-sides.** ADR-0005's isolated-aperture relaxation (`lambda_pp/12 = 1.9850 mm`)
would not rescue the worst two anyway: its **C4** grants *"no relaxation whatsoever inside a
launch region"* and requires separation from any discontinuous feature, and its **§3.3** says
that at four or more defects you revert to `lambda_pp/20` for all of them. ADR-0005 is
correctly **not claimed** by this release, so nothing is being spent — but the wall bound as
ADR-0004 *states* it ("along-arm ground-stitch spacing ≤ λ_pp/20", no interior qualifier) is
not met at these five ends, while the gate that certifies it reports 0 OVER.

**Priced with ADR-0005's own Bethe scaling** (`g(L) = L³/[24(ln(4L/h) − 1)]`, budget
`g = 0.127455` = the compliant wall of one arm): ANT7's arm parallel-plate coupling budget
goes to **+4.06 dB** against the **+3.0 dB** the ADR permits — an excess of **1.06 dB** on a
term the ADR itself measures as *below every practical measurement floor* (< 0.01 dB IL,
< 0.22° phase, arm-to-arm contribution ≲ −80 dB against a switch specified at 41 dB
isolation). **Real, bounded, and not orderable-blocking.**

### F3 — P2, information — the convention-free counterweight

Because F2's number depends on two conventions, I also measured something that depends on
none: the **2-D distance from every 0.05 mm sample on every RF arm to the nearest ground
short** — no band, no side, no interior/exterior split.

- Worst **anywhere In1.Cu exists: 1.3038 mm**, on `RX1_TAP` at s = 22.228 (49.500, 28.900) —
  **1.09× the wall bound, 0.66× `lambda_pp/12`.**
- Every value above that (up to 2.2142 mm at ANT3 s = 0.00) falls **inside a launch antipad**,
  where there is no lower plate.
- Per-arm medians run 0.64–1.23 mm; p90 0.88–1.75 mm.

So the residual exposure behind F2 is small and bounded: the board's ground shorts are never
more than 1.30 mm from RF copper anywhere the parasitic mode can exist.

### F4 — P2 — "|Z_node| ≥ 440 Ω → RL ≥ 25.5 dB regardless" does not hold in band

`01_docs/journal/04_placement.md` (22:20) records the fix's justification as:

> *"With the whole 440 ohm lumped at the junction, Z_node = 440 + Z_x with Re(Z_x) >= 0 for
> ANY passive termination, so |Z_node| >= 440 ohm -> RL >= 25.5 dB REGARDLESS of the tap
> arm's length and of the PE42482's off-state port impedance."*

MEASURED, over 0.07–6 GHz × **all** passive switch terminations (|Γ| = 1, 3° phase steps),
with the declared resistor parasitic `0.04 pF` from `02_parts/0402WGF2200TCE/part.yaml`:

| | claimed | measured |
|---|---|---|
| min \|Z_node\| | ≥ 440 Ω | **289.46 Ω** |
| min return loss | ≥ 25.5 dB | **22.42 dB** |
| max insertion loss | (implied ≤ 0.48 dB) | **0.608 dB** |

**Cause, and it is structural not numerical:** the two 220 Ω are **not lumped with each
other**. `RX1_TAP_MID` puts **1.2000 mm of 51.25 Ω line between them** — 15.4° at 6 GHz — so
only the *first* 220 Ω is at the node. A lossless line preserves |Γ| while rotating phase, so
the far-side impedance can rotate to a much lower real part before the second 220 Ω is added.
Only `Re(Z_node) ≥ 220 Ω` is guaranteed by the topology, not 440 Ω. This is unavoidable:
1.18 mm is the pad-to-pad pitch of two adjacent 0402s.

Robust across the parasitic corner: min |Z_node| = 271.01 Ω (ideal R), 284.99 (0.02 pF),
289.50 (0.04 pF), 285.05 (0.06 pF), 256.53 (0.10 pF).

**This does not change the verdict on the fix** — 22.42 dB worst-case return loss and 0.608 dB
worst-case insertion loss on a resistive tap are good numbers, and §1.8 shows the arm-length
independence is genuinely delivered. The *design* is right and the *number written next to it*
is optimistic by 1.52× in impedance and 3.08 dB in return loss. Worth correcting before it is
quoted onward, which is exactly how `eps_eff = 3.350` entered this fleet.

### F5 — P2 — DETAIL_DESIGN §2's tap table is a DC calculation published without a band qualifier

The table gives `return loss at RX1_MAIN 26.2773 dB` and `main-line insertion loss
0.4322 dB`. My model reproduces both **exactly at 0.07 GHz**, confirming the arithmetic. With
the real copper and a matched tap termination the same quantities are frequency-dependent:

| f (GHz) | \|Z_b\| Ω | IL dB | RL dB |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.07 | 489.94 | 0.4322 | 26.277 |
| 2.00 | 445.40 | 0.4547 | 25.849 |
| 4.00 | 373.89 | 0.5103 | 24.804 |
| **6.00** | **322.07** | **0.5702** | **23.233** |

On a board whose headline product is a phase table in degrees per millimetre at 6 GHz, a tap
table quoted only at DC is a caveat that should be printed, not inferred. (§1.8's note that
the table's own regeneration command is printed alongside it is the mitigating fact.)

### F6 — P3 — a declared "critical geometry" is met by one of the two parts it names, and the gate that grades it takes a minimum

`01_docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 lists among the critical geometries:

> **`C_SW1`/`C_SW2` hard against `U_SW` pin 8, span <= 3 mm.**

MEASURED pad-centre to pad-centre from `U_SW.8` (VDD, at 40.250, 50.900):

- `C_SW1.1` (100 nF) — **2.8268 mm**, inside the budget;
- `C_SW2.1` (1 µF) — **6.4595 mm**, **2.15× the declared budget**.

P-ADJ reports PASS with *"tightest margin: PE42482A-X:3V3 U_SW.8->C_SW1.1 2.83mm of 3.0mm"*,
and that is **correct against the budget as declared**: `02_parts/PE42482A-X/part.yaml` writes
it as `{net: 3V3, max_span_mm: 3, anchor_pins: ["8"]}`, and the anchor metric scores the
anchor against its **nearest** qualifying partner — a deliberate, well-argued choice made on
2026-07-29 to stop the old whole-net metric from grading the rail's 21.7 mm diameter.

The consequence is worth naming because it is this session's recurring shape: **a budget of
that form is monotone-satisfiable — it can never fail while one partner is close, no matter
how far the others are.** Adding a second, distant bypass is invisible to it by construction.
So ARCHITECTURE §7 asserts a property of *both* caps that no gate can check and that copper
does not honour for one of them.

**Electrically this is benign and I want to say so plainly**: `C_SW2` is the 1 µF bulk, self-
resonant around 10–20 MHz, hence inductive across this entire band, so its distance barely
matters; the 100 nF that does the control-transient work is at 2.83 mm and inside budget; and
`U_SW` draws 120 µA typ. Nothing here threatens function. It is a documentation-vs-gate
mismatch, filed as P3.

## 5. Summary

The RF engineering on this board is sound and, where I could check it, unusually well
documented. `eps_eff`, `Z0`, `t_pd`, `lambda_g`, the phase constant, `lambda_pp/20`, the
coplanar gap, the In1 voids, the fence verdict, the phase-match spread and the seed-stub
declaration all reproduce under instruments that share no method with the board's own. The
tap fix does what it claims by topology, and the 5943-sample gap denominator in the
ORDER_README matches mine to the sample.

Three things are worth a successor's time, none of them copper:

1. **F1 must be closed before the seal** — a stale, pre-fix verification artifact is hashed
   into the MANIFEST and states an RF aperture the board does not have. One re-run.
2. **F2 is a gate-coverage gap**, not a board defect: `fence_pitch.py` cannot see either end
   of any arm, and five arm-sides carry an In1-referenced aperture over the wall bound there,
   worst 2.1800 mm at ANT7 W. Priced on the board's own model, the cost is ~1 dB of a term
   already below every measurement floor. This belongs in the gate, not in the copper.
3. **F4/F5 are published numbers that are optimistic**, by 3.08 dB (return loss) and 3.04 dB
   (the DC-vs-6 GHz tap table). The designs behind them are right. Fix the numbers before
   they are quoted onward.
