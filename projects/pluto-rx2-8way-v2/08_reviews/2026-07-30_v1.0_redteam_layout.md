# Red-team review — layout / thermal / power-integrity

**What I did.** Every number below was taken by me with `/usr/bin/python3` +
`pcbnew` against
`07_releases/v1.0-2026-07-30/source/pluto_rx2_8way_v2.kicad_pcb`, or derived by
my own closed form from the declared stackup. I did not read the shipped
`fence_pitch.py` / `fence_apertures.py` before writing my own; where I quote
their numbers it is to compare against mine, and the comparison is stated in
both directions (finding L9 is a case where the shipped instrument is
*generous*, not harsh).

**Constants — I regenerate the ADR exactly.** Hammerstad-Jensen with the
Wheeler thickness correction at `w=0.36, h=0.2104, er=4.4, t=0.035, f=6 GHz`:
`eps_eff = 3.3286`, `lambda_g = 27.3868 mm`, `lambda_g/20 = 1.3693 mm`.
ADR-0003 publishes 3.3286 / 27.387 / 1.3693. **The ADR's derivation is
reproducible and correct.** Nothing below disputes the constant set.

**Headline.** The board is DEFECTIVE for one reason that has nothing to do with
the fence, the module or the order paperwork: **the RX1 resistive pickoff is
built as a 10.107 mm branch line, not as the lumped node its own
`DETAIL_DESIGN` section 2 declares.** That branch hits electrical quarter-wave
inside the declared band and takes the RX1 through-path from the published
`-0.432 dB` to a measured-from-geometry **`-13.995 dB` at 4.00 GHz**. The
constraint that would have caught it — `keep_short: RX1_MAIN max_span_mm: 3` in
`02_parts/KH-SMA-KE-Z/part.yaml` — is declared, was deliberately *retained* in a
2026-07-30 correction, is missed by 3.3x, and **no P-ADJ output ships in the
archive.**

## Verdict reasoning

**`design_verdict: DEFECTIVE`.** L1 alone settles it: the board's second
headline requirement (BRIEF G2 — *"element 8 is a tap of the RX1 antenna; RX1
keeps its own path"*) is broken by −13.6 dB against its own published figure
across roughly 2–5.5 GHz, the defect is in copper rather than in a document, and
a declared budget that names the exact failure mode was retained and not graded.
L3–L8 are independently sufficient to withhold a seal on evidence grounds: the
archive proves connectivity and DRC and proves nothing about the RF property it
exists to deliver.

**`order_verdict: DO-NOT-ORDER`.** Cross-checked in both directions.

- *Against the release's own state:* the A-ROT block is real and correctly
  reasoned — `C504007` is a **5-pad** part whose four ground posts must engage
  four 1.4 mm barrels, so a 45° placement error is a part that will not go in,
  not a cosmetic one; `C5121458` is a 34-pad QFN; `C2286` has 180°-asymmetric
  graphics with symmetric pads. `fab/rotations_unsourced.csv` states each
  correctly and the staged CPL is marked NOT ORDERABLE. That alone is
  DO-NOT-ORDER and it is *outside this board's edit partition*.
- *Independent of A-ROT:* L16 would hold the order anyway. With no
  `ORDER_README` and no `MANIFEST`, the package as staged buys the wrong fab
  tier (standard, whose 0.30 mm drill leaves 0.20 mm hole-to-hole on the QFN-24
  against a 0.50 mm floor — `nets.yaml` `fab_tier`), no impedance control on a
  board whose product is an impedance, no through-hole line selected for the ten
  `C504007` jacks (50 plated holes, F.Paste on none), and no instruction that
  `U_MCU` is user-supplied and must not be placed or sourced.
- *And in the other direction — what would NOT block it:* sourcing is clean.
  `verification/stock_check.txt` shows 11/11 coded lines at ≥ 5× build quantity
  (thinnest is `C5121458` at 1294 for 5 boards, then `C3716677` at 5838);
  `bom_source_check.txt` PASS; `bom_legibility.txt` 11/11 MPN cross-checked.
  So this is **not** `BLOCKED-SOURCING` — the parts exist and are in stock. The
  order is blocked on paperwork and on a rotation authority table, both fixable,
  and on L1, which is not.
