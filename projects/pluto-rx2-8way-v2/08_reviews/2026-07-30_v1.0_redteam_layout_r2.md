## Two notes the next agent should not have to re-derive

1. **The `rf_launch` and `ctrl_escape` rule areas are inert.** Both are written
   with `(tracks allowed)(vias allowed)(pads allowed)(copperpour allowed)(footprints allowed)`,
   i.e. they forbid nothing. They still work for their actual purpose — they are
   `insideArea()` anchors for the two scoped-clearance DRU rules — but anyone
   reading them as keepouts (the names invite it) will be wrong. In particular
   `rf_launch` does **not** hold the GND pour off the switch land, which is part
   of why L-02 is true right through the launch box.
2. **`RX1_TAP` is the one arm with no In1.Cu void anywhere along it and 94.3 %
   coplanar coverage** — 25.018 mm of copper, 0 vias, 3.770 mm of detour against
   its 21.258 mm chord, correctly excluded from the match group. Its 2.807 mm and
   2.005 mm fence apertures are the two largest in L-01 after ANT4's.
